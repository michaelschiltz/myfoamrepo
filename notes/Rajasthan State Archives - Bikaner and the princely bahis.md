# Rajasthan State Archives — Bikaner and the princely bahis

Bikaner. **Two record traditions in one repository**: Mughal administrative documents received by the Rajput states — *farmans*, *nishans*, *manshurs*, *akhbarat*, *vakil* reports, *arzdashts*, *khatoot*, in Persian — and the princely states' own administration — ***bahiat*, *pattas*, *parwanas*, *rukkas*, *chithiat***, in Rajasthani.

⚠️ **No holdings figures were established.** A recurring claim of "over one lakh Persian and Rajasthani documents" comes from an AI-generated encyclopedia and is not used here. A published account of digitisation and publication projects for the Persian documents exists but **could not be fetched** in this pass (403).

## Why it matters

**The *bahiat* are the only substantial body of Indian account books in a public archive found in this pass** — and they are **state** books: revenue, household, grants. **The bazaar's books are not here** — see [[Indian merchant account books - bahi khata and kadai kanakku]]. For an institution coded from a merchant's ledger, this archive is a near miss; for the state's transactions with bankers and grain dealers it may be the best place in northern India. ⚠️ Untested.

**Access**: on site; microfilming, reference library and research rooms. ⚠️ Terms and any online catalogue not established.
