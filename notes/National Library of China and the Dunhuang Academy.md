# National Library of China and the Dunhuang Academy

**About 16,000 Dunhuang items at the National Library of China** — what was not carried off, plus recoveries such as the **621 scrolls moved from the Lüshun Museum** out of the Ōtani material. The NLC runs an IDP centre: **1,448 items and 46,527 images since the beginning of 2023**, including 836 "new-pressmark" items, and **120 volumes published with NLC Press by the end of 2025**. ⚠️ Which series those volumes belong to was not established.

**The Dunhuang Academy** — the site's own institution, at the Mogao caves — **launched a "Digital Library Cave" platform** in 2025. ⚠️ Contents and terms not examined.

## The honest assessment

**Central for the cave's own record and for the Chinese transcription programmes** — see [[The Silk Road layer 3 is a national editorial programme]] — **and not where the census's items are**: the *she* bylaws are split between London, Paris, Beijing and Turfan, and the largest documentary shares are British and Russian.

**What China holds that no one else does is the site**, the archaeological context, and the editorial capacity that has been turning other countries' holdings into transcriptions for thirty years.
