# Comanda coding collated with the editors' study 2026-11-01

**Withhold this note by slug (`madurell-translation-test`) from any bundle that codes or recodes `comanda` or `comanda_alloc`.** It states the values of eight comanda cells and what the editor of the source says against or for them.

The comanda pass of 2026-10-27 coded 255 Barcelona acts from the OCR text layer of Madurell Marimón & García Sanz 1973 and never read the edition's own introduction. That introduction, García Sanz's study of pp. 9-148, was read from the page images on 2026-11-01 as a translation test, and collated against the coding. The record is `records/COLLATION-madurell-study-2026-11-01.md` in `HistorEE_codebooks`; the translation is in Drive, `translations/Madurell-GarciaSanz-1973/`, Docs 01-14.

## What kind of witness

- **Not independent on content.** García Sanz edited the documents; his reading of them and the census's are one reading twice. See [[Split provenance into priority and independence]].
- **Independent on appearance.** He read the originals and the coder read the OCR, so on dates, amounts and names a disagreement is a question for the page image.
- **Not independent on doctrine** of Martínez Gijón 1966, whom he says he follows (pp. 74-76).

## What it found

- **Four coded years disagree with the editors** of 48 they date: doc 17 in the instance file (1252 against 1263), docs 55, 178 and 251 in the act census; doc 255, undated, is placed in the second half of the 14th century against the census's `15th c.` No sample instance changes 25-year bin. A first draft reported seven conflicts in the instance file; five were not, and the record says why.
- **A fixed fee on capital the census has not read:** 2-3 per cent outward and 2 per cent on return, from 1426 (docs 214, 238). If the clauses read as reported, it is evidence against `LR2` = coupled.
- **The agent-bears acts are exchanges in the editor's reading.** Docs 3 and 191, the two acts against `CF2`, are a proto-exchange and a trajectitious exchange *sine risico*; doc 93 is bottomry.
- **A *comanda-seguro*.** Doc 255: a Florentine carrier takes the whole risk of saffron to Geneva in comanda form. The next directed reading for `MC1` and `PR1`.
- **Cessions are novations between kin**, with the agent's consent (`CI3`).
- **The 48 abbreviated acts** are register notes whose style clauses the full deed expanded (pp. 133-134): open item 4 of the coding notes, now answerable by convention, and MS's to decide.
- **The printed set** joins a census of the cathedral archive to a purposive selection from the notarial registers (p. 143).
