# Ōkurashō 1915 — a Finance Ministry survey of mujin with eleven rulebooks

大蔵大臣官房銀行課 編『無尽ニ関スル調査』 (大正4, 1915; 398 pp.), **open online without login at NDL Digital, pid 944336, public domain**. Compiled by the Finance Ministry's bank section in the year of the 無尽業法 ⚠️ verify the sequence; a different selector, and twenty-one years earlier, than [[Norinsho 1938 - forty-three ko rulebooks in print]].

**The appendix prints eleven rulebooks**, numbered 一 to 十一 (frames 137–147; on frame *f* the right page is printed page 2*f* − 18, the left 2*f* − 17). As recorded by the blind coder of 2026-10-22: two 沖縄縣 模合 (金四拾圓模合規約書例, 壹圓模合契約書例) and a third 模合 with no place; three from 埼玉縣北葛飾郡早稻田村 (永續講, 1902 among them) and a fourth from 埼玉縣 (1912); two **model rulebooks with blanks** from 鳥取縣; one from 島根縣 (1914), a goods fund paying in cloth; and the 大正共益講規則 of 高知市.

## Why it matters

- **The only unread, independent, public set of kō rulebooks found at NDL** in the catalogue scan of 2026-10-22 ([[RD-test coding and re-test session 2026-10-22]]). It was used for the `RD-test` re-test, so it is now spent for blind coding.
- **It holds the licensing template** behind the Tottori and Shimane 管理人 with unlimited liability ([[Where ko rules name a bearer beyond the drawer's security it follows the drafting regime]]).
- **It reaches regions the census has not coded**: Okinawa, Saitama, Tottori, Shimane, Kōchi.

## Cautions

- **A regulator's selection, at the moment of regulation.** The bank section chose 數例, examples, for a survey written as the business was being brought under statute. Two are model texts, not any association's rules in force.
- **Not independent cases.** Three rulebooks share one village (早稻田村); the two Tottori models and the Shimane rule share one template.
- **Spent.** Its eleven rulebooks were read by the re-test's coder, and 例一 and 例二 in part by the operator.
