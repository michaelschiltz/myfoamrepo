# Voigtländer and Voth measured the relaxation time and reported a mixture

A candidate WP3 case. The framing matters more than the verdict, because the obvious framing is wrong and a referee will say so.

## Why the obvious attack fails

The tempting move is to restate the five conditions for a valid space-for-time substitution and show the design violates them. That attack collapses on contact, because **Voigtländer and Voth tested several of the conditions themselves and published the failures.** Their Table X is a mixing-time analysis in all but name: Hanseatic membership, an openness index for southern cities, and post-1750 population growth each drive the transmission coefficient to zero. A footnote separates migration from industrialisation and finds inflow does the work. A further footnote computes migration at roughly two per cent per generation before 1820 and ten per cent after, across nineteen generations.

That is a relaxation-time calculation and an estimate of where mixing has occurred. Voth's later warning against reading persistence coefficients as structural parameters compounds the difficulty: naming him as the demonstration case invites the reply that the concession came from inside the house.

## The framing that survives

The objection is not that they ignored mixing. It is that, **having measured heterogeneous mixing times across the sample, they pooled the units into a single coefficient and reported the residual as persistence.** A coefficient estimated across units with different relaxation times is a mixture, not a parameter. Regime homogeneity fails, and it fails on their own evidence.

Written that way the case holds. Written the obvious way it does not.

## The independent objection

The stronger and genuinely novel line is the sample construction, treated separately at [[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]]: the estimation sample requires a Jewish community at both endpoints, so inclusion is conditioned on non-absorption six centuries downstream of the treatment, and the reported balance tests cannot detect it because they run within the selected sample.

## Statistical fragility, recorded but not load-bearing

The rhetorical confidence far exceeds the statistical content. The headline six-fold claim rests on nineteen events in 232 treated towns against one event in 88 controls — twenty events in total, one of them in the control group. Adjusted R² across the main specifications runs roughly 0.04 to 0.10; the first principal component of the six outcomes captures 27 per cent of sample variance. In the pre-plague chain, *Judensau* sculptures appear in about 5 per cent of pogrom towns and in **none** of the ninety controls, and perfect separation on that few observations is characteristically a coding artefact rather than a finding.

Coding is documentary survival, and non-attestation is read as non-occurrence in a source whose survival probability tracks city size, ecclesiastical presence and later record-keeping — which also track the outcomes. This is the same defect [[The deficit reading of absence is the scalar ranking in evidentiary form]] identifies in the historiographical register.

None of this is a WP3 argument. It is ordinary empirical criticism, it does not need the framework, and leaning on it would make the work package look like a literature review with a formalism attached.

## What must not be conceded

Two of their moves meet the standard this project imposes on itself, and dismissing them would forfeit that standard.

The DNVP test is a genuine differential prediction. After the Rathenau murder the DNVP expelled its radical antisemites and the DVFP split away; pogrom towns show DVFP gains against DNVP losses of comparable size. The project's own methodology holds that comparison falsifies only where stated as a differential prediction. They stated one and it survived.

The pre-plague chain — earlier attacks, *Judensau*, the 1819 riots — is a different design from the headline regression, closer to a sparse within-locality series than to a cross-section, and it must be attacked on coding grounds rather than on space-for-time grounds.

## The title-text gap, which is the more interesting object

The text disclaims the level claim: a footnote states the results do not suggest local deep-rooted hostility is what enabled the party's electoral rise, and the conclusion concedes the correlation vanishes by 1933. The *title* asserts what the text refuses.

That gap is the mechanism by which a conditional-mean finding on a few per cent of variance enters the wider literature as a structural fact, and it is a better WP3 exhibit than the regressions. Compare [[Whiggish history of finance names the ascent narrative]]: in both cases the defect survives the author's own hedging because it lives in the framing rather than in the estimate.

## Rival accounts of the treatment

Finley and Koyama attribute plague-era persecution intensity to political fragmentation — bishoprics, archbishoprics, imperial free cities — rather than to local sentiment. Becker and Pascali find the geography of German antisemitism inverting after 1517 with the Protestant loss of the moneylending advantage. Both imply the treatment proxies a structural variable with dynamics of its own, against which the paper's defence (that medieval correlates do not predict twentieth-century outcomes) is underpowered.
