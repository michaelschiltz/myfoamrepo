# Extending a pool along kin proximity buys N without buying decorrelation

The moral radius and the risk radius of a cooperating group are ordered by different variables, and the two orderings are anti-aligned. This is not a detail of one tradition's ethics; it is a constraint on where a pooling institution can grow, and it explains a class of forms that the concentric model cannot generate.

**The moral ordering is by proximity.** Hierocles supplies its sharpest ancient statement — Stobaeus IV.27.23 (4.671-673 W.-H.) [verify line numbers against Hense] — as nested circles running self, household, kin, remoter kin, demesmen, tribesmen, citizens, neighbouring towns, *ethnos*, humankind, with the prescription that the outer circles be drawn inward and the technique of drawing them in being the transfer of kin terms to non-kin. Cicero's *gradus societatis* (*De officiis* 1.53-58) grades the same series in Latin. In both, the sequence of enlargement is fixed by nearness.

**The risk ordering is by correlation.** Under the synchrony-extended variance, pooling raises the time-average growth rate only to the degree that the pooled exposures fail independently; at ρ→1 the operation averages nothing and merely relabels N members as a single unit. See [[Pooling's sign is set by the correlation of its baskets]] and [[A perfectly correlated peril leaves only the time average]].

**The two therefore pull apart.** Kin, neighbours, and the next village share a harvest year, a price, a river, a fire, an epidemic, a tax authority, a raiding party. Extension along Hierocles' circles adds members whose shocks arrive together: N rises while ρ stays near unity, so the realised gain is a fraction of what the member count suggests. A pool that grows the way the moral geometry recommends grows in the direction of its own uselessness. The concentric ordering is not merely a weaker ordering than a risk-optimal one — it is close to the worst available ordering, because proximity is very nearly the definition of shared exposure.

## What the claim predicts

If the constraint is real, pooling institutions that achieved scale should show one of two signatures, and not a smooth concentric expansion.

- **Kin idiom stretched over decorrelated exposures.** The trade diasporas are the candidates: New Julfa, the Sogdian network, the Geniza traders, the *ortoq*. Each extends the language of brotherhood and household along *routes* rather than neighbourhoods — that is, along the axis that lowers ρ — and each does so without the circles being contracted in Hierocles' sense at all. On this reading the diaspora is not an instance of the moral community writ large but a solution to a problem the moral community cannot solve.
- **A proximate pool that stays small and shallow, and covers only idiosyncratic loss.** The village-scale *kō*, *mujin*, and confraternity fund can mutualise a death, a roof, a daughter's dowry — losses that strike one household at a time — and cannot mutualise a famine. Their coverage boundary should track the correlation structure, not the membership boundary. Whether it does is a coding question, not a speculation.

The generalisation to guard against: this does not say kin pools are ineffective. It says their effectiveness is bounded by the *class of loss* they face, and that the bound is set by ρ rather than by N. The two are conflated whenever a pooling institution is credited with resilience on the strength of its size.

## Consequences

Objective 1's census should record, for each coded form, not only who was in the pool but whether the members' exposures were plausibly independent — the same discipline that [[Forms are not independent observations when a village runs a portfolio]] imposes on the units. Without it, a form's pooling facet is scored on membership and reads as pooling where the mathematics says relabelling.

The Fugger case shows the constraint operating on the other side of the ledger: baskets deliberately decorrelated in *activity* while the counterparty, and hence the barrier, stayed concentrated. See [[The Fugger diversified activity while concentrating the barrier]].
