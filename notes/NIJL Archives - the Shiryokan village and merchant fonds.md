# NIJL Archives — the Shiryōkan village and merchant fonds

収蔵歴史アーカイブズ, National Institute of Japanese Literature, Tachikawa. <https://archives.nijl.ac.jp/>

**The nearest thing Japan has to a national repository for early-modern private documents.** Founded as the **Ministry of Education's Shiryōkan in May 1951** to save historical materials endangered by war and postwar upheaval; now the archives division of the NIJL.

## Holdings

- **Owned: 637 fonds, about 500,000 items** — early-modern and modern documents, records and objects: village headmen's papers, merchant houses, domains.
- **Deposited: 18 fonds, about 7,300 items.**
- **Microfilm only: 329 fonds** — the product of the 1970s turn to filming in place; see [[Genchi hozon - keeping documents where they are]].
- Printed catalogues (*Shiryōkan shozō shiryō mokuroku*, now some ninety volumes) as PDFs in the NIJL repository; catalogue data being added online progressively.

## How the fonds arrived — and it matters

**By purchase, gift and deposit, and the purchases came through the book trade.** The Satō family papers of Iwade village, Echigo, arrived in two parts: **5,232 items bought in 1953 from a Tokyo second-hand bookseller**, and **1,075 items found in the village in 1982 and donated by the family**. **One headman's archive, split by the market and reunited by a catalogue thirty years later.** That is [[MOC - Acquisitions and the antiquarian trade]] on the supply side, and it means a Japanese village fonds may be *where the dealer's lot was*, not where the village was.

## Layer and access

- **Layer 1 at fonds and item level for catalogued fonds; layer 2 rarely.** The Echigo *kubiki-gun* fonds page states images are not online and database search is not yet available.
- **Viewing by reservation at least one week ahead**, except microfilm. ⚠️ Photography and reproduction terms not captured.
- **The institute's own journal is a census source**: Matsunaga 1999, behind `ko_hokinosawa_1820` and `ko_kosaburo_1802`, appeared in its *Shiryōkan kenkyū kiyō* 30. See [[Echigo - the NIJL fonds behind the census's Echigo rows]].
