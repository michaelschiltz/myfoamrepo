# Multiplicative amplification is indifferent to what it amplifies

The strongest hereditarian rejoinder to [[Fat tails do not require fat-tailed inputs]] uses this project's own machinery, and it must be answered in print rather than left for a referee to find.

## The rejoinder

Under multiplicative dynamics, small persistent differences in growth rate compound into large differences in terminal outcome. So a trait difference far too small to matter under additive accounting becomes decisive over a lifetime of compounding. Multiplicativity, on this reading, is the hereditarian's ally: it explains how a modest, approximately Gaussian, heritable advantage sits behind a wildly dispersed outcome distribution without any further assumption. Jensen does the work for them.

The rejoinder is correct as far as it goes, and conceding it costs nothing.

## The answer

**Amplification does not discriminate among the things it amplifies.** Under compounding with an absorbing barrier, an early advantage in inherited capital, in a marriage, in a patron, in a network position, or in a single fortunate draw at the right moment is magnified by exactly the same factor as an advantage in endowment. The map from initial advantage to terminal outcome is many-to-one in the *source* of the advantage.

Multiplicativity therefore does not adjudicate between candidate causes; it destroys the possibility of adjudicating between them from outcome data. The greater the amplification, the less the terminal distribution constrains the initial one — the ordering of outcomes becomes progressively more informative about the *path* and less about the *endowment*.

Stated at the level the project uses elsewhere: ergodicity breaking is not an argument that a genetic account is false. It is an argument that the question is not settleable by inspecting the distribution of results, which is what the literature in question does.

## Why the concession strengthens the position

Refusing the rejoinder would require denying that compounding amplifies small differences, which the project asserts everywhere else — [[The ascent narrative is the Jensen error as historiography]], [[Reinforcement selects an attractor the ensemble average never occupies]]. A framework cannot claim amplification when it explains institutional divergence and deny it when an opponent invokes it. Granting the mechanism and denying its discriminating power is both consistent and stronger, because it turns the opponent's premise into a further reason why his inference fails.
