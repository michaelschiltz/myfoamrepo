# Polonio on the Barcelona maritime comandas 1349-1450

Source note for Gloria Polonio Luque's work on the AHPB maritime comandas: the doctoral thesis *Exportación e importación en y desde el Mediterráneo en la Baja Edad Media* (Universitat de Barcelona, 2012; hdl 2445/35571; Zotero TQCM4NCH) and "Mercaderes, protagonistas del comercio exterior de Barcelona en la Baja Edad Media: una visión de las comandas marítimas (1349-1450)", *Acta historica et archaeologica mediaevalia* 31 (2011–2013, publ. 2014), 239–264 (EXRTPC38; printed page = PDF page + 238).

## What it holds

More than three thousand maritime comandas, 1,075 commendataries (77 % merchants; 64.5 % Barcelona citizens), investors 84 % Barcelona citizens, with wives and widows second after merchants and Jews active until 1391; 746 voyages by 473 patrons (2014, 240–241). Notaries kept dedicated registers, e.g. AHPB, Arnau Lledó, *Liber quartus comandarum de viagio* (2014, 245 n. 9).

## Status

**Prosopographical, not clause-level.** The article does not tabulate risk clauses, profit shares or security, so it carries participation and scale, not contract terms. **Of the thesis, only the documentary volume has a usable text layer**; the text volume (824 pp.) has a broken font encoding and needs OCR before it can be read.
