# Fat tails do not require fat-tailed inputs

This is the ergodicity route to Clark, and it reaches him without the cladistic apparatus. [[Never let the units slide from instruments to populations]] records as an open question whether such a route exists; it does, and it is prior to the execution attack rather than a variant of it.

## The naive version, which loses

The tempting argument runs: heritable traits are approximately Gaussian, the outcomes that matter are fat-tailed, therefore the inference from trait to outcome is misspecified. Both premises are weak.

Approximate normality of polygenic traits follows from additive architecture and the central limit theorem, but dominance, epistasis and threshold-liability traits are all markedly non-Gaussian, and fitness itself is not Gaussian. More damagingly, **the Gaussianity of the psychometric measure is imposed rather than observed** — raw scores are normed to a fixed mean and dispersion, so the bell curve is a property of the scoring convention. Height's normality is measured; the psychometric analogue is constructed. Nothing about the latent trait follows from the shape of the instrument. That cuts against the hereditarian, but it also means the premise carries no weight for us either.

## The argument that holds

Run Gibrat. Take a population whose members follow *identical* multiplicative growth processes, with identical log-increments and **no heterogeneity of endowment whatever**. Wealth goes lognormal and the variance of log-wealth grows without bound. Add a reflecting barrier or a resetting mechanism — death, bankruptcy, partition at inheritance — and the stationary distribution acquires a Pareto tail (Kesten). The observed dispersion is reproduced in full by a model containing no trait variation at all.

The consequence is an identification claim, not a magnitude claim. **The distribution of outcomes carries no information about the distribution of endowments unless the dynamics have already been specified.** Inequality of results is exactly what a homogeneous population under multiplicative dynamics with a barrier produces, so an inference running from dispersion of outcomes to dispersion of endowments is unidentified before any data arrive.

This is the same move the vault makes elsewhere against the persistence literature — name which term is unidentified rather than dispute the standard errors. Compare [[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]], which is the selection-side statement of the same defect, and [[Deep-roots variables are symplesiomorphies]], which is the character-side statement.

## Why this form of the objection is worth preferring

It is symmetric, and it does not require asserting that heritable variation is absent or unimportant. That assertion is unfalsifiable-adjacent and reads as motivated. The claim here is that outcome dispersion under non-ergodic dynamics is uninformative about endowment dispersion — a statement about identification, indifferent to whether trait effects are large or small.

It is also independent of the phylogenetic route, which matters for the reason set out at the end of [[Deep-roots variables are symplesiomorphies]]: two foundations reaching one conclusion, neither derived from the other, and the argument survives a referee who declines to accept the ergodicity framework or declines to accept the cladistic import.

## The limit of the claim

This does not show that a genetic account of status transmission is false, and it must not be written as though it did. It shows that the account cannot be established from the distribution of outcomes. Clark's actual evidence is the *decay of correlation with lineage distance*, not the dispersion of status, so this note constrains the interpretation of his result rather than refuting his estimate. The complementary objections are [[Never let the units slide from instruments to populations]] on the covariance structure and [[A persistence parameter stable across regime change indicts the measure]] on the invariance.
