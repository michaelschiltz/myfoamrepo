# Source editions — Low Countries and the Hanse

**The Dutch printed record is exceptionally well served: the whole *Rijks Geschiedkundige Publicatiën* (RGP) "Bronnen" programme is free at Huygens retroboeken.** The Hanse's great editions are free too.

## The RGP trade and business series (all free, Huygens retroboeken)

- **Winkelman (ed.), *Bronnen voor de geschiedenis van de Nederlandse Oostzeehandel in de zeventiende eeuw*** — **six volumes, 1971–1983 (RGP GS 133, 161, 178, 184, 185, 186), not one.** The census's "Winkelman 1983" is **deel IV**. Vols II and IV–VI calendar Amsterdam notarial **freight contracts, protested bills and *bodemerijen*** (notaries Bruyningh, Meerhout and others, 1593–1625); vol. III adds notarial acts 1585–1600 and the merchant archive of Claes van Adrichem. **Charter-parties are c. 85% of vols IV–VI; insurance and partenrederij contracts are not covered.** ⚠️ The Huygens description page mislabels the series with Poelman's GS numbers.
- **Poelman (ed.), *Bronnen tot de geschiedenis van den Oostzeehandel, 1122–1499*** (RGP GS 35–36, 1917).
- **Smit (ed.), *Bronnen tot de geschiedenis van den handel met Engeland, Schotland en Ierland, 1150–1585*** (RGP GS 65, 66, 86, 91).
- **Sneller, Unger & Drost (eds), *Bronnen tot de geschiedenis van den handel met Frankrijk*** (RGP GS 70, 190, 211; 1930–89) — **freight contracts and merchant–skipper agreements**, a direct comparator to Winkelman.
- **Heeringa & Nanninga (eds), *Bronnen tot de geschiedenis van den Levantschen handel (1590–1826)*** (RGP GS 9, 10, 34, 95, 115, 120) — the merchant-run **Directie van de Levantse Handel** with its own levies and **convoy-cost sharing**.
- **Van Dillen (ed.), *Bronnen tot de geschiedenis der wisselbanken*** (RGP GS 59–60, 1925) — c. 1,035 items, 1603–1820.
- **Van Dillen (ed.), *Bronnen tot de geschiedenis van het bedrijfsleven en het gildewezen van Amsterdam*** (RGP GS 69, 1929; 78, 1933; 144, 1974), 1512–1672 — guild ordinances and **notarial acts on business life; the main Amsterdam printed source for guild funds and company contracts.** ⚠️ Act-level contents not checked.
- **Posthumus (ed.), *Bronnen tot de geschiedenis van de Leidsche textielnijverheid, 1333–1795*** (RGP GS 8, 14, 18, 22, 39, 49) — guild keuren and welfare funds.

## Antwerp and Bruges

- **Renée Doehaerd (ed.), *Études anversoises: documents sur le commerce international à Anvers, 1488–1514***, 3 vols (Paris: SEVPEN, 1962–63; Ports – Routes – Trafics XIV) — **a French calendar of the aldermen's *certificats* and *lettres échevinales***: the printed layer of [[Antwerp schepenregisters and the regesten]] for the boom years. Print only.
- **G. de Longé (ed.), *Coutumes du pays et duché de Brabant. Quartier d'Anvers***, 6 vols (Brussels, 1870–77; *Recueil des anciennes coutumes de la Belgique*) — the Antwerp *costuymen* from the *Antiquissimae* to the **Compilatae of 1608**, Dutch with French translation: **the text behind `compagnie_antwerpen_1582` and `compagnie_antwerpen_1608`**. ⚠️ Which volume holds the Compilatae titles on companies, sea law and insurance unconfirmed; Monballyu's online transcriptions timed out.
- **L. Gilliodts-van Severen, *Cartulaire de l'ancienne estaple de Bruges***, 6 vols (Bruges, 1904–06), and its sequel on the *grand tonlieu* (1908) — merchant nations, brokerage, maritime rules; **free on HathiTrust**.
- **Gilliodts-van Severen, *Cartulaire de l'ancien consulat d'Espagne à Bruges*** (Bruges, 1902–) — **the Castilian *nación* at Bruges, the direct link to the *consulado* model**; free on archive.org. ⚠️ Volume count unverified.
- **Studies that print documents**: Denucé, *Inventaire des Affaitadi, banquiers italiens à Anvers, de l'année 1568* (1934); Goris, *Étude sur les colonies marchandes méridionales à Anvers* (1925); Brulez on the Della Faille firm (1959).

## The Hanse

- ***Hansisches Urkundenbuch***, 11 vols (1876–1939; vol. 7.2 never published) — free PDFs from the Hansischer Geschichtsverein, archive.org, HathiTrust. The privileges of the Kontore.
- ***Hanserecesse***, four divisions, 26 vols (1870–1970) — diets, correspondence; **wreck, salvage, shipmasters, the collective levy (*Schoss*)**. Free (HGV, MDZ, archive.org).
- ***Codex diplomaticus Lubecensis* / *Urkundenbuch der Stadt Lübeck*** (from 1843; 11 vols to 1470 + register) — partnership and ship-share precedents (Sendeve, Gesellschaft). Free at MDZ.
- **Wilhelm Stieda (ed.), *Hildebrand Veckinchusen. Briefwechsel eines deutschen Kaufmanns im 15. Jahrhundert*** (Leipzig, 1921) — **547 letters of the best-documented Hanseatic merchant partnerships and their failure in Bruges** — a case for [[The record of non-survivors survives where failure was administered]]. ⚠️ Account-book editions (Lesnikov 1973; Lesnikov & Stark 2013) unverified.
- ***Inventare hansischer Archive des sechzehnten Jahrhunderts*** (Kölner Inventar from 1896; Danziger Inventar 1913) — the Antwerp Kontor years. ⚠️ Snippets only.
- **Sound Toll Registers Online** — already recorded: [[The Sound Toll Registers]].
