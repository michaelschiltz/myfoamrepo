# Rijksarchief in België — the state archives network

<https://www.arch.be/> · search: <https://search.arch.be/>

The federal state archives: the Algemeen Rijksarchief in Brussels plus a network of provincial repositories — Antwerp, Beveren, Bruges, Ghent, Hasselt, Liège, Namur and others.

## What is online

Free access to **millions** of digitised documents through AGATHA, with the digitisation programme aimed at *ruggengraatreeksen*: backbone series chosen for research value rather than by demand. Eight published categories, of which **medieval records** and **early modern period documents** are the relevant ones here.

⚠️ **Registration is required to view images.** No CC0 dedication, no OAI-PMH endpoint and no bulk-download route are advertised. Establish the reuse terms explicitly before any image enters a working set — this is a register column that cannot be left blank, and the Dutch answer must not be assumed to carry across a border.

## What the state archives hold for this project

**Notarial protocols.** In Belgium the old notarial archives are largely with the Rijksarchief rather than with the city archives — the opposite of the Dutch arrangement, where Amsterdam's notariate is municipal. See [[Rijksarchief te Antwerpen and Beveren - the notarial series]]. **Getting this backwards costs a trip**, and it is the single most likely Belgian mistake for someone arriving from the Dutch material.

**Ecclesiastical and institutional fonds** from the suppressed religious houses, which is one of the three places beguinage records can be — see [[Begijnhof archives - where the beguinages records are]].

**Provincial and seigneurial jurisdictions**, and the aldermen's benches of the smaller towns.

⚠️ Reference codes, extents and imaging status are unestablished for every series named above.
