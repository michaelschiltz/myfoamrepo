# Maharashtra State Archives — the Peshwa Daftar

The Pune record office of the Maharashtra State Archives holds the **Peshwa Daftar**, the fiscal and administrative archive of the Maratha state to 1818, in **Modi**.

**Scale: not established.** Popular and examination sources repeat "about five crore" — fifty million — Modi documents at Pune; the figure circulates without a source and **should not be printed**. ⚠️ One reference listing gives the Maharashtra State Archives "over 500,000 files" with Peshwa material from 1666 to 1818, and that source is an AI-generated encyclopedia, so it is worth no more than the first. **Both are recorded here only to be checked.**

## Why it is the most interesting unread archive in the survey

**It is a pre-colonial state's own fiscal record, continuous, in one place** — the thing the Ottoman pass found at Istanbul and the Chinese pass found split across a strait. **The Maratha state taxed, borrowed from and adjudicated between exactly the bankers and merchant houses whose own books did not survive** — see [[Indian merchant account books - bahi khata and kadai kanakku]]. A *daftar* series on state borrowing from the Poona sowkars would be the nearest South Asian equivalent of the Edo magistrate's record.

**And it is behind a script wall that machines have only just begun to lower** — [[Modi script and the forty million documents]]. Selections were printed from the 1930s in the *Selections from the Peshwa Daftar* series (45 volumes). ⚠️ Volume count and dates from general knowledge; not verified. **That printed selection, not the archive, is what most historians have used** — the Chinese compilation problem again.
