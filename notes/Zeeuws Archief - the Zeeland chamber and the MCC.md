# Zeeuws Archief — the Zeeland chamber and the MCC

<https://www.zeeuwsarchief.nl/> · Middelburg

Two holdings, and the second is the reason to come.

## The Zeeland chamber of the VOC

The chamber archives sit here rather than at [[Nationaal Archief - The Hague]], and they are **not part of the GLOBALISE corpus**, which targets the incoming correspondence at The Hague. So the Zeeland material is layer 1–2 while the OBP is layer 3 — **an asymmetry inside one company's archive**, and one worth naming, because a researcher working from the transcribed corpus will silently be working from the Amsterdam-centred half of the VOC.

## The Middelburgsche Commercie Compagnie

**Toegang `20`, `Middelburgsche Commercie Compagnie (MCC), 1720–1889`**, inscribed on the UNESCO **Memory of the World** register ⚠️ *inscription year to confirm*.

The MCC was a chartered trading company — principally a slave-trading company — whose archive survives with exceptional completeness: the company's own governance records together with the ships' papers, accounts and voyage documentation. **For this project its value is that it is a complete company archive of modest size**, where the VOC's is vast and the EIC's is behind a paywall.

That makes it the right place to ask a question the VOC cannot answer cleanly: **what a chartered company's constitution looked like when the company was small**. The forms the census codes — capital lock-in, transferable claims, perpetual succession — were designed for bodies of very different scale, and the literature reads them almost entirely off the two giants. [[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]] is the reason a second, smaller Dutch company matters: where the MCC's incidental drafting differs from the VOC's while the structure agrees, that is evidence about how much of the VOC's charter was necessary and how much was circumstance.

**The ethical dimension is not separable from the archival one.** This is the record of a slave-trading enterprise, and its completeness is why it was inscribed. Any use of it in a project about institutional durability has to be explicit that the institution whose durability is being described was in this case an instrument of the trade. The data management plan's rights and ethics section is where that gets stated; it should not be left to a footnote.
