# Stadtarchiv Augsburg and the Staatsarchiv

City archive: <https://www.augsburg.de/kultur/stadtarchiv-augsburg/die-bestaende> · Staatsarchiv Augsburg: <https://www.gda.bayern.de/augsburg>, part of the Bavarian archive administration

**Two archives in one city, on different tiers, and the split is the ordinary German situation.** The *Stadtarchiv* holds the free imperial city's own record; the *Staatsarchiv* holds what came to the Bavarian state, with a printed *Beständeübersicht* for the *Altes Reich*.

## Why Augsburg

Because the sixteenth century's two greatest merchant-banking houses were Augsburg firms, and the city's civic record is the frame their business sat in. The Fugger papers are private — [[Fuggerarchiv - Dillingen]] — but **the city's own record of them is public**: tax assessments, civic offices, guild and trading privileges, litigation.

**That asymmetry is usable.** Where a firm's own archive is closed or conditional, the city's record of the firm is an independent witness to the same institution, and it was created by a body with no interest in the firm's reputation. [[Independence of apparatus is not independence of question]] is usually a warning; here it points at a remedy.

⚠️ Series, extents and imaging unverified for both archives.
