# Skin in the game

The binding of a party's payoff to the venture's *realized* trajectory — the property that risk-sharing instruments enforce and that the joint-stock veil dissolves. Its loss is staged in [[The four-step decoupling from participation to the veil]]; its doctrinal defense is [[Islamic doctrine refuses risk-commodification at step one]].

Taleb's register, and the stub that stood here asked for it to be made rigorous. The apparatus to do so is now in place.

## The formal statement

**Skin in the game is a claim about which trajectory carries the absorbing barrier.** Per [[An absorbing barrier breaks ergodicity by construction]], an absorbing set is invariant, and shielding does not remove it but *relocates* it — it changes which trajectory the invariant set is attached to. Skin in the game is the condition in which the decision-maker's own trajectory carries it. Its absence is the condition in which the barrier sits on some other trajectory: a separable legal person, a counterparty, a guarantor, or the state.

So skin in the game and [[Entity-shielding]] are **inverse operations on the same object**, and the object is well defined. That is the rigorous content, and it requires no appeal to fairness, incentive, or desert.

## Two things that must be separated

Taleb runs together two bindings the vault holds apart, and they come apart in the material.

**Barrier-exposure.** Whether the party's own trajectory can be absorbed. This is the condition above.

**Outcome-coupling.** Whether the payoff tracks the realised result rather than a return fixed independently of it. This is the second binding of [[Lock-in and outcome-coupling are distinct bindings]] and the condition of legibility in [[Outcome-coupling is the condition of legibility]].

The two are independent, and the discriminating case is already in the vault: [[Skin in the game is asymmetric in the mudaraba]]. The *muḍārib* is fully outcome-coupled — no fixed return, payoff welded to the venture — and **not** barrier-exposed, since capital loss falls on the *rabb al-māl*. Outcome-coupling without barrier-exposure. Conversely the *ʿisqa* agent, per [[The isqa refuses the agent's shield]], carries exposure the *qirāḍ* agent does not.

**Any sentence using "skin in the game" unqualified is therefore ambiguous between two conditions that the comparative material separates.** Say which one. The phrase is admissible as a heading and not as a variable.

## The finding that qualifies Taleb from inside

Taleb defends skin in the game as an **incentive** device: exposure aligns the agent because the agent bears the consequence. The vault's result is that under the dynamics the project studies, its **evidentiary** function degrades exactly where the incentive rationale is strongest.

[[Non-ergodicity collapses the likelihood ratio on outcomes]]: under multiplicative dynamics with a barrier, honest failure is a normal feature of the honest trajectory, the likelihood ratio on a bad outcome collapses toward one, and every mechanism that reads conduct off results degrades in proportion. Coupling an agent to a ruin-dominated venture couples him to noise. So exposure may continue to align incentives while ceasing to generate evidence — and the institutional response, per [[Institutions substitute state-verification for outcome-inference as ruin exposure rises]], is to substitute verification for inference.

That is a genuine qualification of the doctrine rather than an application of it, and it is available because the project has the dynamics and Taleb has the aphorism.

## Register

Do not inherit the ethical framing. Taleb treats the absence of skin in the game as a moral failure — bailouts, agency, cheating. The project treats shielding as a **technology** whose consequences are traced: it relocates the barrier and thereby changes which orderings of shocks are lethal, which is [[Terminal wealth is permutation-invariant but survival is not]] and [[The ensemble average is the local descriptor of a barrier-shielded agent]]. The first is a claim about desert, the second about dynamics, and a book that slides between them will be read as polemic. The same caution governs the inferential direction: [[Taleb reads survival as evidence about the survivor and we read it as evidence about the filter]].
