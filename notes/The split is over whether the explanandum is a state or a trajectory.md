# The split is over whether the explanandum is a state or a trajectory

The difference between the orthodox framing and the ergodicity framing is not static versus dynamic, and it is not that one attends to sequence and the other does not. It is a difference in **what is taken to require explanation.**

Orthodox practice explains *states*. It asks what conditions make a configuration self-consistent — why the system is here, why no participant wishes to deviate, why the accounts balance. The question is answered by exhibiting simultaneous conditions and showing they are jointly satisfiable, and it is well posed whether or not anything is iterated.

The ergodicity framing explains *trajectories*. It asks what becomes of an entity that must keep going and cannot step outside its own path — what it grows at, what it survives, what removes it. The question is answered by following the dynamics forward and reporting a rate, a hitting time, or an attractor.

The two questions are not rival answers to one problem. They are different problems, and they come apart precisely where the dynamics are non-ergodic in the sense the vault is careful about: a configuration can be self-consistent at every date while every trajectory through it declines. [[The sustainability criterion assumes the ergodicity it should be testing]] is this abstract point in concrete form — Philip's debt passes a state test built on period means while the realised path crosses the line twice, at the two dates that are the defaults.

The diagnostic value is that most "why does X occur despite its cost" puzzles are **state questions in disguise**. They presuppose that behaviour must be justified by a payoff comparison at a moment, and the residual they cannot account for is then attributed to preferences, reputation, or norms. Restated as trajectory questions many of them dissolve without any additional mechanism; the procedure is in [[Restate the cost puzzle as a question about a trajectory]].

## What this does not license

It does not license the claim that state explanations are wrong. Where the dynamics are ergodic over the relevant horizon the two questions have the same answer, and the state question is easier. The claim is that the equivalence is a *condition*, that the condition is checkable, and that it is currently assumed rather than checked — which is the hygiene argument of [[Route Kolmogorov to ergodic theory not the theory of means]] restated at the level of the explanandum rather than the operator.

Nor does it license reading the orthodoxy as unaware of time. See [[Arrow-Debreu dissolves time rather than neglecting it]] for why the accurate charge is narrower, and [[Do not claim history-dependence as the novelty]] for what must not be claimed.
