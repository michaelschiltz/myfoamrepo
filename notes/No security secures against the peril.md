# No security secures against the peril

A pledge, a surety or a general obligation of goods secures whatever obligation survives the allocation of the peril. It never secures the capital against the peril itself. The bottomry pledge is the proof: the ship secures repayment only if the ship arrives, and when the peril strikes the pledge goes down with the debt. The lender's protection against the sea is the premium, not the security.

**Why this needed saying.** The comanda batch put `RB3 = general-estate` on the Catalan loss rows against `RB3 = none` on the Genoese commenda, and the 2026-10-09 retrieval report then called `RB3` defective: the Catalan pledge "secures performance, not the capital against the peril", so it seemed to sit misleadingly beside the *bodemerij*'s hypothecation of the ship. The objection proves too much. Every value in `RB3` secures performance of the obligation the allocation leaves standing: repayment on arrival in bottomry, account and restitution of what was not lost by an excused cause in the comanda, contributions in arrears in a kō. What separates the forms is the **object** of the security (the venture's own asset, the agent's whole estate, a parcel of land, a third party), and the object is exactly what `RB3` records. Who bears the peril is `RB1`; whether the obligation survives a total loss is `RB4`. Read together, the three cells say what a split of `RB3` would have said, without a new characteristic.

**The distinction that does matter.** A clause that creates a security must be kept apart from a **liability rule** that already exposes the agent's estate. `isqa_alloc RB3 = none` because the manager's liability on the loan half is a rule of the contract's construction, carried at `AP3` and `LR1`; coding it as `general-estate` would count one fact twice. The Catalan *obligando me et omnia bona mea* is a clause the parties write in, which under *ius commune* creates a general hypothec on the agent's goods: Martínez Gijón (1966, 401) calls the guarantees in the Catalan acts "personales y reales a la vez" *(confirmed 2026-10-11; the earlier [to confirm] is discharged)*.

Ruled by MS on 2026-10-11 (`records/RULINGS-comanda-adjudications-2026-10-11.md` in HistorEE_codebooks, ruling 1); `RB3`'s definition carries the sentence.
