# The morphological matrix is dictated by the shape of the feature space

The choice of a Zwicky morphological box to represent the typology of organizational forms is not a display preference. It is forced by three properties of the feature space and one commitment of the project, and the wrong representation would make the figure argue the opposite of the book. A visualization is a claim; this one has to be the claim we are willing to defend.

## What the data is

Three properties constrain any honest representation.

- **Categorical, not metric.** The characteristics are binary or nominal — *has legal personality*, *authorization source* — with no underlying magnitude. There is no distance from an origin, so any radial or continuous encoding fabricates a quantity that does not exist.
- **High-dimensional.** Thirty-one dimensions across eight facets. Any two-axis projection keeps two and discards twenty-nine.
- **Only partially ordered.** Forms are routinely *incomparable*: neither feature-set contains the other, so no line holds them. The corporation does not sit "above" the waqf; their profiles cross. A representation that supplies a single axis invents an order the data denies.

## The commitment

The project refuses the scalar ascent narrative — there is no omega form, and absence is a refusal to be interpreted, not a deficit to be scored (see [[The corporation is not the omega - the naties are an undisplaced niche]], [[The deficit reading of absence is the scalar ranking in evidentiary form]], [[The ascent narrative is the Jensen error as historiography]]). Any figure that reads as *more equals more advanced* contradicts the thesis at the level of the image, before a word is read.

## Why the alternatives fail

- **Radar / spider.** Three faults, the last disqualifying. It imposes false continuity (a spoke length for a yes/no is fiction); its silhouette and enclosed area are artifacts of the arbitrary order the spokes happen to sit in (reorder them and the "shape" changes while the data does not); and the enclosed area reads as completeness, rendering the Whig ascent narrative as geometry. It would draw the joint-stock corporation as the fullest polygon and the waqf as a thin sliver — the exact ranking the book dismantles.
- **Quadrant / 2×2.** Legitimate but narrow. Forcing thirty-one dimensions onto two master axes discards the rest. Its proper job is a single *pairwise* claim — lock-in × outcome-coupling, and the underexplored durable-and-coupled cell (see [[The durable-and-coupled cell is the underexplored one]]) — not the master instrument.
- **Composite index / summed score.** Collapses the profile to one number, which is a total order: the deficit reading in numerical dress. It is the scalar ranking named as a method rather than smuggled in a shape.
- **Node-link graph.** Encodes *relations between* entities; here the data is *attributes of* entities. A similarity graph (dendrogram, MDS) is a legitimate view but a *derived* one — computed from the matrix — not a substitute for it.

## Why the Zwicky box fits

Fritz Zwicky's morphological analysis represents each object as a point in a discrete multidimensional parameter space: each characteristic an independent axis carrying a small, defined set of admissible values. It satisfies exactly the constraints above. It preserves every dimension at once. It admits the non-checkmark states the project needs kept distinct — `na` for inapplicable, `contested` for disputed, `P` for genuine degree — so that refusals and citation uncertainty stay visible on the face of the instrument rather than laundered into presence or absence (see [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]], [[The determinate-contingent typology carries the richness]], [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]]). And it imposes no ordering, so convergence (shared profiles) and incomparability (crossing profiles) remain legible instead of being flattened onto a rank.

The choice also pays forward. Because the box is a coded parameter space, the analytically richer views derive from it without recoding: hierarchical clustering and ordination for the convergence pairs, a Hasse lattice for the partial order that puts no form at the top. None of these is recoverable from a radar or a score. The matrix is the figure that refuses the ranking; the radar is the figure that asserts it, and that is the whole reason to prefer it.
