# Stadsarchief Brugge — the Hanse and the nations

<https://www.brugge.be/> · finding aids: <https://www.archiefbankbrugge.be/>

**Bruges before Antwerp.** The city archive holds the *Oud Archief*, searchable through the Probat inventory system at Archiefbank Brugge, and its **Hanseatic documents are on the UNESCO world register** ⚠️ *listing details to confirm*.

## Why Bruges belongs in the set

Because the merchant *nations* were there first, and the census's `natie` row reaches back through them. The Castilian, Genoese, Florentine, Portuguese and Hanseatic communities at Bruges had consuls, privileges, houses and their own internal jurisdiction — which is the **identity-wrapper** facet the vault separates from the perpetual-monopoly facet in [[Identity-wrapper and perpetual-monopoly are distinct shielding facets]].

It is also the other end of a thread this survey has already picked up in Spain: the **Burgos consulate at Bruges** is a Castilian merchant body operating inside this city's jurisdiction, and [[Consulado de Burgos - the universidad de mercaderes]] records it as an uncoded identity-wrapper with dispersed records. **Bruges is the other half of that custody problem**, and the two notes should be worked together rather than separately.

⚠️ Nothing here has been checked against a finding aid: extent, imaging and the Probat system's coverage are all unestablished.
