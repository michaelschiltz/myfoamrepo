# Answering EU's failures with more psychology instantiates the complaint

Section 5 of [[Doctor Wakker and Wang 2020 on the ergodicity problem]] is the most rhetorically effective move in the document and the most revealing. The argument: expected utility has been falsified for decades — Allais, Hershey and Schoemaker, Starmer's survey — and economists know it, which is why they use prospect theory and the behavioural apparatus recognised by the 2002 and 2017 Nobel prizes. A falsification of EU therefore costs economics nothing.

As a defence of *economics* against a charge levelled at *EU*, this succeeds. As a defence against the charge actually being made, it demonstrates it.

**The replacement is more psychological, not less.** Prospect theory answers EU's failures by adding a probability weighting function and a loss-aversion parameter — two further free functions calibrated to observed behaviour. The complaint was that where an agent faces repeated multiplicative exposure, economics posits a taste rather than asking whether the dynamic was misspecified. The reply is that when the first taste failed, the field added two more. That is the movement being objected to, offered as the answer to the objection.

The authors make the position explicit and quote Peters making it: the field "drifted in a direction that places too much emphasis on psychology." Their answer is to name the psychological turn as the discipline's achievement. Both sentences can be true. What neither party notices is that they are not disagreeing about a fact but about whether the direction was progress, and no falsification of EU bears on that.

**The narrow reply, and the limit of it.** State only this: the behavioural programme is a live and successful research tradition, its results are not in dispute here, and the question of whether observed risk aversion is better read as a preference parameter or as a property of the dynamics is not settled by pointing at prizes. Do not argue that prospect theory is wrong; it is well evidenced and the vault has no business attacking it. Argue that its evidential base — cross-sectional choice experiments over single gambles — is the wrong instrument for distinguishing a taste from a dynamic, because the design holds the horizon at one round and thereby removes the variable in question. The comparison the framework needs is over horizons, and that is what the Copenhagen and related experiments attempt.

**What this means for our use.** The vault should not run the argument as "economics is stuck." It is not stuck; it is well defended and has answered EU's failures energetically. Run it as the hygiene claim instead — that the conditions for the ensemble equivalence are nowhere stated, whichever utility functional sits on top — which is [[Route Kolmogorov to ergodic theory not the theory of means]] and the guardrail at [[Do not claim history-dependence as the novelty]]. Adding parameters to the objective function leaves the averaging operator untouched, and it is the operator the criticism is about.
