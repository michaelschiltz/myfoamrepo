# Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic

Functional agreement across traditions is weak evidence of anything, because function is constrained by the problem. Two societies facing ruin under multiplicative dynamics will both arrive at pooling for the same reason two engineers arrive at the arch. Agreement of that kind cannot separate descent from independent arrival, and a comparison that rests on it is answering [[Galton's problem]] by ignoring it.

The evidence that discriminates lies in the **arbitrary particulars** — the features that had no reason to be one way rather than another. Stemmatics has the canonical version: shared *Leitfehler*, indicative errors, prove common descent precisely because an error is unmotivated, and there is no reason two scribes would independently produce the same mistake. Run in reverse, the diagnostic gives what the comparative programme needs: complex functional agreement carried in *wholly divergent* arbitrary particulars indicates independent arrival, because the shared part is explained by the problem and the divergent part shows no common hand.

In institutional material the arbitrary particulars are the distribution key, the numerical shares, the terminology, the ritual and doctrinal framing, the default rules nobody had to choose that way, the treatment of the residual case. The Aslanian material on New Julfa is the type specimen: a distribution key that differs from the Islamic partnership forms is a *parametric* difference within a shared functional type — the functional agreement is the constrained part, the key is the free part, and it is the key that carries the evidentiary weight.

Two disciplines follow.

**Arbitrariness has to be argued, not assumed.** Some particulars are less free than they look. Halves, thirds and quarters recur because they are natural divisions, not because anyone copied; a term of one year recurs because of the sailing season. A particular counts as evidence only to the extent that the space of alternatives was genuinely open, and establishing that is philological work in its own right.

**The corpus needs a third column.** The coding ontology as specified records the functional component; [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]] adds the actor-stated reason. This note adds the arbitrary particulars themselves, coded as values rather than as presence, so that agreement and divergence in the free parameters can be computed across traditions instead of being adjudicated case by case in prose. Component, rationale, particulars: with all three the independence judgement becomes a property of the corpus, which is what makes [[Transmission versus independent recurrence is the load-bearing test]] testable rather than arguable.

This is also the answer to the objection that the comparison reduces to two cases. It does — see [[The western family needs only plausible common descent not proven genealogy]] — and it does not matter as much as it appears to, because the inference does not run on enumeration. A single pair of traditions agreeing in complex structure while diverging in every free parameter is stronger evidence than twenty cases counted without regard to their ancestry.
