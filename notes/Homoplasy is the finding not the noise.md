# Homoplasy is the finding not the noise

[[Galton's problem]] states a burden of proof. It does not supply an instrument for discharging one. Phylogenetic systematics spent a century building that instrument, under a vocabulary — homology, analogy, homoplasy — that the comparative-institutions literature borrows loosely and therefore uselessly. Imported precisely, the vocabulary does three things: it names what the project is actually claiming, it converts the arbitrary-particulars heuristic into a procedure, and it exposes three preconditions the historical material does not meet. The first two are gains. The third is the referee's opening, and it is better written down here than discovered in the report.

## The three-way distinction, which is usually collapsed into two

Owen's 1843 contrast is the origin: **homology** is the same organ under every variety of form and function; **analogy** is the same function discharged by different structures. Homology is a claim about ancestry, analogy a claim about the job. Lankester in 1870 supplied the pattern-level term for similarity that is real but not inherited — **homoplasy** — and thereby separated the *inference* (these are alike, and not by descent) from the *functional observation* (these do the same work).

The collapse to be avoided is treating functional agreement as though it were evidence about descent in either direction. It is not. Function is constrained by the problem, so analogy is compatible with descent, with independent arrival, and with anything in between; this is the point already made in [[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]], and the phylogenetic vocabulary simply gives it a name and a place in a system.

Hennig then splits homology itself, and this second cut is the one institutional historians consistently miss. Only **shared derived** homology — synapomorphy — groups anything. **Shared ancestral** homology — symplesiomorphy — is genuine homology and evidentially inert: a feature inherited from a substrate that every tradition in the comparison shares tells you nothing about which traditions are close to which. Establishing common descent is therefore not the same as establishing a grouping, and a demonstration that two forms both descend from a Sasanian legal substrate is, on its own, a demonstration of nothing about their relationship to each other.

## Homoplasy has three species and the project needs one of them

Homoplasy is a genus. Its species are not interchangeable, and the difference between two of them is the difference between the project's headline result and a considerably weaker one.

- **Convergence** — the same state reached independently from *different* ancestral conditions. This is independent arrival in the full sense.
- **Parallelism** — the same state reached independently from the *same* ancestral condition, where a shared substrate disposes both lineages toward the same solution. No one copied; the outcome was not free either.
- **Reversal** — secondary loss returning a lineage to an ancestral state, which then counterfeits similarity with anything that never left it.

The independence claim in Objective 1 requires convergence. A shared Sasanian legal substrate across the western cluster delivers, at best, parallelism. **This is the technical name for why the Islamic-versus-Mediterranean test failed**, and why the test was relocated to East Asian forms against the western cluster as a whole (see [[The qirad's antecedents are Sasanian not Sogdian]], [[The western family needs only plausible common descent not proven genealogy]]). A referee with any cladistic training will press exactly here, and the concession is worth far more volunteered than extracted.

The terminology in this vault needs auditing accordingly. "Convergence pair" is, in the strict sense, a claim of convergence rather than parallelism, and it has been used without that weight being tested. [[Natie and kabu-nakama are a convergence pair]] very likely survives the audit — there is no plausible shared ancestral condition. Any pair drawn from within the western cluster very likely does not.

Reversal deserves a line of its own, because the project has a live case for it: a tradition that once held a form and abandoned it will resemble a tradition that never held it, and the resulting similarity is an artefact. This is the character-level statement of [[The waqf partition converts absence into refusal]] and [[The deficit reading of absence is the scalar ranking in evidentiary form]] — absence has at least two histories, and the coding must keep them apart.

## Homology is a conclusion, not an observation

The most valuable import is epistemological. Nothing is homologous or homoplastic *in isolation*. De Pinna's distinction is the clean statement: **primary homology** is a conjecture generated from similarity of position and structure — Remane's criteria of topological position, special quality, and continuity through intermediate forms — while **secondary homology** is that conjecture after it has survived testing against the whole character matrix. The test is congruence. A character that fits the tree the other characters imply is retained as homology; a character that requires extra steps is diagnosed as homoplasy, and the consistency and retention indices quantify how much of that residue the dataset carries.

This is what turns the arbitrary-particulars argument from a good instinct into a method. The judgement is not made pair by pair in prose, adjudicating whether *this* distribution key is arbitrary enough to count. It is made once, over the whole matrix, by asking which characters the most parsimonious arrangement cannot accommodate. The Zwicky box specified in [[The morphological matrix is dictated by the shape of the feature space]] is already a character matrix; it has simply not been read as one. Read as one, together with the rationale column of [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]], it makes the independence judgement a computed property of the corpus, which is what [[Transmission versus independent recurrence is the load-bearing test]] requires in order to be testable rather than arguable.

## The inversion, and why it works in the project's favour

In systematics homoplasy is the error term — the thing that misleads you about the tree, to be minimised and explained away. Here it is the finding. Every safeguard cladistics built to stop the analyst mistaking homoplasy for homology has to be run backwards, because this project's characteristic failure is the opposite one: mistaking homology for homoplasy, which is [[Galton's problem]] restated in character terms. The discipline transfers; the direction of the burden does not.

The consequence is better than it first looks. **Parsimony is not on the project's side, and that is the point.** The tree that minimises homoplasy is the tree that maximises transmission, so a homoplasy claim can never be reached by preferring it; it survives only where the most parsimonious arrangement *still* requires independent gains. A hypothesis that must be forced through a criterion actively biased against it is in a far stronger evidentiary position than one argued from the improbability of coincidence. This is the same structure as [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]]: the finding is what remains after a procedure designed to eliminate it.

## Three preconditions the material does not meet

**There is no outgroup.** Character polarity — which state is ancestral, which derived — is established by comparison with a taxon outside the study group. Institutional history has no society outside the comparison; there is no unaffected outgroup against which to root a tree of risk-pooling forms. Without polarity, symplesiomorphy cannot be separated from synapomorphy, and the Hennigian machinery loses its purchase. The available partial repair is stratigraphic: first attestation in the documentary record substitutes for outgroup polarity. It is a weak substitute, because attestation dates are filtered by survival, and treating them as origination dates reintroduces the survivorship error the project exists to attack.

**Characters are not independent.** Parsimony assumes characters evolve independently. The five components of the corporate bundle are functionally coupled — entity shielding is difficult to draft without legal personality, capital lock-in without perpetual succession is unstable — so coding them as five characters counts one innovation five times and inflates apparent homoplasy exactly where the project would most like to find it. Correlated evolution among the components has to be tested before the components are admitted as independent evidence. Absent that test, the five-component framework is a machine for manufacturing the result. See [[Lock-in and outcome-coupling are distinct bindings]] and [[Identity-wrapper and perpetual-monopoly are distinct shielding facets]] — the vault has been separating facets on conceptual grounds, which is necessary but not sufficient; separability has to be shown empirically in the matrix.

**Descent is reticulate, and in one mode it is textual.** Cultural lineages borrow as a matter of course, so the true object is a network rather than a tree, and tree methods will return a tree regardless of whether one exists. Biology's version of this — horizontal gene transfer, hybridisation — is bounded; here it is the normal case. Worse, cultural transmission has a mode with no biological analogue at all: a form can be resurrected from a stored description long after its lineage has died. The twelfth-century reception of Roman law is neither continuous descent nor independent invention, and coding it as either is wrong. It needs its own state in the ontology — call it textual reticulation — because a dead form recovered from a manuscript is precisely the case that will otherwise be scored as convergence and inflate the headline result.

To this a referee may add the general ethnogenetic objection to cultural phylogeny — that societies are not lineages, that their boundaries are constructed and permeable, and that the tree metaphor imports a mode of descent the object does not have. The reply is not to defend the metaphor but to narrow it: the units here are *instruments*, not societies, and instruments are transmitted as documents and drafting practices with far more tree-like descent than the populations that use them.

## Taleb: the transmission-speed claim is a hostage, not an ally

"Ideas travel faster than genes" is a commonplace of the dual-inheritance literature — Cavalli-Sforza and Feldman, Boyd and Richerson, Durham — rather than a Taleb formulation. [verify] I have not located a verbatim sentence to that effect in Taleb and it should not be attributed to him without a page reference; none of the works named in this section is currently in the Zotero library.

The substance, whoever owns it: cultural transmission has no meiotic bottleneck, no two-parent constraint, and no generational clock, so its rates exceed genetic ones by orders of magnitude. The consequence for this project is unwelcome. **High transmission rates raise the prior on homology and therefore raise the evidentiary bar on homoplasy.** Taken seriously, the claim is an argument against Objective 1, and it should be stated as the hostage it is rather than recruited as though it were support.

There are two replies, and only the second is worth anything.

The weak reply is that fast transmission tends to be *documented* transmission, since speed requires routes and routes leave records — which is what makes the *ortoq* usable as a calibration case ([[The ortoq is the discriminating instrument]]). The hard case is slow, deep, undocumented diffusion, which is not what the speed claim describes. This is true and it is a thin defence, because absence of a documented route is weak evidence in a record shaped by survival.

The strong reply abandons the origination question entirely, and here Taleb's actual contribution is on the selection side rather than the transmission side. If expected further survival scales with age, then persistence is evidence about the environment's continuing selection for a form, and that is a statement about *retention*, indifferent to how the form arrived. [[The naties are a six-century Lindy institution]] is not weakened one step by a demonstration that the *natie* was copied from somewhere. This is [[The theory predicts recurrence not independent origins]] in Taleb's vocabulary: **retention is transmission-proof, and Lindy is its measurable form.** The homology/homoplasy ambiguity, which is where the project is most exposed, simply does not touch it.

One condition attaches, and it is Taleb's own. The retention inference requires that those retaining the form bore its downside; where they were insulated, persistence is evidence about the insulation and not about the form's fitness. See [[Skin in the game]] and [[Perpetuity splits into adaptation and petrification]] — a form that persists because its holders are shielded from its failures is a petrification, and Lindy claims about it are worthless.
