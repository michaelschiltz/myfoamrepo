# Archivio di Stato di Roma — the luoghi di monte

<http://ricerca.archiviodistatoroma.beniculturali.it/OpacASRoma/>

**The papal public debt held as transferable shares**, and the second member — with [[Casa di San Giorgio - the Genoese public debt archive]] — of a pair the project needs and does not yet have.

## The fondo

**`Luoghi di monte`, 1532–1824.** The *monti camerali* were papal debt issues divided into *luoghi*: transferable, inheritable claims paying a fixed return, sold to the public and traded. ⚠️ Extent not captured in this pass; the archive's OPAC redirects awkwardly and the description should be read directly on site.

The surrounding context is the **`Camerale`** sections — `Camerale I` (14th–19th c.), `Camerale II` (16th–19th c. with earlier material), `Camerale III` (15th–19th c.) — the archive of the Apostolic Chamber's temporal administration.

## Why the pair matters

Genoa and Rome ran transferable-share public debt in overlapping periods under quite different political authorities — a republic funding war, a papacy funding a court and a state. **The forms are similar and the arbitrary particulars differ**, which is exactly the diagnostic [[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]] describes. If the *luoghi* of Genoa and the *luoghi* of Rome differ in their incidental drafting while agreeing in structure, that is evidence about descent; if they agree in the incidentals too, it is evidence of transmission and the shared name is doing real work.

Note the name itself: *luogo* in both. [[A migrated institution collects local names not local forms]] warns against the reverse inference, and here the warning runs the other way — a **shared** name across two traditions is weak evidence of anything, and the coding has to be done on the clauses.

## The usury problem is not incidental

The *monti* had to be reconciled with the prohibition on usury, and the reconciliation was argued out in canon law and in the theologians. That literature is a **stated rationale** for a financial structure, of the kind the project prizes and rarely gets — and it is the same doctrinal apparatus at work in [[Naviganti - Latin Christendom rejected the sea loan too]]. The archival counterpart is at [[Archivio Apostolico Vaticano]].
