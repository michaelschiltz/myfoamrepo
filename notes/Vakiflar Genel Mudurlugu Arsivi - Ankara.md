# Vakıflar Genel Müdürlüğü Arşivi — Ankara

**The Vakıf Kayıtlar Arşivi of the Directorate General of Foundations.** <https://www.vgm.gov.tr/>. **Successor custody in its strongest form: the records of the endowments are held by the body that administers the endowments.**

## Holdings

- Origin in the **Evkaf Nezareti, founded 1242/1826**, which drew in records previously kept by palace offices; **moved to Ankara by train on 1 March 1936**.
- **8,317 registers, more than 2,000,000 documents, 39 fonds** (the Directorate's own page).
- **Deeds:** 2,370 original *vakfiyes* and **27,021 copied into registers** (İSAM 2015); "over 27,000 *vakfiyes* and *zeyl*" and 410 illuminated stand-alone deeds (Çam 2014).
- **623 *vakfiye* registers** — Istanbul, Anatolian and Rumelian series.
- **43 translation registers**: from the early Republic, **Arabic deeds translated into Turkish and Ottoman deeds transcribed into the new alphabet**.
- Account registers, appointment (*hurufat*) registers and orders. Pantık 2024 cites *vakfiye* registers by number (e.g. 572, 580, 1483–1484) and **register 2205, the Ayasofya waqf's accounts for 1790–1810**.

## The structural fact

**Pattern 2 of [[Handoff - the non-European archive survey]] predicted this, and the prediction was too weak.** In Europe successor custody meant a surviving business keeping its own papers — the Fuggers, EDF, the Bank of England. **Here the successor is a state administrator of the same endowments**, and a deed is not a historical deposit but a **title instrument** for property the Directorate still manages. ⚠️ The legal succession from the Evkaf Nezareti and the present administrative use of the deeds are stated from general knowledge of the Directorate's role, not from a document read in this pass.

**What follows, as expectation to be tested:** access by the administrator's permission; reproduction treated as an administrative act; and a catalogue organised for administration — by foundation, not by date or type. ⚠️ **Researcher access rules, including for foreign researchers, were not found.** This is the first question to put before any trip.

## Language and script — the column is not "Ottoman Turkish"

**Many deeds are in Arabic.** That is why the translation registers exist. **Accounts are in siyakat.** A *vakfiye* register and a *muhasebe* register from the same waqf may need two different readers.

**The 43 translation registers are a layer 3, and they carry the Dubrovnik trap.** Made by the successor administrator, in the early Republic, for administering — not for history. **They may locate a deed; only the deed may witness it.** Same rule as [[The Dubrovnik Ottoman documents in three scripts]].

## What it bears on

- `waqf_khayri` — coded from Kuran 2011 and Çizakça 2000, not from deeds. **This is where the deeds are.**
- `waqf_ahli` — uncoded umbrella. The Istanbul, Anatolian and Rumelian *vakfiye* series are the population a family-waqf row would be drawn from.
- `cash_waqf` — parked. Deeds here; transactions in the court registers — see [[Istanbul Muftulugu Seriyye Sicilleri Arsivi]].
