# Nordic archives online — the access layers

**The expectation going in was that the Nordics would be thinner. That is right about institutional variety and wrong about everything else — and the two halves point in opposite directions, which makes this region the exact mirror of Belgium.**

[[Belgian archives online - the access layers]] recorded a country with poor infrastructure and first-rank holdings. **The Nordics are the reverse: the best evidence infrastructure in Europe over the thinnest institutional variety.** Taken together the two cases retire any remaining temptation to read one from the other.

## Where the Nordics lead Europe

| Country | Service | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Sweden | [[Riksarkivet Sweden - the national archives and the AI lab]] | Heavily imaged — **and publishing open-source HTR models** |
| Denmark | [[Rigsarkivet Denmark - Daisy and Arkivalieronline]] | Themed digitised collections, including the trading companies |
| Norway | [[Digitalarkivet and the Norwegian archives]] | Near-total for major series |
| Finland | [[Kansallisarkisto - Finland]] | ⚠️ unverified |

Free at point of use throughout, with a public-sector openness posture closer to the Dutch than to anything further south.

## And one dataset that has no European equal

[[The Sound Toll Registers]] — **every ship and cargo through the Danish straits, 1497/1557 to 1857, transcribed into a database.** No other single European series gives a denominator of that kind over that span.

## What is genuinely thin, and why

Stated properly at [[The Nordic thinness - what is actually missing]]. In short: **no great notarial archives, no merchant corporations of the *consulado* kind, and few medieval commercial instruments** — because the institutions that generate those records were southern and the Nordic commercial world was organised differently, largely *through* the Hanse rather than in competition with it.

**That is a fact about institutions, not about archives**, and it is the one place in this survey where a region's thinness is genuinely evidential rather than an artefact of custody or funding.
