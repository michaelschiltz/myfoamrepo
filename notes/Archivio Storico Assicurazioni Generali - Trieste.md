# Archivio Storico Assicurazioni Generali — Trieste

<https://heritage.generali.com/>

**A corporate historical archive with an online digital archive**, held by the insurer founded at Trieste in **1831**. Outside every state system, as corporate archives are — the Italian case of the split that [[SIAS SIUSA and SAN - the Italian access layers]] makes structural.

## Why a nineteenth-century insurer is in a project about pre-modern forms

Two reasons, and the second is the real one.

**The terminal state.** The project describes a movement from peril conceived as a hazard to peril conceived as a priced distribution. Generali is what the far end of that movement looks like institutionally: a joint-stock insurer with actuarial pricing, operating across an empire. **Naming the endpoint is what keeps the earlier forms from being read as approximations to it** — the reverse-teleology guard in [[Guard against reverse-teleology]]. An endpoint you can point at is easier to refuse than one you leave implicit.

**Continuity of the Adriatic.** Trieste inherits the Venetian maritime world under Habsburg administration, and a marine insurer founded there in 1831 sits at the end of a line whose beginning is at [[Archivio di Stato di Venezia]]. ⚠️ Whether the archive holds anything bearing on that continuity, as against the firm's own corporate record, is unestablished.

## The caution

⚠️ **A company's own historical archive is a curated corporate asset**, presented through a heritage programme. That is not a reason to avoid it, but it is a provenance fact for the register's rights column, and the selection principle behind what is digitised is the company's, not a researcher's.
