# Westfries Archief — the Hoorn and Enkhuizen chambers

<https://www.westfriesarchief.nl/> · Hoorn

**The two small VOC chambers, and the WIC chamber of Enkhuizen.** The archive holds the *Bewindhebbers van de West-Indische Compagnie ter Kamer Enkhuizen* among its old city archives, and the Hoorn and Enkhuizen VOC chamber material is here rather than at The Hague. ⚠️ Reference codes, extent and imaging all unverified; this note rests on the archive's own finding-aid listing and nothing more.

## Why the small chambers are not a footnote

The VOC is coded in the census as three period rows, and every one of them reads the company as a single constitutional object. **It was six chambers with unequal shares, unequal voices and, in the small chambers' case, a permanent structural minority.** The Amsterdam chamber held half; Hoorn and Enkhuizen held a sixteenth each ⚠️ *proportions to verify*.

That is not an administrative detail for a project about **entity shielding and member exit**. A member who cannot exit and cannot outvote is in a different position from one who can do either, and the small chambers are the census's nearest available case of locked-in minority participation inside a chartered body. [[Transferable claims reconcile lock-in with member exit]] states the general resolution; the small chambers are where it can be tested against a body whose members were *corporate* rather than individual.

⚠️ Whether the chamber records actually document the chambers' internal politics, as against their shipping operations, is the question that decides whether this is worth a trip. The presumption should be against: an operating chamber's archive is likely to be freight, personnel and accounts.
