# Landesarchiv NRW and the Rhineland

<https://www.archive.nrw.de/> — the state archive of North Rhine-Westphalia, and the portal through which the region's archives are described

## What the region holds

The Rhineland was the German end of the Low Countries commercial world, and its towns — Cologne above all, but also Aachen, Duisburg and the Wesel corridor — traded into Antwerp, Bruges and the Dutch Republic continuously.

**Cologne's own archive is [[Historisches Archiv der Stadt Koln and the 2009 collapse]] and is a special case.** The rest of the region's record, and everything that passed to Prussian and then North Rhine-Westphalian custody, is described here.

## Why it belongs beside the Belgian and Dutch hubs

Because the commercial region does not stop at the modern border and the archives do. The same point already recorded at [[Archives departementales du Nord - the Chambre des comptes de Lille]], from the other side: **a Low Countries question worked only from Belgian and Dutch repositories has an unexamined eastern edge**, and it is here.

⚠️ Series, holdings and imaging unverified throughout. `archive.nrw.de` is a *Land* portal in the federal pattern described at [[German archives online - the federal access layers]], and it is the entry point rather than a catalogue of everything.
