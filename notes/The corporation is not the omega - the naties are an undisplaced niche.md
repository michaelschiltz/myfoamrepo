# The corporation is not the omega - the naties are an undisplaced niche

Harris's "convergent evolution towards the joint-stock company" (Le Bris et al.) reads organizational history as a scalar ascent with the anonymous, tradable-share corporation at its summit. This is a survivorship reading (see [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]]): it names the scalable survivor and calls the whole landscape a convergence, discarding the forms that persisted by *refusing* scale. The Antwerp harbor *naties* break the teleology — not by antiquity, but by occupying a niche the joint-stock form never reached into and never displaced.

Two disciplines, or the argument leaks.

First, scope the survival claim to the *harbor* naties alone. The Cádiz *nación flamenca* — an ethno-jurisdictional merchant community, the identity-wrapper facet — did not outlive the ancien-régime commercial order; the harbor *natie* — a native cargo-handling monopoly, the perpetual-franchise facet — did. One word over opposite membership logics (see [[Natie is a false cognate]]); merging them to inflate the living-counterexample claim reintroduces exactly that conflation.

Second, name the functionally distinct structure, or Harris absorbs the case. Had the naties survived *by becoming ordinary corporations* — Katoen Natie incorporated 1854, Hesse-Noord Natie under Singapore's PSA — they would corroborate convergence, not refute it. What resists absorption is the governance form itself: the *alleenrecht held as tradable shares* (see [[The alleenrecht is monopoly held as tradable property]]) — a **perpetual, locally embedded, asset-partitioned monopoly franchise**, fenced to a defined class of goods at defined berths. That is a different point in the design space, not a primitive stage of the corporation's. The corporate wrapper it later took is substrate; the franchise-as-shielded-corpus is the load-bearing continuity ([[The naties are a six-century Lindy institution]]).

The niche is legible through [[Pooling's sign is set by the correlation of its baskets]]: the natie solves dimensional collapse by staying small-and-decorrelated — low N, high effective K, exposure fenced to one cargo class — rather than by scaling behind firewalls. It is the family-firm branch of that corollary, institutionalized and made to persist six centuries. Organizational evolution is therefore not a ladder with one summit but a space of barrier-management solutions. The corporation is one peak, not the omega.
