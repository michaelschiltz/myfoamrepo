# Archives départementales de la Gironde — Bordeaux

The Atlantic counterpart to Marseille: the wine trade, the colonial trade, the slave trade, and the *amirauté de Guyenne* in série `B`.

## What is here

**Série `B`** — the admiralty and the *Parlement de Bordeaux*, a sovereign court in its own right, which gives Bordeaux both a first-instance maritime jurisdiction and an appellate one. **Série `E`** — the notaries, and with them the *contrats à la grosse*, insurance policies and shipping partnerships.

⚠️ Everything here is unverified as to codes, extents and imaging.

## The role in the design

Bordeaux, [[Archives departementales de la Loire-Atlantique - Nantes]] and [[Archives departementales de la Seine-Maritime - Rouen]] are **three Atlantic ports under one crown and one ordinance**, and that is the set that makes the *amirauté* sweep worth running — see [[The amirautes - a series across the coastal departments]]. Their divergences measure local practice; their agreements measure the 1681 ordinance.

The operator's side of the ventures financed from Paris is here: [[Minutier central des notaires de Paris]] holds the investor's documents for the same voyages, and **a form described from one end only is described once**.
