# Nōrinshō 1938 — forty-three kō rulebooks in print

農林省経済更生部『農村金融資料 第4号 賴母子講規約例』 (昭和11–13, i.e. 1936–1938; NDL call number 14.2イ-784), **open online without login at NDL Digital, pid 1074152, public domain** (保護期間満了). About 120 printed pages. It is the companion to the ministry's February 1934 census of kō, 『農村金融資料 第1号』 (pid 1077357), which counts associations and prints no rules.

## What it is

The 凡例 (read on the page image, 2026-10-21):

- **Forty-three associations, each with its full rulebook** (規約, or 講則, 規則, 會則), chosen as 適當と認め得るもの from **秋田, 靜岡, 山口 and 福岡**.
- The rules are transcribed **原文を其の儘** as far as legible, with dialect and obscure terms glossed at the end and an editorial summary placed before each.
- Some carry the **forms used with them** (借用證書, 借入申込書) at the end.
- **The names of 講親, 世話人 and others are erased or concealed**, hence 甲野乙造, 丙川丁作 and the like in the headings.
- **All but two or three were still running in March 1936.**

By prefecture: Akita 10, Shizuoka 10, Yamaguchi 11, Fukuoka 12.

## Why it matters

**It satisfies the census's acquisition rule at scale.** No *kō* moves a cell without a *shihōsho*, and here are forty-three, in print, in four prefectures the census has never coded, with the instruments that went with them. Every Japanese row so far is Kinai, Setouchi, Echigo, Yamagata or medieval.

**It is the first test bed for the relief-kō claim.** Twelve rulebooks are classed under relief of the 講親 (1–10, 37–38) and nine more under relief together with saving (13–19, 40–41). [[Relief ko for a named person is claimed to be unattested outside Japan]] rests on 有本ほか's reading that relief kō normally ran at zero interest. These rules state whether the 親 repays and on what terms, so that reading can be checked on the instruments rather than repeated.

**And it holds the contrasts the coded block lacks.** It has kō with and without a 親, money and rice side by side in one prefecture, and kō run through an organisation (a 實行組合, a 信用貯金講會). These are where the 2026-08-20 batch found its only separations: governance, allocation, security.

## Cautions, before any of it is coded

- **One compiler, one filter.** The ministry chose what it judged 適當, so this is a selection by a regulator of rural credit in the years of prefectural 取締規則, not a sample. Kō that broke the rules, or were wound up, are the ones least likely to be here. The note [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] applies directly.
- **The classes are the ministry's, and they build in its definition.** The 1934 census defines the 講親 as one relieved by the kō, so "relief of the 講親" is partly a category imposed on the rules, not read from them. Code from the clauses, not from the heading.
- **Phase.** These are Shōwa associations under regulation. The census already holds a Meiji kō (`ko_mochiyori_yutsu_1876`), so the period is not excluded, but a 1930s rule set is not evidence for Tokugawa practice in the same prefecture.
- **Independence.** Four prefectures and forty-three associations, but one editor. Associations from one village or one 世話人 would be `same-community` or `same-persons`, and the anonymisation hides exactly that. Read the editorial summaries for place before counting cases.

## Companion items at NDL Digital, open online

- 池田竜蔵『稿本無尽の実際と学説』 (全国無尽集会所, 1930), pid 1444166. The 池田 1930, 29–30 that 恩田 2017 relies on for "not imported from abroad". Two cautions: the publisher is the licensed mujin industry's own association, and the book periodises the institution as 共濟組合時代 → 射倖團體時代 → 金融機關時代, the stage account 並松 refuses ([[The bidding ko transfers from the liquidity-constrained to the liquid]]). The 1918 edition (pid 956756) is restricted to individual transmission.
- 『頼母子講の法律関係』 (1934, pid 1445784) and 『頼母子講と其の法律関係』 (1935, pid 1280052), on the law of kō.
- 『営業無尽ト頼母子講』 (1918, pid 986238).
- 『頼母子利分引早法独案内』 (1888, pid 900848), a reckoner for kō interest.
- The four-part 1928 judicial study 民事裁判より観たる無尽講頼母子講 (pids 1907420–1907434), restricted. Presumably the source of the litigation counts the vault cites second-hand ⚠️ not checked.
