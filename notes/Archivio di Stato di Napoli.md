# Archivio di Stato di Napoli

<https://archiviodistatonapoli.cultura.gov.it/>

The state counterpart to [[Archivio Storico del Banco di Napoli]], and the two must be searched separately — one is in SIAS, the other in SIUSA.

## The fondi that matter

**`Regia Camera della Sommaria`** — the kingdom's fiscal and accounting magistracy, from the fifteenth century. Crown finance, tax farms, and the *arrendamenti* — revenue farms sold to syndicates of financiers, which is a pooling arrangement in fiscal dress and is uncoded.

**`Notai`** — the notarial protocols.

**`Consiglio Collaterale`** and the viceregal series — the government's own record.

⚠️ Reference codes, extents and dates all need transcribing from SIAS. Nothing in this note has been checked against an inventory.

## The wartime loss, and why it must be stated

**A large part of the archive was destroyed in 1943**, when German troops burned the repository at San Paolo Belsito where the material had been evacuated. This is one of the few cases in the European set where an argument from absence is *not* available in the ordinary way — and equally where it must not be treated as ordinary attrition.

The methodological consequence is precise and cuts against the project's usual caution. [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] treats absence as evidence about the filter; here the filter is a single catastrophic event with no relation whatever to what the documents contained. **Neapolitan gaps are uninformative in both directions** — they license neither an inference about the past nor an inference about selection. Say so explicitly wherever a Neapolitan series is thin, or a referee will read the silence as meaning something.

⚠️ Establish which of the fondi above survived and in what proportion before any of them enters the register as a working series.
