# The Nordic thinness — what is actually missing

**A note about an absence**, written because this is the one place in the survey where a region's thinness is evidential rather than an artefact.

## What is missing

- **No great notarial archives.** The Nordic towns worked through civic registers — the *tänkebok*, the *Niederstadtbuch* pattern — not through a notariate. See [[Stockholms stadsarkiv and the Swedish town records]].
- **No merchant corporations of the *consulado* kind.** The form this survey has now found in five countries — Marseille, Bilbao, Burgos, Lucca, Hamburg — has no clear Nordic member.
- **Few medieval commercial instruments** of the commenda or *societas maris* type, and no Nordic member of the commenda family in the census.
- **No indigenous marine insurance market** of the Genoese, Antwerp or Amsterdam kind before the eighteenth century ⚠️.

## Why this is not an archival fact

**Because the Nordic archives are the best-kept and best-digitised in Europe.** If the instruments existed in quantity they would be findable — this is the one region where that inference is safe, and it is safe precisely because [[Nordic archives online - the access layers]] records infrastructure good enough to rule out the usual explanation.

**Everywhere else in this survey, absence has had a custodial or funding explanation available.** Spain's devolution, Italy's unimaged notariates, Britain's unfunded company records, the four catastrophic losses. Here those explanations do not apply, which means the absence is about the institutions.

## What it is about, most likely

The Nordic commercial world operated **through** the Hanse rather than in competition with it for the crucial centuries — [[Bergen and the Hanseatic Kontor]] is the form that arrangement took. **A region whose long-distance trade is conducted by foreign merchants under their own law does not generate its own commercial institutions**, and its archives will record the trade without recording the instruments.

⚠️ That is a hypothesis and it is the sort the project is most at risk of liking too much, because it is tidy. **The test is whether the Nordic commercial record is thin in the periods when the Hanse dominated and thickens afterwards.** If it does not thicken after the Hanse's decline, the explanation is wrong.

## The two exceptions, which are large

[[The Sound Toll Registers]] and [[Stora Kopparberg - the 1288 share]] are both first-rank, and neither is a commercial *instrument*: one is a fiscal series and the other is a property arrangement in a mine. **The region is thin in contracts and rich in the two things that are not contracts**, which is itself a coherent picture rather than a contradiction.
