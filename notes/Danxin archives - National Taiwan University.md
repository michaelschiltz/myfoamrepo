# Danxin archives — National Taiwan University

淡新檔案, National Taiwan University Library. <https://dl.lib.ntu.edu.tw/s/Tan-Hsin/page/home>

**The surviving yamen documents of the Tamsui subprefecture and Hsinchu county**, from the Qianlong reign to 1895–96. **1,163 cases: 574 administrative, 224 civil, 365 criminal.** Taken over with Taipei Imperial University in 1945; organised by Dai Yanhui from 1947 to 1953; printed in part in 1971 (479 cases) and in four administrative volumes in 1995; **digitised from 1996**.

## Why it has a note

**It is the open Qing county archive.** The mainland county archives are read under provincial rules; **this one has been online for a generation.** It is also **a Qing frontier county under a later colonial administration**, and survived because a Japanese imperial university kept it. **Survival rule: colonial custody.**

## The honest assessment

**Marginal for the census, useful as a check.** Its civil cases carry land, debt and partnership disputes on the Taiwan frontier. **If a Chinese partnership or association row wants a second instance that does not share Chongqing's survival rule, this is the cheapest place to look.** ⚠️ Document counts beyond cases, and current access terms, not established — the NTU page could not be fetched.
