# Shielding raises the variance an entity can carry

Pooling across decorrelated ventures lowers the variance of each member's trajectory; that is how it raises time-average growth (Peters) and lowers the probability of ruin (the barrier route). **Shielding does the opposite job.** Owner shielding and entity shielding move the absorbing barrier off the members, so that the enterprise can fail without taking its members with it. The enterprise can then take larger and more concentrated positions than any member could survive, because the downside it can inflict on them is capped.

On the multiplicative limb this is a statement about leverage. A trajectory exposed to its own ruin must stay well below the growth-optimal leverage, because overshooting leads to absorption. A shielded trajectory can be run closer to it, since overshooting now ruins the entity and not the people behind it. Peters and Adamou's "Leverage Efficiency" (2026) sets out the growth-optimal leverage for an investment in a risky asset and hypothesises that stability draws it towards one [abstract only; verify before relating it to shielding].

## Consequences

- **The corporation is not a variance suppressor.** It combines a mechanism that lowers members' variance (pooling across ventures, permanent capital) with one that raises the variance the entity may bear (shielding). Calling the corporate form "variance-suppressing" misdescribes half of it.
- **Shielding relocates the barrier; it does not remove it.** Ruin is moved onto the entity's creditors and onto the entity itself, which is why [[The ensemble average is the local descriptor of a barrier-shielded agent]] holds: a shielded agent can behave as if the ensemble average described it, because the barrier that would make the time average bind sits elsewhere.
- **The relocation has to land somewhere.** The vault's reading of the *Persona ficta* — no internal principle limiting to whom the tail may be shifted — is the moral form of this point: [[Persona ficta made the corporation a-moral ab initio]]. *Al-ghunm bi'l-ghurm* refuses exactly the relocation that shielding performs.
