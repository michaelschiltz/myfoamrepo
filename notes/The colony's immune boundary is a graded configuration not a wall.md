# The colony's immune boundary is a graded configuration not a wall

**Fernandes et al. 2026 find the honey bee colony's chemical defences arranged as a gradient from the bees' own tissues out to the entrance, with antimicrobial activity and microbial communities changing together across the compartments, and the effect concentrated on hive pathogens rather than on microbes in general. If that holds, the boundary that colony immunity draws ([[Social immunity is a consequence of individuation not evidence for it]]) is not a single wall but a set of zones of graded permeability, a property of the arrangement rather than of any material in it, and one that degrades measurably under stress. The institutional counterpart is the graded structure of shielding, which the vault has so far treated as present or absent.**

## What the paper shows, and how firmly

The authors call antimicrobial social immunity a colony-level property emerging from spatial organisation, not the property of any single material — the comb's lesson again ([[The hexagon is optimal only among neighbours]]): the quantity is defined on the configuration. Stressed hives lose spatial structure, and the authors propose spatial order as a marker of colony health.

The evidence is modest. One site, one sampling event, twenty-four colonies; distance-decay R² near 0.2; compartments ranked by functional proximity, so the ordering does part of the work; selective tolerance of beneficial microbes inferred, not assayed ([[Social immunity and superorganism immunity - loci]]). It is a correlational picture consistent with a configured boundary, not a demonstration of one.

## Two refinements for the coding probe

- **Gradation.** [[Three immune functions mark a pool that has become a person]] codes border defence as entity shielding, yes or no. The legal literature already grades it: weak entity shielding gives the entity's creditors priority over members' creditors in the entity's assets; strong entity shielding adds that members' creditors cannot force liquidation (Hansmann, Kraakman and Squire 2006); owner shielding is a separate layer facing the other way ([[Entity shielding and owner shielding protect different trajectories]]). Hansmann, Kraakman and Squire in fact name three grades — weak, strong and complete, the last for non-profits and charitable trusts, where the *waqf* belongs (verified 2026-11-13, SSRN version, printed 2–3). A graded boundary is the expected case, and the codebook fields should be read as zones, not a switch [check against the HistorEE_codebooks schema].
- **Selective admission.** A colony's defences admit some microbes and suppress others; its boundary separates admitted from excluded, not inside from outside. Institutions likewise admit non-members on terms — agents, factors, depositors — so the membership function should be coded as terms of admission, not as a member list.

## The scaffolding point

Much of the colony's armoury is foraged — resins into propolis, nectar into honey, pollen — and placed by the colony; some is secreted by the bees. The materials come from outside and the arrangement is the colony's own: a partial internalisation of the kind [[Legal scaffolding gives a pool collective properties it does not yet own]] predicts, where the components are borrowed and the configuration is what the unit holds.
