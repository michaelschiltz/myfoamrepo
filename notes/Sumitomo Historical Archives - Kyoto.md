# Sumitomo Historical Archives — Kyoto

住友史料館. <https://www.shiryokan.jp/>

**About 30,000 early-modern and 60,000 modern documents** of the Sumitomo house and group companies. Collecting began in **1887** at the Osaka residence; a history office (*shushishitsu*) from 1950; the archive in Kyoto from **1987**. Publishes ***Sumitomo shiryō sōsho*** (source editions), ***Senoku sōkō*** and the ***Sumitomo shiryōkan hō***.

## Access — the tightest in the survey

**By appointment, for academic research, on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10 to 4, and only for catalogued material; researchers read reproductions or digital data, not originals.** No exhibition space and no tours. **Successor custody with the access regime of a company office** — the European comparison is the Fuggerarchiv, and this is narrower.

## What it holds for HistorEE

**Copper and money.** The house's early-modern base was copper refining and the Besshi mine, with money-exchange and lending beside them. ⚠️ The *ryōgae* and lending series and their extent not established. **Its print editions are the practical route**: the *Sumitomo shiryō sōsho* are layer 3 for whatever they cover.
