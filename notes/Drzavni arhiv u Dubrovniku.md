# Državni arhiv u Dubrovniku

<https://dad.hr/> · repository code `HR-DADU`

**The most complete surviving archive of a medieval Mediterranean commercial republic, and the census already argues from it without having read it.**

## The scale

- **Over 490 fonds and collections**, about **eight and a half kilometres** of material.
- From the Republic period alone (**1022–1808**): over **100,000 individual documents**, more than **7,000 manuscript codices**, and **15,000 Ottoman documents** — see [[The Ottoman documents at Dubrovnik]].
- **All 73 fonds of the Dubrovnik Republic are inscribed on the UNESCO Memory of the World register.** ⚠️ Inscription year not captured in this pass.

**Not a selection — the whole archive of the state.** That is the thing to understand about Dubrovnik and it is nearly unique: where every other repository in this survey holds what survived of an institution, Dubrovnik holds the institution.

## Why it matters to the census specifically

The vault argues [[Ragusan collegantia is unilateral and Venetian collegantia is bilateral]] and [[Unilateral and bilateral commenda collapse on profit and separate on loss]]. **Both are claims about Ragusan instruments, and both currently rest on secondary literature.** The instruments are here, in series, complete, and inscribed.

**That makes Dubrovnik the single strongest candidate in the whole European survey for turning an argued claim into a coded one from primary evidence** — not because the archive is the richest in absolute terms, but because the match between what the census asserts and what one repository holds is exact.

See [[The Ragusan chancery and notarial series]] for the series themselves.

⚠️ Access conditions, photography and reproduction terms unestablished.
