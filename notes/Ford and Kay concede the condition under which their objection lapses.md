# Ford and Kay concede the condition under which their objection lapses

The central charge in [[Ford and Kay 2023 on growth-optimal decision making]] is that ergodicity economics supplies no rule for *which dynamic applies*. A gamble of length one can be described additively or multiplicatively; the object is identical either way; the EE agent's preference flips on the description. The eleven-author reply calls the dynamic a hidden variable, and Ford and Kay answer that a hidden variable "has no impact on what actually occurs."

They are right about the case they construct, and the vault already carries the vulnerability at [[Jensen supplies the gap but only the dynamic privileges the logarithm]]: specifying a dynamic is formally as free a choice as specifying a utility function.

**Then they write the escape themselves.**

> "This is especially true for short gambles where the dynamic is not given by the gamble but instead by what will happen to the person's wealth over their lifetime: for example if we assume that excess wealth gets put into a diversified portfolio then it is likely to grow multiplicatively. From this perspective, we could say that **the dynamic is an aspect of someone's life, rather than a particular gamble**, which would tie them down to a single utility." (Ford and Kay 2023, 326)

That is the condition under which the indeterminacy disappears, stated by the critics, in the course of the critique.

## Why the historical case satisfies it

For a laboratory subject facing an abstract lottery, whether the dynamic is additive or multiplicative is a modelling choice, because nothing outside the description fixes it. For a historical institution it is not a choice. It is a documented property of the instrument, and the documents survive.

Whether a merchant's capital compounds across voyages or is distributed and reconstituted at each one is settled by the contract. Whether a partnership dissolves on the death of a partner or persists is settled by the legal form — this is the temporal binding of [[Lock-in and outcome-coupling are distinct bindings]], and it is coded rather than assumed. Whether losses fall on a separable legal person or on the natural one is settled by the shielding provisions, per [[Entity shielding and owner shielding protect different trajectories]]. The *ie* either outlives its head or it does not. The *mudaraba* is revocable and dissolves on death, which is why [[The mudaraba is revocable not locked in]] can be asserted from the fiqh rather than posited.

**The dynamic is in the archive.** That is what converts the asymmetry claimed at [[Jensen supplies the gap but only the dynamic privileges the logarithm]] — dynamics are observable, preferences are not — from an assertion into a property of the evidence. And it is why the `organizational_forms` and `loss_mitigation_forms` matrices matter beyond typology: coding accumulation, revocability, perpetuity and liability *is* pinning the dynamic, one form at a time, before any argument is run on it.

## The claim, stated at the width it will bear

Not that Ford and Kay endorse the historical application; they do not discuss it. Not that their objection is wrong; against a decision theory addressed to individuals facing gambles it lands, and [[The three published critiques do not agree with each other]] records that this is where all three critiques operate.

The claim is narrower and holds: **the objection is conditional, its condition is stated in the source, and the condition is satisfied where the dynamic is a fact about an entity's constitution rather than a description of a wager.** A referee who reaches for Ford and Kay against this project must either deny that legal form fixes accumulation dynamics — which the documents refute — or accept that the objection has been answered on its own terms.

## What this does not rescue

The vault's *own* stated worry remains, and is now the live one. From [[Jensen supplies the gap but only the dynamic privileges the logarithm]]: for historical cases the dynamic is inferred, frequently from the same evidence the argument then explains. Coding a form's accumulation properties from a contract is not circular; inferring a multiplicative dynamic from the observed persistence of the institution and then explaining the persistence by the dynamic is. **The discipline is that the dynamic must be coded from the instrument's terms, never from its fate.** That rule should be written into the codebook conventions, not merely into a note [verify — check whether `CHARACTER-CODING.md` already forbids outcome-derived coding].
