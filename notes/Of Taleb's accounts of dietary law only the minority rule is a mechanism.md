# Of Taleb's accounts of dietary law only the minority rule is a mechanism

**Across the two books Taleb gives dietary prescription five functions, one genealogy and one mechanism. A functional account that can supply a function for any particular explains none of them. The mechanism is different in kind. The minority rule of *Skin in the Game* ch. 2 is a dynamic with stated conditions and a prediction that can fail. It explains how prevalent a prescription becomes, never its origin or its content.**

## The seven accounts

| Account | Locus | What it would explain | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Populations holding the rule survived | *SG* ch. 18, "The Nondecorative in the Decorative" | that kashrut persists | Backward inference; traditions treated as populations; choice set unspecified |
| Cohesion, and trust in remote commerce | *SG* ch. 18, same section | that a common table binds | Holds for any shared rule; silent on content |
| Taming the iatrogenics of abundance | *AF* ch. 22, "If It's Wednesday, I Must Be Vegan" | that fasting is kept | Moral psychology; no test stated |
| Keeping the marginal patient from the doctor | *AF* ch. 22, "Religion and Naive Interventionism" | religious practice generally | *Via negativa*; not specific to diet |
| Convexity of irregular intake | *AF* ch. 22, "Convexity Effects and Random Nutrition" | fasting | Biology his and unchecked; see below |
| **Descent from sacrifice** (genealogy) | *SG* ch. 2; ch. 16, "The Gods Do Not Like Cheap Signaling" | slaughter rules | Historical, not functional; the better kind |
| **Minority rule** (mechanism) | *SG* ch. 2, "Criminals with Peanut Allergies" | the prevalence of kosher and halal supply | A dynamic with stated conditions |

*SG* is *Skin in the Game*, *AF* is *Antifragile*. Taleb himself sets aside the obvious ex ante purposes for kashrut, the insalubrity of pigs and their competition with humans for food (ch. 18).

## Why the minority rule is different

The rule rests on an asymmetry: the observant will not eat the non-compliant, and the non-observant will eat the compliant. Given an intransigent minority of a few percent, evenly distributed in space, and a small cost differential, supply converges on compliance. Taleb states what switches the rule off: segregation in space, a large cost differential, and a counter-taboo in the majority (Al-Akhtal's refusal of sacrificial meat; the Christian martyrs who would not eat it). **Conditions stated in advance make it falsifiable.** None of the other six accounts states a condition under which it would fail.

What it explains is narrow. It does not say why the rule says what it says, or how the rule arose. It says why the majority ends up eating as the minority requires.

**For the project.** A rule can become general without anyone adopting it for its reason. Prevalence is then not evidence of majority preference, and a count of how widespread a practice was must allow for the minority-rule route. Most practitioners under the rule have no rationale at all, which is why a null in the stated-rationale field is a finding and not a gap ([[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]]).

## Fasting is the one time-average convexity in the two books

*Antifragile* ch. 22 argues that the same average intake, delivered irregularly to an organism with a nonlinear response, has a different effect from steady delivery. The average here runs over one organism's own days, so the Jensen gap is taken along a trajectory and not across an ensemble. That makes it unlike the convexity bias of the glossary ([[Antifragility is defined by an ensemble Jensen gap]]). But the response accumulates additively, not by compounding, so no ergodicity claim follows, and the dose–response biology is Taleb's, unchecked. Use it to illustrate a Jensen gap along a trajectory, not as evidence about fasting.

## The general lesson

The arbitrary particulars of dietary law, such as four sets of dishes, or the line between fish and shellfish, are where functional stories meet no constraint, and so they are where the stories multiply. Read them historically, as [[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]] requires. Keep any functional claim at the level of form ([[Ritual carries its function in its form and its circularity not in its content]]). Where an explanation of persistence is wanted, specify the choice set ([[The isqa is Lindy because captive not because robust]]).
