# Consulado de Burgos — the universidad de mercaderes

**The earliest of the Castilian merchant corporations (1494), and the one that wrote insurance law first.** Its 1538 ordinances on marine insurance predate the Bilbao code by two centuries and governed the Castilian wool trade to Flanders through the Burgos merchants' *consulado* at Bruges.

## Why it belongs in the comparative set

Two reasons, and the second is the interesting one.

First, the insurance ordinances are a codified allocation of maritime peril by a merchant body, directly comparable to Bilbao's and to the Mediterranean codes — a third point on the line the coding already runs through Venice, Genoa and Barcelona.

Second, **the Burgos consulate at Bruges is an identity-wrapper institution in the same family as the Low Countries *naties***. [[The naties are a six-century Lindy institution]] and [[Identity-wrapper and perpetual-monopoly are distinct shielding facets]] describe the form; the Castilian *nacion* at Bruges is one of its members, and it is currently uncoded. This is a live gap, not a decorative parallel — and it is the same institution the project already reads from the Flemish side.

## Where the records are

⚠️ **Dispersed, and this is the problem with Burgos.** There is no single Consulado archive. Material is split across the Archivo Municipal de Burgos, the Archivo Historico Provincial, the Diputacion, and — for the Bruges consulate — Belgian municipal custody. PARES holds an authority record for the Consulado de Burgos but the fondo itself is not consolidated there.

Known published anchors, useful as finding aids rather than as sources: Bienvenido Oliver's *La universidad de mercaderes y consulado de Burgos*; a 1509 insurance policy published by Floriano Ballesteros Caballero; and the *Espacio, Tiempo y Forma* literature on the Burgos merchants and the Bruges consulate.

## What to do about it

Do not plan a Burgos trip until the custodial map is drawn. This is the clearest case in the Spanish set where the register's *repository* column cannot yet be filled, and where filling it is a discrete piece of preparatory work — probably a morning with the secondary literature's footnotes rather than an archival visit.
