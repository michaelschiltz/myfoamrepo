# Naviganti delayed the priced premium it is credited with dating

Premium insurance appears around 1300, and the staging reads that date as the moment a peril became
priceable — the hazard conception giving way to the risk conception. Lohsse's reconstruction of the
juristic discourse inverts the causation. The conception of a payment made purely for bearing peril
is documented **before** the date, and what arrived around 1300 was permission to write it down.

The decretal *Naviganti vel eunti ad nundinas* (Gregory IX, 1236) prohibited the sea-loan interest.
Its reach is the point:

> Grund der Abspaltung war freilich nicht erst die Dekretale *Naviganti* — im Gegenteil: Da ihr
> spezifisch gegen den Seedarlehenszins gerichtetes Verbot auch Zinsversprechen erfasste, die
> **ausschließlich als Gegenleistung für die Gefahrübernahme** gedacht waren, hielt die Dekretale
> die Anerkennung einer solchen bloßen **Gefahrtragungsprämie zunächst auf**.

Three steps, and each is dated:

- Jurists at the **beginning of the thirteenth century** — the controversy Raymund de Peñafort
  reports — were already arguing for a lawful sea-loan interest conceived as consideration for
  assuming peril, and nothing else. The concept is available and articulated.
- *Naviganti* catches precisely those promises. The voices are *zunächst erstickt*.
- They prevail in the **fourteenth century**, and the separation of credit purpose from insurance
  purpose follows.

The legists lag entirely: *die Legisten … verharrten noch ganz bei der Erörterung der antiken
Quellen*. The movement is canonist, and it is a movement in what may be said, not in what may be
thought.

## Why this bites

The staging infers a conceptual threshold from an instrumental one. If priced instruments appear
late because a prohibition suppressed them, their appearance dates the lifting of the prohibition.
An instrument built to detect the hazard-to-risk shift by asking whether the cost of bearing a peril
was set in advance will return a low reading wherever canon law reached, and a high reading wherever
it did not — irrespective of how anyone conceived the peril.

The control case is available and it is decisive. Schuster prints a Lübeck *Bodmereibrief* of
**1431** whose premium is stated openly at a third of capital for a six-month voyage, and the
Hanseatic and Dutch material states its premiums as a matter of course. Mediterranean notaries of the
same century were folding theirs into freight rates and exchange rates. Same instrument, same period,
two answers, and the variable that separates them is jurisdiction rather than conception. See
[[A concealed premium is evidence about the notary not about the price]].

## The second instance

This is the second independent chronology that defeats the staging. The first is the tontine's —
see [[The tontine's chronology defeats the hazard-to-pricing staging]] — which works from the dating
of an instrument. This one works from the juristic record of a concept and its prohibition, which is
a different kind of evidence and therefore not a repetition. Two defeats from unrelated directions
put the burden on the staging rather than on the objection.

None of this requires abandoning the shift the project describes. It requires distinguishing the
shift from the record of the shift, and treating the ecclesiastical prohibition as a filter on the
record. Refusals are observations of the filter — see
[[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]].
