# National Archives of India — Abhilekh Patal

<https://www.abhilekh-patal.in/>. The portal of the National Archives of India, New Delhi.

**Reported on the government's own press release of March 2026: 7,403,109 reference media records, 4,009,504 digitised files and 189,820,637 digitised pages**, with most of the digitisation done "during the last three years". Public records transferred from ministries and departments, private papers, photographs, maps, microfilms and rare documents; online registration and remote access; **digitise-on-demand and page-on-demand for what is not yet scanned**. ⚠️ What is free and what is charged, and how much of the scanned material is actually served to a remote reader, were not established.

## The honest assessment

**The largest digitisation programme in the survey outside the Geniza and the VOC, and almost certainly the least relevant per page.** The NAI's holdings begin in **1748** and are the record of the Company's and the Crown's administration: revenue, foreign and political, home, legislative. **The census's South Asian institutions appear in it only when the state investigated, taxed or adjudicated them** — which is the layer described at [[The colonial enquiry as South Asia's layer 3]], and much of that was published anyway.

**The one thing worth a targeted search**: departmental files on the regulation of chit funds, *nidhis* and indigenous banking from the 1920s and 1930s, which is where the enquiry committees' working papers would sit. ⚠️ Not searched; the portal's search was not exercised in this pass.
