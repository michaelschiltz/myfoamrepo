# Natie archives — where the Antwerp nations' records are

⚠️ **This note records an unresolved custody question, not an answer.**

`natie` is a coded row, and the vault argues from it repeatedly — [[Natie is a false cognate]], [[The alleenrecht is monopoly held as tradable property]], [[The naties are a six-century Lindy institution]], [[Identity-wrapper and perpetual-monopoly are distinct shielding facets]]. **For a form carrying that much argumentative weight, the census does not know where the documents are**, and the placement in the archives hub has been a flagged guess since it was written.

## The two things called *natie*, and the custody follows the distinction

[[Natie is a false cognate]] already establishes that the word does not mean what an English reader assumes. The custody question runs along the same seam:

- **The foreign merchant *nations*** — the Hanseatic, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and English communities at Bruges and Antwerp, with their consuls, privileges and houses. Their records are partly in the host city's archive, partly in the home polity's, and partly nowhere. Bruges holds the Hanseatic side: [[Stadsarchief Brugge - the Hanse and the nations]].
- **The Antwerp *naties* as porters' and carriers' corporations**, which held the *alleenrecht* and survived into the modern port economy. **Several have corporate successors still trading**, which means their archives may be in private hands rather than public custody — and a private archive in Belgium is registered, if at all, through Archiefbank Vlaanderen or Archiefpunt rather than through any state catalogue.

## Why this matters more than a filing question

The Lindy claim in [[The naties are a six-century Lindy institution]] is a claim about **continuity**, and continuity is exactly what a corporate successor's own archive would document and what a state archive's fragmentary deposit would not. **If the records are with the successor bodies, the evidence for the continuity claim is held by the institutions whose continuity is being claimed** — which is not disqualifying, but is a provenance fact that has to be stated rather than discovered by a referee.

## What to do

⚠️ Establish, in this order: what [[FelixArchief - Antwerp city archive]] holds under the gild and corporation series; what is registered in Archiefbank Vlaanderen under the successor companies; and what the Bruges material covers for the earlier merchant nations. This is a desk task and it should be done before the `natie` row is cited in the grant material.
