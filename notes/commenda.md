# commenda

The medieval Mediterranean profit-sharing contract pairing a sedentary investor with a travelling agent: the investor supplies capital, the agent labour, and gains split by agreed ratio while the investor's downside is capped at the capital advanced. As the *accomandita* it marks a first genuine step toward owner shielding — the sleeping partner's exposure bounded — against the unlimited-liability baseline of the [[The fraterna and compagnia diverge in formation but converge at the barrier|fraterna and compagnia]]. Compare its Islamic structural cognate, the *qirad*/*mudaraba*, on the asymmetry of [[Skin in the game is asymmetric in the mudaraba|skin in the game]].
