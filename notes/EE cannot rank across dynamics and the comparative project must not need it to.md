# EE cannot rank across dynamics and the comparative project must not need it to

The completeness objection in [[Ford and Kay 2023 on growth-optimal decision making]] is the one with teeth for this project, and it has not been answered in the literature.

**The objection.** Growth-optimal approaches cannot in general rank gambles with different dynamics against one another. Ford and Kay ask how to choose between an additive gamble growing at five units per round and a multiplicative one growing at one percent of wealth per round. The two growth rates are not commensurable, because they are rates of different quantities. Their time-varying construction is worse: three deterministic gambles ordered $G_+ \succ G_C \succ G_-$ by time average, whose realised wealth orderings reverse at every finite date, and for which **no minimum length exists** at which the time-average ordering becomes sensible. The eleven-author reply does not address this construction.

**Why it threatens us specifically.** The project's comparative claims run across forms whose dynamics differ by construction. A *qirad* dissolving at the end of each venture and a joint-stock company accumulating across ventures are not two settings of one dynamic; they are different dynamics, which is the whole content of [[Lock-in and outcome-coupling are distinct bindings]]. If the framework cannot rank across dynamics, then any sentence of the form "form X was better adapted than form Y" is unavailable in the framework's own terms — and comparative institutional history is made of such sentences.

## The escape, and it is the same escape as before

**The selective claim requires no ranking.** [[Time-average optimization is a survivorship property not an intention]] does not assert that one form is preferred to another by any agent; it asserts that forms whose realised trajectories crossed the barrier were removed. Survival is a fact about a trajectory, not a preference ordering over prospects, and completeness is an axiom about preference orderings. A filter does not need to rank the things it does not eliminate.

This is the same structural move that answers Toda at [[The selective claim is falsifiable where the prescriptive claim is not]], and its recurrence is worth noticing: **all three published critiques attack the prescriptive limb, and the selective limb is not vulnerable to any of them.** That is either a real feature of the project's position or a sign that the selective framing is doing too much work, and the honest response is to state the constraint it imposes rather than to celebrate the escape.

## The constraint it imposes

Three prohibitions follow, and they bind.

**No cross-dynamic optimality claims.** Do not write that a form with a higher time-average growth rate was therefore superior to a form with a different dynamic. The comparison is not defined. What is defined is whether each form's trajectory crossed its own barrier, which is a within-form question with a within-form answer.

**Absorption is the commensurable quantity, not growth.** Two forms with incommensurable growth rates have comparable *survival*: the barrier was crossed or it was not, and the ordering of shocks relative to reserve accumulation determines which. This is a further reason to prefer the ruin limb over the variance limb, already the instruction at [[An absorbing barrier breaks ergodicity by construction]] and reinforced independently at [[The published critique never engages absorption]].

**Where a comparison genuinely requires ranking across dynamics, say so and stop.** [[Isqa qirad and commenda are a liability-variable triad]] and the convergence pairs are comparisons of *structure*, which is safe. A claim that the corporation outcompeted the *natie* on growth grounds would not be, and [[The corporation is not the omega - the naties are an undisplaced niche]] is already the vault refusing exactly that move on other grounds. The completeness objection supplies a second and independent reason for that refusal.
