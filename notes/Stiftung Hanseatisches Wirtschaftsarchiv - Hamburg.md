# Stiftung Hanseatisches Wirtschaftsarchiv — Hamburg

Attached to the Handelskammer Hamburg, which publishes a *Findbuch* for the foundation's holdings.

## Why it is separate from the Staatsarchiv and must be searched separately

Because the **Handelskammer Hamburg** is itself one of the institutions the project is interested in — a merchant corporation with its own archive, its own *Commerzbibliothek*, and a continuous existence from the seventeenth century.

**That puts it in the family this survey has now met five times:** the Marseille chamber of 1599 ([[Chambre de commerce de Marseille - the 1599 archive]]), the Bilbao and Burgos *consulados*, the Lucchese *Corte dei Mercanti*, the French *juridictions consulaires*, and now Hamburg.

**Five countries have produced a merchant corporation that governs, adjudicates and keeps its own archive, and `organizational_forms` has no row for any of them.** That is no longer an observation; it is a gap with five instances, and it should go to a selection brief rather than accumulate one more note at a time.

⚠️ The foundation's holdings, date coverage and access terms are unverified. The *Findbuch* is published and is the entry point.
