# National Library of Russia — the Firkovich and Antonin collections

St Petersburg. **More than 18,000 Hebraica and Judaica items**, of which the Geniza-relevant parts are two:

- **The Second Firkovich Collection**, acquired 1876: **more than 15,600 manuscripts and fragments** (Ktiv's partner page; about 17,000 with Firkovich's personal archive on the library's own exhibition page). Firkovich bought in Cairo — "antiquities kept in the *geniza* of the old **Karaite** synagogue" — and also in Crimea and at Nablus. Hebrew, Aramaic, Arabic; **Arabic in Hebrew characters and Hebrew in Arabic characters** both.
- **The Antonin Collection: 1,189 Genizah fragments**, received at the end of the nineteenth century.

## Why it is not simply another collection

**1. A different *geniza*.** The Firkovich Cairo material is, on the library's own account, from the **Karaite** synagogue, not the Rabbanite Ben Ezra. **The survival rule at [[Cairo Geniza - the deposit and its survival rule]] is a community rule, and this is a second community.** ⚠️ How much of the Second Firkovich Collection is Cairene, and how much of that is documentary, not established.

**2. Access is a present-day political fact.** ⚠️ Western researchers' access to Russian state libraries since 2022 was not investigated. **Where Ktiv carries images** — from the National Library of Israel's microfilm programme and later digitisation — **the collection is consultable without going**; where it carries only records, it is not. The partner page gives no image count.

## The honest assessment

**Marginal for the census and possibly important for the dispute.** No census row draws on it. But if Rabbanite partnership instruments follow Jewish law and Karaite ones differ, **the positionality point about the Ben Ezra deposit becomes testable inside the Jewish community** before it is argued across the Jewish–Muslim line. ⚠️ Speculative; no literature on Karaite partnership practice was read.
