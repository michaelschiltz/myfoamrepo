# Bad luck is what the ensemble average calls the modal trajectory

Drelichman and Voth close on Spanish decline. Spain declined "not because of the way its fiscal policy was conducted but rather in spite of a first-rate system of public finances," undone instead by "a combination of insufficient state building and bad luck on the battlefield" (2014: 7). The supporting device is a counterfactual: had the Netherlands been subdued by the late 1580s, Philip would have ended his reign owing 39 million ducats rather than 66, and the debt-to-revenue ratio would have resumed the downward path it was on before the Armada (129–30, figure 9).

The counterfactual is an average over unrealised siblings, offered as evidence about the realised one. Its arithmetic is careful and conservative and is not what is at issue. The inference is.

For an entity making repeated ventures with heavy left tails, the modal trajectory lies well below the mean of the ensemble, and "bad luck" is the name the ensemble average gives to the modal path. Explaining decline by the residual between the realised path and the ensemble mean therefore explains nothing, because that residual is what the dynamics predict for nearly every member. The explanandum has been defined as a deviation from a benchmark that almost nobody attains.

This is the mirror of the error [[Guard against reverse-teleology]] warns against. Reading the survivor's path as necessary and reading the non-survivor's path as accidental are the same mistake made from opposite ends, and both follow from installing the ensemble mean as the reference trajectory.

## What is not established here

The strong version of the claim is a hypothesis, not a finding. To assert that Spanish decline was the modal outcome of Spanish strategy requires naming the multiplicative variable and showing that it compounds. Fiscal capacity, the *tercios*, silver receipts and the credit relationship itself behave differently, and only some are candidates. Until that is done, the claim here is negative: the residual-from-the-mean explanation is unavailable, whatever replaces it [verify — open question, deliberately left unresolved].
