# Source editions — Italian and Mediterranean commerce

The printed notarial and business-record editions behind the census's *commenda*, *societas maris*, *collegantia*, *compagnia*, sea-loan and insurance rows. **Maritime-law compilations have their own note**: [[Source editions - maritime law compilations]].

## The wanted item, and a page correction

- **Robert S. Lopez & Irving W. Raymond (tr. and eds), *Medieval Trade in the Mediterranean World: Illustrative Documents Translated with Introductions and Notes*** (New York: Columbia UP, 1955; Records of Civilization 52), xi + 458 pp.; reprints 1961, 1990; Norton paperback; Columbia reissue 2001 with a foreword by O. R. Constable. **The pages the census wants: "The Sea Loan and the Sea Exchange" from p. 168; "The *Commenda* Contract" from p. 174; "Partnerships … in Land Trade" from p. 185.** So pp. 174–184 is the whole commenda chapter, and **the sea-loan chapter at pp. 168–173 should be taken too**. Online only via archive.org's print-disabled service or the ACLS Humanities E-Book on Fulcrum (subscription).

## Genoa

- **"Notai liguri del sec. XII" (later "dei secoli XII e XIII")**, within *Documenti e studi per la storia del commercio e del diritto commerciale italiano* (Turin: Lattes / Editrice Libraria Italiana; Genoa: Deputazione di Storia Patria), full Latin transcriptions of notarial registers dense in commenda, societas and sea-loan contracts:
  - *Il cartolare di Giovanni Scriba*, ed. Chiaudano & Moresco, 2 vols (1935; repr. 1970) — ⚠️ 1,306 acts, 1154–64, secondary figure
  - *Oberto Scriba de Mercato (1190)*, ed. Chiaudano & Morozzo della Rocca (1938)
  - *Guglielmo Cassinese (1190–1192)*, ed. Hall, Krueger & Reynolds, 2 vols (1938)
  - *Bonvillano (1198)*, ed. Eierman, Krueger & Reynolds (1939)
  - *Oberto Scriba de Mercato (1186)*, ed. Chiaudano (1940)
  - *Giovanni di Guiberto (1200–1211)*, ed. Hall-Cole et al., 2 vols (1939–40)
  - *Lanfranco (1202–1226)*, ed. Krueger & Reynolds, 3 vols (1951–53) ⚠️ from memory
  
  **This is the corpus behind van Doosselaere 2009.** Reti Medievali Open Archive has records for *Guglielmo Cassinese* I–II and *Oberto 1186* ⚠️ site would not open; HathiTrust search-only for the Oberto volumes.
- **Not reached, and high priority**: the "Notai genovesi in Oltremare" series (Caffa, Pera, **Chios**, Famagusta) — relevant to `maona_chios`.

## Venice

- **R. Morozzo della Rocca & Antonino Lombardo (eds), *Documenti del commercio veneziano nei secoli XI–XIII***, 2 vols (Rome & Turin, 1940; Documenti e studi 19–20; repr. 1971) — **the main *collegantia* corpus**. (WorldCat wrongly gives the co-editor as "Attilio".)
- **Lombardo & Morozzo della Rocca (eds), *Nuovi documenti del commercio veneto dei sec. XI–XIII*** (Venice: Deputazione di Storia Patria per le Venezie, 1953; Monumenti storici n.s. 7), xiv + 129 pp.
- **Tafel & Thomas (eds), *Urkunden zur älteren Handels- und Staatsgeschichte der Republik Venedig***, 3 vols (Vienna, 1856–57; Fontes Rerum Austriacarum II, 12–14) — treaties and privileges 814–1299; free on archive.org.
- **"Fonti per la storia di Venezia", section III (notarial archives)** — Venetian notaries in Crete and Ayas. ⚠️ Not verified.

## Marseille, Provence, Barcelona, Pisa

- **Louis Blancard (ed.), *Documents inédits sur le commerce de Marseille au Moyen Âge***, 2 vols (Marseille, 1884–85) — hundreds of *commandae* and *societates*; **free on archive.org and Gallica**.
- **John H. Pryor, *Business Contracts of Medieval Provence: Selected Notulae from the Cartulary of Giraud Amalric of Marseilles, 1248*** (Toronto: PIMS, 1981; Studies and Texts 54) — Latin with English translation.
- **J. M. Madurell Marimón & A. García Sanz, *Comandas comerciales barcelonesas de la Baja Edad Media*** (Barcelona: Colegio Notarial & CSIC, 1973) — the Catalan commenda corpus.
- **Antonio de Capmany, *Memorias históricas sobre la marina, comercio y artes de … Barcelona***, 4 vols (Madrid: Sancha, 1779–92; annotated ed. 1961–63) — consular and insurance ordinances; free on archive.org.
- **Francesco Bonaini (ed.), *Statuti inediti della città di Pisa dal XII al XIV secolo***, 3 vols (Florence, 1854–70) — the *Constitutum usus* on the *societas maris* and *compagnia di terra*; vol. 1 free on archive.org. ⚠️ Which volume holds which text unverified.

## Tuscany: companies and insurance

- **Federigo Melis, *Documenti per la storia economica dei secoli XIII–XVI*** (Florence: Olschki, 1972; Istituto Datini ser. I, 1) — 194 documents, Datini-centred: companies, bills, insurance, accounts.
- **Federigo Melis, *Origini e sviluppi delle assicurazioni in Italia (secoli XIV–XVI)*, vol. I, *Le fonti*** (Rome: INA, 1975) — **the printed corpus of early insurance policies**; the source base for the `marine_insurance` row that the census reads through Ceccarelli.
- **Company ledgers** (Italian vernacular): Sapori, *I libri di commercio dei Peruzzi* (1934), *I libri della ragione bancaria dei Gianfigliazzi* (1946), *I libri degli Alberti del Giudice* (1952), *Libro giallo della compagnia dei Covoni* (1970); Chiaudano, *Il libro vermiglio* (1963); Goldthwaite, Settesoldi & Spallanzani, *Due libri mastri degli Alberti* (1995) — **the internal capital accounts (*corpo*, *sopracorpo*) of the compagnia form.**
- **Enrico Bensa, *Il contratto di assicurazione nel Medio Evo*** (Genoa, 1884 ⚠️) — French tr. 1897 free on archive.org; reputed documentary appendix ⚠️.

## Toulouse: the Bazacle

**No free-standing printed edition of the Bazacle *pariage* acts or charters was found.** Sicard 1953 (Paris: Colin) and its English translation (Yale, 2015, ed. Goetzmann) carry no documentary appendix. **The documentary base is archival: Archives départementales de la Haute-Garonne, fonds 5 J** (charters, general-meeting minutes and accounts, twelfth century to 1946) — see [[The Bazacle records - a custody problem]]. Printed charters are scattered in cartularies (Douais, *Cartulaire de l'abbaye de Saint-Sernin*, 1887 ⚠️).
