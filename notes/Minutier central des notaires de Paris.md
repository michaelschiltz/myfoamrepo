# Minutier central des notaires de Paris

Série `MC` at [[Archives nationales - Paris and Pierrefitte]]. <https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/notaires-de-paris>

**The largest notarial archive in Europe after Barcelona's**, holding the minutes of the Paris notaries from the fifteenth century to the nineteenth, in a single centralised deposit.

## Why it is the French instrument archive

Everything this project codes from drafted clauses is here for Paris: *sociétés*, *rentes constituées*, marine insurance policies written by Paris underwriters, *contrats à la grosse aventure*, company subscriptions. The Parisian notariate served a capital that was a financial centre without being a port, which makes its maritime instruments **investor-side documents** — the Paris capital behind a Bordeaux or Nantes voyage.

That is a distinction worth keeping: [[Archives departementales de la Gironde - Bordeaux]] and [[Archives departementales de la Loire-Atlantique - Nantes]] hold the operator's side of the same ventures. **A form looks different from the two ends**, and the census's `loss_mitigation_forms` characteristics are mostly investor-side questions.

## The finding-aid position

⚠️ The Minutier is inventoried study by study, with a long tradition of published *répertoires* and thematic indexes, and **the quality is high and the coverage uneven**. Whether any of it is imaged at scale is unestablished; assume layer 1 with excellent description, which is the opposite of the Dutch position.

**Do not assume an Amsterdam-style query is possible.** [[Stadsarchief Amsterdam - the notarial archive]] is full-text searchable; the Minutier is a set of finding aids over an enormous series, and a question that takes minutes in Amsterdam takes a research programme here. That asymmetry, between the two largest northern-European notarial deposits, is itself a fact about digitisation policy and not about the notaries.
