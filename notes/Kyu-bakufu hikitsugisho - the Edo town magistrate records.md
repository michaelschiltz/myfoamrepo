# *Kyū-bakufu hikitsugisho* — the Edo town magistrate records

旧幕府引継書, National Diet Library, Tokyo main library, rare books room.

**Records of the shogunate handed over to Tokyo Prefecture and deposited by it with the Imperial Library** — mostly reference compilations of the **Edo town magistrates (町奉行所)**, with some from the Hyōjōsho, the magistrates of temples and shrines, and of works. **About 9,000 items**, all backed and rebound at the Imperial Library; **digitised except for some items** and viewable through NDL Digital Collections.

## Why this is the census's Edo archive

**It is where the Edo money market and the licensed guilds were administered from**, and it has been at layer 3 in print for decades:

| Compilation | Edition |
|---|---|
| ***Shoton'ya saikōshirabe*** 諸問屋再興調 — the re-establishment of the wholesale guilds after 1851 | 大日本近世史料, 15 vols, 1956–1980 |
| ***Shichū torishimari ruishū*** 市中取締類集 | 大日本近世史料, 1959–, in progress |
| *Senyō ruishū* 撰要類集 | facsimile, 旧幕府引継書影印叢刊, 1985–86 |
| town ordinances | 江戸町触集成, 22 vols, 1994–2012 |

**`kabu_nakama`** is an uncoded umbrella over `kabu_local` and `kabu_edo_export`, both from Nagata 2008. **The guilds' abolition in 1841 and re-establishment in 1851 are documented here from the administrator's side**, and the *Shoton'ya saikōshirabe* is the obvious primary source a `kabu_nakama` batch would need. ⚠️ Not read in this pass.

**The fudasashi.** The NDL holds a *Fudasashi jūshogaki* 札差住所書 among its holdings. ⚠️ Whether it and other *fudasashi* material belong to the *hikitsugisho*, and what the magistrates' records hold on the 1789 and 1843 debt cancellations, not established — **a question for the PI's own work before it is one for the census**.

## The survival rule

**What survived is what the magistrates compiled as reference, not what they received.** The *hikitsugisho* are the office's own digests and precedent books. **The loss side — petitions, suits, bankruptcies — survives where a clerk chose it as precedent.** Pattern 7 again.
