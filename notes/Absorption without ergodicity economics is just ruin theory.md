# Absorption without ergodicity economics is just ruin theory

The sharpest objection available against this project is not one that has been published. It follows from the project's own best move, and it should be answered in the manuscript rather than met in a report.

**The objection.** If absorption is doing the central work — if the argument runs off ruin rather than off variance, as [[The published critique never engages absorption]] recommends — then what is the ergodicity apparatus contributing that Feller could not have supplied in 1950? Gambler's ruin, first-passage times, branching processes and survival analysis are classical. None of them requires a word about ensemble averages, time averages, or the ergodic theorem. "A merchant house that goes bankrupt stops trading" needs no new framework, and neither does "institutions that failed are not in the sample."

Stated at full strength: **strip out the ergodicity vocabulary and the project may be ruin theory with archival examples.** That is respectable work. It is not the book's advertised contribution, and a referee who reaches this thought will say so.

## The answer, and its limits

Absorption on its own yields a *survival analysis*: which trajectories terminated, when, and at what hazard. It tells you who is left. It says nothing about **what the survivors were selected on**, and that is the whole of the historical claim.

The ergodicity apparatus supplies two things a ruin calculation does not.

**First, the criterion.** [[Time-average optimization is a survivorship property not an intention]] asserts that what persisted were arrangements whose *realised* trajectories tracked the time-average growth rate. That is a claim about which quantity the filter operates on, and it is not derivable from the existence of a barrier. A barrier tells you that some paths end; it does not tell you that the paths which do not end are those maximising $\mathbb{E}[\log r]$ rather than, say, minimising variance, or maximising expected wealth subject to a constraint. Kelly, Breiman and Latané established that the growth-rate criterion is the surviving one under repeated multiplicative exposure. That result is what converts a ruin calculation into a selection claim.

**Second, the diagnosis of the error being corrected.** The project is not only saying that institutions died. It is saying that the historiography evaluating them used the wrong average — that "bad luck," "sustainable debt," and "reasonable ex ante" are ensemble constructions applied to single trajectories. [[Bad luck is what the ensemble average calls the modal trajectory]], [[The sustainability criterion assumes the ergodicity it should be testing]] and [[A single throw of dice cannot be evaluated by its expectation]] are the worked instances, and **none of them is a ruin argument**. Each turns on the gap between a period mean and a realised path, and each would survive intact if no institution in the sample had ever been absorbed.

So the apparatus has two limbs which are formally independent — the demonstration is at [[Ergodicity breaking and order-dependence are independent axes]] and, in its cleanest form, at [[Galton-Watson is the extinction branch Peters named and did not develop]] — and the objection lands on only one of them.

## What must be conceded

The objection is not fully answered, and pretending otherwise would be the failure mode of [[Do not claim history-dependence as the novelty]] in a new place.

**The barrier limb genuinely is classical.** Absorption, first-passage, extinction probability: 1875 to 1950, and the project imports rather than invents it. The novelty at [[Absorption is a path-dependence mechanism distinct from reinforcement]] is not that absorption is a new mathematical object but that it has not been developed as a *path-dependence mechanism* alongside reinforcement, which is a claim about a literature rather than about a theorem. State it that way.

**Where a chapter runs purely off ruin, it should not claim ergodicity as its warrant.** If the argument is that a form was eliminated and its elimination conditioned the sample, that is a selection argument and it needs [[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]], not the time-ensemble distinction. Invoking the framework where it is not load-bearing is what invites the charge that it is decorative.

**The strongest formulation is conjunctive, and should be written as one sentence.** Ruin supplies the mechanism of elimination; the time-average criterion supplies what the survivors were selected *on*; entity-shielding supplies the institutional technology that relocates the barrier and thereby changes which trajectories are eliminated. Remove any of the three and the argument is either classical, or unfalsifiable, or not about institutions.
