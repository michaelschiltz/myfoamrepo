# Medici Archive Project — the BIA platform

<https://www.medici.org/>

A project rather than a repository: decades of document-level annotation of the **Mediceo del Principato** at [[Archivio di Stato di Firenze]], served through the BIA platform.

## What it actually is, and why the distinction matters

**Not a transcribed corpus in the GLOBALISE sense.** BIA is an annotation and entity-linking layer over selected documents — people, places, subjects, with abstracts — built by scholars working document by document. That makes it **layer 1 of extraordinary quality over a selection**, rather than layer 3 over a series.

The difference decides what can be asked of it. A machine-transcribed corpus supports a query over everything and an unmeasured error rate; a scholar-annotated selection supports a precise query over **whatever the scholars chose to annotate**, and the selection is invisible from inside the interface.

**Any count taken from BIA is a count of the project's attention.** That is the same structure as [[The QDL corpus is conditioned on its funder's geography]] — a selection honestly made and openly stated, which nonetheless does not have a denominator — and the failure mode is identical: mistaking a well-built research instrument for a sample.

⚠️ Current access terms — open, registered or subscription — are unverified and have changed over the platform's life. Establish before relying on it.
