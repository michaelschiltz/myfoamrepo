# Delpher — the full-text layer

<https://www.delpher.nl/> · KB, the national library

**The only layer-3 resource of its size in the European set that is not an archive.** Over **18 million** Dutch-language newspaper pages, full-text searchable, plus digitised books and periodicals — and the newspaper texts for **1618–1879 are downloadable as open datasets**, not merely searchable through a web form.

## Why a newspaper corpus belongs in an archive hub

Because for this project's questions it is not a newspaper corpus. The Dutch commercial press carried the serial financial record that archives hold only in fragments:

- **Price currents and course lists** — the prijscouranten, the raw material of any claim about what was traded and at what price.
- **Shipping intelligence** — arrivals, departures, losses. The denominator for any statement about maritime peril.
- **Insurance and average notices**, bottomry advertisements, and the public side of the business whose private side is at [[Kamer van Assurantie en Averij - Amsterdam]].
- **Bankruptcy and liquidation notices** — the public record of failure, which is where entity and owner shielding become visible as practice rather than doctrine.

## The methodological reason it is valuable, and the one it is dangerous

Valuable: it is the one Dutch source that can supply a **denominator**. Almost every claim this project would like to make about frequency founders on not knowing what the population was; a serial press run over two centuries is the nearest thing to a population register the commercial world left. See the standing prohibition in [[The VOC-EIC digital asymmetry is an access regime not a survival difference]] against frequencies computable on one side only — Delpher is what makes the Dutch side computable, and therefore what makes the asymmetry worse, not better.

Dangerous: **OCR over two centuries of Dutch typography has an unmeasured and non-uniform error rate**, and it is not uniform in a way that is innocent — older, denser, more damaged issues read worse, so a naive frequency series will show an upward trend that is an artefact of the scanning. Any Delpher-derived count must be reported with its error rate estimated on a sample, or not reported. The same discipline [[Open-source HTR stack - eScriptorium Kraken Loghi]] demands of HTR output applies here and is more easily forgotten because the interface is a search box.

⚠️ Establish the OCR quality figures the KB publishes, if any, and whether the downloadable 1618–1879 texts carry per-page confidence.
