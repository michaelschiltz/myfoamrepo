# Stadtarchiv Leipzig and the fairs

⚠️ **Unverified throughout.** Recorded as the second half of the German fair question opened at [[Institut fur Stadtgeschichte Frankfurt]].

Leipzig's fairs were the eastern node of the central-European payments network and the point of contact with the Polish, Bohemian and Russian trades. The city archive holds the civic record; the Saxon state archive holds what passed to the electorate.

## The question worth asking here

Whether a **fair settlement** is a pooling institution in the census's sense. At a payment fair, mutual obligations were set off against one another and only the net balances moved — which is a **multilateral netting arrangement** whose participants collectively bore the risk of a defaulting member.

**If that is pooling, the census has no row for it and should.** If it is not, saying why would sharpen `MC1` — the characteristic whose three values the logbook already records as non-exclusive and non-partitioning.

⚠️ This is a question for a selection brief, not a coding, and it rests on no source. Recorded so that it is not lost.
