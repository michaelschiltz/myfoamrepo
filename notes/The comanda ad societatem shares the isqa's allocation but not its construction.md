# The comanda ad societatem shares the isqa's allocation but not its construction

The Catalan loss-sharing comanda puts the working party in the position the triad had located only in the *ʿisqa*: exposed to the principal's capital loss. It reaches that position by a different route, and the difference of route is the evidence that matters.

**Same allocation.** In the loss census `comanda_ad_societatem_alloc` matches `isqa_alloc` on `RB1`, `RB2`, `RB4 = 0` and `PR1`, and differs on `RB3` alone; it differs from `societas_maris` on `RB3` and `RB4`. In the organizational census both have `LR6 = symmetric` and an agent exposed to the principal's loss (`CF2 = P` against `1`).

**Do not count those cells.** `CF2`, `RB4`, `LR6` and `RB1` largely record one fact, whether the agent stands to lose the principal's money, seen through four characteristics. A tally would put the Catalan form nearer the Jewish one than the Genoese one, and the tally would be wrong. See [[Count degrees of freedom not cells]].

**Different construction.**

- **The *ʿisqa* by vesting.** Half the advance vests in the manager as a loan (*pelga milveh*, BT *Bava Metzia* 104b), so his liability on that half runs against his estate whatever the venture does. Exposure is built into the ownership of the stake. See [[The isqa refuses the agent's shield]].
- **The comanda *ad societatem* by a loss clause.** A diminution is shared in the profit proportion until the investor's capital is restored (*quousque … penitus reintegretur*, Vic 1273), secured by a general obligation of the agents' goods and, in that act, their liability *quisque in solidum*. Exposure is an allocation term in a contract that leaves ownership of the capital with the investor.

**What that licenses.** A shared function reached by unrelated arbitrary particulars is the configuration in which the stemmatic diagnostic favours independent arrival over transmission ([[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]]). No descent is claimed and none is excluded; the Catalan form sits inside the Latin drafting tradition, so its relation to the *ʿisqa* is a question of [[Transmission versus independent recurrence is the load-bearing test]], not a finding. For the triad ([[Isqa qirad and commenda are a liability-variable triad]]) the gain is structural: the agent-exposed regime now has a western instance, so the liability variable no longer separates the Jewish form from the others by tradition alone.

## Where this is soft

**`RB4 = 0` may not hold.** The pilot's stage-5 reading of Amalric 429 and 236 showed that a promise to bring back the capital does not assume the peril: the same formula stands in acts that put the peril on both partners pro rata. Vic 1273 survives that objection on `CF2`, because its clause speaks to diminution and to shares. `RB4` asks about total loss, and a make-whole clause drafted for a diminution may presuppose a surviving venture. The text of García Sanz 1959, doc. 5 decides it. The evidence base is about six acts.
