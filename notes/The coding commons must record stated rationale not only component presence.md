# The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence

The shared coding ontology represents each tradition's instruments against the five-component grid. As specified, it records presence and absence. That is not enough, and the *asiento de avería* shows why: a grid that enters "transferable, depersonalised claim: present" for both the *asiento* and the VOC has erased the fact that one manufactured anonymity to accommodate noble honour and the other to scale an impersonal pool. See [[The depersonalised share was produced by honour not by pooling logic]].

The defect is structural rather than incidental. If components are coded by function alone, then every instance of a component confirms the functional reading, because the function is what the coding scheme is made of. Rival causal pathways cannot appear in the corpus, since the corpus has no field in which they could be written. The instrument would then guarantee the result — which is the Maslow's-hammer objection realised in software rather than in prose, and far harder to see.

The remedy is a mandatory **stated-rationale** field beside each component: the reason the sources themselves give for the component's presence, coded from the source's own vocabulary, versioned, dated and attributed like every other coding decision. Presence and rationale then cross-tabulate. A component that recurs across traditions under divergent stated rationales is evidence of independent arrival at a solution; one that recurs with the *same* stated rationale is a candidate for transmission. The field is therefore not decoration on the comparative claim — it is what makes [[Transmission versus independent recurrence is the load-bearing test]] operable at the level of the corpus rather than the level of the argument.

Three design consequences follow. The field must permit **null** — an institution whose advocates articulated no rationale is a finding, and must be coded distinctly from one that has simply not been read. It must permit **multiple, conflicting** rationales, since traditions argue with themselves. And it must be coded **before** the time-average analysis is brought to bear, since the auditable sequence is the whole point of versioning: a reader must be able to inspect what was claimed of a source before the framework was applied.

This is a WP4 schema requirement, not a methodological aspiration, and it should be settled in the codebook at the first reconciliation rather than retrofitted. The prototype's schema is additive-extensible by design, so the cost of adding it now is small and the cost of adding it in year four is the recoding of everything already entered.
