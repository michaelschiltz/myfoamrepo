# Geniza partnership documents — where the census's *ʿisqa* and *qirāḍ* evidence sits

**The one place this pass touches a coded row directly.** `isqa_alloc` in `loss_mitigation_forms` carries a **DISPUTE HELD OPEN AS A CODING**: Ackerman-Lieberman 2011, 664 reports that Geniza legal documents for investment partnerships "allocate both partners a share of both profits and losses, with very few documents indemnifying the active partner" — *ʿisqa*-shaped on his reading, *qirāḍ* in practice on Udovitch's and Goitein's. `qirad_alloc` cites the same pages for "the Geniza documents that cut against the doctrinal coding".

## Where the documents are

**Across the major collections, not in one.** Ackerman-Lieberman reports **more than one hundred unpublished partnership agreements** in the main collections, in addition to those already edited. The shelfmarks he gives as examples of partnerships contracted for a year or more:

| Shelfmark | Collection |
|---|---|
| `T-S 10 J 7.6 B`; `T-S NS J 74` | [[Cambridge University Library - the Taylor-Schechter Genizah Collection]] |
| `ENA NS 17.35` | [[JTS Library - the Elkan Nathan Adler Geniza collection]] |
| `Bodl. MS. Heb. c 28.32`; `Heb. e 98.63` | [[Bodleian Library - the Oxford Genizah fragments]] |
| `Mosseri L 39b` (a letter) | Cambridge, Mosseri Collection — on loan |

**Already edited, and therefore at layer 3 in print:** Moshe Gil, *Be-malkhut Yishmaʿel bi-tequfat ha-geʾonim* (Tel Aviv, 1997); Gershon Weiss, *Legal Documents Written by the Court Clerk Halfon ben Manasse (Dated 1100–1138)* (PhD, University of Pennsylvania, 1970); and for letters, Goitein, *Letters of Medieval Jewish Traders* (Princeton, 1974, as cited). ⚠️ The article's own appendix of documents was not read — the text read ran to p. 655 — so the list above is a sample, not the corpus.

## The finding: the dispute is a record-type difference

**Udovitch and Goitein argued from *letters*; Ackerman-Lieberman argues from *legal documents*.** He says so in terms: letters "often lack detail that allows the researcher to determine precisely which form of commercial cooperation was actually used", while legal documents supply it (654); and Udovitch's own ratio of informal to formal arrangements was fifteen or twenty to one (655).

**That is pattern 4 of [[Handoff - the non-European archive survey]] — the record type is a variable — arriving on a coded row.** Two readings of "Geniza practice" that disagree may each be right about the record type it read: **a letter records what the correspondents needed to say; a deed records the clauses a court would enforce.** Before the census resolves the `isqa_alloc` dispute in either direction it should ask whether the two sides are describing the same documents. **They are not.**

## What the digital layers now allow

- **Find**: [[MiDRASH - the machine transcription of the Geniza]] makes every Hebrew-script fragment searchable for the terms; [[Princeton Geniza Project - the documentary database]] types and dates documents and links them to scholarship.
- **Not count**: machine text is noisy, the documentary share is a filtered sample, and the deposit is one community's. **Never set a Geniza frequency beside a Genoese notarial one** — different record types, different survival rules.
