# Centrum för Näringslivshistoria — Stockholm

<https://www.naringslivshistoria.se/>

Sweden's centre for business history: a **foundation holding the archives of Swedish companies**, funded by the companies themselves.

## The fifth national answer to one question

| Country | Business archive |
|---|---|
| France | [[Archives nationales du monde du travail - Roubaix]] — national, state |
| Netherlands | [[IISG and NEHA - Amsterdam]] — one institution, non-state |
| Germany | [[The German Wirtschaftsarchive - a distributed business archive]] — regional, chamber-attached |
| Belgium | none; private archives merely registered |
| Sweden | **a foundation funded by the depositing companies** |

**Five countries, five arrangements, and the Swedish one is the most revealing**: the companies pay to have their own history kept. That is the successor-custody pattern — [[Fuggerarchiv - Dillingen]], [[The Bazacle records - a custody problem]] — **institutionalised and made respectable**, but it is the same structure, and it carries the same consequence: **the archives of durable firms are held by arrangements those firms fund.**

⚠️ Whether that affects access or appraisal in practice is not something this survey can establish, and it should not be asserted. **What can be said is that the custody is not neutral, and a register column recording who pays for a repository is not a paranoid column.**

⚠️ Holdings, period coverage and access terms unverified; a modern business-history centre is unlikely to reach the project's core period, as at [[Archives nationales du monde du travail - Roubaix]] and [[KADOC - religious archives at Leuven]].
