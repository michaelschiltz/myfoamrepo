# Tamil Nadu Archives — the Madras records and the Dutch series

Chennai, the former Madras Record Office. **Holdings include the records of the East India Company from 1670 to 1857**, the Madras government's departmental series to the present, settlement and *inam* registers, field measurement books, **and Dutch, Danish and Persian records**.

## The Dutch series, and why it closes an open flag

**About 1,763 volumes of VOC records** created by the Company's establishments on the Coromandel coast. Under a project of the Tamil Nadu Archives with the **Nationaal Archief** and the **Royal Netherlands Embassy in New Delhi**: cleaning, mending and boxing of **about 1,800 volumes**, and **scanning of the unique material — about two-thirds of the series**. ⚠️ Whether the scans are online was not established.

**This answers part of the flag left at [[VOC digital corpus - what is online and on what terms]]**, which recorded the Colombo, Chennai and Cape Town holdings as "described but only patchily imaged, unresearched". **Chennai is imaged for what is unique; Colombo has had the same treatment** — see [[Sri Lanka National Archives - the Dutch records and the thombos]].

## For the census

**Marginal, with one exception.** The Dutch and Company series are the trading companies' own operational record, and the census's company rows are coded from elsewhere. **The exception is the Coromandel merchant-financing material** — advances to weavers, joint contracts with merchant groups — which is the Indian Ocean counterpart of the *commenda* questions. ⚠️ Named as a possibility, not examined.

**Access**: on site. ⚠️ Terms not established; the archive's own pages list stacks, not conditions.
