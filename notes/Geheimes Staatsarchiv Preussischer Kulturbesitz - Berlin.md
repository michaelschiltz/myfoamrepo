# Geheimes Staatsarchiv Preußischer Kulturbesitz — Berlin

<https://gsta.preussischer-kulturbesitz.de/>

The archive of Brandenburg-Prussia, with a thematic guide to the state's pre-1806 external relations and a published list of digitised holdings.

## The case it holds

The **Brandenburgisch-Africanische Compagnie**, chartered 1682 — a German chartered overseas trading company, with a fort on the Gold Coast, built in explicit imitation of the Dutch and English models, **and a failure**. It was wound up and its African holdings sold to the Dutch in the early eighteenth century.

## Why a failed chartered company is the point

The entity census codes the VOC in three period rows, the English chartered corporation, the deed-of-settlement company, the Casa di San Giorgio. **Every one of them is a survivor.**

[[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]] is the project's own statement of why that is a problem, and [[Insolvente Boedelskamer - Antwerp]] is the loss-census remedy — an archive of firms that failed. **The Brandenburg company is the entity-side counterpart**: a chartered body with the standard apparatus that did not last, whose constitutive documents survive in a state archive precisely because the state chartered it.

**A form's characteristics are not explanatory if every case coded has the same outcome.** Adding a chartered company that failed is the cheapest available way to put variance into the outcome the census never records.

⚠️ Which series hold the company's record, and whether they are digitised, is unestablished; the GStA publishes a list of digitised holdings which should be checked first.
