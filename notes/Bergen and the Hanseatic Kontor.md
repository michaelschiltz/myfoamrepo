# Bergen and the Hanseatic Kontor

Bergen byarkiv and the regional *statsarkiv*; museum holdings at Det Hanseatiske Museum / Stiftelsen Bryggen.

**One of the four Hanseatic *Kontore*** — with Bruges, London and Novgorod — and the only one whose physical quarter substantially survives.

## Why the Kontor is the interesting object

A *Kontor* was **a foreign merchant community with its own jurisdiction, its own statutes, its own property and its own admission rules, operating inside another kingdom's city.** That is the identity-wrapper form the vault separates at [[Identity-wrapper and perpetual-monopoly are distinct shielding facets]], and Bergen is one of its four canonical instances.

**It therefore belongs with a set the survey has been assembling across four countries**: the *naties* at Antwerp ([[Natie archives - where the Antwerp nations records are]]), the merchant nations at Bruges ([[Stadsarchief Brugge - the Hanse and the nations]]), the Castilian consulate at Bruges ([[Consulado de Burgos - the universidad de mercaderes]]), and the French Levant consulates ([[Archives diplomatiques - La Courneuve and Nantes]]).

**Five instances of one form, in five archival systems, and `organizational_forms` codes `natie` alone.** The row exists; the comparative set it should sit in does not.

## The custody problem, which is the usual one for this form

⚠️ The Kontor's own records are **not all in Bergen.** Hanseatic administrative records were kept by the league's cities, and Lübeck holds a substantial part of the Bergen material — see [[Archiv der Hansestadt Lubeck]]. Museum custody at Bryggen holds a further part. **Establish the division before planning anything**, and expect the answer to be that no single repository has the institution.

That is the same structural point as [[Hanse documents on the Memory of the World register]]: a body without a state leaves its record distributed across its members.
