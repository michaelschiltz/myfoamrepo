# Archivio di Stato di Venezia

<https://www.archiviodistatovenezia.it/> · catalogue: <https://asve.arianna4.cloud/>

**The single richest European repository for the forms this project codes**, and the one whose material is already load-bearing in the vault: the *colleganza*, the *fraterna compagnia*, and the Venetian side of the commenda comparison in [[Ragusan collegantia is unilateral and Venetian collegantia is bilateral]] and [[Unilateral and bilateral commenda collapse on profit and separate on loss]].

## The fondi that matter

**`Notarile`** — the instruments. *Colleganze*, *fraterne*, insurance policies, partnership agreements. Everything the coding is actually built on.

**`Giudici di Petizion`** — the commercial court, and for the *fraterna* the crucial series: partnership disputes and estate divisions are where the terms of an undivided fraternal holding get written down, because that is when they fail. ⚠️ The *fraterna* placement in the form table rests on this and has not been checked against an inventory.

**`Cinque Savi alla Mercanzia`** — the magistracy for commerce from 1507. Policy, regulation and the state's own view of what merchants were doing.

**`Senato Mar`** — maritime policy, convoy regulation, the *muda* system. The convoy is a pooling arrangement the project has not yet coded and probably should.

**`Provveditori sopra Banchi`** — supervision of the Venetian banks.

⚠️ Reference codes and extents for all five need transcribing from SIAS or from *moreveneto*; the descriptions consulted here were secondary.

## moreveneto and Arianna4

*moreveneto* is the archive's re-description of its own holdings — a genuine intellectual achievement and **layer 1 at high quality**. The consultation catalogue runs on Arianna4 at `asve.arianna4.cloud`, which exposes item-level records.

**Imaging is thin.** ⚠️ Establish what, if anything, of `Notarile` or `Giudici di Petizion` is imaged before assuming any of this can be worked remotely. Working assumption: a Venice trip, with a camera, subject to the archive's own photography rules.

## Why the convoy question is worth raising here

The *muda* pooled peril across ships by regulation rather than by contract — the state compelling an arrangement the parties did not negotiate. **Parked** in `HistorEE_codebooks`, logbook 2, 2026-09-10, where it bears on a question the loss census has already recorded and cannot answer: `averia_pool` is a compulsory convoy levy, `PY0`'s definition names convoy escort as its worked example of a collective good, and nothing explains the compulsion.

**The boundary problem is why it is parked and not recommended.** If the *muda* pools by regulating sailing rather than through a fund, there is no fund for the census's characteristics to describe, and a row would put a regulatory rule and a contributory pool in one column on the strength of a shared word. It is also possible the codable object is the *incanto* — the auction of the galleys to merchant syndicates — which is a different institution and would belong to the entity census. Venice is the archive that would settle it; [[A scheme for extending the cooperative pooling census]] is where the case would be argued.
