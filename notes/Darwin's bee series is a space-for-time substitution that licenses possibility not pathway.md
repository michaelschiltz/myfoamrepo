# Darwin's bee series is a space-for-time substitution that licenses possibility not pathway

**Darwin answered the design argument from the honeycomb by arranging living bees in a graded series — the humble-bee's rough cylinders and spheres, the stingless *Melipona*'s rough comb of nearly spherical cells, the hive bee's hexagons — and reading it as the route by which comb-building could have evolved. The three are sister lineages; none descends from another. The series is a cross-section read as a trajectory: a space-for-time substitution, made by the founder of evolutionary theory. It is legitimate in its weak form only. It shows that each state is *reachable* by small steps from the one before it, which defeats a claim of impossibility. It cannot show that any lineage *passed through* those states. The same discipline binds every series HistorEE lines up.**

## The argument and its target

Brougham's *Dissertations* (1839) presented the cell as proof of a designing teacher and denied that any bee built cylindrical cells, so that no gradual path from a simple cell to the hexagon existed. Darwin, who had annotated Brougham, knew the humble-bee's cylindrical and near-spherical honey and brood cells, took delight in *Melipona domestica*'s intermediate comb, and ran his own wax experiments at Down in the 1850s. The argument in *Origin* ch. VII ("Instinct") is that if *Melipona* set its spheres at regular distances in a double layer, their intersections would yield the planes and rhombic bases of the hive bee's comb: a short sequence of small modifications, each useful in its own right, connects the simple cell to the hexagon. (Darwin Correspondence Project, "The evolution of the honeycomb"; *Origin* 1859, ch. VII [verify page against the first edition].)

## Why it is a space-for-time substitution

*Bombus*, *Melipona* and *Apis* belong to the corbiculate bees, whose tribes are sister lineages; the hive bee is not a descendant of either [verify the current tribal topology, which has been debated]. Darwin's stages are three contemporaries ranked by the form of their cells. The series has the same structure as a persistence design: units observed at one time, arranged on a variable, interpreted as moments of one process.

The vault's licence condition fits exactly ([[A significant persistence coefficient is evidence against its own licensing condition]]): space-for-time is licensed when one unit's present is a state another *could have reached*. That condition has two strengths. **Reachability** — the state lies within the dynamics' reach of its neighbours — is what Darwin's series establishes, and all his argument needs, because Brougham's claim was an impossibility claim. **Passage** — this lineage actually went through these states — is what the series cannot establish, and what a historical narrative needs. The series is sound against design and silent on history.

## The series is ordered on the outcome

The middle term exposes the second weakness. *Melipona* is not intermediate in sociality: stingless bees are highly eusocial with perennial colonies, yet store honey in clusters of oval cerumen pots, dismantled when empty and their material reused. Darwin's series is ordered by cell geometry — the outcome — not by the variable whose evolution it is meant to display. A series ranked on the dependent variable cannot test the mechanism that is supposed to drive it; it can only illustrate that the outcome varies by degrees. That is the ascent narrative's construction ([[The ascent narrative is the Jensen error as historiography]], [[Whiggish history of finance names the ascent narrative]]).

## The transfer

- **Licit.** A series of extant or attested forms can defeat a claim that some component *requires* another: the fictive *ie* shows partition and perpetuity without juridical personality ([[The fictive household supplied partition and perpetuity without legal personality]]). That is Darwin's use — reachability against impossibility.
- **Illicit.** *Qirāḍ* → *commenda* → *compagnia* → joint-stock company, or *kō* → *mujin* → bank, ranked by resemblance to the corporation and read as a path. That is ordering on the outcome and reading a cross-section as a trajectory at once.
- **Coding consequence.** Any series drawn from the codebooks must be ordered on an independent axis — date of first attestation, documentary series, region — never on component count or resemblance to an endpoint; and a claim of passage requires a within-lineage documentary series, the drafting record of [[The optimum does not identify the route that reached it]] [check against the HistorEE_codebooks schema].

The phylogenetic vocabulary is already in the vault for the inverse case: shared ancestral characters cannot group ([[Deep-roots variables are symplesiomorphies]]). Darwin's series is the complementary error's respectable ancestor — it groups by outcome resemblance, which is homoplasy when the forms arose independently ([[Homoplasy is the finding not the noise]]); honey bees and social wasps reached hexagonal comb independently (Smith et al. 2023, *PLOS Biology* 21: e3002211).
