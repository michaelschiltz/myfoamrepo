# Individuation moves the absorbing barrier from the member to the unit

**In a pool, the absorbing barrier is each member's ruin. In a unit, it is the unit's dissolution, and a member's ruin need not reach it. That is a third mark of individuation beside closure and the immune boundary, and it gives *Meditations* 6.54 an exact reading: the swarm's ruin entails the bee's, the bee's ruin does not entail the swarm's. The reading needs nothing beyond the first-passage result and is less speculative than the horizon reading in [[Fitness decoupling may be a short-horizon artefact]].**

## The colony

The colony removes mite-infested brood (social apoptosis, Page et al. 2016) and loses foragers without loss to itself, because a worker's death is not an absorbing event for the colony. The colony's death is absorbing for every worker. The asymmetry of the Stoic sentence — the swarm's advantage is necessary, not sufficient, for the bee's — is the asymmetry of two nested barriers. The honey store is the colony's buffer against its own barrier, the winter it must outlast ([[Under absorption pooling raises the probability of ruin to the power of the pool]] for the role of the buffer $s$ in $e^{-2\mu s/\sigma^2}$).

## The hive shields in one direction, the corporation in both

In the vocabulary of [[Entity shielding and owner shielding protect different trajectories]], the colony has entity shielding — no worker's fate drags it down — and no owner shielding: no worker survives the colony. The limited-liability corporation has both. The member's exit, which [[The hive models the corporation's emergent layer not the route by which it formed]] already sets against the hive, is the owner-shielding half stated in barrier terms: the member's barrier and the unit's are decoupled both ways.

## Perpetuity

Perpetual succession states the same move for lines of descent. A unit that survives while any of $k$ independent lines survives dies out with probability $q^k$ ([[Perpetual succession redefines the unit of survival from a line to a union of lines]]). This gives the reason perpetuity pays. It does not give the means, which come from law ([[Legal scaffolding gives a pool collective properties it does not yet own]]). It corrects the 2026-11-08 claim in [[Pooling raises the rate and individuation draws the boundary]] that ergodicity does not "explain the perpetuity": the barrier route explains why it is worth having.

## For the coding

The test of individuation in a historical form becomes, in part: does a member's ruin dissolve the form? A *commenda* that ends when the travelling partner is lost has its barrier at the member; a form that replaces the lost member and continues has moved it. That is an observable in contract clauses (death, bankruptcy and withdrawal provisions), and it is coded already in pieces (`LP1`, `TS1`, the liquidation-protection and succession variables) without having been read as one variable.
