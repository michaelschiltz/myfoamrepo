# The London Archives

<https://www.thelondonarchives.org/> — formerly the London Metropolitan Archives

The archive of London and its predecessor authorities, and one of the three London repositories this project needs alongside [[Guildhall Library and the City livery companies]] and [[The Bank of England Archive]].

## What is here

⚠️ Series unverified throughout. The holdings that matter in principle: the City's own record, the Middlesex and Surrey sessions, hospital and charity foundations, and **Lloyd's Captains Register, 1869–1947**, which is held here rather than at Guildhall.

**The London custody map is the trap.** Lloyd's material is split — the Marine Collection at Guildhall, the Captains Register here, the Corporation of Lloyd's own records elsewhere again. **Three London institutions divide one market's archive**, and a search that stops at the first will report an absence. Same structure as the Antwerp notarial trap at [[Rijksarchief te Antwerpen and Beveren - the notarial series]], in one city rather than across a border.
