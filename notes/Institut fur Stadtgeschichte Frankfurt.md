# Institut für Stadtgeschichte Frankfurt

<https://www.stadtgeschichte-ffm.de/> · described in Hesse's **Arcinsys** system

Frankfurt's city archive, holding the record of the imperial city and of its **fairs**.

## Why the fair matters institutionally

A fair is a **periodic legal regime, not a place**: for its duration a different law of contract, credit and enforcement applied, with its own court and its own settlement conventions. The Frankfurt and Leipzig fairs were the German nodes of the European payments system, and the *Zahlungsmesse* — the payment fair at which bills fell due — is the clearing mechanism that makes bill finance work at all.

**The project has a dataset called `clearing_records`, and the fairs are the European instance of what it describes.** ⚠️ Whether the census's Japanese clearing material and the European fair-settlement material are the same kind of object is a live and unasked question; Frankfurt and [[Stadtarchiv Leipzig and the fairs]] are where the European side would be evidenced.
