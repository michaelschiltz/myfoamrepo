# Real Chancillería de Valladolid

<https://www.cultura.gob.es/cultura/areas/archivos/mc/archivos/acv.html> · catalogue via [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]]

**The appellate civil court for Castile north of the Tagus**, and the Spanish member of the litigation-series family this survey keeps returning to: [[Hof van Holland - the civil litigation record]], `Giudici di Petizion` at [[Archivio di Stato di Venezia]], the *Mercanzia* at [[Archivio di Stato di Firenze]].

## The series

**`Pleitos civiles`** — the civil suits, which are the point. Commercial partnerships, *compañías*, insurance and freight disputes reach this court on appeal, and an appellate record has a property the first-instance ones lack: **the parties have already stated their positions once and are now arguing about what the first statement meant.** That is where a drafting convention gets explained rather than merely used.

⚠️ Extent, date range and the proportion of commercial matter are all unestablished. The archive's own exhibition work on foreign merchants suggests the material is there; that is a curatorial judgement, not a finding aid.

The **`Pergaminos`** collection has been digitised. ⚠️ Whether any of the *pleitos* are imaged is unverified, and given the volume it is unlikely at scale.

## Why it was omitted from the first Spanish pass and should not have been

The first pass was scoped to the Atlantic complex, the Crown of Aragon and the consulado towns, and litigation was not a category in that scheme. **The category arrived later, out of the Dutch and Italian passes**, and this is the row it generates in Spain. Recorded here so the Spanish set is not silently organised on a principle the other countries' sets have since abandoned.
