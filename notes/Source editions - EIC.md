# Source editions — EIC

The printed source series for the English East India Company. **The archive and digital layer is already recorded** at [[British Library - the India Office Records]] and [[EIC digital corpus - what is online and on what terms]]. **Almost everything below is free**, which is the reverse of the EIC's commercial digital collection.

## The court minutes — a continuous run in print, 1599–1679

- **Henry Stevens (ed.), *The Dawn of British Trade to the East Indies as recorded in the Court Minutes of the East India Company, 1599–1603*** (London: H. Stevens & Son, 1886), xxiv + 331 pp. **Full transcription of the first minute book: the 1599 subscription, the formation of the Company, the First Voyage.** Free on HathiTrust and archive.org.
- **W. Noel Sainsbury (ed.), *Calendar of State Papers, Colonial: East Indies, China and Japan***, 5 vols (London: Longman, 1864–92; CSP Colonial vols 2, 3, 4, 6, 8), covering **1513–1634** — **which calendars the Court Minutes for 1603–1634 as well as State Papers**, including share prices of the first and second joint stocks and remarks on private trade. **Free keyed text on British History Online.** *This fills the gap before the next item.*
- **Ethel Bruce Sainsbury (ed.), *A Calendar of the Court Minutes etc. of the East India Company***, 11 vols (Oxford: Clarendon, 1907–38), **1635–1679** (minutes for July 1637–July 1639 lost). **The core source for the joint stocks, dividends, the 1657 permanent stock and private-trade licences.** HathiTrust full view for vols 1, 2, 4–6; archive.org for others.

## Charters, letters, factories

- **John Shaw (ed.), *Charters relating to the East India Company from 1600 to 1761*** (Madras: Government Press, 1887), xx + 310 pp. **The constitution itself.** Free on archive.org.
- **Birdwood & Foster (eds), *The Register of Letters &c. of the Governour and Company … 1600–1619*** (London: Quaritch, 1893) — the "First Letter Book": commissions, instructions, limits on private trade. Free.
- **Danvers & Foster (eds), *Letters Received by the East India Company from its Servants in the East***, 6 vols (1896–1902), 1602–1617 — full transcriptions; all free on HathiTrust.
- **Foster (ed.), *The English Factories in India, 1618–1669***, 13 vols (Oxford: Clarendon, 1906–27), and **Fawcett's new series**, 4 vols (1936–55), 1670–84 — calendars; private and country trade.
- ***Records of Fort St George***: *Diary and Consultation Book*, 86 vols (Madras, 1894–1946), 1672–1756 — mostly on archive.org.
- **Hakluyt Society**: of the EIC volumes, **Yule's edition of *The Diary of William Hedges* (1887–89)** is the one for **interlopers and the challenge to the monopoly**; the rest are voyage narratives.
- **John Bruce, *Annals of the Honorable East-India Company, 1600–1707/8***, 3 vols (1810) — a chronicle built from the records; the stocks, the 1698 New Company, the union of 1702–09. Free.

## Why this matters for the census

**The EIC's constitutional record is in print and free from 1599 to 1679 without a break** — Stevens, then Noel Sainsbury's calendar, then Ethel Sainsbury's. **The VOC's equivalent series, the Heren XVII resolutions, is not in print at all** ([[Source editions - VOC]]). **The printed asymmetry runs the opposite way to the digital one** recorded at [[The VOC-EIC digital asymmetry is an access regime not a survival difference]].
