# Itinera Nova — the Leuven aldermen's registers

<https://www.itineranova.be/> · registers: <https://schepenbankregisters.leuven.be/>

**Belgium's layer-3 corpus, and proof that the country's thin digital infrastructure is a policy fact rather than a capability one.** Spain has nothing like this. Italy has nothing like this.

## What it is

The Leuven *schepenbankregisters* and *stadsrekeningen*, **fourteenth to eighteenth century**, scanned and then **transcribed by volunteers** so that the texts are fully searchable. The project passed its **hundred-thousandth transcribed act** in 2024 and has since begun using machine recognition to accelerate the remaining registers. Free and public.

## What an aldermen's register is, and why it is the right series

The *schepenbank* registered the acts that in Amsterdam went to a notary: conveyances, obligations, annuities, partnership agreements, guarantees. **For the Low Countries the aldermen's register is the notarial archive**, and a transcribed run from 1362 is a source of a kind that exists in exactly two places in Europe — here and at [[Stadsarchief Amsterdam - the notarial archive]].

## What it is good for and what it is not

**Good for:** the *rente* and the annuity, urban credit, guarantee and suretyship, and the terms on which townspeople bound themselves and their heirs. These are the instruments the entity census reaches least well, because they are neither maritime nor corporate.

**Not good for:** the maritime and company material this project's spine runs on. Leuven is an inland university and brewing town, not a port. **Do not let searchability decide the corpus** — the temptation to work the transcribed source because it answers queries is precisely the mechanism [[The VOC-EIC digital asymmetry is an access regime not a survival difference]] warns about, and it bites hardest when the searchable corpus is genuinely excellent.

⚠️ The transcription is volunteer work over six centuries of hands, with a correspondingly uneven and unpublished error profile. Establish whether the platform exposes any per-act confidence or review status before any count is taken from it.
