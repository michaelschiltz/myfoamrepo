# `BT 41`, `BT 31` and the company files

At [[The National Archives - Kew]]. **The single hardest access fact in the European survey, and it bears on a coded row.**

## The series

**`BT 41`** — files of joint-stock companies registered under the **1844 Act** and dissolved before 1856, plus those re-registered under the 1856 Act and dissolved before 1860. Contents: the nature of the business, company address, **names of promoters and their solicitors, a prospectus, details of capital, the issue and allocation of shares, balance sheets.**

**`BT 31`** — dissolved companies registered from 1856: **memorandum and articles of association** with the company's constitution, **lists and stakes of shareholders**, registered office, register of directors, annual returns. **A sample selection, not comprehensive.**

**`BT 34`** — liquidators' accounts for companies dissolved 1890–1932.

## The fact

**TNA states that none of the company records it holds are available to view online.** Access is Kew or ordered copies.

## Why this is not just inconvenient

`deed_of_settlement_company` is a coded row, and `chartered_corporation_england` beside it. **The serial constitutional record of the English company — the thing that would let either row rest on documents rather than on a monograph — is description-only.**

That is the origin of the whole funding-map argument: [[The VOC-EIC digital asymmetry is an access regime not a survival difference]] was written about exactly this. Five countries later the conclusion is unchanged and better evidenced: **the Dutch company record is transcribed at corpus scale and the English company record cannot be read from a desk at all**, and nothing about the seventeenth or nineteenth century explains the difference.

⚠️ `BT 31` being **a sample** is a second and separate problem, and it is the more dangerous one. A sample selection made by a records administrator for storage reasons is a filter with an unknown rule. **Any frequency computed over `BT 31` is a frequency over that selection**, and the selection criteria should be established before the series is used quantitatively at all.
