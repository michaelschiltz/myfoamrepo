# IDP — the digital reunification of a dispersed archive

The **International Dunhuang Programme**, founded at the British Library in **1994**. <https://idp.bl.uk/>

**As of January 2026: 154,919 catalogue entries, 114,332 items digitised, over 173,850 images**, from **more than 40 institutions in 15 countries**, in more than twenty scripts. Seven founding partners: the BnF, the Berlin-Brandenburg Academy, the British Library, the Dunhuang Research Academy, the Institute of Oriental Manuscripts, the National Library of China and **Ryukoku University**. ⚠️ Figures from the programme's Wikipedia entry; the quarterly update read separately reports the National Library of China centre alone completing **1,448 items and 46,527 images since the beginning of 2023**.

## Why it is the closest thing in the survey to the Geniza's image layer

**Same problem, same answer, twenty years earlier.** One deposit in dozens of collections; a union database with an identifier per item and images from each holder. **Compare [[Friedberg Genizah Project and Ktiv - the image union layer]]**: IDP is older, multilingual, and **it did not reach the text layer** — there is no IDP counterpart to [[MiDRASH - the machine transcription of the Geniza]]. **Layer 2 is solved for the Silk Road; layer 3 is a national publishing programme** — see [[The Silk Road layer 3 is a national editorial programme]].

**The catalogue is the finding aid of record, and it is a union catalogue of expedition shelfmarks** — so a search crosses collections but not the deposit's original order.

⚠️ **Licence and download terms were not established**, and the update read does not address the British Library's post-2023 systems disruption; **check what is actually served before planning any bulk use.**
