# Taleb 2012 on antifragility — loci for the time-ensemble distinction

Nassim Nicholas Taleb, *Antifragile: Things That Gain from Disorder* (New York: Random House; London: Allen Lane, 2012). Read in full from the Random House ebook (ISBN 978-0-679-64527-6). MS works from the Penguin hardback. **Loci below are given by chapter and section heading. Penguin page numbers have not been mapped yet** [verify].

**The book has the time-average ethic and an ensemble formalism, and does not reconcile them.** "Ergodic" occurs zero times. "Ensemble" occurs once, non-technically (the Swiss cantons, ch. 5). "Time average" never occurs. Taleb adopts the ergodicity vocabulary explicitly only in *Skin in the Game* (2018), in the chapter "The Logic of Risk Taking", after Peters and Gell-Mann 2016 [verify chapter number and citation]. The argument is at [[Antifragility is defined by an ensemble Jensen gap]].

## Time-average loci

- **Ch. 11, "On the Irreversibility of Broken Packages."** "The strong logical precedence of survival over success." Path dependence is defined by operations that do not commute: kidney-stone surgery before or after anaesthesia. Effective versus nominal speed: 250 mph in New York City gives zero. "If a gambler has a risk of terminal blowup … the 'potential returns' of his strategy are totally inconsequential." The university endowment earning 20 percent.
- **Ch. 11, "Seneca's Barbell."** The 90/10 split between cash and speculation, with a maximum known loss. Dalio's rule: "Make sure that the probability of the unacceptable (i.e., the risk of ruin) is nil."
- **Ch. 23, "The Problem of Frequency, or How to Lose Arguments."** How often one is right is irrelevant. "For the fragile, a single loss can be terminal."
- **Appendix II notes, "Kelly Criterion vs. Markowitz."** Kelly "requires no joint distribution or utility function." Cites Thorp (1971, 1998) and Haigh (2000), and reports Aaron Brown on economists' rejection of Kelly. This is the only place the book touches the growth criterion, and it sits in the notes, not the text.
- **Ch. 2, footnote to the sentence on "a standard model of selection."** "A stochastic process subjected to an absorbing barrier will have an observed mean higher than the barrier" (the forty-centimetre neck).

## Ensemble loci

- **Glossary, "Philosopher's Stone, also called Convexity Bias."** Antifragility is defined as a positive convexity effect. The bias is "the difference between x and a convex function of x."
- **Chs. 18–19 (Book V).** The convexity apparatus: the large stone against a thousand pebbles, the fragility-detection heuristic, Fannie Mae.
- **Ch. 20, "From Simonides to Jensen"**, and the Lindy effect. **Ch. 25:** "Time is volatility." Lindy is already handled at [[Taleb reads survival as evidence about the survivor and we read it as evidence about the filter]].

## Selection and layering

- **Ch. 4, sections "Antifragility by Layers", "Why the Aggregate Hates the Individual", "What Does Not Kill Me Kills Others", "Me and Us" and "National Entrepreneur Day."** Restaurants. Airlines against banks, i.e. independent against contagious errors. "A system in which nobody's fall can drag others down." The Nietzsche/Gulag inversion. "As a humanist, I stand against the antifragility of systems at the expense of individuals." The cited source for the population framing is Antoine Danchin. Argument at [[Taleb secures the collective by exit and has no theory of pooling]].

## Ethics — Book VII

- **Ch. 23, Table 7.** No skin in the game, skin in the game, soul in the game.
- **Ch. 23, "Hammurabi."** The builder clauses, quoted without section numbers (standardly §§229–231 [verify]). Fat Tony's two heuristics: the pilot is on board; there is also a copilot (redundancy).
- **Ch. 23, "The Talker's Free Option."** "Speculative risk taking is not just permissible; it is mandatory." Used at [[A fair wager lowers both trajectories which maysir forbids and skin in the game permits]].
- **Ch. 23, "The Ancients and the Stiglitz Syndrome."** Roman engineers made to stand under the bridge; the *sacramentum*; decimation; Byng 1757. Tarek 711 is under "To Burn One's Vessels."
- **Ch. 23, "Options, Antifragility, and Social Fairness."** Stock options as a transfer of antifragility. Path 1 and Path 2: the same average reached by different paths. Rubin. Clawbacks are rejected because "at no point is their own net worth endangered." The Catalan banker beheaded in 1360 (Francesco Castello). Unlimited liability for top bankers in Brazil.
- **Ch. 23, "Which Adam Smith?"** Joint-stock directors are "the managers rather of other people's money than of their own." ⚠️ Taleb places the passage in Book IV of *The Wealth of Nations*. It is in Book V, ch. i, part iii, art. 1 [verify against the Glasgow edition paragraph]. Argument at [[Taleb specifies the firewall in chapter 4 and condemns it in chapter 23]].
- **Ch. 24, "The Ethical and the Legal" and "Casuistry as Optionality."** The Blinder deposit-insurance product. An ethical rule is to be stated before the act, not fitted to it afterwards. Table 8.
- **Ch. 25.** Religious law is the refinement of the Golden Rule. "Ethics is largely about stolen convexities and optionality."

## Teleology — two arguments, not one

- **Book IV preface, "The Teleological Fallacy."** Aquinas quoting Averroes: *agens non movet nisi ex intentione finis*. The rational *flâneur*.
- **Ch. 15, "History Written by the Losers."** The losers are the non-practitioners who write the history of technology and credit it to theory (Table 5, "lecturing birds how to fly"). This is misattribution and epiphenomena, not teleology. The survivorship argument usable against teleological history is the ch. 2 footnote read together with ch. 4. It is already worked out at [[The ascent narrative is the Jensen error as historiography]] and [[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]].

## What is not in the book

- **No Islamic contract doctrine.** There is no *gharar*, *riba*, *maysir*, *ḍamān*, *kharāj* or *qirāḍ*. The Islamic material is incidental: ch. 7 (the Qur'an on those "wrongful while thinking of themselves that they are righteous"), ch. 20 (*nabi*), ch. 23 (Tarek; Almutanabbi), ch. 24 (*yad el hurr mizan*). Book VII draws on Babylonian, Roman and Catalan sources and on Smith. The bridge to fiqh is the project's own.
- ⚠️ [[Islamic doctrine refuses risk-commodification at step one]] says Taleb praises Mediterranean PLS finance. **That is not in *Antifragile*.** Locate it in *Skin in the Game* or elsewhere before citing [verify].
- **No theory of cooperation or pooling.** See [[Taleb secures the collective by exit and has no theory of pooling]].
