# Scots partnership has legal personality and English partnership does not

**A difference on the census's most theoretically loaded axis, inside one state, one commercial economy and one currency — and `organizational_forms` codes only the English side.**

## The fact

Under Scots law a partnership is **a legal person distinct from the partners who compose it.** Under English law it is not: an English partnership is a relation between persons, and the firm has no personality of its own. The distinction survives into the modern law and is why a Scottish limited partnership behaves differently in insolvency from an English one; it is treated in the Scottish Law Commission's partnership work and in the Edinburgh literature on partnership personality.

⚠️ **Read at one remove in this pass, from secondary and professional sources. Verify against the Commission's papers before citing.**

## Why it matters to the census, precisely

`deed_of_settlement_company` and `chartered_corporation_england` are coded, and the pair is built to carry an English contrast: the unincorporated form against the chartered one, differing on `LP1` **by construction** — one has legal personality by charter, the other does not.

**Scotland breaks the construction.** It supplies a form with legal personality **and no charter**, which is a cell the English pair cannot occupy. If `LP1` is doing the work the census asks of it, a Scottish partnership should separate from an English one on that characteristic and agree on most others — **a near-minimal pair, with personality varying and almost everything else held constant.**

That is the same shape as [[The talha and the Zubusse are the same mechanism with opposite liability rules]], and it is rarer: there, the mechanism was constant and the rule varied across legal families; **here the commercial world is constant and the rule varies within one state.**

## What it would test

**Whether legal personality does any work.** The literature the census sits inside treats personality as a component of entity shielding and argues about its consequences. Scotland and England ran adjacent, deeply integrated commercial economies from 1707 with opposite answers on this point. **If personality matters as much as the theory says, the difference should show somewhere; if it shows nowhere, that is a finding about the theory.**

⚠️ **And the obvious objection has to be taken seriously first.** Scots law also differs in much else — the law of obligations, of diligence, of bankruptcy — so the pair may not be minimal at all, and a difference attributed to personality may belong to the law of diligence. **That is a reason to specify the comparison before coding it, not a reason to drop it.**

## The strongest reason to want it

[[The creditor limb of entity shielding has no witness]]. The census cannot say whether members' creditors reach the firm's assets, because no coded source addresses it. **A system in which the firm is a person and a neighbouring system in which it is not must answer that question differently, and Scottish diligence against a firm is where the answer is written down.**
