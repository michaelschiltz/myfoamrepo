# The colonial enquiry as South Asia's layer 3

**Where Japan's layer 3 is the municipal history and China's is the scholar's contract compilation, South Asia's is the colonial state's enquiry into indigenous institutions** — printed, indexed, and free.

## The genre

- **The Provincial Banking Enquiry Committees, 1929–1930.** Every province reported: Madras, Bombay, Burma, the United Provinces, Punjab and the rest, each in several volumes of report, evidence and investigators' reports. **They describe indigenous bankers, chit funds, *nidhis*, village moneylenders and their instruments, with evidence taken from the practitioners.** Volumes of the Madras and United Provinces committees are scanned in full on the Internet Archive.
- **District manuals and gazetteers.** William Logan's *Malabar Manual* (1887) is the earliest description of the *kuri* reached in this pass.
- **Glossaries of legal and revenue terms.** H. H. Wilson's *Glossary of Judicial and Revenue Terms* (1855) — **the census's only South Asian item**.
- **Customary-law compilations**, recorded district by district for the courts. ⚠️ Named from general knowledge; not examined in this pass.

## Why it matters, and the warning that goes with it

**It is the same shape as [[Taiwan Shiho - the colonial survey of Chinese associations and shares]]**: an administration that needed to decide how to tax, regulate or adjudicate an institution wrote down its terms. **For the census this is an evidence base that already exists at layer 3 and costs nothing** — and the 1930 committees are close in date and purpose to the 1935 Japanese ministry survey behind the *kō* magnitudes.

**The warning is the same too, and stronger.** **An enquiry is a selection by the enquiry's purpose.** The banking committees asked whether indigenous credit could be harnessed to a modern banking system; they recorded what bore on that question. **Categories such as "indigenous banker" are the committee's, not the institutions'** — `articulation=analyst-imposed` in the census's terms, and the analyst is a colonial commission. **And the committees heard those who could be summoned**, which favours the organised and the urban.
