# Legal scaffolding gives a pool collective properties it does not yet own

**Black, Bourrat and Rainey 2020 show that ecology can impose Darwinian properties on groups before groups possess them: patchy resources and periodic dispersal give patches variation, differential reproduction and heredity, properties that would vanish at once if the scaffold were removed. Endogenising the scaffold is a further step. The concept transfers to institutions without strain. Many historical pools held collective properties — a protected fund, a continuing name, an enforceable membership — because a court, a guild, a temple, a notarial formula or a charter supplied them from outside. The corporation is then the form in which the scaffold has been internalised.**

## Why it fits the decomposition strand

The vault treats the corporation as a bundle of components — entity shielding, capital lock-in, transferable claims, legal personality, perpetual succession — each available long before their combination. Scaffolding gives the observation a dynamic: a component first appears as an external support conferred case by case, and the transition is its relocation into the unit's own constitution. That predicts why recombination rather than invention is the event to date, and it gives a reading of hinge cases such as [[The asiento de averia is the pooling-to-corporation hinge]], where a privileged company acquires by grant what a pool had borrowed from the Crown.

## What it predicts in the sources

Scaffolded properties should be conferred by an outside party in each instance (a court order, a confirmation, a privilege renewed), be revocable by that party, and lapse when its jurisdiction lapses. Internalised properties should be stated once in the unit's own instrument and survive changes in the outside party. [[Three immune functions mark a pool that has become a person]] supplies the functions to watch; the scaffolding reading says where each was held at a given date.

## Limits

Black, Bourrat and Rainey's scaffold is ecological and causes selection among patches; a legal scaffold is an instrument conferred by a party with its own interests, which may withdraw it. The transfer is an analogy of structure. Test against two or three coded cases — one pooling contract, one *waqf*, one shielded form — before it enters proposal text.

## Revision 2026-11-13 — the scaffold is generalised, not internalised

Hansmann, Kraakman and Squire 2006 (read in full, SSRN version, printed 4–6) defeat the internalisation thesis above. Entity shielding "requires law; owner shielding does not": members can contract for limited liability and for liquidation protection among themselves, but no contract among members binds their personal creditors, so the boundary against third parties exists only as a property rule imposed by the legal order. The corporation's boundary therefore never ceases to depend on an outside party. What changes is the *form* of the dependence: a privilege conferred case by case, revocable and jurisdiction-bound, becomes a standard form available to anyone who files — a general rule of property law, and a *numerus clausus* type. Black, Bourrat and Rainey's distinction survives in altered shape. The relevant transition is from particular scaffold to general scaffold, not from scaffold to self-support. The prediction in the section above stands for liquidation protection against owners, which Hansmann, Kraakman and Squire say contract can supply in degree. It does not stand for protection against creditors.
