# The word ergodic carries four incompatible senses

The vocabulary is contested in a way the applied literature rarely acknowledges, and the four senses are not nested. Controlling which one is in play at each step is a condition of the argument's survival under review.

**First, the measure-theoretic sense.** Triviality of the invariant σ-algebra of a measure-preserving system; the sense of Birkhoff and von Neumann, and the only one in which the ergodic theorems are stated. See [[Ergodicity is indecomposability not randomness]].

**Second, the applied-probability sense.** An "ergodic Markov chain" is irreducible, aperiodic and positive recurrent — a strictly *stronger* condition, guaranteeing convergence to a unique stationary law (Meyn and Tweedie 1993). A reader trained in this tradition hears "non-ergodic" as "fails to converge to a unique equilibrium distribution", which is not what we mean.

**Third, the econometric sense.** "Ergodic for the mean" — a mixing-rate condition licensing consistent estimation from a single realisation (Hamilton 1994; White 2001). This is the sense closest to what the persistence literature tacitly assumes, and therefore the sense in which our inferential critique should be pressed, because it is the one that tradition already recognises as an assumption capable of failing.

**Fourth, the Post-Keynesian sense.** Descending from Samuelson's "ergodic hypothesis" as the precondition for economics as a science, taken up polemically by Davidson to mean that history does not repeat. This sense has been criticised as mathematically loose, and the practical danger is specific: a referee predisposed against the project will assimilate our criterion to Davidson's and dismiss it as unfalsifiable rhetoric without examining it. The application should therefore mark the distinction explicitly rather than trusting it to be inferred, and should be seen to be making a claim of the *third* kind.

This is the same hygiene point as [[Route Kolmogorov to ergodic theory not the theory of means]], applied to the word rather than to the lineage. Physics did not move on by declaring ensemble averages bad; it moved on by stating its conditions. Our comparative advantage over the existing critique is that we can name which condition, over which observable, in which sense.
