# SIAS, SIUSA and SAN — the Italian access layers

**Italy has no PARES.** Where Spain's problem is that the state portal covers only the state archives — [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]] — Italy's problem is the opposite and worse: there are several national systems, they overlap, they describe rather than image, and **the images live on each archive's own website, outside all of them**.

## The four systems

| System | URL | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **SIAS** — Sistema Informativo degli Archivi di Stato | <https://sias-archivi.cultura.gov.it/> | Fondo- and series-level descriptions for the *state* archives. The workhorse: this is where a fondo's date range, extent and series structure is stated |
| **SIUSA** — Sistema Informativo Unificato per le Soprintendenze Archivistiche | <https://siusa-archivi.cultura.gov.it/> | The **non-state** archives — bank, corporate, ecclesiastical, family. Everything in Italian custody that is not an *Archivio di Stato* |
| **SAN** — Sistema Archivistico Nazionale | <https://san.beniculturali.it/> | The aggregator over both, plus thematic portals |
| **Guida generale degli Archivi di Stato** | <http://www.guidageneralearchivistato.beniculturali.it/> | The digitised print guide. Old, but often the only place a series is described at all |

## The rule that follows

**Search SIAS for a fondo, SIUSA for an institution, and the archive's own site for images.** The three answer different questions and none of them subsumes the others. A bank archive — [[Archivio Storico del Banco di Napoli]], [[Archivio Storico del Monte dei Paschi di Siena]] — is in SIUSA and not in SIAS, and a researcher who searches only the state system concludes it does not exist.

This is the same failure the Spanish set produced through devolution, arriving here through a distinction between state and non-state custody instead. In both cases **a null result is a fact about the system queried**, which is the rule [[Isqa reading list to acquire]] established for catalogues and which keeps recurring because it is structural rather than accidental.

## Layer

SIAS and SIUSA are layer 1. **There is no Italian layer 3 outside individual projects** — no national transcription programme, nothing comparable to GLOBALISE or to Amsterdam's notarial HTR. What transcription exists is project-scale and is noted per repository.

⚠️ *Portale Antenati* holds mass-digitised civil registration and is layer 2 at scale, but its content is genealogical and of no use to this project. Do not let its volume create the impression that Italian archives are broadly imaged. They are not.
