# DHARMA — an ERC Synergy project that produced a TEI corpus of inscriptions

*The Domestication of "Hindu" Asceticism and the Religious Making of South and Southeast Asia*. **ERC Synergy Grant, project 809994, 2019–2025**, four beneficiaries — CEIAS, EFEO, Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin and L'Orientale, Naples — with four principal investigators. Output: **TEI/XML corpora of inscriptions** from the Tamil country, central and eastern India, Cambodia, Campā and the Nusantara, published on **GitHub** and moving to a portal at dharmalekha.info. ⚠️ Inscription counts and licence not established.

## Why it has a note in an archive survey

**Two reasons, and the second is about this project's own grant.**

**1. It is the machine-readable layer for [[Indian inscriptions - the endowment recorded in stone]]** — the only one in South Asian epigraphy. Whether it covers the endowment inscriptions the census would want is **unestablished and cheap to check**, because the data is on GitHub.

**2. It is an ERC Synergy grant in the adjacent field, and it is finished.** Four PIs, six years, four institutions, a digital corpus as a deliverable, and a named data standard. **Anyone assessing HistorEE's plan for a "public lexicon layer" and a digitisation strand has a completed comparator here**, including for what a Synergy team can produce in six years and how it publishes it. ⚠️ Its budget and outputs were not examined.
