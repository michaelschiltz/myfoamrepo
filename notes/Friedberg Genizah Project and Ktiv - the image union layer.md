# Friedberg Genizah Project and Ktiv — the image union layer

- **Friedberg Genizah Project (FGP)**, now within the **Friedberg Jewish Manuscript Society** — <https://fjms.genizah.org/>. Founded 2008 by Albert Dov Friedberg. **Over 400,000 digital images** gathered from about sixty holding institutions, with bibliography and identification of joins.
- **Ktiv — the International Collection of Digitized Hebrew Manuscripts**, National Library of Israel, with FJMS — <https://www.nli.org.il/en/discover/manuscripts/hebrew-manuscripts>. Built on the Institute of Microfilmed Hebrew Manuscripts, founded 1950, which had filmed close to 95 per cent of known Hebrew manuscripts; integration with FJMS announced 2017.

## Why this is the most important fact in the pass

**The whole deposit is reunited as images, online, by two bodies whose purpose was to do exactly that.** Europe's union layer is description (Archives Portal Europe); the Ottoman union layer is image but on site (İSAM); **the Geniza's union layer is image and online**. It is the only corpus in the survey where the dispersal the archive suffered has been undone at layer 2 — and it was undone by **private philanthropy and a national library**, not by any holder.

**It is also what makes joins findable.** A document split between Cambridge and New York is one document only once someone has put the two images side by side. **The join, not the shelfmark, is the Geniza's unit**, and this layer is where joins are established.

## Access and rights

⚠️ **Not established in this pass**: whether FJMS requires registration, whether Ktiv images are free to download, and what reuse terms either sets. Ktiv's own page promises "open access to all Hebrew manuscripts". **Rights are per holding library underneath**, and the union layer does not change them.
