# Zhang Chuanxi 1995 — the contract compilation behind the census's Chinese rows

张传玺 ed., **《中国历代契约会编考释》** — *Annotated Compilation of Historic Chinese Contracts* — two volumes (Beijing: Peking University Press, 1995). Zelin 2019 cites it as "Zhang, *Annotated Compilation*", and calls its introduction "an excellent synopsis of the content and provenance of early contract materials".

## Why it gets its own note

**Because the census's Chinese evidence is one article reading, in part, one compilation.** Every coded Chinese cell cites **Zelin 2019**:

| Row | Zelin 2019 | Underlying source as Zelin gives it |
|---|---|---|
| `shenhui_gu`, `shenhui_gu_alloc` | 335–336 | **Zhang, *Annotated Compilation*** (Shanyin, Shanxi) |
| `hegu_yingu` | 337–350 | contracts and later compilations |
| `hegu_shengu` | 345–346 | *Qingbai leichao*; Morck and Yang on Rishengchang |
| `hegu_gangu` (parked) | 346 | one instance, Ruifeng ironworks, Chongqing |
| `gugu_death_share` (parked) | 346 | Morck and Yang, a 1940s example |

⚠️ Only pp. 325–335 of Zelin were read in this pass; the right-hand column for pp. 337–350 is from the census's `key_source` fields, not from Zelin's footnotes.

## What that does to the evidence

**1. It is a compiler's selection of a collector's world.** A compilation of "historic Chinese contracts" is chosen for range across dynasties and types, from what editors could reach in the early 1990s. **Zelin herself notes that most finds since its publication date from no earlier than the sixteenth century** — the compilation's early material is its reason for being, and the commercial sixteenth to nineteenth centuries are thinner than the corpora now known. **The census's Chinese institutions are therefore sampled through a selection built for another purpose.**

**2. The dependence rule applies.** Two census rows coded from the same compilation page, through the same article, are **one witness**. `shenhui_gu` and `shenhui_gu_alloc` already declare themselves the same institution in two censuses; **any agreement between Chinese rows traceable to Zelin 2019 is not a finding.**

**3. Pattern 4 applies.** A contract records a constitution, not a practice. **The census's Chinese share classes are coded from what the instruments say a share is**, which is the European notarial problem the survey flagged at [[The notarial security clause is boilerplate and cannot carry a typology]].
