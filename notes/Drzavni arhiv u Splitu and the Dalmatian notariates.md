# Državni arhiv u Splitu and the Dalmatian notariates

Repository code `HR-DAST`, with fonds described through [[Croatian archives online - ARHiNET and the access layer]] and visible in Archives Portal Europe.

Split, Trogir, Šibenik and Hvar are the southern half of the Venetian Dalmatian set introduced at [[Drzavni arhiv u Zadru]], and the same argument applies: **a chain of small Adriatic notariates under one sovereign, facing an independent republic across a short stretch of water.**

## The specific value of a chain of small towns

Not depth — none of these will rival Dubrovnik — but **replication**. The recurring difficulty in this project is telling a legal tradition apart from a local drafting habit, and the only fix is several independent local practices inside one legal frame. The French *amirautés* offer that at [[The amirautes - a series across the coastal departments]]; the Wendish towns offer it at [[Stadtarchiv Stralsund and the Wendish towns]]; **Dalmatia offers it with the additional advantage that the comparison republic is next door and better documented than either.**

⚠️ Wholly unverified as to series and survival.
