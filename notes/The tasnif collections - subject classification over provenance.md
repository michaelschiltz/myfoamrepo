# The *tasnif* collections — subject classification over provenance

**The structural fact about the Ottoman Archive in Istanbul.** Before the archive was arranged by the bureau that produced each document, much of it was arranged by people.

## What was done

The loose documents of the pre-Tanzimat state were sorted into classification collections named for the men who directed them:

- **Ali Emiri**, 1914–1924 — **by reign**. A code such as `AE.SMST.IV` means *Ali Emiri, Sultan Mustafa IV*.
- **İbnülemin Mahmud Kemal İnal**, 1924–1926 — **by subject, under 23 headings**.
- **Muallim Cevdet**, from 1931.
- **Cevdet** — **by subject**: `C.ML` *Maliye*, `C.AS` *Askeriye*, `C..ADL` *Adliye*, `C..İKTS` *İktisat*, and an *Evkaf* class.

Iranica records their printed catalogues at 54 volumes for Ali Emiri, 37 for Muallim Cevdet, 30 for İbnülemin. Dates and methods from Baş 2019. ⚠️ The Cevdet classification's dates and the date at which provenance became the archive's governing rule were not established in what was read.

## What it does to the evidence

**It takes a document out of the series it was written into and puts it beside documents a cataloguer thought were about the same thing.** For the census that has three consequences.

**1. There is often no series in the European sense.** The register schema at [[MOC - European archives and digital collections]] keys on the *series* because the series is the unit a frequency can have a denominator over. **A subject collection is not a denominator for anything but a cataloguer's judgement.** A count of waqf documents in the Cevdet *Evkaf* class measures what someone in the 1930s decided was about *evkaf*.

**2. The same institution's record is scattered, and the scatter is findable only by searching everything.** The worked case: Sunar 2025 found the surviving account registers of the Janissary *orta* chests in **four different collections** — four in `D.YNÇ.d`, one in `MAD.d`, two in `D.BŞM.d`, one in `D.PYM.d`. See [[Orta sandigi records - the guild and Janissary chests]]. A researcher who searched the obvious one would have found half.

**3. The rule is the one already in force, one level down.** [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]]: **never compute a frequency across a tasnif collection**, and never read a thin class as a thin record.

## The same years, the other loss

The classification campaigns ran through the years in which the Istanbul finance office sold a depot of Treasury papers to Bulgaria as waste paper, in 1931 — Baş 2019 records the scholarly protest it provoked. **Classification and destruction were contemporaneous, and the survivors of the second are now in Sofia.** See [[National Library Sofia - the Oriental Department and the 1931 sale]].
