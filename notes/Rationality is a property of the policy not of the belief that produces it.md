# Rationality is a property of the policy not of the belief that produces it

**Taleb's claim that rationality resides in action and not in belief is right, and narrower than he makes it. A belief reaches a trajectory only through the policy it induces. Where the payoff carries an absorbing barrier the criterion of a good decision is therefore defined on policies, and the accuracy of the belief is neither necessary nor sufficient. Where the payoff is additive and the loss symmetric, accurate belief and good policy coincide, and the primacy of process over correctness has no content. The primacy is a consequence of the dynamic, not an epistemology.**

## What Taleb says

- *Skin in the Game* ch. 18, "Revelation of Preferences": beliefs are not the object of scientific judgement; rationality is a property of action; action is judged by survival. Binmore's revealed preference is the warrant.
- Ch. 18, "'Tawk' and Cheap 'Tawk'": decorative beliefs against beliefs that map to action, distinguishable only by what is staked on them.
- *Antifragile* ch. 17, "The Sucker-Nonsucker Distinction" and "Fragility, Not Probability": one decides on the payoff of the True and the False, not on their probability; passengers one believes innocent are searched anyway.
- *Skin in the Game* ch. 19, "Summary": rationality is avoidance of systemic ruin.

Loci at [[Taleb 2018 on skin in the game - loci for rationality, belief and ritual]].

## The formal content

Write a decision as belief $b$, policy $\pi(b)$, trajectory $x_t(\pi)$. Any criterion evaluated on trajectories sees $b$ only through $\pi$, so two beliefs that induce the same policy are indistinguishable to it. That is the whole of the revealed-preference point, and it needs no theory of mind.

Taleb's illustrations are two failures of different kinds, and they must not be filed together.

- **Correct estimate, wrong objective.** Russian roulette for a million (ch. 19, "Ergodicity"). The ensemble expectation of $833,333 is *true*, and acting on it repeatedly is ruin. The fault lies not in the belief but in the objective it is fed into: the ensemble average, where the trajectory has a barrier ([[An absorbing barrier breaks ergodicity by construction]]).
- **Biased estimate, safe policy.** The refusal to sleep under dead trees in Papua New Guinea (ch. 18, "What Is Religion About?", after Diamond). Whatever reason is given, the policy keeps the trajectory off the barrier.

**What Taleb calls the primacy of action over belief is, stated rigorously, the primacy of the objective over the estimate.** Under a non-ergodic dynamic a correct estimate passed to the wrong objective ruins, and a biased estimate passed to an objective that respects the barrier protects. Under additive dynamics with symmetric loss, certainty equivalence holds: the optimal policy needs the unbiased estimate, and the distinction collapses. The claim is licensed exactly where the [[Time-average]] and the ensemble average part company, and nowhere else.

## Where he slides

"What is rational is that which allows for *survival*" (ch. 18, "What Does Lindy Say?") has two readings.

- **Forward, as a criterion.** A policy is rational if, under a specified dynamic, it keeps the trajectory off the barrier. That is the time-average criterion stated for a decision-maker, and the vault can use it.
- **Backward, as evidence.** A practice is rational because those who held it survived. Ch. 18 runs this reading within two pages ("The Nondecorative in the Decorative": look at the survival of the populations that hold a belief; what survives survives for a reason).

The backward reading is the inference [[Taleb reads survival as evidence about the survivor and we read it as evidence about the filter]] reverses, and it makes the definition circular: rationality is defined by survival and then read off survival. The forward reading needs the dynamic and the barrier specified in advance. The backward reading needs only the record, which is why it is attractive and why it explains nothing.

## Indexed to a layer

The criterion attaches to whichever trajectory carries the barrier. Ch. 19 ("Who Is 'You'?", "Courage and Precaution Aren't Opposites") places the binding barrier at the top layer, of kin, tribe, species and ecosystem, and redefines courage as accepting ruin at one's own layer to lower it at a higher one. An act irrational for the individual can then be rational for the collective, but only by exit, the mechanism of [[Taleb secures the collective by exit and has no theory of pooling]]. Say at which layer a policy is being called rational, or the word floats.

## Rule for the vault

Write "process over correctness" only with the dynamic named: the primacy of the policy over the belief holds where the barrier is and lapses where it is not. Never derive a practice's rationality from its persistence ([[Time-average optimization is a survivorship property not an intention]]). The institutional counterpart, where it is not the decider's belief but the judge's evidence that is set aside, is [[Formalism refuses evidence of results and of reasons because neither discriminates]].
