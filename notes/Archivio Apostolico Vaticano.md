# Archivio Apostolico Vaticano

<https://www.archivioapostolicovaticano.va/>

**The doctrinal archive.** The project already argues from canon law — [[Naviganti - Latin Christendom rejected the sea loan too]], [[The double refusal of the sea loan is evidence about the filter not about independence]] — and this is where the administrative record behind that doctrine sits.

## What it is for, in this project

Not the decretals themselves, which are printed and long since edited. The archive's value is the **Camera Apostolica** material and the licensing, dispensation and litigation record: the point where a general prohibition met a particular transaction and someone had to decide. A doctrine's refusals are visible in its printed sources; **its accommodations are visible in its paperwork**, and the accommodations are what tell you whether the prohibition bound.

That is the same evidential move the project makes for Islamic doctrine in [[Islamic doctrine refuses risk-commodification at step one]] and for the *heter iska* in [[The heter iska restores the fixed return by evidentiary barrier]] — the *hiyal* question, asked of Latin Christendom.

## Access is the constraint, and it is severe

Verified, and it changes trip planning:

- **Qualified scholars only.** A master's degree or equivalent; clergy need a licence or doctorate.
- **Admission card obtained in person** at the Acceptance Office, in narrow morning hours, with an online application, a **letter of recommendation** from an accredited institute, a certificate of academic credential and ID.
- **Consultation runs to the end of the pontificate of Pius XII (October 1958)** — no constraint for this project.
- **No personal cameras or portable scanners in the study halls, and photographing indexes is expressly forbidden.** Digital reproductions are supplied at designated workstations.
- **A maximum of three archival units per day**, requested before noon.

Three units a day, no camera. **This is not an archive one visits speculatively.** Every unit must be identified in advance from printed inventories, which makes the preparatory bibliography the real cost and puts this repository in the same category as [[Consulado de Burgos - the universidad de mercaderes]] — the work before the trip is larger than the trip.

⚠️ Establish whether the *Indice dei Fondi* PDF the archive publishes is sufficient to build a request list remotely. If it is, this is a desk task; if not, it is two visits.
