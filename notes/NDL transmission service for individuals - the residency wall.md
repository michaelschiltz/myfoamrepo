# NDL transmission service for individuals — the residency wall

国立国会図書館 個人向けデジタル化資料送信サービス. <https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/use/digital_transmission/individuals_index.html>

**Out-of-print and hard-to-obtain material from NDL Digital Collections, delivered to a personal screen — for "an official registered user of the NDL, who resides in Japan".** Non-residents and simple registered users are excluded. Printing up to 100 frames at a time.

## Why it gets its own note

**Because it is the Japanese equivalent of the Ottoman Archive's e-Devlet route, and it splits the team the same way.** The material behind it is precisely what the census reads Japanese institutions through: out-of-commerce scholarship, municipal and prefectural histories and their source volumes, journal runs. ⚠️ That municipal histories are substantially inside this tier is expected from their out-of-commerce status and not checked title by title.

**For HistorEE that means:**

- **The PI in Hokkaido has it.** A postdoc at LML or Stanford does not, unless resident in Japan.
- **Reading for the Japanese rows cannot be delegated abroad** without library purchase, interlibrary loan or travel.
- **This is a staffing fact, not an archival one**, and it belongs in Decision_log A1–A2 (where the Japanese posts sit) as much as in A7 (travel).
