# Osaka Museum of History — the Kōnoike family documents

大阪歴史博物館, Osaka. <https://www.osakamushis.jp/>

**The papers and household goods of the Kōnoike Zen'emon house**, Osaka's leading money-exchange house, based at Imabashi, originally sake brewers of Itami. **Donated to Osaka City in 1996–1997: more than 590 lots and 3,300 items**, now at the museum. Content: **money-exchange business records, lending to daimyō (*daimyōgashi*), reclamation of new fields (*shinden*)**, and the goods of the Imabashi residence.

## The survival rule includes the donor

**The fourteenth Kōnoike Zen'emon decided on the gift after the 1995 Hanshin–Awaji earthquake.** A private house archive entered public custody because a disaster made a family reconsider where its papers were safe. See [[Shiryo Net and the disaster survival rule]]. **What reached the city is what the family chose to give**, and the collection's weight on household goods — the museum's 2015–18 joint research project was a survey and conservation of its textiles — says something about the choice. ⚠️ The balance between business records and goods not measured.

## What it holds for HistorEE

**The Osaka side of the money market, and daimyō lending.** With the Mitsui ledgers — [[Mitsui Bunko - the Mitsui house archive]] — and the Bank of Japan's *ryōgae* documents — [[Bank of Japan Currency Museum - the Senpeikan documents]] — **this is the third leg of any real clearing dataset.** ⚠️ Access, cataloguing and imaging of the business records not established.
