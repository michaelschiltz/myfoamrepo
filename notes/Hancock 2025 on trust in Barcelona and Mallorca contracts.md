# Hancock 2025 on trust in Barcelona and Mallorca contracts

Source note for Annabel Laura Hancock, *Trust in Trade in Barcelona and Mallorca, 1240–1350*, DPhil thesis, University of Oxford, 2025 (Zotero NYIFPB3T, ORA, text layer good; printed page = PDF page − 17).

## The corpus

1,432 contracts in a Symphytum database, from 86 registers (23 Arxiu Capitular de Mallorca, 36 Arxiu del Regne de Mallorca, 23 AHPB, 4 Arxiu Capitular de Barcelona), plus **136 contracts transcribed by Madurell i Marimon** (16). Three main document types: the comanda, the ship loan or charter, the *societas* (16–18).

**Not independent of the anchor edition.** The 136 Madurell transcriptions are part of the set, so Hancock and Madurell & García Sanz 1973 are one witness on what they share.

## Findings relied on

- **Three ways of holding comanda capital** (17): *a esparç*, managed separately from the traveller's own; *simil cum meis mercibus*, used together with his goods as a common fund; and a community of all investments divided into shares. She cites Madurell & García Sanz, I, 94–96.
- **The risk clause.** *Ad redegum / risicum (et fortunam)* appears in 352 agreements (24.6 %), stable 1243–1349; most state that the investing party alone accepted the risk (191). One 1348 Mallorcan act calls the allocation customary, "ad risicum videlicet et fortunam mei, ut moris est" (192). In partnership acts the risk is shared "per solidum atque libram" (191–192).
- **Profit split** commonly 3:1 in comanda investments, *ad quartum lucri*, the *quart diner* (192–193).
- **Security.** A general pledge of all goods appears in 788 agreements (54.9 % of the set), *obligare* in 835 (58 %); specific collateral in only 43, named guarantors in 34 (2.4 %) (91–101). **The thesis does not break the general pledge down by contract type**, so its frequency in comanda acts alone is not given.
- **Oath and belief.** Oaths are rarer in comanda acts than in partnerships; one comanda states that the investing partner was to be believed (ch. 5).
