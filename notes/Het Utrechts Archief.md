# Het Utrechts Archief

<https://hetutrechtsarchief.nl/>

## What is online

**The notarial archives are largely online**, with scans, and the archive maintains an index of notarial deeds. Utrecht is therefore a third Dutch notarial series at layer 2 with indexing, after Amsterdam at layer 3 and Haarlem — see [[Noord-Hollands Archief - Haarlem]].

⚠️ Whether the Utrecht material is word-searchable or only indexed by party and date is unestablished, and it is the difference between a corpus and a finding aid.

## What Utrecht holds that the coastal archives do not

**Ecclesiastical and hospital foundations.** Utrecht was the ecclesiastical capital of the northern Netherlands, and its *gasthuizen*, chapters and religious houses generated exactly the property-holding-institution record that the entity census is thinnest on. The Dutch counterpart to the beguinage question at [[Begijnhof archives - where the beguinages records are]], and less dispersed, because the Republic's confiscations concentrated rather than scattered.

**An inland, non-maritime commercial record.** Almost everything this project reads from the Dutch material is maritime. Utrecht is the control: a Dutch urban economy with annuities, guarantees and institutional credit and **no ships at all**. Where a form appears in Amsterdam and Utrecht alike it is not a maritime adaptation; where it appears only in Amsterdam it may be.

That is a real diagnostic and it is cheap, which is why the note exists.
