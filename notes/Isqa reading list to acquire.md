## Priority — the live controversy

- **Ackerman-Lieberman, Phillip I.** *The Business of Identity: Jews, Muslims, and Economic Life in Medieval Egypt.* Stanford UP, 2014. ISBN 9780804785471; DOI `10.11126/stanford/9780804785471.001.0001`. Argues, against Goitein and Udovitch, that Geniza Jews transacted in distinctively **Talmudic** rather than Islamic partnership forms. This is the evidentiary crux for [[The isqa refuses the agent's shield]] and the terminological caution in [[Isqa qirad and commenda are a liability-variable triad]].
- **Ackerman-Lieberman.** 'Contractual Partnerships in the Geniza and the Relationship between Islamic Law and Practice.' *JESHO* 54 (2011). **DOI unverified** — resolve before citing; the article version of the above argument.

## Ribbit and the isqa

- **Gamoran, Hillel.** *Jewish Law in Transition: How Economic Forces Overcame the Prohibition against Lending on Interest.* HUC Press, 2008. ISBN 9780878204625 (HUC); a Pittsburgh imprint circulates as 9780822963721; JSTOR `j.ctt16rpqsn`. **Verify which imprint you want.** Strong thesis — economic pressure overcoming doctrine — that [[The heter iska restores the fixed return by evidentiary barrier]] flags as contestable rather than assumable.
- **Elon, Menachem.** *Jewish Law: History, Sources, Principles.* JPS, 1994 — on legal fictions and the *heter ʿisqa*.
- **Soloveitchik, Haym.** 'Pawnbroking: A Study in Ribbit and of the Halakhah in Exile.' *PAAJR*, c. 1970–71. **Volume and year unverified.**
- **Stein, Siegfried.** On the development of Jewish interest law, *Historia Judaica* 17 (1955). **Citation unverified — I am working from memory here; confirm or drop.**
- **Bleich, J. David.** 'Hetter Iska', in *Contemporary Halakhic Problems*. Halakhic rather than historical, but sets out the modern mechanism cleanly.
- **Resnicoff, Steven H.** On the *heter ʿisqa* in American courts — a Cardozo faculty-articles piece exists; useful for the partnership-versus-disguised-loan question, which is the modern replay of the evidentiary-barrier argument.

## Comparative and theoretical

- **Goitein, S. D.** *A Mediterranean Society*, vol. 1.
- **Goldberg, Jessica.** *Trade and Institutions in the Medieval Mediterranean.* CUP, 2012.
- **Çizakça, Murat.** *A Comparative Evolution of Business Partnerships.* Brill, 1996.
