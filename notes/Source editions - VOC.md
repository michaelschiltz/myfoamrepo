# Source editions — VOC

The printed source series for the Dutch East India Company, and their online versions. **The digital layer is already recorded** at [[VOC digital corpus - what is online and on what terms]] (GLOBALISE, Boekhouder-Generaal, Generale Missiven, TANAP, Sejarah Nusantara) and [[Van Dam's Beschryvinge - the VOC described by its own advocaat]].

## The constitution and the capital

- **J. G. van Dillen (ed.), *Het oudste aandeelhoudersregister van de Kamer Amsterdam der Oost-Indische Compagnie*** ('s-Gravenhage: Nijhoff, 1958; NEHA Werken 14), 294 pp. **The 1602 subscription register of the Amsterdam chamber** — who subscribed, how much, the instalments. **The key printed source for the VOC's first capital.** No free copy found (in copyright). A study of the Enkhuizen register exists (Willemsen) ⚠️ citation unverified.
- **The 1602 octrooi**: printed in **J. A. van der Chijs, *Geschiedenis der stichting van de Vereenigde O.I. Compagnie*** (Leiden: Engels, 1856; digitised copy dated 1857) — free on archive.org; octrooi at pp. 118–135 of the 1856 printing per Schalk, Gelderblom & Jonker 2012 ⚠️ pagination may differ in 1857. English translation by P. Reynders ⚠️ details from memory.
- ***Resolutiën der Staten-Generaal*** (RGP): oude reeks 1576–1609 (14 vols), nieuwe reeks 1610–1670 (7 vols) — **and Goetgevonden (REPUBLIC project, Huygens): scans and transcriptions of all States-General resolutions 1576–1796, entity-tagged.** The route to the charter's passage and renewals (1622/23, 1647 and later).
- ***De opkomst van het Nederlandsch gezag in Oost-Indië*** (de Jonge, van Deventer, Heeres; 's-Gravenhage: Nijhoff, 1862–95; 18 vols + index) — vol. 1 covers **1595–1610, the *voorcompagnieën* and the 1602 merger**. Free on HathiTrust.
- **Linschoten-Vereeniging editions of the first voyages** (Houtman; van Neck and Warwijck, Keuning ed. 1938–51) — contracts, accounts and instructions of the Compagnie van Verre. ⚠️ Series numbers conflict between sources.

## Asian administration

- ***Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek, 1602–1811***, ed. van der Chijs (Batavia/'s-Gravenhage, 1885–1900), 17 vols — full texts of edicts, including the repeated rules on **servants' private trade**. **All volumes free on HathiTrust**; also archive.org, Leiden Digital Collections.
- ***Corpus Diplomaticum Neerlando-Indicum***, ed. Heeres & Stapel, 6 vols (KITLV, 1907–55) — **1,193 treaties and contracts, 1595–1799**, searchable with scans at ANRI's Corpus Diplomaticum database.
- ***Dagh-Register gehouden int Casteel Batavia*** (1887–1931), 31 vols, 1624–1682 with gaps — free on HathiTrust.
- **Colenbrander (ed.), *Jan Pietersz. Coen: bescheiden omtrent zijn bedrijf in Indië***, 7 vols (1919–53) — Coen and the Heren XVII on private trade and the use of capital. Free on HathiTrust.
- ***Generale Missiven*** **RGP Grote Serie numbers**, for citation: deel 1 GS 104 · 2 GS 112 · 3 GS 125 · 4 GS 134 · 5 GS 150 · 6 GS 159 · 7 GS 164 · 8 GS 193 · 9 GS 205 · 10 GS 250 · 11 GS 232 · 12 GS 257 · 13 GS 258 (per Huygens retroboeken).

## The gap

**No printed or digital edition of the resolutions of the Heren XVII was found.** Apart from Van Dam's digest, the directors' own decisions exist only in the Nationaal Archief (1.04.02). **For a project coding the VOC's corporate governance, that is the single most important unedited series in the survey.**
