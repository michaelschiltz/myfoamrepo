# Qingshui River documents — forest contracts in shares

清水江文书, the lower Qingshui River basin, southeastern Guizhou — a region of Miao and Dong communities and of commercial fir plantations floated downstream.

**About 300,000 documents held in private hands**, by scholars' estimate, **mainly forest contracts from the Qing onward** — sales of hillsides, planting tenancies and the division of timber proceeds — with genealogies, litigation drafts, hillside registers, **account books**, notices, letters and copied inscriptions. **Sun Yat-sen University's Center for Historical Anthropology** has collected and published them since 2001, **keeping the holders' own grouping and order** and re-sequencing only by date. Local county collections (Jinping) also hold and publish them. ⚠️ Custodians and database access not established.

## Why it has a note

**Because the forest contract is a share contract.** Planting and harvest proceeds were divided between landholders and planters in fractional shares, and shares in a hillside were bought and sold. **That is the `hegu` question outside commerce, in a non-Han frontier, recorded by the holders themselves** — Zelin's forestry examples are Huizhou; this is a second region with a far larger corpus. ⚠️ The share mechanics are stated from the corpus's description and general knowledge, not from documents read.

**And its custody is the best in China for this project's rule on context**: the documents are published in the holders' order. **The household box survives as a unit.**
