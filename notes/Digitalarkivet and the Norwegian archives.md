# Digitalarkivet and the Norwegian archives

<https://www.digitalarkivet.no/>

Norway's national digital archive, free and near-total for the major series, run by Arkivverket.

## What Norway holds for this project

Less than Denmark or Sweden, and the reason is constitutional: **Norway was in union with Denmark until 1814**, so the state record of the Norwegian trade for most of the period this project cares about is in Copenhagen — see [[Rigsarkivet Denmark - Daisy and Arkivalieronline]].

What is distinctively Norwegian is **maritime and local**: shipping registers, customs, the coastal trade, and the regional *statsarkiv* holdings.

**And Bergen** — see [[Bergen and the Hanseatic Kontor]], which is the one Norwegian case of first-rank interest.
