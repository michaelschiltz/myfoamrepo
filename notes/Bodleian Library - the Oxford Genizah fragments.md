# Bodleian Library — the Oxford Genizah fragments

<https://genizah.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/> · shelfmarks `MS. Heb. a–f`, bound into volumes.

**Acquired before and just after Schechter**: through dealers and travellers, and from Count d'Hulst's excavation at the synagogue, which sent sixteen sacks to Oxford by May 1898. Half-owner, with Cambridge, of the Lewis-Gibson Collection.

## Size — two counts, one probably a unit problem

- **"Some 4,000 fragments"** (Medieval Digital Resources, describing the online catalogue).
- **"25,000 folios"** (the Wikipedia collection table).

**Fragments bound into volumes and counted by folio will give the larger figure.** ⚠️ Not resolved.

## Layer

- **Layer 1:** an online catalogue built on Neubauer and Cowley's 1906 catalogue and Cowley's 1926 supplement — **a catalogue a century old**, carried online.
- **Layer 2:** partial. "Some entries are digitised; most have only catalogue descriptions" (MDR). ⚠️ Current extent in Digital Bodleian not checked, and the MDR description may predate later imaging. Reuse on credit, no fee.
- **Layer 3:** by machine through Ktiv, for whatever is imaged there.

## What it holds for the census

**`Bodl. MS. Heb.` shelfmarks carry two items this census already touches.** Ackerman-Lieberman cites `Heb. c 28.32` and `Heb. e 98.63` among long-term partnerships; Khalilieh 2006, held in the library, works a Geniza responsum on the Islamic law of the sea from a Bodleian fragment — a Geniza witness the maritime rows have not yet used.
