# National Palace Museum Taipei — the Qing palace memorials

國立故宮博物院. 清代檔案檢索系統: <https://qingarchives.npm.edu.tw/>

**More than 150,000 palace memorials (宮中檔奏摺) and more than 190,000 Grand Council files (軍機處檔摺件)**, with other Qing and historiographical archives, **taken to Taiwan with the Palace Museum's collections** at the end of the civil war.

## Layer and access

- **Catalogue index free; the full-text image database historically paid** (sinology resources guide). ⚠️ Current terms not captured; the retrieval system was relaunched in recent years and could not be fetched in this pass.
- The palace-memorial catalogue is also published as open government data on data.gov.tw. ⚠️ Licence not captured.
- Cross-searchable with the IHP's Grand Secretariat archive.

## What it holds for the census

**The palace memorials are where provincial officials reported grain prices, granary stocks, disasters and relief** — the material behind [[Qing grain price database - a denominator from the memorials]] and the Qing granary literature. **A census row on state granaries would be coded from this apparatus and its Beijing counterpart.** ⚠️ No row exists; see logbook 2, 2026-09-11 (vii).
