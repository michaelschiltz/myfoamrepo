# Sri Lanka National Archives — the Dutch records and the thombos

Colombo, with a branch at Kandy. **310 metres of VOC records made on Ceylon, 1640–1796**, much of it different from what survives at The Hague or Jakarta; Portuguese material from the sixteenth century; the British record from 1796 to 1948.

**The *thombos*** — the Dutch administration's land and head registers — are the best-known series, **and their index is searchable online** at the archives' own site, by province, *korale*, *pattu*, village and *ge* name; **translated extracts can be ordered for a fee.** ⚠️ Whether images are online was not established; on a researcher's account, digitisation at Colombo is otherwise limited to a newspaper-photograph collection.

**Conservation and access work was done with the Nationaal Archief and the Netherlands embassy**, with 40 per cent of the cost from the Sri Lankan government — the same programme as at Chennai. **Access**: registration and a reader card; **the Kandy branch needs the director's permission before travelling.**

## For the census

**Marginal, and interesting for one thing.** The *thombo* is a register of persons, land and obligation covering a whole population — **a denominator**, of the kind the survey has found only three times. **If a South Asian pooling institution is ever coded from Ceylon, its exposure base is here.** ⚠️ No use of the thombos for that purpose was found; this is a possibility, not a finding. Ceylon is also one of the three Chettiar theatres — see [[Chettiar banking - the enquiry is public and the account books are not]].
