# Fitness decoupling may be a short-horizon artefact

**Hypothesis, to be put to Peters and Connaughton before use. Bourrat et al. 2022 show that during a transition in individuality the fitness of cells and of collectives cannot diverge when both are measured over the same timescale and events: both grow at the rate of the same dominant eigenvalue, and apparent "decoupling" comes from comparing short-window cell performance with long-window collective performance. An asymptotic growth rate is a time-average quantity. Read through ergodicity, the result says that levels diverge only on short horizons and coincide on the long one. If so, the asymmetry of *Meditations* 6.54 is an asymmetry between horizons.**

## The two results

- † Bourrat, Doulcier, Rose, Rainey and Hammerschmidt 2022, "Tradeoff Breaking as a Model of Evolutionary Transitions in Individuality and Limits of the Fitness-Decoupling Metaphor," *eLife* 11: e73715, doi:10.7554/eLife.73715. Commensurate whole-life-cycle particle fitness equals collective fitness asymptotically (10–11); decoupling observations reflect incommensurate windows (6–7, 11); alternative description by *trade-off breaking* (14–23). See also Bourrat 2015, *Philosophy & Theory in Biology* 7.
- † Black, Bourrat and Rainey 2020, *Nature Ecology & Evolution* 4: 426–36 (read in the 2019 bioRxiv version). With slow dispersal, slower-growing cells lose within each patch but win over the patch cycle (8): the ensemble-versus-time divergence between cells and patches.

## The reading

On the time-average horizon, what does not profit the swarm does not profit the bee. On shorter horizons the bee can profit at the swarm's expense, and that is exactly the condition under which a colony needs policing and an immune boundary ([[Social immunity is a consequence of individuation not evidence for it]]). The Stoic sentence states a necessary condition, not an identity; the horizon reading says why the condition is one-directional.

## The discipline it owes

[[The growth-rate criterion is asymptotic and the horizon comparison cuts both ways]] binds the vault to naming the horizon and the relaxation time whenever an asymptotic criterion is invoked. The same holds here: the claim is that levels coincide in the limit, and the historical question is whether an institution's horizon reaches the limit. It connects to WP3's spatial-versus-temporal ergodicity distinction and to [[Pooling approaches the infinite ensemble and perpetual succession the infinite horizon]], where perpetual succession is the device that lets a trajectory reach the long horizon at all.
