# The mutual pole entered the library through the wrong census

Sibling of [[A scheme for extending the entity census]], written four rounds later against the same
dataset at 643 rows and 28 forms. The operator brief that carries the holdings, the offsets and the
acquisition list is staged at `HistorEE_codebooks/proposed-of/NOTES-mutual-pole-2026-09-04.md`.

## The claim

`FP1=mutual-provision` has had no instance in `organizational_forms` since the column existed. The
obvious reading is that mutual institutions are rare, or late, or not entities. **The reading I want
to argue is that the emptiness is an artefact of recruitment.**

Every form in the entity census arrived because somebody asked an entity question about it: the waqf,
the commenda family, the maone, the VOC, the *fraterna*. Every mutual institution in the project
arrived because somebody asked a loss question about it: the guild box, the confraternity fund, the
friendly society, the *avarız* endowment, forty-seven of them. The two intakes ran on different
questions and never crossed. So when the entity census is asked what purposes its forms declare, it
answers with the purposes of the forms that were recruited for their asset-partitioning — and returns
no cooperative, because no cooperative was ever asked.

That is not a finding about institutions. It is a finding about a bibliography, and it is the second
time this project has caught one: on 2026-08-29 the *partenrederij*/*begijnhof* batch established that
**coverage is a property of the bibliography, not the institutions** — one form filled WP1 and was
empty on WP2, the other the reverse, on the same census. This is the same defect one level up. There
the accident was which questions a source happened to answer; here it is which census a source was
read into.

## Why this matters for the 2026-08-30 failure

The credit-cooperative batch could not run because the library held no Raiffeisen source. The
conclusion drawn at the time was that the mutual pole needed acquisition. **That conclusion was wrong,
and it was wrong in an interesting way**: the library held four Turkish studies of the *mahalle avarız
vakfı* off court registers and account books, a volume on the medieval Italian confraternity that
discusses *universitas* and legal personality directly, and a run of friendly-society articles — all
of it catalogued, tagged and read, and none of it visible from inside the entity census, because it
had been filed under mutual aid.

The search failed on the word *Genossenschaft*. It would have failed on *cooperative* too. Institution
names are how a library is indexed and not how a census asks its questions, and a census that looks
for its gaps by name will keep finding that the material does not exist.

## The corollary about blinding, which reverses an earlier claim

The 2026-08-29 scheme observed that **blindness and cheapness are inversely ordered**: the forms the
vault has argued at length are the ones the library can already support, and the forms available for a
blind test are the ones nothing has been acquired for. On that basis the well-argued forms were coded
openly and the blind bundle was reserved for the acquisition tier.

The mutual pole breaks the ordering, and the mechanism above says why it must. The vault's entity notes
were written against the entity intake; the mutual material came in through the other door; so the
vault has argued almost none of it — *friendly societ\**, *scuola*, *collegium*, *guild box*,
*Genossenschaft*, *Raiffeisen*, *mont-de-piété* all return **zero files** — while the library supports
it well enough to code without a single acquisition on two of the three rows.

**Wherever two intakes have run on different questions, the second census's forms are cheap and blind
at once.** That is a general prescription, not a fact about mutuals, and it predicts where to look
next: any form that one census has read and the other has not.

## What would falsify it

If the three rows come back agreeing with their loss-census siblings on the characteristics the two
datasets share at component level, the two intakes were not independent after all and the material was
already inside the entity census in substance. And if `avariz_vakfi` separates from `waqf_khayri` on
`FP1` and on nothing else, then purpose and architecture are not the independent axes this note
assumes — the entity census would have been right to recruit on architecture alone, and the empty
`FP1` cell would be telling us something true.
