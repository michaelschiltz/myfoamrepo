# The Dubrovnik quarantine of 1377

⚠️ **A candidate form, not a repository note, and nothing has been read.**

In **1377** the Ragusan authorities instituted what is generally described as the first formal quarantine: arrivals from plague-affected places were held in isolation before admission, later in purpose-built *lazaretti*. The decision would be in the councils' records at [[Drzavni arhiv u Dubrovniku]] — plausibly in the *Acta Consilii Rogatorum* or the *Consilium Maius* ⚠️.

## Why this is not a digression

**Because it is a hazard institution, and the census's vocabulary is built on the hazard/risk distinction.**

The repo's own terminology rule holds that a **hazard** is a peril conceived as an event that may befall — no distribution over outcomes, not priceable, met by avoidance, sharing or propitiation — while a **risk** is a peril conceived as a quantified distribution and therefore transferable. **Plague in 1377 is the paradigm hazard**, and quarantine is the paradigm response: pure avoidance, collectively imposed, with no fund, no contribution and no payout.

That is a **limiting case for the loss census**, and limiting cases are where a characteristic's boundary gets tested:

- `MC1` — is avoidance a mitigation mechanism at all, or outside the three values? The logbook already records that the three values are not exclusive and do not partition.
- `PY0=collective-good` — the definition names convoy escort and city walls. **Quarantine is the same kind of thing and has no fund at all**, which bears on the observation that `PY0`'s real gate is the existence of a fund and no characteristic asks.
- `MB3=compulsory` — a third compulsory case, beside `averia_pool` and the Venetian *muda* parked on 2026-09-10.

**Three compulsory collective-good institutions with no contributory fund is no longer a curiosity; it is a cell the census may be unable to occupy.** ⚠️ Whether that is a gap or a correct exclusion is a question for a selection brief.
