# Stadsarchief Amsterdam — the notarial archive

<https://archief.amsterdam/> · transcribed corpus: <https://amsterdam-city-archives.transkribus.eu/>

**The most valuable single European repository for this project after the GLOBALISE corpus, and the only notarial archive in Europe that is at layer 3.**

## What is transcribed

Hundreds of thousands of pages of seventeenth- and eighteenth-century notarial deeds, machine-transcribed by HTR and **free-text searchable with adjustable fuzzy matching**, filterable by collection, period and notary. The *Alle Amsterdamse Akten* / *Crowd Leert Computer Lezen* programme (2018–2023) built the models from crowd transcription.

**What that means in practice:** a question like *which perils were named in bottomry contracts* is a query here and a research programme everywhere else in Europe.

## What is in it for the coded forms

**`bodemerij`.** The Amsterdam notarial practice is where the form lives, and the census row `bodemerij_amsterdam` is coded from Winkelman's RGP edition of 46 acts. **The edition is a printed selection from this archive.** Going back to the archive turns a sample of 46 into a population — and it is the rare case where the census could state its own denominator.

**`partenrederij`.** Ship part-ownership contracts, though the Zaan country is the other centre: [[Gemeentearchief Zaanstad - the partenrederij country]].

**Ship declarations.** The *scheepsverklaringen* that the average adjudications cite — see [[Averijgrossen - the general average adjudications]] — are notarial acts in this archive, and they carry the voyage narrative the court rulings compress.

## The other Amsterdam series, each with its own note

- [[Kamer van Assurantie en Averij - Amsterdam]] — the insurance court from 1598
- [[Averijgrossen - the general average adjudications]] — the indexed rulings, 1700–1810
- [[Desolate Boedelkamer - Amsterdam]] — the bankruptcy chamber

⚠️ Toegang numbers for all three, and for the notarial archive itself, need transcribing from the archive's own inventory pages rather than taken from secondary citation.

## The caution that comes with a searchable corpus

A fuzzy full-text search over HTR output has an **unmeasured character error rate**, and a query that returns nothing is a statement about the model's reading of a hand, not about the notariate. That is [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] with a machine in the filter, and it is easier to forget here than anywhere else precisely because the interface answers instantly.
