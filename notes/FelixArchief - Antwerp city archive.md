# FelixArchief — Antwerp city archive

<https://felixarchief.antwerpen.be/>

**The most important municipal archive in the European set after Amsterdam's**, and for one specific question the most important full stop. The city archive of the sixteenth century's commercial capital.

## What is here

**[[Insolvente Boedelskamer - Antwerp]]** — its own note, and the reason to come.

**`Schepenregisters`** — the aldermen's registers, the Antwerp equivalent of a notarial series: conveyances, obligations, partnership agreements, and the *certificatieboeken*. The archive publishes *regesten* — calendared abstracts — as a separate finding aid. ⚠️ Extent, date range and the relation between registers and regesten need establishing; the regesten are a nineteenth- and twentieth-century selection and are a filter, not the series.

**The gild and natie records** — see [[Natie archives - where the Antwerp nations records are]].

## Why Antwerp is the test the census actually needs

The census codes `compagnie_antwerpen_1582` and a second Antwerp phase row, and the vault has already recorded the problem with them: **both rows and `partenrederij` rest on one scholar's reading of Low Countries commercial law**, and may not be treated as independent witnesses to anything they agree on.

**That is a repairable defect, and this is the archive that repairs it** — not by finding a second scholar who has read the same literature, but by reading the instruments. [[Independence of apparatus is not independence of question]] is the statement of why the distinction matters; Antwerp is where it becomes actionable, because the firm archives survive whole.

⚠️ Whether the *schepenregisters* are imaged, and on what terms, is unestablished.
