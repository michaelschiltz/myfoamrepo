# Source editions — late-imperial China

Printed compilations on shareholding, deity and lineage associations, *huiguan* and native banking **not already recorded** at [[Zhang Chuanxi 1995 - the contract compilation behind the census's Chinese rows]], [[Huizhou documents - the largest private contract corpus]] and [[Guild stele inscriptions - the huiguan's surviving record]].

## A correction to a held item

- **张传玺 (ed.), 《中国历代契约粹编》** (Beijing: 北京大学出版社, 2014), 3 vols, 2,148 pp., **more than 2,500 contracts** from antiquity to Land Reform — **the enlarged successor to the 1995 《会编考释》** on which `shenhui_gu` rests. Cite both, or this one.

## Contract corpora

- **中国社会科学院历史研究所 (ed.; 王钰欣、周绍泉 chief eds), 《徽州千年契约文书》** (石家庄: 花山文艺出版社, 1991–93), **40 vols** (宋元明编 20, 清民国编 20) — facsimiles, c. 20,000 pp.; leases, land deeds, **commercial and partnership documents**, pawn, tax.
- **田涛, 宋格文 (Hugh T. Scogin Jr.), 郑秦 (eds), 《田藏契约文书粹编》** (Beijing: 中华书局, 2001), 3 vols — plates with transcriptions, nationwide; **the Tian Tao collection Zelin cites**.
- **曹树基 et al. (eds), 《石仓契约》** (杭州: 浙江大学出版社, 2011–15), 4 辑 — c. 8,000 village contracts from Songyang, Zhejiang, Wanli to the Republic; **the project Zelin describes as "several thousands" of contract boxes**. ⚠️ Whether *hui* or 神会 shares appear not checked.
- **陈支平 (ed.), 《福建民间文书》** (桂林: 广西师范大学出版社, 2007), 6 vols, c. 3,000 documents; and **福建师范大学历史系 (ed.), 《明清福建经济契约文书选辑》** (Beijing: 人民出版社, 1997) — **Fujian, where 神明会 and *hehui* are best documented.**
- **四川省档案馆・四川大学历史系 (eds), 《清代乾嘉道巴县档案选编》**, 2 vols (成都: 四川大学出版社, 1989, 1996) — Ba county litigation over partnerships, guilds and brokers; see [[Ba County archive - Sichuan Provincial Archives]].
- **临时台湾旧惯调查会, 『臺灣私法』 and 『臺灣私法附錄參考書』** (1909–11; 3 vols + 3 vols in 9 parts) — **thousands of Qing Taiwan contracts, including *hegu*, 神明會 and 祭祀公業**; Chinese translations in 台湾文献丛刊 (1961–63). **Probably the richest single source for `shenhui_gu` and `hegu`.** The **THDL** at National Taiwan University (free) includes an old-deeds corpus. See [[Taiwan Shiho - the colonial survey of Chinese associations and shares]].

## Huiguan, guilds and banks

- **《徽商会馆公所征信录汇编》** (Beijing: 人民出版社, 2 vols) — **the published annual accounts (征信录) of Huizhou *huiguan* and *gongsuo*: income, donations, property, charity.** ⚠️ Editors and year unconfirmed (probably 李琳琦、梁仁志, c. 2016–18). **This is the record type the census said it could not find for `huiguan`** — see the note below.
- **王日根、薛鹏志 (eds), 《中国会馆志资料集成》 第一辑** (厦门大学出版社, 2013), 10 vols — reprinted *huiguan* gazetteers with **rules, member lists and accounts**, including Singapore and Malaysia.
- **彭泽益 (ed.), 《中国工商行会史料集》** (Beijing: 中华书局, 1995), 2 vols — the standard reader of guild rules and *huiguan* records.
- **江苏省博物馆 (ed.), 《江苏省明清以来碑刻资料选集》** (三联书店, 1959) — 370 inscriptions, the precursor of the 1981 Suzhou volume, with texts not in it.
- **仁井田陞 (ed.), 『北京工商ギルド資料集』**, 6 vols (東京大学東洋文化研究所附属東洋学文献センター, 1975–83) — **Niida's 1940s field transcriptions of Beijing guild and *huiguan* inscriptions, rules and interviews**; fuller than Li Hua 1980 (confirmed: 北京: 文物出版社, 1980).
- **张海鹏、王廷元 (eds), 《明清徽商资料选编》** (合肥: 黄山书社, 1985) — extracts from c. 230 sources, nearly 100 of them genealogies.
- **中国人民银行上海市分行 (ed.), 《上海钱庄史料》** (上海人民出版社, 1960) — native banks and clearing.
- **東亜同文会, 『支那経済全書』**, 12 輯 (1907–08) — contemporary field reports on guilds, *hegu*, *qianzhuang* and **piaohao**.
- **仁井田陞, 『中国法制史研究』**, 4 vols (東京大学出版会, 1959–64) — analysis quoting many documents in full, especially vols 2–3.
- ⚠️ From a survey article only: 刘伯山 (ed.), 《徽州文书》, 5 辑 (2005–15); 王万盈, 《清代宁波契约文书辑校》 (2008); 张介人, 《清代浙东契约文书辑选》 (2011).

## What bears on an existing row

**`huiguan` is uncoded because "no source here shows" a fund**, and the Chinese pass attributed part of that to the record type — steles name donors and give no terms. **The *zhengxinlu*, the association's printed annual accounts, is the missing record type**, and the Huizhou *huiguan* ones are collected in print. Recorded in logbook 5, 2026-09-24; nothing coded.
