# Shanxi *piaohao* records — letters, house rules and a collector's compilation

**The remittance banks of Shanxi** — Rishengchang, Datong and others — whose records survive in four kinds: **account books** (monthly settlements, annual summaries by branch), **letters and letter-copies** (copied, registered and filed by clerks), **house rules (号规)** — the Datong house revised its rules six times between 1884 and 1921, the first version in 29 articles — and **partnership contracts** fixing capital and roles.

## Where they are

- **Pingyao**: the China Piaohao Museum holds bound letter drafts.
- **Tokyo**: the **University of Tokyo's Institute for Advanced Studies on Asia** (formerly the Institute of Oriental Culture) holds **piaohao letters of the Daoguang and Xianfeng reigns**. ⚠️ Extent not established.
- **In print**: 《山西票号史料》 (1990; enlarged 2002), and **《晋商史料集成》, edited by the collector Liu Jianmin (2018)**.

⚠️ Provincial archive holdings not established.

## What it bears on

- **`piaohao`** — an uncoded umbrella in `organizational_forms`. **The house rules are the rulebooks the kō batch found indispensable**: a *hao gui* is to a piaohao what a *shihōsho* is to a *kō*. **Six dated revisions of one house's rules are a phase-split waiting to happen.**
- **`hegu_shengu`** — the body share — is coded from Zelin's reading of Morck and Yang on Rishengchang.

**Two custody warnings.** A significant share of the surviving record is in **a collector's compilation**, which is a selection by purchase; and **a branch letter-book survives in Tokyo**, which means someone carried it there. **Both are the acquisitions route, not the archive route.**
