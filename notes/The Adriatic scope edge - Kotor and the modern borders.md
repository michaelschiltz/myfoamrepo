# The Adriatic scope edge — Kotor and the modern borders

⚠️ **A scope note, not a repository note.**

The commercial world this survey has been calling "Dalmatian" does not stop at Croatia's modern border. **Kotor** — a notarial town with medieval series comparable to the Dalmatian ones — is in Montenegro; Venetian Istria runs into Slovenia; the Ragusan hinterland was Ottoman and is now Bosnian.

## Why it is worth writing down

Because the survey has now made the same mistake once and caught it. [[Rijksarchief te Luik en Namen - the Walloon repositories]] records that a pass which quietly followed a language boundary had imported a nineteenth-century political fact into a study of the fifteenth. **This is the same error available on a different border**, and the Adriatic is where it is easiest to make, because "Croatia" is a convenient container for a set of city archives that were never one polity.

**The rule to carry:** the unit is the *notariate* or the *chancery*, not the modern state. A survey organised by country will produce country-shaped findings, and the countries were not there.

⚠️ Kotor, Slovene Istria and the Bosnian material are all unexamined, and they may be thin. **Their absence from this survey is a decision about scope, not a finding about the Adriatic**, and should be stated as such wherever the Dalmatian evidence is used.
