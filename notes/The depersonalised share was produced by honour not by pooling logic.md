# The depersonalised share was produced by honour not by pooling logic

The project explains the transferable, anonymous share as the dissolution of a personal bond — to kin, to named partners, to designated beneficiaries — which lets a pool become impersonal and indefinitely scalable. The *asiento de avería* produces the same feature from an entirely different cause, and says so in its own documents.

Hierro Anibarro's §3.2 is explicit. The difficulty was not scale but **contempt**: by the early seventeenth century Spanish elites despised productive and mercantile activity, so the capital existed but would not move. The authorities concluded that if they could neither redirect merchants toward trade nor defeat the social current that disdained work, they could at least channel the unproductive capital of any social class into the new vehicle. They therefore wrote into the *asientos* a capitalist enterprise expressly **"desvinculado del ejercicio personal del comercio"** — decoupled from the personal exercise of commerce — precisely so that investment would be compatible with the standing of the *hidalgo*, the noble and the cleric. One capitulation goes so far as to insist that the contract "no se le dè nombre de arrendamiento," since many participants were nobles with pretensions to habits and honours, and the label would injure their nobility.

The Junta de Comercio names the same pair of problems when it turns to the company form in 1624–25: the institutional vehicle, and "el modo de eliminar el menosprecio social" attached to commerce. The company is recommended because it "posibilitaba la inversión de capital sin que fuera preciso que quienes aportaban esos fondos se dedicaran al ejercicio del comercio."

So the impersonal, passive, depersonalised share here is a **solution to a status constraint**, engineered to let honour and capital coexist. Its cause is the derogation rule, not the arithmetic of pooling. And it arrives with its sibling in the same sentence: "en el asiento no es importante la calidad del aportante, sino la de su crédito" — the depersonalisation of the investor stated as the enterprise's operating principle.

This is a **rival causal account of the project's Objective 2 pivot**, advanced by contemporaries, in the evidentiary register the ethnohistoriographical method privileges above all others. It cannot be absorbed as a further instance of the framework's own mechanism. Absorbing it would be the Maslow's-hammer move in its purest form — the reading confirming itself on material that in fact says something else — and it is exactly what [[Guard against reverse-teleology]] exists to prevent.

The honest response is to treat status derogation as a **second, independent pathway to anonymity**, coordinate with the pooling pathway rather than subordinate to it, and to ask which pathway each case took. That is a better research question than the one the framework started with, and the comparative payoff is immediate: the Japanese *ie* and the lineage trust refuse anonymity to protect a kin bond; the *asiento* manufactures anonymity to protect an honour code. Same feature, opposite relation to personal standing.

## Consequence for the coding commons

The coding ontology must therefore separate two things it currently conflates: **whether a component is present**, and **what the actors said it was for**. A grid that records "transferable, depersonalised claim: yes" for both the *asiento* and the VOC has destroyed the distinction on which this note turns.

The requirement is a distinct, mandatory field carrying the actor-stated rationale, coded from the source's own vocabulary, versioned and attributed like every other coding decision — so that the presence of a component and the reason given for it can be cross-tabulated, and so that rival causal pathways become visible in the corpus instead of being flattened by it. Without it the framework will confirm itself, because every feature will have been coded by its function alone. See [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]].
