# Rekihaku — the National Museum of Japanese History and khirin

国立歴史民俗博物館, Sakura, Chiba. Integrated collections portal **khirin**: <https://khirin-a.rekihaku.ac.jp/>

**A museum that holds archives, and a portal that aggregates other holders.** khirin carries Rekihaku's own collections alongside partner universities' (Naruto University of Education, Chiba University) and municipal ones (Ōshū City); recent additions include the **Shimoiizaka house documents** (March 2025), the **Yanagawa Date house documents** and the **Gotō house documents** at Naruto. **Rekihaku is also an operator of [[Minna de Honkoku - crowdsourced transcription]].**

## The honest assessment

**Marginal for the census on present knowledge.** No coded row draws on a Rekihaku fonds. **Its value is structural**: khirin is the model of a national museum acting as the digital publisher for **house documents held by others** — the one route by which *komonjo* in university and municipal custody reach layer 2 without each holder building a system. ⚠️ Record and image counts, licences, and whether any merchant or village fonds with *kō* material is carried, not established.
