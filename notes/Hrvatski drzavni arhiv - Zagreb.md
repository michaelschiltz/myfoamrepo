# Hrvatski državni arhiv — Zagreb

<https://hda.arhiv.hr/> · runs [[Croatian archives online - ARHiNET and the access layer]]

The Croatian State Archives: the record of continental Croatia and Slavonia, of the Habsburg administration, and of the modern state.

## The honest placement

**Continental Croatia is a secondary region for this project and the note says so.** The commercial material the census needs is Adriatic and maritime; Zagreb's holdings are the record of a land frontier, a Habsburg province and a nineteenth-century state.

Its value here is **institutional rather than documentary**: it runs the national descriptive system, and it is the entry point to every Croatian repository including the Dalmatian ones.

⚠️ One thing worth a check rather than an assumption: the **Military Frontier** (*Vojna krajina*) was an unusual institutional arrangement — a militarised zone with collective landholding by extended households (*zadruga*) in exchange for military service. **The *zadruga* is a household-corporate form**, and the entity census reads a comparable object in the Japanese *ie*. ⚠️ Whether it belongs in the census at all is a scope question, and it should be asked properly rather than assumed either way.
