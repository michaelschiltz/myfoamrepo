# A significant persistence coefficient is evidence against its own licensing condition

Objective 3's organising move is that the substitution of space for time is licensed if and only if the process is ergodic. Taken one step further than the proposal currently takes it, the conditional closes into a squeeze: **the finding a persistence design reports is a claim that its own licence fails.**

## The argument, stated in the right sense of the word

Ergodicity is indecomposability, not the slogan about averages — [[Ergodicity is indecomposability not randomness]]. A system is ergodic when the state space admits no non-trivial partition the dynamics never connect.

Now read what a persistence result asserts. Its content is that units separated by a condition laid down centuries ago are still separated today, in a way that the intervening history has not connected. That is a claim that a partition of the state space has gone uncrossed over the design's horizon. Which is the licence's failure condition, stated in the licence's own vocabulary.

The direction of the tension is what makes it worth having. **The larger and the better identified the coefficient, the stronger the evidence that the cross-section from which it was estimated could not be read as a trajectory.** Sharpening the estimate sharpens the objection to it.

## Which kind of breaking, and why the answer must be the weak one

[[Distinguish strong from weak ergodicity breaking]] binds here, and the note is worth nothing if the distinction is fudged.

**Strong breaking** — genuine decomposition, invariant sets no amount of waiting connects — would give the squeeze in its crudest form, but it is not available. Societies are not irreducible; the partition a persistence result reports is not one the dynamics literally cannot cross. Asserting decomposition where none exists is exactly the slippage a referee will locate.

**Weak breaking** is the real case and it makes the argument quantitative rather than rhetorical. The partition has not been crossed *over this horizon*: relaxation is slower than the observation window. Written that way, the squeeze becomes an inequality between two timescales.

- Space-for-time is licensed when the relaxation time is short relative to the horizon, so that units are interchangeable and one unit's present is a state another could have reached.
- A non-zero persistence coefficient over *n* centuries is a **lower bound on the relaxation time** relative to those *n* centuries.
- The design therefore reports a quantity whose magnitude measures the extent to which its own identifying assumption is violated.

That inequality, written down for a named design, is the deliverable. Everything above it is the argument for why the comparison is worth writing down, and everything below it is bookkeeping.

## The fork, which is where this must be argued rather than asserted

A defender will refuse the temporal framing of the estimand: the coefficient identifies a causal contrast across units under an exchangeability assumption, no within-society propagation was ever claimed, and the ergodicity condition is beside the point. Take that reply seriously and it costs the other horn.

- **On the temporal reading** — an initial condition propagating through one society's history — the licence is contradicted by the finding, as above.
- **On the causal-inference reading**, the estimand is a contemporaneous contrast between units, and every temporal word attached to it (persist, propagate, root, shadow, *longue durée*) is decoration on a cross-sectional quantity. The identification survives; the historical claim that made the paper interesting does not.

Neither horn is escaped by a better standard error. That is the property worth having, and it is the same species of conclusion the vault reaches by two other routes: [[Deep-roots variables are symplesiomorphies]] on the inert character, and [[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]] on the collider. Three independent arguments, one conclusion — a result may be true, precisely estimated, and evidentially empty.

## What must be conceded

**Ergodicity is model- and observable-specific.** Part 2 already carries this as the hinge of the objective and it binds here: a process may relax quickly in one functional and slowly in another. The inequality must be run on the particular observable the design uses. Asserted at the level of "the process", it is a slogan.

**The bound is one-sided.** A non-zero coefficient bounds the relaxation time from below; it does not establish that no mixing occurred, and it says nothing about heterogeneity of relaxation across units — which is a separate defect, and already the argument of [[Voigtlander and Voth measured the relaxation time and reported a mixture]]. That note says a coefficient pooled over heterogeneous relaxation times is a mixture rather than a parameter. This one says that even under homogeneous relaxation the magnitude reports the violation. They are complements and should be cited as two, not merged into one.

**The reflexive cost is the one already on the books.** Objectives 1 and 2 also read a cross-section of traditions, and a referee will put the two side by side. The reply recorded in [[Deep-roots variables are symplesiomorphies]] holds unchanged — persistence papers claim causal transmission along a lineage and owe an account of the lineage; this project claims functional recurrence and treats transmission as the rival to be eliminated. Different claims, different burdens. It has to be written out each time it is relied on.

## A consequence for the pre-committed case

Part 2 pre-commits to naming a canonical persistence result the critique is expected to leave standing, which is the right guard against a self-confirming method. The squeeze constrains what that case can look like: a design survives where relaxation is fast relative to its horizon for the observable at issue, and by the inequality above that is a setting in which a large retained effect should not be expected in the first place.

So the pre-committed case should be sought among designs with modest effects, or with observables that plausibly mix quickly, and the selection criterion should be stated in those terms *before* the case is chosen. Pre-committing to a headline result and then finding it ergodic would be the more impressive outcome; it is also the less likely one, and promising it is a hostage.
