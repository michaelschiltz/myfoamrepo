# The western family needs only plausible common descent not proven genealogy

The Sasanian genealogy is currently stated as a finding: the *qirāḍ*'s antecedents lie in Sasanian partnership law, preserved through the East-Syriac law books, therefore the Sasanian, Islamic and Mediterranean forms are a single family connected by descent, therefore the real test of independent recurrence is East Asia against that whole cluster. Stated that way the design is hostage to a philological claim that no consulted expert has yet affirmed.

It does not need to be stated that way, and it is stronger when it is not. **The argument requires only that the western forms cannot be *assumed* independent.** Plausible, undisproven common descent is sufficient for that, because the burden runs in the direction the argument needs: under Galton's problem the default for neighbouring, contemporaneous, contact-exposed traditions is non-independence, and independence is the thing that has to be earned. A common Sasanian substrate that is plausible and unrefuted therefore disqualifies the western forms as independent trials *whether or not the specific descent of the qirāḍ is ever demonstrated*.

The genealogy, in other words, is functioning as a **precaution**, not as a finding. Restated as a precaution the claim is weaker in rhetoric and stronger in logic, and it becomes robust to expert caution rather than damaged by it. When Simonsohn judges the conduit plausible and the specific *qirāḍ* descent a separate question turning on textual evidence, that is not a blow to the design — it is the precaution's premise, supplied by the relevant expert. See [[Simonsohn 2016 on East Syrian civil law and the Sasanian substrate]].

The reframing carries two obligations.

**Do not launder it in the other direction.** If the western forms are only *possibly* connected, the honest statement of the comparative design is that it yields at most two independent trials and possibly fewer, not that it yields a clean East–West contrast. The precaution buys conservatism about counting, and conservatism about counting is a cost as well as a defence. The reflexive objection — a project that polices inference from small ill-posed comparisons while conducting one — is not answered by this move and has to be answered separately, by insisting that the argument turns on structured resemblance with divergent arbitrary particulars rather than on enumeration.

**Code independence as a judgement, not a default.** The corpus must record, for each pair of traditions, whether independence is asserted, on what evidence, and with what confidence — the same discipline as [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]]. A grid in which independence is the unmarked case will manufacture independent recurrences, which is Galton's problem realised in software.

Demonstrating the genealogy would still be worth having, and [[The qirad's antecedents are Sasanian not Sogdian]] records what that would take. But the design should not be built so that its failure is fatal.
