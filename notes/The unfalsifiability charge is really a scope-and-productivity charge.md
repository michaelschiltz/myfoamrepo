# The unfalsifiability charge is really a scope-and-productivity charge

The title of [[Toda 2023 on ergodicity economics as pseudoscience]] and its argument come apart, and separating them is what makes the paper answerable.

**What the argument actually establishes.** Read §IV without the Popperian framing and the complaint is this: ergodicity economics has a prescription for the two-asset terminal-wealth portfolio problem and no prescription for the general consumption–investment problem — three periods, labour income, dynamic budget constraints — which economic theory has solved since Merton and Samuelson in 1969. A generalisation beyond purely additive and multiplicative dynamics was promised in 2016 and has not appeared. Twelve years have produced a reformulation of Kelly.

That is a claim about **domain** and about **rate of production of new content**. Both are serious, and the second is arguable on the evidence he gives.

**What it does not establish.** Unfalsifiability. Toda concedes in the same section that the portfolio problem *is* a testable implication, and [[Toda's own example makes EE more falsifiable than expected utility]] shows it is a sharper one than the comparison theory offers. A theory with a narrow domain and one point prediction inside it is not unfalsifiable; it is narrow. Popper's criterion is about what a theory forbids, not about how much it covers or how fast its adherents publish.

**The framework he needs is Lakatos, not Popper.** "Few new testable implications over twelve years, and those it has are reformulations of prior results" is precisely the description of a *degenerating research programme* — and the concept exists because Popperian falsification alone cannot make that judgement. But invoking it carries obligations Toda does not discharge: a degeneration verdict requires comparing the programme's novel corroborated content against its rivals over the same period, and it requires distinguishing content-increasing from content-preserving modifications. He does neither. He counts years and journals.

**Why the distinction matters to us rather than to Peters.** Because the two charges have different implications for the project, and conceding the wrong one is expensive.

If the charge is unfalsifiability, the project is implicated: a historical application of an unfalsifiable framework inherits the defect. That charge should be resisted, and the resistance is cheap because his own §IV supplies it.

If the charge is narrow domain and slow production, the project is **not** implicated at all, and may even be the answer to it. The domain complaint is about consumption–investment problems for individual decision-makers, which is not what the vault does; and new testable content applied to a new evidentiary domain is exactly what a stalled programme is accused of failing to generate. That argument is at [[The selective claim is falsifiable where the prescriptive claim is not]].

**The concession to make, and to make first.** Toda's priority point is correct and already conceded at [[Nothing in the ergodic theorem fails in geometric Brownian motion]]: the growth-rate result is Kelly, Latané and Breiman, and the ergodicity framing is a reframing rather than new mathematics. Repeating that concession in a reply costs nothing already defensible, and it purchases the standing to separate the two charges — which is the only move that matters here.
