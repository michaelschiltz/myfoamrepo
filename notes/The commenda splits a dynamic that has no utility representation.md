# The commenda splits a dynamic that has no utility representation

A hypothesis, not a finding.

**The formal fact.** Peters and Adamou's inverse condition (eq. 5.7) admits a utility for $dx = a\,dt + b\,dW$ only if $a(x) = b(x)\,(\alpha/\beta + b'(x)/2)$, so that the volatility fixes the drift up to one constant. Take the simplest realistic household: return on capital plus a wage, $dx = (\mu x + c)\,dt + \sigma x\,dW$. With $b = \sigma x$ the right-hand side is proportional to $x$ and has no constant, so the condition fails for every $c \neq 0$. **Capital-plus-labour wealth has no utility representation in the framework.** The right risk exposure depends on how wealth divides between the two parts, not on its level.

**The historical hypothesis.** The *commenda* separates exactly those two parts. The investing partner's capital is exposed multiplicatively and his loss is capped at the stake; the travelling partner contributes labour, roughly additive, and takes a share of profit without bearing loss of capital — the allocation fixed at [[The commenda family fixes the allocation of loss and proves it by the agent's oath]]. Read this way, the contract converts one mixed process, which no fixed criterion ranks, into two components that each have a transform.

## The prediction

The role a party takes should sort by **income composition**, not by wealth: those whose wealth is mostly capital take the investing side, those whose wealth is mostly labour income the travelling side, whatever their level of wealth. Testable in the deed-level instance files, where a party's other holdings and contracts are recorded — the Barcelona and Seville series above all. The rival account (wealthy invest, poor travel) predicts sorting by level.

## Guardrails

- The comparison is within one form and requires no ranking across dynamics, so it is not exposed to [[EE cannot rank across dynamics and the comparative project must not need it to]].
- Where the agent also contributes capital the split is incomplete — [[Comanda capital was mixed with the agent's own unless the contract said otherwise]] — and those deeds are where the prediction is weakest.
- Do not write that merchants chose the form to obtain a representable dynamic. The claim is about what the form does to the dynamic, and the selective reading at [[Time-average optimization is a survivorship property not an intention]] governs.
