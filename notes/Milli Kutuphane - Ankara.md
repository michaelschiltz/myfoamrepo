# Milli Kütüphane — Ankara

The National Library of Turkey. <https://www.mkutup.gov.tr/>.

## Why it has a note

**Two reasons, and both are smaller than they were.**

**1. It was the custodian of the Anatolian court registers from 1991 to 2005**, and the literature of those years cites it as such. It kept the microfilms when the originals went to the State Archives — the *TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi* records 8,693 Anatolian and 10,369 Istanbul register microfilms. See [[The Anatolian court registers - museum to National Library to State Archives]]. **For a reader, İSAM's digital copies have superseded the microfilm.**

**2. Its manuscript collection holds the account book `avariz_vakfi_kirkcesme` is coded from** — the Kırkçeşme *mahalle avarız akçesi vakıf defteri*, 1249/1834–1284/1867, on which Küçük 2025 works. ⚠️ Shelfmark not recorded in the census and not found in this pass.

## The honest assessment

**Marginal as a repository, and worth watching for one reason.** A waqf account book in a national library's manuscript collection is a document that **left its institution** — through a trustee's heirs, a dealer, a collector. The Kırkçeşme book is evidence precisely because it survived outside the Evkaf's own series. **Every account book found this way is selected by dispersal, and the census's fullest Ottoman accounting source is one of them.** ⚠️ How the book reached the library is not known; if it came by institutional transfer rather than dispersal, this paragraph falls. That is not a reason to doubt the coding; it is a reason not to treat Kırkçeşme as typical until a book from inside a series says the same.
