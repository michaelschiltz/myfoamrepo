# Academia Sinica IHP — the Grand Secretariat archives in Taipei

中央研究院歷史語言研究所, 明清檔案工作室, Nangang, Taipei. <https://newarchive.ihp.sinica.edu.tw/>

**About 310,000 items of the Qing Grand Secretariat's archive** — edicts and patents, memorials and routine reports (*tiben*), historiographical drafts, dispatches, **yellow registers and account books**. Bought by the Institute in 1929 from the collector who had bought them from the scholar who had bought them back from the paper dealers — see [[The eight thousand sacks - the Grand Secretariat archives sold as waste paper]] — and taken to Taiwan with the Institute.

## Layer and access

**Catalogued and served through the 内閣大庫檔案資料庫**, and cross-searchable with the National Palace Museum's Qing and Republican archives. ⚠️ Image coverage and registration terms not captured; the Institute's workroom page could not be fetched in this pass.

## What it holds for the census

**The routine paperwork of a central office**, including registers and accounts. **The survival rule is the dealers' and buyers' selection**: what was not pulped between 1921 and 1929. ⚠️ No census-relevant series identified. **Honest assessment: marginal for the census's private forms**, important as the second custodian of the one archive the Chinese state produced at scale.
