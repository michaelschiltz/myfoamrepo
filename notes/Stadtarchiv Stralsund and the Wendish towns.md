# Stadtarchiv Stralsund and the Wendish towns

Stralsund is one of the eleven archives in the [[Hanse documents on the Memory of the World register]] inscription, and the Wendish quarter — Stralsund, Wismar, Rostock, Greifswald — is the Hanse's Baltic core.

## Why the Wendish towns are a set rather than four towns

**They were a voting bloc within the league**, met as a group, and their civic registers run in parallel under closely related town law — Lübeck law, received across the Baltic.

That is the same configuration the French *amirautés* offer and for the same reason: **one legal frame, several independent local practices.** Where the Wendish towns' registers diverge in incidental drafting, the divergence measures civic habit rather than legal tradition, which is the baseline [[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]] needs and which the project currently lacks for the Baltic.

## The complication that has to be named

**Wismar and Stralsund were Swedish from 1648.** A century and a half of Swedish sovereignty over Hanseatic towns under Lübeck law is either a confound or an experiment, depending on how it is handled — and it is the Baltic counterpart of the Montpellier situation at [[Archives departementales de l'Herault - Montpellier]]: the same town, the same law, a different crown.

⚠️ Wholly unverified. Series, survival, access and imaging are all unknown, and Swedish-period material for these towns may be in Stockholm rather than in the towns.
