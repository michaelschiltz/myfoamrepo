# Archivo General de Simancas

<https://www.cultura.gob.es/cultura/areas/archivos/mc/archivos/ags.html> · catalogue via [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]]

**The fiscal archive of the Habsburg monarchy**, and therefore the archive behind the project's sovereign-debt strand. [[Drelichman and Voth 2014 on the debts and defaults of Philip II]] is built on this material, and the claims the project makes about it — [[Syndication and the misma moneda are pooling not prudence]], [[The moratorium held because leaving the pool is not a one-period cost]], [[A contingency clause priced at zero is evidence for a time-average criterion]] — are claims about documents held here.

## The sections that matter

**`CONTADURIA MAYOR DE CUENTAS`** — the audited accounts, in three *epocas*. The *asientos* with the Genoese, Fugger and Spinola bankers are audited here.

**`CONTADURIA MAYOR DE HACIENDA`** — the Treasury's own administrative series.

**`CONSEJO Y JUNTAS DE HACIENDA`** — the deliberative record. For the project this is the more valuable of the two, because the *reasoning* behind a contingency clause is what carries the argument, not the clause's existence.

**`ESTADO`** — diplomatic and political correspondence, including the negotiation of the *medios generales* that settled the defaults.

## Why the deliberative series is the load-bearing one

The project's claim is that a clause priced at zero is evidence for a time-average criterion — that is, evidence about *why* the parties wrote what they wrote. An accounting series cannot supply that; it records the outcome. [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]] is the methodological rule, and at Simancas it translates directly into a preference for `CONSEJO Y JUNTAS DE HACIENDA` over `CONTADURIA MAYOR DE CUENTAS` wherever both would answer.

## Digitisation

Substantial and growing; PARES carries imaged series from Simancas, and the archive has been the subject of a sustained digitisation programme. ⚠️ Which of the four sections above are imaged, and to what completeness, is unverified — establish this before assuming a series can be worked remotely.

⚠️ **The Drelichman and Voth legajo citations should be extracted and turned into a shelf-mark list.** Their footnotes are the best existing finding aid into this material for this project's purposes, and re-deriving it from the archive's own inventories would be wasted effort.
