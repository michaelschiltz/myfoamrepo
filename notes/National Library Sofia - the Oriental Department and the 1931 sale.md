# National Library Sofia — the Oriental Department and the 1931 sale

The Oriental Department of the **St Cyril and St Methodius National Library**, Sofia. **More than a million Ottoman archival records**, which Altay 2017 calls the third-largest Ottoman archive anywhere. ⚠️ The ranking is his.

## The sale

**In 1931 the Istanbul finance office sold a depot of Treasury papers as waste paper, to a paper factory near Sofia**, at a price per *okka*. The columnist İbrahim Hakkı Konyalı exposed it; Muallim Cevdet petitioned the prime minister, İsmet İnönü, whose circular stopped the transaction. **Most of what had been sold was already in Bulgaria, and only a few hundred items were returned.** Baş 2019 records the same sale and the protest.

## What that does to the evidence

**This is the Ottoman instance of pattern 3, and it is the unusual kind: a catastrophic loss with a shape.** A fire takes what happens to be in the building. **A sale of a Treasury depot takes Treasury paper** — tax, *mukataa*, debt and accounting registers — from one store in one year. So:

- **A gap in Istanbul's fiscal series of the right kind may be in Sofia, not lost.** Check here before inferring an absence.
- **Sofia's Ottoman holdings are not a sample of the Ottoman record.** They over-represent whatever that depot held. ⚠️ Not all of the department's holdings came from the 1931 sale — the Bulgarian provinces' own court registers did not — and the proportion was not established.

## Holdings bearing on the census

- **170 court registers** from Sofya, Vidin, Rusçuk, Silistre, Hacıoğlupazarcığı and Eskicuma (Sofia, Vidin, Ruse, Silistra, Dobrich, Targovishte); the earliest, Sofia's, **1542–1550** (*TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi*).
- 720 registers of state administration; 328 fonds arranged by settlement; tax, debt, population, *mukataa*, *timar* and **waqf** documents.
- More than 90 per cent Ottoman Turkish, the rest Arabic and Persian.
- **The court registers are reported digitised and free online.** ⚠️ URL and completeness not checked.

## The honest assessment

**For the census, marginal: no coded row draws on Bulgarian material, and the waqf content is unmeasured.** Its value is as the **survival rule for the Istanbul Treasury record** — the first thing to check when a fiscal series at [[Osmanli Arsivi - Istanbul]] has a hole in it.
