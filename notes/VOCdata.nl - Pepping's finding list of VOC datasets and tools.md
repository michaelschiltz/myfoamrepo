# VOCdata.nl — Pepping's finding list of VOC datasets and tools

**A single-maintainer (Kay Pepping) Quartz site indexing 55 entries, 30 datasets and 25 tools, built on or around the VOC archive (NA 1.04.02). Its use here is as a DOI-level finding list for machine-readable VOC series: shipping, prices, commodities, weights and measures, polities, and the Japan-facing people index. Most datasets sit in DataverseNL under the prefix `10.34894`.**

Site structure: `vocdata.nl/1-indexes/` (datasets, tools, metadata index, topic index), `vocdata.nl/2-topics/` (geographic, enslavement, population), and one entry page per item at `vocdata.nl/notes/<slug>`. Geographic facets live at `vocdata.nl/notes/dots/<region>.base` (asia, japan, batavia, cochin, makassar, cape-of-good-hope, etc.).

## Datasets (30)

Format for each: contributors; period; geo facet; licence; file format; DOI.

### Archival indexes and finding aids

- **National Archives VOC Index**: Nationaal Archief; 1602–1799; Global; CC0; scans. `nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/archief/1.04.02`
  - The full inventory of 1.04.02 with scans. It is the base layer for most other entries.
- **Digitized Indexes of the VOC OBP (1602–1799)**: K. Pepping, R. Smit, M. Kuruppath; 1602–1799; Global; CC BY 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/OP20NM](https://doi.org/10.34894/OP20NM)
  - A data version of the TANAP-era indexes to the *Overgekomen Brieven en Papieren*.
- ***Overzicht van Generale Missiven in het archief van de VOC, 1.04.02***: K. Pepping; 1618–1793; Global; CC BY-SA 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/SRDMFU](https://doi.org/10.34894/SRDMFU)
  - Identified *generale missiven* located within the 1.04.02 inventory.
- **Non-Western people described in the General Missive footnotes**: F. Brink, K. Pepping; 1610–1767; Global; CC BY 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/CGQ4J1](https://doi.org/10.34894/CGQ4J1)
  - Person observations from the footnotes of the RGP *Generale Missiven* volumes, linking Dutch renderings of names to biographical detail.

### Shipping, trade, prices, measures

- **Dutch-Asiatic Shipping (DAS), Enriched Dataset**: L. Petram; 1595–1795; Asia, Europe; CC BY 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/RJ7UEA](https://doi.org/10.34894/RJ7UEA)
  - A normalised version of the Bruijn–Gaastra–Schöffer DAS: direct Republic–Asia voyages.
- **Dutch Ships in Asian Waters in the 17th Century**: M. Leenstra, R. Parthesius; 1595–1700; Asia; CC BY-SA 4.0; TSV/CSV; [10.34894/5HGTCD](https://doi.org/10.34894/5HGTCD)
  - Intra-Asian voyages: ships, crew, cargo, dates. ⚠️ The site claims ~60,000 voyages. Unverified, and it looks high for a 1595–1700 intra-Asian set.
- **Monsoon Traders: 18th Century Makassar Shipping Data**: G. Knaap, H. Sutherland; 1700–1799; Makassar; CC BY-SA 4.0; Excel; [10.34894/SAENX2](https://doi.org/10.34894/SAENX2)
  - Non-European ships calling at Makassar, from the Knaap–Sutherland *Monsoon Traders* data.
- **VOC Prices in the Dutch-Asian Trade, 1608–1800**: P. de Zwart; 1608–1800; Asia, Europe, Global; CC BY-SA 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/UFGGCY](https://doi.org/10.34894/UFGGCY)
  - Commodity prices at purchase and sale locations, marked as original, extrapolated, interpolated or imputed.
- **Weights and Measures in the 18th-Century Indian Ocean World**: H. Vellinga, B. Nijman; 1700–1799; Asia; CC BY-SA 4.0; Excel/TSV; [10.34894/GDEPNF](https://doi.org/10.34894/GDEPNF)
  - VOC units of weight and measure.
- **GLOBALISE Thesaurus: Commodities**: K. Pepping, H. Vellinga; 1700–1800; Asia, Africa, Europe; CC BY-SA 4.0; .trig, PDF; [10.34894/5PZPPK](https://doi.org/10.34894/5PZPPK)
  - A SKOS commodity hierarchy drawn from the Bookkeeper-General Batavia ledgers.
- **Dutch Textile Trade Dataset**: C. Anderson, M. Kehoe (Dutch Textile Trade Project); 1600–1800; Asia; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (the site writes "CC-BY-SA-NC"); CSV. `dutchtextiletrade.org/data/`
  - 300+ textile terms from cargo lists and invoices, with spelling variants. No DOI.

### Polities, places, groups

- **Early Modern Polities in the VOC Archives**: M. Hids, F. Brink; 1600–1815; Asia, Europe, Global; CC BY-SA 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/SIAUPO](https://doi.org/10.34894/SIAUPO)
  - Polities, rulerships and rulers named in the VOC archives.
- **Kerajaan2 Indonesia**: H. Hägerdal; no period given; Indonesia; CC BY-SA 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/CBO80N](https://doi.org/10.34894/CBO80N)
  - An alphabetic list of the former princely states of Indonesia, partly folded into the polities set above.
- **Places in the VOC Archives**: T.D. Pham, B. Nijman, R. Land; 1602–1799; Africa, Asia, Global; CC BY 4.0; Excel; [10.34894/UFFFNO](https://doi.org/10.34894/UFFFNO)
  - A gazetteer of places named in VOC records.
- **ESTA Database Locations**: P. Konings; 1500–1900; Asia; CC BY-SA 4.0; TSV/CSV; [10.34894/3Q0DLH](https://doi.org/10.34894/3Q0DLH)
  - ESTA project locations, also merged into *Places in the VOC Archives*.
- **Necessary Reunions dataset**: T.D. Pham, M.R. Mathai et al.; 1600–1800; Kerala; CC BY 4.0; Excel; [10.34894/FN1DBL](https://doi.org/10.34894/FN1DBL)
  - Kerala place names from Dutch maps and texts.
- **Ethnicities, Religious Groups and Castes in the VOC Archives (1602–1799)**: H. Vellinga, T.D. Pham; 1602–1799; Asia, Africa; CC BY 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/RI0KGV](https://doi.org/10.34894/RI0KGV)
  - A SKOS thesaurus of group designations.

### People, Japan, East Asia

- **Japanese Individuals relevant to the VOC Archives**: M. Mackenzie; 1600–1868; Japan; CC BY 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/PPUJZB](https://doi.org/10.34894/PPUJZB)
  - Biographical observations on Tokugawa-period Japanese figures appearing in VOC records.
- **China Biographical Database Project (CBDB)**: Harvard, Academia Sinica, Peking University; 600–1899; China; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; SQL; [10.34894/O8QZVG](https://doi.org/10.34894/O8QZVG)
  - The CBDB as a downloadable SQL dump.
- **Namebooks of the VOC (1730–1794)**: P. Maillard, K. Pepping, M. Kuruppath, R. Land; 1730–1794; Africa, Asia; CC BY 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/MD59SC](https://doi.org/10.34894/MD59SC)
  - Personnel employment data from the *naamboekjes*.
- **VOC Census Records of Cochin (1700–1775)**: K. Pepping, S. Mundukkal; 1700–1775; Cochin; CC BY 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/O12KME](https://doi.org/10.34894/O12KME)

### Cape of Good Hope

- **Overview of People in the TEPC Inventories (1673–1844)**: K. Pepping; Cape; CC BY 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/K5BYMN](https://doi.org/10.34894/K5BYMN)
- **TANAP Cape of Good Hope Transcriptions: Orphan Chamber**: TEPC Transcription Project; 1673–1844; Cape; CC BY 4.0; XML. `docs.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl/tanap/cape-transcriptions/`
  - Transcribed estate inventories of the Weeskamer.
- **VOC Court Records Cape Town, 1705–1774**: S.R. Reyes; Cape; CC BY-SA 4.0; Excel; [10.34894/V7IZI0](https://doi.org/10.34894/V7IZI0)
  - ⚠️ The site says this set is wholly contained in the 1637–1828 court records below.
- **Vrijburgers Kaap de Goede Hoop 1716**: R. van der Spiegel; 1716; Cape; CC BY-SA 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/KGV6MB](https://doi.org/10.34894/KGV6MB)
  - Civil and military servants and spouses.

### Courts and enslavement

- **Early Modern Dutch Colonial Court Records, 1637–1828**: I. Canfijn, A. Geelen (Resilient Diversity); Cape, Asia; CC BY-SA 4.0; Excel; [10.34894/S1BIHJ](https://doi.org/10.34894/S1BIHJ)
- **Enslaved Demography: Daghregisters Batavia (1678–1807)**: P. Konings; Batavia; CC BY-SA 4.0; TSV/CSV; [10.34894/EZDYZL](https://doi.org/10.34894/EZDYZL)
  - Census summaries appended to the Batavia Castle *dagregisters*.
- **Slave Trade Prices in the Indian Ocean, Indonesian Archipelago and Atlantic Worlds**: M. van Rossum; 1600–1850; CC BY-SA 4.0; CSV; [10.34894/JPJJ0M](https://doi.org/10.34894/JPJJ0M)
- **French and Dutch Slave Trade on Madagascar (1641–1810)**: R. Thiebaut; Madagascar; CC0; TSV/Excel. ⚠️ The site gives DOI `10.34894/O8QZVG`, which is the CBDB DOI. The correct DOI is unverified.
- **Mozambique Slave Traders (1750–1897)**: J. Capela; Mozambique; CC0; Excel; [10.34894/P1RERM](https://doi.org/10.34894/P1RERM)
  - Data from Capela, *Dicionário de Negreiros em Moçambique 1750–1897*.

## Tools (25)

- **Transcription and full-text search**:
  - GLOBALISE Transcription Search (`transcriptions.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl`): full-text search of ~4.5M pages of HTR'd VOC documents. The site marks it temporary.
  - HTR Hub (`htrhub.nl`) and its Language Telescope (`telescope.htrhub.dekok.xyz`).
  - GLOBALISE Word2Vec Lab (`lab.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl`).
  - VOC Gloss Explorer (`voc-gloss-explorer.streamlit.app`).
  - Transkribus Table Mapper (`table-to-globalise-mapper.streamlit.app`).
  - BlackLab Search, General Missives (`corpora.ato.ivdnt.org`). ⚠️ The site reports it offline as of August 2026.
- **Trade and shipping**:
  - Bookkeeper-General Batavia (`bgb.huygens.knaw.nl`): 18th-century intra-VOC commodity flows from the BGB ledgers, 18,000+ voyages.
  - DAS (`resources.huygens.knaw.nl/das`).
  - Dutch Textile Project (`dutchtextiletrade.org`).
- **Maps and places**:
  - Atlas of Mutual Heritage (`atlasofmutualheritage.nl`).
  - CapAsia Factory Atlas (`atlas.capasia.eu`): European factories in Asia, 1500–1800.
  - GLOBALISE Places Visualization (`globalise.shinyapps.io/mapping_places`).
  - Necessary Reunions (`necessaryreunions.org`).
  - Maritime Asia Heritage Survey, CSEAS Kyoto (`maritimeasiaheritage.cseas.kyoto-u.ac.jp`).
  - Japanese Historical Maps, David Rumsey (`davidrumsey.com/japan`): 2,000+ manuscript and woodblock maps. This entry is not VOC-derived.
- **Enslavement and courts**:
  - Exploring Slave Trade in Asia (`exploringslavetradeinasia.com`): 5,300+ voyages.
  - Early Modern Colonial Court Records (`courtcases.exploringslavetradeinasia.com`).
- **Other archives**:
  - Digitarq, Portuguese national archives (`digitarq.arquivos.pt`), including the *Monções do Reino*.
  - Prize Papers Portal (`portal.prizepapers.de`).
  - Dutch Atlantic Sources (`dutchatlanticsources.com`), WIC, marked beta.
  - 1762 Archive Project (`1762archive.org`), the San Pablo convent library in Manila.
  - China Biographical Database interface (`projects.iq.harvard.edu/cbdb`).
  - Glottolog (`glottolog.org`).
  - Combatting Bias (`combattingbias.huygens.knaw.nl`).

## Source metadata problems

- The Madagascar slave-trade DOI duplicates the CBDB DOI (`O8QZVG`). One of the two is wrong on the site.
- *VOC Court Records Cape Town* is flagged by the site itself as redundant.
- *Kerajaan2 Indonesia* has no period.
- The site renders the NC licence as "CC-BY-SA-NC". It is normalised to CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 above.
