# Archives nationales du monde du travail — Roubaix

<https://archives-nationales-travail.culture.gouv.fr/> · catalogue: <https://recherche-anmt.culture.gouv.fr/>

**France's national business archive**, and the counterpart to [[IISG and NEHA - Amsterdam]]: a state repository whose collecting area is firms, banks, insurers, trade associations and unions, with its own *état général des fonds* online.

## Why it is in the set

Two reasons.

**Insurance companies.** The project's argument about the movement from shared to priced peril has a nineteenth-century terminus, and a national deposit of insurers' archives is where that terminus is documented. Same function as [[Archivio Storico Assicurazioni Generali - Trieste]], but public and national rather than corporate.

**Mutuals and cooperatives.** The entity census contains no cooperative, and the loss census is full of mutual institutions never coded as entities. A repository collecting *le monde du travail* holds the *sociétés de secours mutuels* — the French friendly societies — which are the direct comparandum for the census's `mutual_pole` and friendly-society rows, currently reachable only through British material at Kew.

⚠️ Whether the ANMT's holdings reach back before the nineteenth century in any quantity is **unestablished**, and the same caution applies as at [[KADOC - religious archives at Leuven]]: an institution founded to document the industrial era may hold very little of the ancien régime. Check that first, because it decides whether this repository serves the project's spine or only its endpoint.
