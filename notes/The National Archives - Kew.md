# The National Archives — Kew

<https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/>

The archives of the English and then British state, and the repository on which three of the census's coded rows depend.

## The shape of the problem here

**Kew's description is superb and its imaging is selective.** Where a series has genealogical demand or a commercial partner it is online; where it has neither it is a reading room with a camera policy.

**Every series in the table above except the Prize Papers portion of `HCA` falls in the second category.** For a project whose British evidence is the constitutional record of companies and the adjudication of maritime peril, that means **the British programme is a Kew programme**, and it should be costed as travel rather than as access.

⚠️ Establish the current self-photography rules and any per-day production limits before costing a trip. Compare the extremes already recorded: [[Archivio Apostolico Vaticano]] permits three units a day and no camera; [[Stadsarchief Rotterdam - the notarial archive and scanning on demand]] scans three items a day free and publishes them.
