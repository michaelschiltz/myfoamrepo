# The mudaraba is revocable not locked in

Classical fiqh predominantly classifies the *mudaraba*/*qirad* as an *ʿaqd jāʾiz* — a non-binding, revocable contract — rather than an *ʿaqd lāzim*. Either party may in principle rescind; the contract terminates on the death or incapacity of either party; and it dissolves at the venture's conclusion rather than persisting. Maliki jurists restrict revocation once the agent has commenced work [verify against fiqh sources — the schools diverge here]. *[2026-10-14: verified, and the framing corrected. Both schools bar the investor's recall once the agent has bought goods (al-Sarakhsī, *al-Mabsūṭ* 22, 53; *al-Mudawwana* 3, 662; *al-Muwaṭṭaʾ* 2027). What divides them is who may force the liquidation: the Ḥanafī investor can compel the sale (22, 72); the Mālikī cannot, and the goods are held to their market (662–663; *Muwaṭṭaʾ* 2052). Coded in HistorEE_codebooks as `mudaraba TS3 = 1`, `CI2 = 0` against `qirad TS3 = P`, `CI2 = 1` (pass `mudaraba-qirad-2026-10-14`). See [[Hanafi and Maliki law part on who may force the liquidation of a mudaraba]].]*

This kills a tempting but false formulation: that Islamic doctrine requires both partners to see the venture through to the end and forbids any uncoupling. It does not. The *qirad* is among the *most* easily dissolved commercial forms in the pre-modern world. *[2026-10-14: true of the Ḥanafī *muḍāraba*; the Mālikī *qirāḍ* locks the capital in from purchase until the goods meet their market, against the investor's wish — see [[Hanafi and Maliki law part on who may force the liquidation of a mudaraba]].]* What doctrine binds is **payoff to outcome**, not **partner to duration**. See [[Lock-in and outcome-coupling are distinct bindings]].

The correction is load-bearing rather than pedantic. Harris's entire corporation thesis turns on lock-in being precisely what the Islamic forms lacked, and Kuran builds the *Long Divergence* on dissolution-on-death. Asserting Islamic trajectory-binding in the temporal sense, in support of the coupling argument, would hand a referee the contradiction on the first page.
