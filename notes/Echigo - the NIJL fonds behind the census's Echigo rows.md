# Echigo — the NIJL fonds behind the census's Echigo rows

**Three census rows are Echigo rows**, and the NIJL Archives hold Echigo *Kubiki-gun* village fonds that are the likely homes of at least two of them. ⚠️ Every placement below rests on place-name and institution, not on the authors' apparatus.

## The rows

- `ko_hokinosawa_1820` — the *Kyūzaemon hajime tanomoshi* of Hōkinosawa-shinden, Nakazato village, from a 1820 *tanomoshi kōchū kime shōmon*; and
- `ko_kosaburo_1802` — the *Kosaburō ichibu-kō* of Tanaka village, from the Zentokuji documents of Yoshikawa-machi —

**both coded from Matsunaga 1999, published in the NIJL Archives' own *Shiryōkan kenkyū kiyō* 30.** ⚠️ Whether Matsunaga's documents are in the NIJL's holdings, or in Yoshikawa/Jōetsu local custody, not confirmed.

- `warichi_iwade` — periodic reallocation of arable at **Iwade village**, from Brown 2006 and 2011.

## The fonds

**越後国頸城郡岩手村佐藤家文書 — the Satō family papers of Iwade village.** About **6,200 items, 1598 to Meiji**; the Satō were *ōkimoiri* of the Iwade group (1742–1768) and then headmen. **5,232 items bought in 1953 from a Tokyo second-hand bookseller; 1,075 found in the village and donated in 1982.** Printed catalogue: *Shiryōkan shozō shiryō mokuroku* 38 (1983). Online search available. ⚠️ **That this Iwade is Brown's Iwade is not confirmed**; the catalogue's introduction does not mention *warichi*.

**越後国頸城郡諸村・諸家文書 — assorted villages and houses of Kubiki-gun.** 551 items from a dozen villages. **Images not online; database search not yet available.**

## Why this matters more than a placement

**If `warichi_iwade`'s village is the Satō papers' village, the census's one periodic-reallocation row rests on a headman's archive that the book trade split for thirty years.** The half bought in 1953 and the half kept in the village could not be read together before 1982. **Any study of Iwade's *warichi* before then read half an archive** — which is a question to put to Brown's sources, not a finding.
