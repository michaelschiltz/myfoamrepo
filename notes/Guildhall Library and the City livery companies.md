# Guildhall Library and the City livery companies

<https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/history-and-heritage/guildhall-library/>

## The two things here

**The Lloyd's Marine Collection** — see [[Lloyd's and the marine insurance records]].

**The livery companies.** The City's craft and merchant guilds — Mercers, Grocers, Drapers, Merchant Taylors and the rest — are **corporate bodies with charters, perpetual succession, common seals, property and their own jurisdiction over a trade**, continuously in existence from the fourteenth century to the present.

## Why the livery companies are a gap rather than a curiosity

**Every characteristic in the entity census's `entity-shielding` and `perpetual-succession` columns applies to them**, and `organizational_forms` codes none.

More pointedly: the Merchant Adventurers and the regulated companies are **the English member of the family this survey has assembled in five countries** — the Antwerp *naties*, the Bruges nations, the Burgos consulate, the French Levant consulates, the Bergen *Kontor*. Harris separates the **regulated** corporation from the joint-stock one on the census's own characteristics (`CI1`, `LR3`), and that exclusion was made by a coder and flagged for the maintainer in the 2026-09-07 batch. **The regulated company is the row that exclusion pointed at, and the livery companies are where its records are.**

⚠️ Custody is dispersed: each company keeps its own archive, some deposited at Guildhall, some retained at their halls. **Successor custody again** — the fourth instance after [[Fuggerarchiv - Dillingen]], [[The Bazacle records - a custody problem]] and [[Natie archives - where the Antwerp nations records are]], and the most numerous: a hundred-odd bodies each holding its own record.
