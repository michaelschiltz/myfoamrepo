# A difference between coded forms is a difference of evidence, vocabulary or form

Two rows that disagree on a cell disagree for one of three reasons, and only the third is a fact about the institutions. Every cross-form contrast has to be sorted before it is reported, because the matrix prints all three identically.

**1. Evidence.** One row holds a value and the other `.NR`, because one literature asked the question and the other did not. Between `comanda` and `commenda`, `LP1`, `AP1`, `AP3`, `LR1` and `LR4` all separate this way: the Catalan sources treat the comanda only between its parties, and under ruling 1 their silence stays `.NR`. The consequence is stark: every entity-side component is empty on the Catalan rows, which describes the literature and says nothing about whether the comanda had entity shielding. The converse holds: if the pilot's `.NR` readings of the Genoese acts are adopted, the two families converge on these cells because one rule has been applied to both, not because anything new was learned.

**2. Vocabulary.** The rows hold different values because the value set forces a choice the sources do not make. `comanda CI1 = several-accounts` against `commenda CI1 = common` describes one partition from opposite sides: the Genoese warrant is investors' capital merging into one stock with the traveller's own kept apart, the Catalan warrant is a comanda kept out of a company's stock. Ruling 3 has logged that `CI1` has no value for a single investor's capital in a single agent's hands, the commonest case in both. See [[Comanda capital was kept apart unless the contract said otherwise]].

**3. Form.** The rows hold different values on the same question, asked of comparable evidence. Only these are candidate findings, and even these need their characteristic checked: `RB3` was a third-kind difference that turned out partly to be a second-kind defect ([[Genoese notaries drafted the commenda as equity and Catalan notaries as credit]]).

**Three asymmetries of footing come first.** Before sorting cells, state what makes the rows unequal as evidence:

- **source layer:** a normative text, a secondary synthesis and a notarial register answer different questions, so a law-book default compared with a register's mode is already a mixed comparison;
- **rater:** a cell coded by a different model differs partly for that reason; the commenda pilot measured the effect at 52 of 61 values in agreement on fixed evidence ([[Hold the rater fixed and vary the evidence]]);
- **non-independence:** forms in one drafting tradition agree by parallelism, and their agreement corroborates nothing ([[Homoplasy is the finding not the noise]]).

This is the facet discipline of [[Unilateral and bilateral commenda collapse on profit and separate on loss]] and the counting discipline of [[Count degrees of freedom not cells]], applied one step earlier: before asking how many facts separate two forms, ask which of the separating cells are facts about forms at all.
