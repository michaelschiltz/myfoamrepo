# Stadsarchief Mechelen

<https://stadsarchief.mechelen.be/>

Mechelen was the seat of the **Grote Raad** — the Great Council, the supreme court of the Burgundian and Habsburg Netherlands — and that, rather than the city archive, is why it belongs in the set.

## The distinction that matters

**The Great Council's own archive is not at the city archive.** ⚠️ Its records are with the Rijksarchief; establish which repository before planning anything. The city archive holds Mechelen's municipal and notarial material, which is useful and ordinary.

The Great Council is the missing appellate tier for the Low Countries. [[Hof van Holland - the civil litigation record]] covers Holland from 1428; the Great Council sat above the provincial courts of the whole Burgundian complex, which means **a commercial dispute appealed out of Antwerp, Bruges or Ghent could end here**, and its judgments would be the nearest thing the southern Netherlands has to a unified doctrinal record.

For the Antwerp entity rows — which rest on one scholar's reading, as [[FelixArchief - Antwerp city archive]] records — an appellate series is a different order of witness from a treatise.

⚠️ Everything in the paragraph above is a hypothesis about a court's jurisdiction, not a statement about a surviving series. Verify the archive's location, extent and finding aids first; if the records are thin or unindexed the note is worth little.
