# Formalism refuses evidence of results and of reasons because neither discriminates

**Two separate arguments converge on judging conduct against a rule fixed in advance. Under non-ergodic dynamics the result does not discriminate honest conduct from dishonest; and on Taleb's own account the stated reason does not discriminate one policy from another. A formalism is an institution that refuses both kinds of evidence at once. It asks neither how the venture came out nor why the party acted, only whether the act had the prescribed form. That gives the procedural ethic an evidentiary ground rather than a moral one.**

## The two refusals

- **Results.** After a catastrophic loss the question "nature or the agent?" is usually unanswerable, because the likelihood ratio on outcomes collapses toward one ([[Non-ergodicity collapses the likelihood ratio on outcomes]]). The *amāna*/*ḍamān* doctrine declines the inference: loss as such is evidence of nothing, and liability attaches only on a conduct fact, *taʿaddī* or breach of instructions ([[The amana-daman structure refuses to infer fault from loss]]).
- **Reasons.** *Skin in the Game* ch. 17, "Religious in Words": religiosity is measured by distance from an atheist's action in the same situation, never by stated belief. Ch. 18: belief is cheap talk. The legal analogue is validity irrespective of motive. The judge does not ask why the obligation was assumed.

The decider's version of the second refusal is [[Rationality is a property of the policy not of the belief that produces it]]. This note is its third-person counterpart: there the decider sets belief aside, here the judge sets aside both belief and outcome.

## Instances in the vault's material

- **The claimant's oath.** `VF1 = claimant-oath` in the commenda family: a loss is proved by the agent's own sworn account, and the form of the oath stands in for an inquiry neither party can conduct ([[The commenda family fixes the allocation of loss and proves it by the agent's oath]]).
- **General average.** Contribution turns on proof that the jettison was deliberate and for the common safety: a conduct fact, not an outcome fact (amana note, comparative grid).
- **The bill of exchange.** ⚠️ Hook, not yet argued. The bill obligation is abstract: valid by form, and in the hands of a holder in good faith insulated from defences arising out of the underlying transaction. See the Geneva Uniform Law of 1930, art. 17 on personal defences, and the Japanese *Tegata-hō* that follows it [verify article numbers]. If it holds, abstraction is the refusal of evidence about reasons, the *causa*, made into the condition of negotiability. Whether it is structurally parallel to entity shielding, which partitions a pool from its members' histories, is unargued and belongs to [[MOC - Entity-shielding and corporate forms]].

## Ex ante, or it is not a test

A test chosen after the outcome can be fitted to it. *Antifragile* ch. 24 asks that an ethical rule be stated before the act, and treats casuistry as a free option ([[Taleb 2012 on antifragility - loci for the time-ensemble distinction]]). Formalism meets the requirement by construction: the form is fixed before anything happens, so it cannot be adjusted to what happened.

## Where this meets Taleb's ethics, and where it parts

Taleb wants two things: that agents bear the outcomes of their acts, and that they be judged by deeds, not words. The two coincide only where outcomes discriminate conduct. Where they do not, outcome-coupling still disciplines the incentive but stops being evidence (the qualification in [[Skin in the game]]), and judgement has to move to the form of the conduct. **Formalism is what "deeds, not words" becomes when the deed's result is noise.** The substitution of state-verification for outcome-inference ([[Institutions substitute state-verification for outcome-inference as ruin exposure rises]]) is the same move. What is verified there is a state of the world; what is verified here is compliance with a form.

## The cost

A formalism that refuses evidence of reasons cannot tell compliance in good faith from compliance that defeats the rule's purpose. The *heter ʿisqa* keeps the loss-sharing form and pays a coupon ([[The heter iska restores the fixed return by evidentiary barrier]]); the *ḥiyal* exploit the same opening in Islamic law. Robustness to argument is bought with exposure to drafting. The ritual case is in [[Ritual carries its function in its form and its circularity not in its content]].
