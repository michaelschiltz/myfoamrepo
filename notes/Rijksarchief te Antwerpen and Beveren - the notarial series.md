# Rijksarchief te Antwerpen and Beveren — the notarial series

Part of [[Rijksarchief in Belgie - the state archives network]].

**The custody trap for anyone arriving from the Dutch material.** In the Netherlands the great commercial notariate is municipal — Amsterdam's notarial archive is at the city archive, and it is the transcribed corpus described at [[Stadsarchief Amsterdam - the notarial archive]]. **In Belgium the old notarial protocols are largely with the state archives**, not with the city archive, so a search of [[FelixArchief - Antwerp city archive]] for Antwerp's notarial instruments will return the wrong answer confidently.

## What is here

Old notarial protocols for the Antwerp region, and the judicial archives of the ancien-régime jurisdictions. The Beveren repository holds the bulk of the Antwerp province's transferred series. ⚠️ Reference codes, date ranges, extent and imaging all unestablished — this note records a custody fact and nothing more.

## Why the distinction is worth a note of its own

Because the census's Antwerp rows need instruments, not more commentary — the single-witness problem set out at [[FelixArchief - Antwerp city archive]] — and **the instruments are in a different building from the firm archives**. A trip planned for the Insolvente Boedelskamer that assumes the notarial protocols are in the same reading room will lose days.

The general rule this is an instance of: **custody conventions do not cross borders, and the Low Countries are the place that most invites the assumption that they do.** Same language, adjacent cities, one legal tradition until 1585 — and a different answer to where the notary's papers ended up.
