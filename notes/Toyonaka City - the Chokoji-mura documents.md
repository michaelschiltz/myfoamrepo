# Toyonaka City — the Chōkōji-mura documents

長興寺村文書付大伊勢講文書, owned by **Toyonaka City**, Osaka Prefecture; municipal designated cultural property since 1 September 1987.

**4,619 items, early-modern to early Shōwa**: the village administration's papers of Chōkōji, a Hoshina (Iino domain) holding in Toyoshima district, Settsu, with the records of its **Ō-Ise-kō** pilgrimage association.

## The survival rule is an accident, and it is written down

**The documents were found in the ceiling of the old village assembly hall when it was demolished.** Nobody deposited them; nobody catalogued them in place; they survived because a roof was not opened. **The purest instance in the survey of a survival rule with no rule in it.**

## Why the census needs this note

**Three census rows come from this village**: `ko_daikokuya_1848`, `ko_gotensama_1843` and `ko_choko_1854`, the three successive domain-ordered *tanomoshi* coded from Kobayashi 1986, who worked from their *ginshūchō* subscription ledgers. **No *shihōsho* rulebook survives** — the census's own `key_source` caps the procedural cells for that reason — **and this is presumably the fonds in which one would be looked for.** ⚠️ That Kobayashi's ledgers are in this fonds is inferred from village, date and subject and not confirmed from his apparatus. *[Checked 2026-10-21: Kobayashi names no custodian — the ledger is 飯野藩領長興寺村にたまたま残された (Kobayashi 1986, 296) — and cites only 『豊中市史』 vol. 2. The city's catalogue puts the 長興寺村文書 (4,500 items) and the 大伊勢講文書 (119) at the 岡町図書館. Among the first 500 entries of the 長興寺村文書 catalogue, 図-2-266 (B土地-その他-6-1), a 嘉永5年「当子年持高見帳」, is annotated 殿様頼母子: a document of the 御殿様 kō is in this fonds. The *ginshūchō* were not among those 500 entries, and the rest of the catalogue was not read. Placement probable, not confirmed.]* *[Confirmed the same day, from the whole catalogue (4,014 numbered entries, read through pdf.js in Chrome). The ledgers are the series **L金融-頼母子-1 to 23, 図-2-3285 to 3307**: 御殿様御頼母子銀寄帳 天保14 (1843) and 銀集帳 for 天保15 to 嘉永5, every year but 嘉永4; 大黒屋善兵衛頼母子覚 弘化5 (1848, 2 通) and 銀集帳 / 掛銀集帳 嘉永1 to 安政2, three of them catalogued as 大里屋; 御役所御講掛銀集帳 安政3; 頼母子係歩勘定 安政3; 惣頼母子孫歩勘定帳, 割付 天保13年12月26日 and running 天保14 to 安政4年12月; an untitled 〔頼母子講〕 安政4; and 御講七拾貫目建弐分銀村持高割符帳 安政5.]*

**And the Ise-*kō* is a second association in the same village and the same fonds.** A pilgrimage *kō* is a pooling association with a different purpose, and the three coded rows share about a hundred members with each other. **A census that codes three domain *kō* from one village without looking at the village's other association is reading one ledger series of a richer record.** ⚠️ Not a candidate: nothing has been read on it. *[Added 2026-10-21: the 大伊勢講文書 catalogue lists 図-3-3, a 天保6年 (1835)「頼母子目録」 with 親 文右衛門 — a credit kō with a named 親, eight years older than the earliest coded Chōkōji association. Whether its members are the same hundred households is not known.]*

⚠️ Catalogue, access and imaging not established; no digital publication found. *[Corrected 2026-10-21: both catalogues are online as PDFs on the city's 古文書紹介 page (`choukoujimuramonjo.pdf`, `ooisekoumonjo.pdf`), which warns that a listed item may still be withheld from viewing. Imaging not established.]*

## What the catalogue adds to the three coded rows

**Three things, none of which has been read, and none of which may move a cell from a catalogue title.**

- **The third association's convener may be named.** The series carries two ledgers titled 大里屋善兵衛頼母子掛銀集帳, for 嘉永7 (1854) and 安政2 (1855) — 図-2-3301 and 3302. 嘉永7 became 安政元 in the eleventh month, and 安政元 is the year Kobayashi starts his 第三次 kō, whose convener he does not name. 大里屋 is presumably a catalogue misreading of 大黒屋: the series spells 大黒屋 six times and 大里屋 three, and the third 大里屋 is the 嘉永1 ledger, the first year of Kobayashi's 大黒屋 kō. Kobayashi says the 大黒屋 record stops at the ninth round (嘉永6) and that a new kō began the next year (299). If the two later ledgers belong to that new kō, then `ko_choko_1854`'s `GV1=.NR` and its type-row warning that 大黒屋 must not be read into it rest on Kobayashi's silence, not on the record. **Read 図-2-3301 before anything is changed.**
- **Two summary accounts that Kobayashi does not cite.** 頼母子係歩勘定 and 惣頼母子孫歩勘定帳 (both 安政3, the second running 天保14 to 安政4) look like reckonings across the village's kō as a whole. Kobayashi says that only *ginshūchō* survive and that no 議定 or 仕法書 does; he does not mention these. They are where to look for whether anything was ever repaid, which is the question behind his 献金講 reading and behind all three rows' `MC1=.NR`.
- **The village carried more domain kō than the census codes.** The adjacent series L金融-拝借 (領主拝借) holds 御役所御講(掛)銀集帳 for 嘉永7 to 安政4, a 新御講 of 安政5 (高割 and 家別), a 子年御発起御講 of 安政6 / 万延1, and 一番 and 二番御積金講 of 文久1 (1861), assessed separately on landholders and on the landless; with 御殿様 loans and 口入金 accounts to 明治4. **The three coded rows are the first three of a series of at least seven levies on one roll.** They add nothing independent, but they would show whether the two-part tariff and the landless strata persist.
