# Toyonaka City — the Chōkōji-mura documents

長興寺村文書付大伊勢講文書, owned by **Toyonaka City**, Osaka Prefecture; municipal designated cultural property since 1 September 1987.

**4,619 items, early-modern to early Shōwa**: the village administration's papers of Chōkōji, a Hoshina (Iino domain) holding in Toyoshima district, Settsu, with the records of its **Ō-Ise-kō** pilgrimage association.

## The survival rule is an accident, and it is written down

**The documents were found in the ceiling of the old village assembly hall when it was demolished.** Nobody deposited them; nobody catalogued them in place; they survived because a roof was not opened. **The purest instance in the survey of a survival rule with no rule in it.**

## Why the census needs this note

**Three census rows come from this village**: `ko_daikokuya_1848`, `ko_gotensama_1843` and `ko_choko_1854`, the three successive domain-ordered *tanomoshi* coded from Kobayashi 1986, who worked from their *ginshūchō* subscription ledgers. **No *shihōsho* rulebook survives** — the census's own `key_source` caps the procedural cells for that reason — **and this is presumably the fonds in which one would be looked for.** ⚠️ That Kobayashi's ledgers are in this fonds is inferred from village, date and subject and not confirmed from his apparatus.

**And the Ise-*kō* is a second association in the same village and the same fonds.** A pilgrimage *kō* is a pooling association with a different purpose, and the three coded rows share about a hundred members with each other. **A census that codes three domain *kō* from one village without looking at the village's other association is reading one ledger series of a richer record.** ⚠️ Not a candidate: nothing has been read on it.

⚠️ Catalogue, access and imaging not established; no digital publication found.
