# Arxiu Historic de Protocols de Barcelona

<https://www.colegionotarial.org/ca/ahpb/> · digital resource *Aurora*: <https://arxiu.colegionotarial.org/>

**Held by the Col·legi de Notaris de Catalunya — a professional corporation, not a public archive.** It is consequently absent from [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]] and from most state-level finding aids, which is the single most common reason researchers miss it.

## Why it is the most valuable Spanish repository for the commenda coding

It is described as the largest historical notarial archive in the world, running continuously from the thirteenth century. The Catalan *comanda*, the *societat*, and the earliest western marine insurance policies are notarial instruments, and this is where they are. For a coding scheme built on drafted clauses, an archive of clauses is worth more than an archive of institutions.

## The digital layer — *Aurora*

<https://arxiu.colegionotarial.org/>

**Layer 2, confirmed: images only, no transcription.** Two search modes, a dynamic filter over the database table and a form-based search. Registration is required for note-taking, annotation and downloads; consultation appears open.

⚠️ Still unverified: how many protocols are imaged, and over what date range. Neither the platform's landing page nor the Col·legi's AHPB page states either, and the answer decides whether a remote workflow is possible at all. **This is a phone call, not a search** — the archive publishes a contact number and address for exactly this.

## The licence is the constraint, and it is restrictive

The stated terms confine use to **research and teaching, with attribution and without modification**; anything else requires the express authorisation of the Col·legi de Notaris de Catalunya.

Two consequences, both operational.

First, **the project cannot openly release images from this archive**, and the data management plan's separation of layers is what saves the situation: the project's own transcriptions, translations and codings are original works it may license openly even where the source image may not be redistributed. Record the restriction in the register's rights column before any image enters a working set, not after.

Second, *without modification* is not an idle clause for a project that will crop, deskew and binarise images to feed an HTR model. ⚠️ Establish whether derivative processing for machine reading counts as modification under these terms. If it does, the corpus can be read but not processed, and that is a different resource.

## What the printed tradition already gives you

Capmany's *Memorias historicas sobre la marina, comercio y artes de la antigua ciudad de Barcelona* printed a large body of these instruments in the eighteenth century, and much of the classic Catalan commercial-law literature quotes from this archive. ⚠️ The printed corpus is a selection made on nineteenth-century antiquarian criteria and should be treated as such — it is a filter, in the sense [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] insists on, not a sample.

## Access on site

⚠️ Reader's regime, appointment lead time and photography permission for the physical archive are still unestablished, and a corporation-held archive is under no obligation to match public-archive practice. Ask when asking about the *Aurora* coverage — one enquiry, both answers.
