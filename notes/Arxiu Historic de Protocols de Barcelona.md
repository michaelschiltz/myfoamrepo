# Arxiu Historic de Protocols de Barcelona

<https://www.colegionotarial.org/ca/ahpb/> · digital resource *Aurora*: <https://arxiu.colegionotarial.org/>

**Held by the Col·legi de Notaris de Catalunya — a professional corporation, not a public archive.** It is consequently absent from [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]] and from most state-level finding aids, which is the single most common reason researchers miss it.

## Why it is the most valuable Spanish repository for the commenda coding

It is described as the largest historical notarial archive in the world, running continuously from the thirteenth century. The Catalan *comanda*, the *societat*, and the earliest western marine insurance policies are notarial instruments, and this is where they are. For a coding scheme built on drafted clauses, an archive of clauses is worth more than an archive of institutions.

## The digital layer

*Aurora* has put thousands of medieval documents online. ⚠️ Coverage, date range and whether the images are accompanied by any transcription are unverified — establish before assuming a remote workflow. Working assumption: **layer 2**, images without text.

## What the printed tradition already gives you

Capmany's *Memorias historicas sobre la marina, comercio y artes de la antigua ciudad de Barcelona* printed a large body of these instruments in the eighteenth century, and much of the classic Catalan commercial-law literature quotes from this archive. ⚠️ The printed corpus is a selection made on nineteenth-century antiquarian criteria and should be treated as such — it is a filter, in the sense [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] insists on, not a sample.

## Access

⚠️ Access conditions for a corporation-held archive are not the same as for a public one. Establish the reader's regime, appointment lead time, photography permission and reproduction rights before costing a trip. This is precisely the row of the register that will be non-standard.
