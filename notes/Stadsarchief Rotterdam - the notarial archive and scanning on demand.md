# Stadsarchief Rotterdam — the notarial archive and scanning on demand

<https://stadsarchief.rotterdam.nl/>

The oldest municipal archive in the Netherlands, and the second maritime notariate after Amsterdam.

## The notarial series

Toegang **`18`, *Notarissen te Rotterdam en daarin opgegane gemeenten* (ONA)**, with separate old notarial archives for the absorbed municipalities (for example `5044`, Rozenburg). ⚠️ Date ranges and extent to transcribe.

Rotterdam's commercial notariate is a genuine second site for the instruments — bottomry, insurance, ship part-ownership — and it matters for a reason that is methodological rather than quantitative: **a form attested in two independent notarial practices is a form, and one attested in Amsterdam alone might be an Amsterdam drafting habit.** The census's own worry about local names collecting on a migrated institution cuts both ways, and Rotterdam is the cheap test.

## Scanning on demand, and it is free

The archive digitises on request, **without charge**: up to **three items per day per person**, nothing larger than A3, nothing under a hundred years old, excluding charters, copyrighted photographs, films and privately-owned collections. Scans are **published on the website within about a month** and are thereafter public.

**This changes what a want-list is for.** Under [[MOC - Acquisitions and the antiquarian trade]] the commissioned archival scan is a per-page, institution-priced route with weeks-to-months latency; here it is free, the latency is a month, and the result is public rather than private. A well-specified request list is worth more here than a trip, and three a day compounds.

⚠️ The hundred-year rule is not a constraint for this project. The A3 limit may be: notarial protocol volumes can exceed it, and whether a bound volume can be requested at all, as against a single act, is unestablished and decides whether this route reaches the series that matter.
