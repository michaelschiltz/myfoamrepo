# Ba County archive — Sichuan Provincial Archives

巴县档案, held at the **Sichuan Provincial Archives**, Chengdu.

**The archive of the county yamen of Ba, the county seat of Chongqing** — the largest and best-known surviving Qing county archive, running from the eighteenth century to the end of the dynasty. The subject of a funded national project for its organisation and study. ⚠️ Counts of files and documents not established in this pass; the widely cited figure of about 113,000 files is unverified here.

## Why it matters, and why it is dangerous

**Most Qing county yamen archives did not survive.** A handful did — Ba, Nanbu (Sichuan), Baodi (Zhili), and Danxin in Taiwan — see [[Danxin archives - National Taiwan University]]. **So Ba stands for the Qing county, and that is a selection problem before it is a source.** The Dubrovnik lesson, inverted: at Dubrovnik a whole republic survived; here **one commercial river-port county survived and the literature on Qing local commerce leans on it**.

**For the census: Chongqing is where its one parked Chinese entity fact comes from.** `hegu_gangu` — the dry share — rests on a single instance at the **Ruifeng ironworks outside Chongqing** (Zelin 2019, 346). ⚠️ Whether that instance comes from the Ba County archive not confirmed from Zelin's apparatus. **The row's condition for coding — a second instance — should be sought outside Ba first**, or the second instance will share the first's survival rule.
