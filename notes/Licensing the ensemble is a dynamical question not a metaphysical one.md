# Licensing the ensemble is a dynamical question not a metaphysical one

The ensemble average is usually defended or attacked on general grounds — either probability is simply the right language for an uncertain world, or it is a fiction about populations that do not exist. Both moves are errors of the same kind. Whether an ensemble is a legitimate object is decided by the dynamics of the specific system, case by case, and is licensable in principle either way.

Turbulence supplies the one clean case where the licence is granted, and it shows what granting costs. Under **spontaneous stochasticity**, the Lagrangian trajectory measure in the vanishing-viscosity limit stays non-degenerate after the initial uncertainty has been taken to zero: fix a single, fully specified deterministic initial condition and the distribution over paths does not collapse onto it. The ensemble is then not a statement about a hypothetical population of systems — it is what the one actual system does. That is as strong a licence as an ensemble can receive.

Note what issues it. Not indeterminism: the underlying dynamics is Newtonian throughout, and the derivation chain that terminates in Navier–Stokes begins from hard spheres obeying Newton's laws (Deng, Hani and Ma 2025). Not ignorance either, since the randomness survives the limit of perfect initial information. What issues the licence is a structural property — in the inviscid limit the velocity field is Hölder rather than Lipschitz, uniqueness of trajectories fails, and infinitesimal separations amplify to order one in finite time. A theorem about the regularity of a field, not a thesis about chance.

Which cuts hard the other way for everything else. Non-Lipschitz amplification is a rare and demanding condition, and the default case is the one this project works in: an ensemble average is a statement about a population that does not exist, and the burden lies on whoever invokes it to name the property licensing the substitution. [[Ergodicity is indecomposability not randomness]] gives the standard licensing condition and what claiming it commits you to; [[An absorbing barrier breaks ergodicity by construction]] gives the standard failure. What the turbulence case adds is that the condition need not be ergodicity — there is at least one other route to a licensed ensemble — and that the route is always a named dynamical property, never a general appeal to the reality of chance.

This is the hygiene demand of [[Route Kolmogorov to ergodic theory not the theory of means]] in its strongest form, and it disposes of the obvious counter to the project: that the critique of ensemble reasoning rests on a philosophical preference for individual trajectories. It does not. It demands that the licence be named, and concedes in advance that in some systems it can be. The demand is falsifiable system by system, which the metaphysical claim in either direction is not.

Why the metaphysics cannot settle it: Kolmogorov's axioms are neutral between ontic chance, probability manufactured by deterministic dynamics (Poincaré's roulette wheel, and the method of arbitrary functions after it), and degree of belief. Nothing done inside the formalism adjudicates between them, which is precisely why the adjudication has to come from the dynamics.
