# The tontine's chronology defeats the hazard-to-pricing staging

The attractive move is to stage the tontine as the hinge between an unquantified conception of peril and a priced one — the aleatory instrument that matures into the actuarial. It does not survive the dates. Tonti proposed to Mazarin in 1653: before Pascal–Fermat, eighteen years before de Witt's *Waardije van Lyf-renten naer Proportie van Los-renten* (1671), forty before Halley's Breslau table (1693). The tontine is a *pre*-quantification instrument, and quantification abandoned it rather than matured it.

The English case supplies the numbers. The Million Act (5 & 6 Will. & Mar. c. 5, 1693) sought £1,000,000; the tontine limb took £108,100, roughly a tenth, while the plain single-life annuities offered alongside and subscribed from February 1694 took £118,506 at fourteen years' purchase. Halley advised against the tontine. The last major British issue was 1789. Verify both figures against Weir (1989) and Milevsky (2015) before they carry weight in print — they are from the secondary literature surveyed August 2026, not from the Exchequer records.

**The instruments supplied the data that displaced them.** Deparcieux, in 1746, derived the survival chances of each age class *by observing the royal tontines of 1689 and 1696* — the subscriber registers were the mortality evidence. So the tontine is not merely prior to actuarial technique; it is one of the observational substrates from which that technique was built, and it became unsellable once the technique existed. A staging account has the causality backwards twice over: quantification did not mature the instrument, and the instrument was not waiting on quantification — it was supplying it.

The instrument that carries the pricing story is the ordinary life annuity. De Witt's tract is a comparative valuation of *lyfrenten* against *losrenten*, prompted by an arbitrage: Holland sold life annuities at a flat fourteen years' purchase irrespective of the nominee's age, and was duly exploited by parents nominating healthy children. Hudde supplied the Amsterdam registers, Halley the survival function and the summed product of survival probability and discount factor. That is where peril becomes a number, and the tontine contributes nothing to it.

Two cautions on the framing itself. The rupture between an unquantified and a quantified conception is contested — Ceccarelli shows canonist reasoning already pricing *periculum* through *aequitas* in aleatory contracts — so staging the tontine as the hinge imports the whole ascent apparatus with none of its qualifications. See [[Whiggish history of finance names the ascent narrative]] and [[Discipline the Whig epithet by pinning it to teleology and survivorship]]. And Goetzmann's own arrow runs the other way: finance as a time machine, the probabilistic apparatus arriving to solve a valuation problem the market had already posed. Using *Money Changes Everything* as the backbone of an epistemic-sequence argument uses him against himself.

The tontine's value is not as a stage. It is the negative control — see [[The tontine is pooling in form and dispersal in function]] — and its abandonment is the retention datum, provided the abandonment is read as selection rather than as progress ([[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]]).

## A second defeat, from a different kind of evidence

The tontine's chronology works from the dating of an instrument. A second case works from the juristic record of a concept and its prohibition: a payment conceived purely as consideration for bearing peril is documented at the beginning of the thirteenth century, suppressed by *Naviganti*, and legitimated only in the fourteenth. Both defeats point the same way, and because they run on unrelated evidence — instrument dating against doctrinal debate — the second is not a repetition of the first.

Two independent chronological defeats shift the burden. The staging now needs a positive argument that the conception, rather than its permissibility, is what moved. See [[Naviganti delayed the priced premium it is credited with dating]].
