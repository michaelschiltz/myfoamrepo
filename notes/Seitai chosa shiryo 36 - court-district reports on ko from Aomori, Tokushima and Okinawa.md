# 『世態調査資料』36 — court-district reports on kō from Aomori, Tokushima and Okinawa

司法省調査部『世態調査資料』第三十六號 (c. 1942; the series runs 1938–1943 in 41 numbers), **NDL Digital pid 1909099, transmission service only (送信サービス): readable under MS's NDL login, and the IIIF image service refuses it.** 109 frames. Three reports from court districts, none in the four prefectures of [[Norinsho 1938 - forty-three ko rulebooks in print]].

## What it is

From the table of contents:

- **Aomori: 青森縣下に於ける小口金融機關特に十錢講及賴母子講に就て (附、方言の研究)**, pp. 1–112. Parts on the 十錢講 (its officers, general meeting and official supervision), on the 賴母子講 (names, meetings, bids, 借用證書, abuses, customs, terms), on crimes and countermeasures, a dialect re-enactment of a kō meeting, and an appendix: 金融概況, **講規約及證書 (十錢講、賴母子講、和合講等) pp. 73–82**, statistics of civil and criminal cases, a glossary, a dialect list.
- **Tokushima: 德島縣下に於ける賴母子講の特異性に就て**, pp. 113–162. A lecture on the organisation of kō and the 講手形, a round-table (南方の状況, 講抵當の登記, **發起人の地位**, 小手形, **講の取締狀況**, 講の整理, 賴母子講と無盡業法, **講役員の責任**), and an appendix: **講則 (イ)(ロ)(ハ) p. 146**, 講通帳, 大手形, 小手形, **講會取締規則 p. 158**, a glossary.
- **Okinawa: 沖繩縣下に於ける舊慣模合に就て**, pp. 163–: origins, development, decline, kinds, organisation, **講元の責任**, and the author's view.

**The rulebooks.** Aomori 其ノ一–其ノ十: two 十錢講 and 信用會 rules, a 頼母子講規約, three 和合講規約 (one of them 龍川寺, 三戸町), a ため講貯蓄規約 (德下村), and three bonds and receipts. Tokushima (イ)(ロ)(ハ): a 救濟頼母子講, a 積立講 and a temple kō (淨智寺明照講), all recorded as 德島市.

## Why it matters

- **A second selector**: court officials reporting on their districts, not a finance or agriculture ministry. Used, with [[Okurasho 1915 - a Finance Ministry survey of mujin with eleven rulebooks]], for the `RD-test` re-test ([[RD-test coding and re-test session 2026-10-22]]).
- **It holds a prefectural regulation** (Tokushima's 講會取締規則) and a round-table on officers' and organisers' liability, the material [[Where ko rules name a bearer beyond the drawer's security it follows the drafting regime]] needs to test its regime claim. Neither has been read.
- **The Okinawa report is the only 模合 account found in print at NDL**; 模合 is the southern name of the same institution.

## Cautions

- **Spent:** Aomori 其ノ三–其ノ七 and Tokushima (イ)(ロ)(ハ) were read by the re-test's coder; the operator saw headings of the Aomori appendix and pp. 140–149 of the Tokushima report at low resolution.
- **Unread and still blind:** the three reports' text and round-tables, the two 十錢講 rules, the Tokushima regulation and the Okinawa report.
- **Selection unstated.** The reports do not say how their rulebooks were chosen.
- **The other forty numbers of the series** have not been listed; others may carry regional kō reports.
