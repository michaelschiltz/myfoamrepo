# Archivio Storico del Banco di Napoli

<https://www.fondazionebanconapoli.it/archivio/en/>

Held by the **Fondazione Banco di Napoli**, not by the state — so it is described in SIUSA and absent from SIAS. See [[SIAS SIUSA and SAN - the Italian access layers]].

## Why it is exceptional

The Neapolitan public banks grew out of sixteenth-century charitable institutions — *monti di pietà* and confraternity foundations — and became deposit banks issuing *fedi di credito* and *polizze* that circulated as money. **A pooling form that became a monetary institution** is a trajectory the project has no other clean instance of.

The **Fondo Apodissario** holds the banks' *apodisse* — the payment instruments and their supporting registers — and is recognised as **UNESCO Memory of the World**. The scale is the point: every payment carried a written *causale*, a statement of what the payment was for, so the fondo is simultaneously a financial series and a description of the transactions behind it.

⚠️ Volume, date range and the series structure need transcribing from the published *Guida ai fondi dell'archivio storico del Banco di Napoli*; secondary descriptions circulate figures that were not verified in this pass.

## Access

Consultation is through the Fondazione's own reading room. An **on-demand commissioned-research service** was launched in 2025 ⚠️ — establish its terms, because for a foreign project commissioned search against a fondo of this size may be cheaper than a trip.

That makes this a decision of the kind [[MOC - Acquisitions and the antiquarian trade]] frames: commissioned surrogate versus travel, with the balance turning on how well the request can be specified in advance.

## The comparative point

The Neapolitan banks, the Genoese *Casa di San Giorgio* and the Roman *monti* are three Italian cases of a charitable or fiscal pool acquiring corporate durability, under three different authorities. Whether that is a family or a coincidence is exactly the question the coding exists to answer, and Naples is the leg currently missing.
