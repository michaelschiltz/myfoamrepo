# The general-particular boundary is unverifiable and its leakage subsidises sailing

Everything in general average turns on whether a loss was deliberate. Voluntary sacrifice for the common safety is shared; accidental damage falls on its owner under *casum sentit dominum*. The whole of the Rhodian law, on Addobbati's reading, rests on this line between human causality and external randomness.

**The line cannot be drawn from the evidence.** Choice "has the defect of being an internal act, at best only hinted at by the concrete evidence," and establishing it "is necessarily an act of faith, because in the end the only custodian of truth is the master who makes the damage declaration." The party with the incentive to misreport is the sole witness to the fact that decides whether he is reimbursed. Addobbati's verdict on the boundary is that it remains "artificial and uncertain" in practice "despite all principles and distinctions of law."

It is nevertheless maintained, and deliberately — "in order to strengthen the seafarers' sense of responsibility and to limit so-called moral hazard." So the doctrine holds a distinction it knows to be unenforceable because the *existence* of the line does incentive work that its *enforcement* cannot.

**The interesting part is the next sentence, and it inverts the usual reading of fraud.** Addobbati: "a certain tolerance of abuses works as a tacit incentive to sail, especially in a context of extreme insecurity and uncertainty." The leakage is not a defect the institution failed to close. Over-generous sharing lowers the ruin exposure of undertaking the voyage at all, and a system that adjudicated deliberateness strictly would push that exposure back onto the individual venturer — where, under multiplicative dynamics near an absorbing barrier, it deters the venture. **Slack verification is a subsidy to risk-taking, delivered through the evidentiary standard rather than through the sharing rule.**

That is a design principle the project should be able to state generally: where a criterion is private to the party it governs, the institution's real parameter is not the criterion but the *tolerance* with which it is applied, and that tolerance is an instrument with its own optimum. Compare [[Scuttling rewards fraud in the sea loan]], where the same private-knowledge problem runs the other way — discharge on total loss rewards the borrower who sinks his own ship — and the doctrinal response was to tighten rather than to tolerate. Two instruments, one unobservable, opposite settings.

**Two consequences.** For coding: `VF1 loss verification mode` records *how* a loss is established and has no way to record how *strictly*, and strictness is where the action is. For the comparative argument: an institution that shares regardless of cause — *warichi* — never faces the trade-off at all, which is a genuine efficiency and not merely a difference of custom ([[General average shares decisions where warichi shares hazards]]).

A caution. Addobbati's tolerance claim is an interpretation of why abuses persisted, not a documented policy, and no adjudicator says "we allow this to encourage sailing." Treat it as his reading of the equilibrium rather than as evidence of intent.
