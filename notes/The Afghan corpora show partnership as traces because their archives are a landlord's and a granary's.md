# The Afghan corpora show partnership as traces because their archives are a landlord's and a granary's

**No partnership instrument survives in the medieval Afghan documentary corpora: no *sharika*, no *muḍāraba* or *qirāḍ*, no Hebrew-script deed of association, no *muzāraʿa*. Partnership is attested only as traces — a legal query that presupposes it, levy entries "with partner", fractional co-ownership, joint debtors.** The absence is a fact about what the find contexts could hold, not about what Bamiyan and Ghur practised. Reading it as absence of the institution is the deficit reading: [[The deficit reading of absence is the scalar ranking in evidentiary form]].

## What the archives are

- **Bamiyan Papers, Group I (395–430/1005–1039)**: the papers of one Jewish landlord and merchant, Abū Naṣr Yehuda b. Daniel, and his son Siman-Ṭov. *Iqrār* deeds of debts owed *to* him, Judaeo-Persian letters, account books (Ms. Heb. 8333.205–207, 8333.220), one lease of three parcels (8333.15, 430/1039). **A creditor's file** (Haim 2019, 72–73).
- **Bamiyan Papers, Group II (549–618/1155–1221)**: some thirty receipts issued by the scribe Abū Bakr b. ʿUmar for deliveries to the royal granary (*anbār-i khāṣṣa*), *iqrār*s, memoranda between diwan officials and *shiḥna*. **A fiscal file** (Haim 2019, 74–76; Ludden and Azad 2024).
- **Firuzkuh Papers (573–618/1177–1221)**: about a hundred documents in New Persian — petitions to landlords who are probably *iqṭāʿ* holders, levy lists, sales. **A seigneurial and fiscal file.**
- **Khalili Bactrian documents (629–771/772)**, list A–P: marriage contract, receipts, deed of gift, manumission, purchase or lease of estates, loans of grain and wine, undertakings to keep the peace, slave sale. No partnership. ⚠️ Q–Y not checked.

**None is a merchant's partnership file and none is a *qāḍī*'s register.** A partnership deed would survive here only if a partner kept the archive, or if the sheet were reused as scrap — the route by which Arabic-script documents survive in the Cairo Geniza ([[Cairo Geniza - the deposit and its survival rule]]). Reuse is common in Group II, so the second route is open. It has not yet produced a partnership deed.

## What the traces are

- **IEDCID1188** (Ms. Heb. 8333.102): the only item the Invisible East typology files as *Legal: Partnership*. An *istiftāʾ* on whether the other partners' testimony stands once one partner is a litigant. It presupposes a commercial partnership and is not one. Ed. Ofir Haim, unpublished. ⚠️ Read through a summary.
- **IEDCID248** (Firuzkuh 60) and other levy lists (IEDCID240, 264, 275, 1154): entries "with partner" (*sharīk*). ⚠️ Whether joint cultivation or joint liability for the levy is not established.
- **IEDCID260** (Firuzkuh 14, 1169): a widow sells her half of a yearling bull to her co-owner. **IEDCID111/112** (Firuzkuh 7–8, 1195/1207): one-sixth of a bathhouse sold, then a debt secured on the share. ⚠️ Read through summaries.
- **Ms. Heb. 8333.216** (401/1011; two debtors; 7 *shiyānī*) and **8333.217** (409/1018; three debtors; 75 *shiyānī*): several debtors in one *iqrār* (Haim 2019, 78). ⚠️ Joint or several liability is for the edition to settle.
- **IEDCID1281** (BL Or.8212/166; eighth century; Judaeo-Persian; Khotan region): "his three shares", "my profit and loss", "a heavy loss". The clearest joint venture in the corpus, and not from Afghanistan. Zhang Zhan, *Bulletin of the Asia Institute* 31 (2023): 105–33. ⚠️ Read through a summary.

**Azad states the negative for tenancy herself**: the corpus "is devoid of such contracts", and lacks land deeds, cadastral surveys and tax-rate inventories (Ludden and Azad 2024, PDF pp. 12, 17).

## The same shape further east

**The Old Uyghur contracts are typed Sa, Lo, Pl, Ad — sale, loan, pledge, adoption** ([[Old Uyghur contracts - the documentary layer under the ortoq]]). ⚠️ Whether any SUK type is a trading partnership is still unestablished; the check is the vol. 2 type table and the glossary under *ortaq*. **The *ortoq* itself rests on chronicles**: Enkhbold 2019 reads Juvaynī, Rashīd al-Dīn, Vaṣṣāf and the Song envoys Peng Daya and Xu Ting, not documents ([[The Silk Road record is documents and the census codes chronicles]]).

**From Bactria to Turfan the documentary record is sale, loan, lease, pledge and receipt; partnership lives in law and in narrative.** The asymmetry recurs because the archives that were kept are households', landlords' and treasuries', and it is a property of the filter.

## Consequence

- **No *commenda* or *qirāḍ* coding can be drawn from these corpora**, and none should be promised in Part 1 or Part 2.
- What they can supply is the subject of [[The Afghan corpora offer loss allocation in tenancy not commenda]].
- The negative is a datum for [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]]: a filter whose composition is known — a creditor's file, a granary's file — predicts which genres are missing.
