# Non-ergodicity collapses the likelihood ratio on outcomes

After a bad outcome the principal must separate two states: nature took the capital, or the agent did. The outcome's value as a signal is the likelihood ratio — how much more probable a bad result is under misconduct than under honesty.

Under additive dynamics with modest variance, honest failure is rare. The ratio is far from one, and a bad outcome is strong evidence of misconduct. Outcome-inference discriminates, and every mechanism built on it — reputation, entrustment, guild sanction, ostracism — works.

**Under multiplicative dynamics with an absorbing barrier, honest failure is a normal feature of the honest trajectory.** Ruin is not the exception the mechanism was designed around; it is what typical trajectories do. The likelihood ratio collapses toward one and the outcome stops discriminating. Every mechanism that reads conduct off results degrades in proportion.

The claim is about the *signal structure the dynamics generate*, not about preferences. This is where the framework separates cleanly from expected utility: concave preferences change what an agent is willing to undertake, and say nothing whatever about how informative a result is about his conduct. Non-ergodicity is what makes honest ruin common, and common honest ruin is what destroys the evidentiary value of outcomes.

Absorption does the damage twice over. It makes bad outcomes frequent, and it destroys the evidence — the cargo is on the sea floor, and there is nothing left to inspect. Hence [[Scuttling rewards fraud in the sea loan]] is the limiting case rather than a curiosity: a contract that pins payoff to an unverifiable ruin event does not merely fail to discriminate, it *pays* for the mimicry of ruin.

## A boundary condition on outcome-coupling

This qualifies [[Outcome-coupling is the condition of legibility]], and the qualification should be carried rather than buried. Coupling makes performance legible **only where outcomes discriminate**. In a ruin-dominated venture, coupling couples the agent to noise: the settlement is still jointly observed and unambiguous as an *event*, but it is uninformative as a *signal about conduct*, because the honest and the dishonest trajectory produce the same reckoning at rates that are too close to tell apart.

So the epistemic defence of *al-ghunm bi-l-ghurm* holds in moderate-variance ventures and weakens exactly where the risk-sharing rationale is strongest. That tension is real, it is interesting, and it is better stated by us than discovered by a referee.
