# Ritual carries its function in its form and its circularity not in its content

**Where ritual has a function, the function lies in its form and in the circularity of its justification, never in its content. A practice justified by an external reason is exposed to the refutation of that reason, and to the argument that the reason does not apply in this case. A practice that is its own reason is exposed to neither. And its content must be arbitrary for compliance to carry information. That gives MS's intuition, that ritual, adornment, formalism and dietary prescription belong with heuristics and the primacy of process, a defensible form. It refuses the functionalist form Taleb gives it.**

## Why the circle is necessary

The circle: the practice's warrant points back at the practice. It is done because it is what is done, and valid because it is in form. Each of three properties follows from the circle, and an external justification would forfeit each.

1. **Robustness to theory change.** *Antifragile* ch. 21 ("Plead Ignorance of Biology: Phenomenology"): explanations of an effect come and go while the effect stays, so practice should be kept robust to changes in theory. A rule justified by hygiene falls when the hygiene theory falls. A rule justified by itself carries no theory to refute. **The circularity is not a defect of the justification. It is the removal of the justification's fragility.** A self-grounding rule is a policy with the belief detached, which is [[Rationality is a property of the policy not of the belief that produces it]] built into an institution.
2. **Closure of the casuist's option.** *Antifragile* ch. 24 treats casuistry as optionality: a rule fitted after the act is a free option ([[Taleb 2012 on antifragility - loci for the time-ensemble distinction]]). A rule with an external reason invites the plea that the reason fails here, so the agent holds an option to exempt himself whenever exemption pays. A rule that is its own reason gives that plea nothing to work on. The circle binds where reasons bend.
3. **Arbitrary content, so that compliance carries information.** If the content were functionally optimal, everyone would adopt it for its function. Compliance would then mark no one, and the content would change whenever the function did. Only an arbitrary prescription makes compliance informative, as commitment (the entry fee of *Skin in the Game* ch. 16) or as membership (ch. 18: those who eat together hang together). **The function is in the form; the content is arbitrary.**

## Consequences for historical reading

- **The content of a ritual is an arbitrary particular** in the sense of [[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]]: free, therefore evidential for descent and independence, and therefore not to be explained functionally. Taleb's own case shows it. Orthodox Lent bans fish and allows shellfish, and he explains the line by luxury status (*Antifragile* ch. 22, "Convexity Effects and Random Nutrition"). That is a historical explanation, not a functional one, and the right kind.
- **Adornment has the same structure.** Entasis (*Skin in the Game* ch. 18, "Ocular Deception") is the limiting case, ornament with a function of content, the correction of perception. Taleb uses it to license belief. The ordinary case of ornament is cost made visible, as in ch. 16, and its content is free.
- **Formal validity in law is ritual of the same structure.** It refuses evidence of results and of reasons: [[Formalism refuses evidence of results and of reasons because neither discriminates]].
- **Unconditional statement is also what transmits.** The circle is the limiting case of the binary, unconditional rule that survives copying ([[A heuristic is defended by its error distribution not by its accuracy]], third argument).

## The price of the circle

- **Exposure to drafting.** A practice that will not hear its reasons cannot tell compliance in good faith from compliance that defeats its purpose. The *heter ʿisqa* keeps the loss-sharing form and pays a coupon: what persisted was the label, not the function ([[The heter iska restores the fixed return by evidentiary barrier]], [[The isqa is Lindy because captive not because robust]]). The *ḥiyal* are the same exposure in Islamic law. Robustness to argument is bought with exposure to drafting.
- **Exposure to a changed world.** A self-grounding practice is robust to changes in theory and fragile to changes in environment. It cannot re-optimise, because it has no reason that a new state could engage. This is perpetuity by freezing ([[Perpetuity splits into adaptation and petrification]]): a bet that the world holds still.

## What is refused

Taleb's version is that a ritual has a function because the populations that kept it survived (*Skin in the Game* ch. 18, "The Nondecorative in the Decorative"). That reads the record backwards ([[Taleb reads survival as evidence about the survivor and we read it as evidence about the filter]]). It treats traditions as populations, which [[Never let the units slide from instruments to populations]] forbids. And it leaves the specificity of the content unexplained. The claim here is weaker and checkable: the form, which is unconditional, self-grounding, costly and arbitrary in content, has properties that can be stated before anyone looks at who survived.
