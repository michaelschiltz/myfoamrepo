# John Rylands Library — the Rylands Genizah Collection

University of Manchester. **Over 11,000 fragments**, tenth to nineteenth century, **bought from Moses Gaster in 1954**; Hebrew, Aramaic, Judaeo-Arabic. The library names "legal and administrative documents and the notebooks of merchants" among the contents.

## Layer

- **Catalogued and imaged 2003–2009** with Friedberg, Safra and AHRC funding; catalogue and high-definition images on the library's LUNA platform. **The imaging let Rylands fragments be joined to pieces in other collections.**
- ⚠️ IIIF, image rights and whether Ktiv carries the images not checked.

## The honest assessment

**Marginal for the census.** A collection bought in 1954 from a collector's holdings is a selection from a selection; its merchants' notebooks are worth a search, not a visit.
