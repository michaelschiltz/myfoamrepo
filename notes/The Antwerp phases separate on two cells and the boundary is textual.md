# The Antwerp phases separate on two cells and the boundary is textual

One coded form became two: `compagnie_antwerpen_1582`, from the *Costuymen Impressae*, and
`compagnie_antwerpen_1608`, from the *Compilatae*. Thirty-two cells each. **They differ on exactly
two** — `AP2` (`P` → `0`) and `FP4` (`0` → `P`) — and agree on the other thirty.

**The boundary between them is textual, not practical.** The Compilatae never displaced the
Impressae in Antwerp, which is why `FP4` is `P` and not `1`: the later compilation states a rule
that did not become the rule in force. The split therefore records **two readings of one city's
law**, made a generation apart by its own jurists, and not two regimes a merchant could have been
under.

## Why this is the compilation prohibition in the other direction

[[A shared compilation never licenses an inference to shared practice]] forbids inferring one
institution from one circulating text: the *Rôles d'Oléron* travelled and the ports decided
differently anyway. The prohibition is symmetrical, and here it runs the other way — **two texts do
not license an inference to two institutions.** A compilation boundary is a fact about jurists, and
coding it as a form boundary would assert that the form changed when what changed is the writing
about it.

What the two rows do buy is real, and it is smaller than a form: a *dated pair of readings* of the
same object, which is exactly the unit this project says it works in. **The unit that varies is the
drafting practice, not the jurisdiction and not the century** —
[[Never let the units slide from instruments to populations]].

## The declined third cell

A third separating cell was available at `FP3` under its identity-masking reading, and was
**refused**. The split had been ordered after the coding pass; taking `FP3` would have widened the
separation by a definitional choice made *after* the decision to separate, which manufactures
evidence for the split out of the act of splitting. Two cells stand because two cells were found.

That refusal is the discipline [[Count degrees of freedom not cells]] asks for, applied at the
moment it is least comfortable — when the extra cell would have made a thin result look thicker.
A pair separating on two of thirty-two is a weak separation honestly reported; a pair separating on
three, one of them chosen afterwards, is a stronger separation that means less.
