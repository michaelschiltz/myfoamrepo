# Liverpool Record Office and the Maritime Archives

Liverpool's city record office, and the Maritime Archives and Library at National Museums Liverpool.

## What is here

The eighteenth- and nineteenth-century Atlantic port: **shipping registers, merchant and shipowner papers, insurance and average material**, and the record of the slave trade at its largest English scale.

National Museums Liverpool publishes research guidance on **Lloyd's marine insurance records** specifically, which means the Liverpool end of that market is documented separately from the London end — see [[Lloyd's and the marine insurance records]].

## The point worth extracting

**Liverpool and Bristol are English ports whose merchant communities were not the City of London**, and the census's English rows are overwhelmingly metropolitan. `deed_of_settlement_company` and `chartered_corporation_england` are London forms read from London-centred sources.

**A provincial English commercial practice is the cheapest available test of whether those rows describe England or describe London** — the domestic version of the point made for Haarlem against Amsterdam at [[Noord-Hollands Archief - Haarlem]], and for Rotterdam at [[Stadsarchief Rotterdam - the notarial archive and scanning on demand]].
