# The Roman universitas makes the JSC case parallelism not convergence

Le Bris, Goetzmann & Pouget state the cladistic test correctly and then fail it on their own evidence. Their cases are **parallelism** — independent arrival from a *shared* ancestral condition — and the correct label inverts the conclusion they draw.

## Their criterion, their counter-evidence

Their definition is exact: convergent evolution is a trait "analogous to one possessed by other organisms that are not closely related, **in the sense that their common ancestor did not have the given trait**." Wings in birds, bats and insects; the latest common ancestor had none.

Their §VI.A, "A common Roman background," then shows that the rediscovered *corpus iuris civilis* supplied the *universitas*, and that the *universitas* already carried entity shielding, limited liability, delegated management and legal personality — read out of Ulpian and Gaius via Avi-Yonah (2005) and Michaud-Quantin (1970). The decisive sentence:

> "since a universitas was endowed with the various characteristics that now define legal personality, this also automatically applied to pariages. Thus, a pariage enjoyed the benefits of legal personality **without the need for any approbation** by the state or any other authority."

That is inheritance, asserted. They derive share equality and depersonalisation from *universitas* membership rules and perpetuity from the same source. **The three hardest components of the joint-stock company arrive pre-installed.** By their own criterion the common ancestor had the traits, so this is not convergence.

## How the contradiction stays out of sight

By reclassifying the substrate. The conclusion files "the presence of the Roman background" among **environmental features** conducive to the form, beside the business orientation of municipal elites.

A received body of law from which forms inherit their traits is not a selective pressure. It is an ancestral condition. Move it to the environment column and "convergence" survives; leave it where the evidence puts it and the word is wrong. Note that **`parallelism` and `homoplasy` appear nowhere in the paper** — they hold only convergence and divergence, so the category that fits their case has no name available in their scheme. See [[Homoplasy is the finding not the noise]].

## Why the label carries the argument

The two readings license different conclusions, and their headline is the one only convergence supports:

> "this business organization form has such strong internal logic ... that it **requires very little doctrinal or institutional precedent** as stimulus for its formation except well-defined property rights."

§VI.A says the reverse. The *pariage* obtained legal personality *for free because it was already treated as a universitas* — maximal doctrinal precedent, a fully-formed corporate law sitting in the water, not minimal. Parallelism licenses only the weaker and more interesting claim: **a shared inherited toolkit was activated independently under similar pressures.** That makes the precedent decisive rather than dispensable, and inverts the moral they draw against La Porta et al. on legal origins.

Their final paragraph almost catches it. Noting that the form did not emerge outside western Europe, they reach for nuclear-family structure — when the variable they have just documented at length is the Roman substrate, which is also absent outside western Europe.

## What this gives the project

**Their evidence, our label.** §VI.A is the best assembled case in print for a common western corporate-law substrate, and it was assembled by authors arguing the opposite. Citing their evidence while declining their term is far stronger than asserting the substrate ourselves — cf. [[The western family needs only plausible common descent not proven genealogy]].

**It confirms the intra-western comparison cannot bear the load.** Toulouse, Genoa, London and Amsterdam all draw on the same revived corpus. A convergence claim built on those four is the comparison [[The qirad's antecedents are Sasanian not Sogdian]] identifies as suspiciously close. The real independence test remains East Asian forms against the entire western cluster.

**It dissolves the Harris dispute rather than settling it.** Harris (2020) is their named opponent, arguing migration against convergence. If both paths descend from shared Roman corporate inheritance, neither hypothesis is under test: migration versus independent invention is a question about branches of one family, and no answer bears on whether the corporate form is a general attractor. [[Transmission versus independent recurrence is the load-bearing test]].

## A second, separable problem

They write that "an analogy can be made between organizations and organisms, the organizational traits being coded into legal rules as phenotypic traits are coded into genes." That is the slide [[Never let the units slide from instruments to populations]] exists to block, and it is worth objecting to separately rather than bundling it with the parallelism point — the two objections have different targets and different remedies.

## Verify

- Read against the **published** version, not only NBER WP 31821 (November 2023). The library holds both a 2025 and a 2026 record; §VI.A may have been revised.
- **The guild path's relation to the *universitas* is my inference, not their claim.** If guilds are themselves *universitates* in canon and Roman law, the northern path shares the ancestral condition too and the argument here becomes general rather than confined to the southern cases. That is the load-bearing step and it is not yet evidenced.
- They cite Malmendier (2009) on the *societates publicanorum* as a possible earlier emergence, and note it is contested (Dufour 2010). Worth knowing before leaning on Roman precedent.
- Their reading of Ulpian *Dig.* 3.4.7.1 and 3.4.2 comes through Avi-Yonah. Check against the Digest directly before quoting.
