# Archives départementales de la Seine-Maritime — Rouen

The third Atlantic port, and the one with the longest continuous commercial record: Rouen was a major trading city under the Plantagenets and remained one under the Bourbons.

## What is here

**Série `B`** — the *amirauté de Rouen* and the *Parlement de Normandie*, another sovereign court. **Série `E`** — the notaries, running deep.

Rouen also carries a distinct legal interest: **Normandy had its own customary law**, codified in the *Coutume de Normandie*, and the customary law of partnership and inheritance differs from the Parisian custom. **A form drafted under one custom and the same form drafted under another, inside one kingdom, is the cleanest available test of how much the local law shapes the instrument** — a smaller-scale version of what the project attempts across traditions, with the confounds removed.
