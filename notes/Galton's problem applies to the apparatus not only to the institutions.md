# Galton's problem applies to the apparatus not only to the institutions

The vault already holds the rule for institutions: persistence across instances is evidence of
robustness **only if the instances are independent**, and if they descend from one source by contact
the effective *n* is one — [[Transmission versus independent recurrence is the load-bearing test]],
[[Split provenance into priority and independence]]. **The same test has to be run on the sources,
and almost nothing runs it.** Two coding batches three days apart found two chains, both by counting
names.

**Televantos and Getzler & Macnair are not independent.** `Getzler` 19 hits, `Macnair` 10; four
substantive citations, one of them Televantos's own statement of the jingle rule — his n51, citing
them at 267 and 275–85 — which is **the rule `AP1` turns on**. Both also sit inside the
Hansmann–Kraakman–Squire framework `AP1` is worded in, Televantos explicitly. Getzler & Macnair in
turn depend on Harris 2000 for the assimilation of the deed of settlement company to partnership.

**The two Harrises are one witness.** Harris 2020 §2.3 restates the printed 128 of Harris 2000, and
both rest on DuBois 1938: `DuBois` returns 26 hits in Harris 2000, three of them the very footnotes
the `AP3` and `LR1` cells rest on (n 37 at DuBois 94; n 40, "an excellent survey of this
development", at 94–104; n 44 at 94–98).

So the census's apparent multi-witness support on English shielding is **one chain**, and it runs
Getzler & Macnair → Harris → DuBois.

## Why this is cheap and therefore inexcusable

**A name count is free and it is not a reading.** It can be run on a source already held, before any
decision about whether to acquire a second, and it answers the only question that matters at that
moment: would this purchase buy a witness or a repetition. Skipping it has a characteristic failure
mode, and the batch that skipped it produced the textbook case — it **recommended its own source's
upstream as the corrective to itself**, ranking DuBois first as "a different author" when Harris
rests on DuBois throughout. The recommendation was wrong on its own terms before it was also found
unobtainable.

## The consequence for an acquisition, and it is not that the acquisition is worthless

A source inside the chain still buys detail, precision, and a page one can cite. What it cannot buy
is a second observation. The distinction bites at the point where a coding claims agreement:
two rows resting on one chain agree by construction, and reporting that agreement as corroboration
is the bibliographic form of Galton's problem. **Where a chain is established, the honest entry is a
declared dependence, not a second witness** — which is what the type rows now carry.
