# Archief van de Stad Brussel

<https://archief.brussel.be/>

The Brussels city archive, with a published set of **online archives** and a research-starting guide.

## Why it is in the set, and why it is placed low

Brussels was the **administrative** capital of the Habsburg Netherlands and not its commercial one. The commercial record of the southern Netherlands is at Antwerp and Bruges; what Brussels holds is a court city's own municipal archive — guilds, property, civic institutions, and the *ambachten*.

**The guilds are the reason to keep it.** The entity census contains no cooperative and the loss census is full of mutual institutions that have never been coded as entities; the Brabantine craft corporations are one of the better-documented European bodies of that kind. ⚠️ Series unidentified.

## The thing to keep separate

**The central government's archives are not here.** The councils of the Habsburg Netherlands — the Council of State, the Privy Council, the Council of Finance — are with the Rijksarchief at Brussels, a different institution in the same city. See [[Rijksarchief in Belgie - the state archives network]]. This is the same confusion, in a different form, as the notarial custody trap at [[Rijksarchief te Antwerpen and Beveren - the notarial series]], and Brussels is where a visitor is most likely to make it.
