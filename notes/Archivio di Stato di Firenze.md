# Archivio di Stato di Firenze

<https://archiviodistatofirenze.cultura.gov.it/>

**The best-digitised of the major Italian archives**, and the one whose commercial-institutional holdings are deepest for the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries.

## What is online

| Collection | Route |
|---|---|
| **Mediceo avanti il Principato** — Medici family papers before 1532 | <https://archiviodistatofirenze.cultura.gov.it/map/> |
| **Diplomatico** — loose parchments, 8th–14th centuries | <https://archiviodistatofirenze.cultura.gov.it/asfi/archivi-digitalizzati/diplomatico> |
| **Catasto 1427** — the Florentine tax survey | via the archive's digitisation section |
| **PAD** (Progetto Archivi Digitalizzati) — assorted fondi and series formerly reading-room only | <https://archiviodistatofirenze.cultura.gov.it/archividigitali/> |

⚠️ No image counts are published for any of these. Layer 2 throughout; no transcription.

**The `Diplomatico` matters more than its description suggests.** Loose parchments from the eighth century forward, digitised, are the earliest continuously available series in the Italian set — and by the argument in [[Archivio di Stato di Genova]] about the weight of earliest evidence, that is where a descent claim is decided.

## The fondi to work, imaged or not

**`Notarile Antecosimiano`** — the pre-1569 notarial archive, with an online inventory. The Florentine instruments: *compagnie*, *accomandite*, insurance.

**`Mercanzia`** — the merchants' court. Commercial litigation, and therefore the failure cases where terms are stated.

**`Arti`** — the guild archives (Calimala, Lana, Cambio). Corporate bodies with their own jurisdiction, and a possible *identity-wrapper* comparison in the sense of [[Identity-wrapper and perpetual-monopoly are distinct shielding facets]].

**`Monte Comune`** and **`Monte delle Doti`** — the Florentine funded public debt and the dowry fund. The *Monte delle Doti* is a genuine pooling instrument with an actuarial structure, and it is **not currently in the census**. ⚠️ This is a live gap, not a note-to-self: a state-run contingent claim on a demographic event, priced, transferable, and well documented.
