# Retrodiction fails on non-invertibility not on induction

Taleb's ice cube is a claim about the direction of a map. Put a cube on a table and the puddle is computable; find the puddle and the cube is not. He calls the second the *reverse-engineering problem*, and notes only that the backward process is "much, much more complicated" than the forward one (*The Black Swan*, ch. 12, "The Melting Ice Cube"). The complication has a name, and naming it changes what the historian is entitled to conclude.

The natural filing for a historian is Hume, and that filing is wrong. **Hume's problem is about the rule; the ice cube's problem is about the map, and it survives every concession Hume asks for.** Grant the uniformity of nature. Grant deterministic dynamics, exactly and completely known. The cube is still unrecoverable, because melting is **many-to-one**: a large set of distinct cubes yields indistinguishable puddles, and perfect knowledge of the rule tells you only which set the puddle came from, never which member of it.

The two failures sit at different places and are repaired by different things.

- **Humean underdetermination** is epistemic, and it bites *forward*: no finite record licenses the next observation. It is addressed, if at all, by exposing the conjecture to refutation.
- **Non-invertibility** is structural, and it bites *backward*: the state is underdetermined by its image even when the rule is granted in full. No number of further measurements of the puddle repairs it, because every one of them measures a quantity from which the cube has already been erased.

That second point is the one with teeth, and this vault has already met its twin. [[Deep-roots variables are symplesiomorphies]] records that a literature's own accumulation can be the mechanism by which it grows more confident without growing more informative. Same shape: precision about a quantity that no longer contains the answer.

## Popper is on the right side, but by the other argument

Not *Logik der Forschung*. The relevant Popper is the 1956 note in *Nature*, which opens on exactly this asymmetry: "a film taken of a classical process should be reversible, in the sense that, if put into a projector with the last picture first, it should again yield a possible classical process" — and observes that some films plainly do not. His explanation is not entropy but **coordination**: running the process backwards would demand initial conditions organised to a degree that the forward process never requires. Irreversibility is thereby a fact about which initial conditions are *realisable*, not about the time-symmetry of the laws.

Transposed, that is the historian's position with unusual precision. The laws, such as they are, are not the binding constraint. The binding constraint is which pasts could have produced this present, and how few of them the present distinguishes.

## The usable form of the claim

The question is never *is the past recoverable*. It is **which functionals of the past survive the map**.

Some do. The puddle's volume fixes the cube's mass, because mass is conserved: that functional passes through the dissipation intact. The cube's shape does not, because nothing carries it. A historian who asks the puddle for the mass is asking a well-posed question; the same historian asking it for the shape is not, and no refinement of the measurement converts the second into the first.

This is the constructive version, and it matches the precedent Objective 3 already follows: the chronosequence literature did not abandon the space-for-time substitution, it specified the conditions of its validity. The parallel deliverable here is a statement, per design, of which features of the initial condition the dynamics preserve.

Two consequences follow directly.

**Evidence that bypassed the erasing map is untouched by any of this.** The ice merchant's receipt is not a reading of the puddle. Archival survival, the filter, and the *articulated* record are channels that dissipation did not pass through, which is one reason this project's evidentiary stance is built on provenance rather than on inference from outcomes. See [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]].

**A method that reads the past off present outcomes is working in the erased channel by construction.** That is the persistence regression's channel, and how much it erases is taken up in [[Attractor selection bounds what a persistence design can resolve]].
