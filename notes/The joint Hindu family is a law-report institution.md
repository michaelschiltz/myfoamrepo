# The joint Hindu family is a law-report institution

**`huf` is the census's only South Asian row. It is uncoded, and its `key_source` recommends removal from `loss_mitigation_forms`** — on the ground that the joint family is a property-holding and succession institution whose comparative place is in `organizational_forms` beside `waqf_ahli` and the Chinese lineage estate, **and that the batch's only South Asian item is a colonial revenue glossary of 1855 whose entries on coparcenary are about villages, not families.**

## What this pass adds: where its record actually is

**The joint family's archive is the law report and the colonial digest.** Its rules — the *Mitākṣarā* and *Dāyabhāga* schools, the *karta*'s powers, partition, pious obligation — were argued case by case before colonial courts and codified in digests and, later, statute. **The record type is adjudication**, and it is published, indexed and free in the same way the banking enquiries are.

**That is not nothing, and it is not what the census needs.** A reported case tells you what a court held about one family's partition; **it does not give a contribution schedule, a payout rule or a fund**, and the census would be coding doctrine — the very thing the Ottoman waqf rows already flag about themselves when they note that their agreement "rests on the law of vakıf, not on two observations of two institutions".

## The recommendation this pass supports

**Remove the row from `loss_mitigation_forms`, and if it is wanted at all, mint it in `organizational_forms` beside `waqf_ahli`** — where it joins the lineage charitable estate parked at logbook 2, 2026-09-11 (viii). **The three together are the perpetual-kin-patrimony family across three legal traditions, and that is a real comparison.** ⚠️ No South Asian source for it has been read by this project.
