# Archives municipales de Bayonne — the Basque coast

⚠️ **The thinnest-evidenced note in the French set, kept because of what it would test.**

Bayonne and the Basque ports face [[Archivo Foral de Bizkaia - the Consulado de Bilbao]] across a bay and a border. The Bilbao ordinances of 1737 are the most influential commercial code in the Spanish-speaking world, and the French Basque ports were trading with and against the Spanish ones throughout the period.

## The question this would answer

**Whether a commercial custom follows a language community or a sovereign.** The Basque coast is one of the very few European places where the two come apart cleanly: one people, one maritime economy, two crowns, two legal systems, two ordinances.

The census's standing worry is that its comparisons confound tradition with jurisdiction. **Here they are experimentally separated**, and the separation was not designed by anyone.

⚠️ Whether the surviving records support the question is entirely unknown. Municipal archives of this size are often thin for the early modern period, and the answer may be that there is nothing to work with. **Check before planning anything**, and treat this note as a hypothesis about a research design rather than as a repository description.
