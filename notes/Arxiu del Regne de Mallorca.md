# Arxiu del Regne de Mallorca

<https://arxiuregnemallorca.com/>

**The Balearic counterpart to the Catalan material, and disproportionately important for marine insurance.** Fifteenth-century Mallorca produced insurance policies in volume, and the island's *Consolat de Mar* had its own jurisdiction; the surviving record is one of the better western series for the moment when risk-pricing separates from risk-sharing.

## The fons that matter

**`Reial Patrimoni`** — royal accounts and the Crown's commercial dealings.

**`Governacio`** — the governor's jurisdiction, including commercial litigation.

**`Universitat`** — the island's own corporate governing body, with its fisc and its debt.

**`Protocols notarials`** — the instruments: *comandes*, *societats*, insurance policies.

⚠️ Reference codes and date ranges for each of the above need transcribing from the archive's own guide; the descriptions consulted for this note were secondary.

## Why the insurance material is the point

The project's cut is between pricing a peril and sharing it — [[Pricing risk does not remove it]] and the whole apparatus of [[MOC - Risk-sharing vs risk-pricing]]. Mallorca is a place where both instruments were in routine simultaneous use by the same merchants, which makes it a natural test site for whether the two are alternatives or complements. That is a question the vault has not yet asked of any single corpus.
