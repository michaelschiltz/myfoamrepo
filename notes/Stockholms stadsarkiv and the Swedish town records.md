# Stockholms stadsarkiv and the Swedish town records

Stockholm's city archive, with the *tänkeböcker* — the town council's minute books — and the civic record of the Swedish capital.

## What a *tänkebok* is, and the family it belongs to

The council's book of proceedings, recording obligations, disputes, conveyances and the decisions made on them. **In a town without a notariate this is the instrument record**, which puts it in the family already assembled at [[Archiv der Hansestadt Lubeck]] (*Niederstadtbuch*), [[Itinera Nova - the Leuven aldermen's registers]] and [[Antwerp schepenregisters and the regesten]].

**Four countries, four names, one record type.** That is worth stating because the census's sources are not comparable in the way their contents are: a notarial minute, a *schepenakte*, a *Niederstadtbuch* entry and a *tänkebok* entry record overlapping but not identical things, and the differences are procedural rather than substantive.

**The coding consequence is real.** Where two rows are sourced from different record types, a difference between them may be a difference in what the record type records — the point already made for `bodmerei` at [[Bodmerei - where the German bottomry records are]], and generalising here into a rule that should be checked whenever a comparison crosses the notarial/civic line.

⚠️ Extent, editions and digitisation unverified. Several Swedish towns' *tänkeböcker* have been published in scholarly editions, which would put the description at layer 3 ⚠️ — worth establishing, because a printed edition is the cheapest route into this material.
