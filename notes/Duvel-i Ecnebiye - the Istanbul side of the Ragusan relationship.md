# *Düvel-i Ecnebiye* — the Istanbul side of the Ragusan relationship

At [[Osmanli Arsivi - Istanbul]]. The *Düvel-i Ecnebiye Defterleri* — registers of foreign states — are the Imperial Council's own copies of what it granted to each foreign power, and **Dubrovnik has its own volumes**. Series code `A.DVNS.DVE.d`.

## Why this is the pair to [[Acta Turcarum - the Ottoman series at Dubrovnik]]

A *ferman* issued to Ragusa exists twice: the original, sent and kept in the Rector's Palace, and the register copy, kept at the Porte. **The Dubrovnik series holds what arrived; this series holds what the issuing chancery chose to enter.** The handoff described Dubrovnik as one relationship in two apparatuses in one building; this series supplies a third view from outside the building, from the other party's registry.

## It has already been done once, and the apparatuses disagree

Cihan Yemişçi set Dubrovnik volumes `ADVN.DVE.d` 13/1, 14/2 and 15/3 (**1611–1663**) against *Acta et diplomata* `7/2.1` and *Litterae et commissiones* (1604–1649) on two cases. **The Istanbul registers show imperial orders upholding Ragusan merchants' tax privileges; the Dubrovnik side shows a provincial governor refusing to obey them.** The Ottoman record is silent on the opening of one dispute for three years.

**That is a finding about the apparatus, and it points the wrong way for any assumption that the centre's register describes practice.** A privilege entered in Istanbul is evidence of what was granted, not of what a merchant at the frontier could get.

## Limits

- ⚠️ Total number of Dubrovnik volumes and their date range not established; Yemişçi's three are a sample.
- ⚠️ The code is given as `ADVN.DVE.d` in the paper and as `A.DVNS.DVE.d` in the archive's current usage for the *Düvel-i Ecnebiye* series generally; which is current for these volumes was not checked in the catalogue.
- The registers are diplomatic and fiscal. **They record privileges, not contracts**, and the census's commercial rows will not be coded from them.
