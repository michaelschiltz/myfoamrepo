# Svenska Ostindiska Companiet — the Gothenburg archive

Digital archive: <https://ostindiska.ub.gu.se/> (Gothenburg University Library) · records also in Riksarkivet's NAD, `SE/GLA/4683`

The Swedish East India Company, chartered **1731**, trading from Gothenburg to Canton until 1813 — and **its archive has a dedicated digital presentation**, maintained and updated by the university library.

## Why this is the easiest company case in the survey

Because someone has already done the work. **A chartered company with a purpose-built digital archive, publicly accessible, is a configuration this survey has not met once** — not for the VOC's constitutional record, not for the EIC, not for the French or Danish or Brandenburg companies.

⚠️ What is actually in it — whether the constitutional record (charter, *oktroj*, shareholder registers, directors' minutes) is digitised, or only the voyage and sales material such as the *försäljningsböcker* — is **unestablished and is the whole question.** The census needs the former; a digitisation project is more likely to have prioritised the latter.

## The third small-state company

With the Danish companies at [[Asiatisk Kompagni and the Danish trading companies]] and the Brandenburg company at [[Geheimes Staatsarchiv Preussischer Kulturbesitz - Berlin]], the survey has now located **three small-state chartered companies**, all built on the Dutch model, all well after it, all with surviving records.

**Together they are a much better instrument than any one of them.** Three copies of one model, made independently by three minor powers, with different outcomes — Sweden's wound up, Denmark's persisted, Brandenburg's failed and was sold. **Outcome variance across structurally similar cases is exactly what the entity census does not currently have**, and it can be had from three archives in three countries that are all open.
