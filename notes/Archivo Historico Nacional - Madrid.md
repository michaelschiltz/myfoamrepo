# Archivo Historico Nacional — Madrid

<https://www.cultura.gob.es/cultura/areas/archivos/mc/archivos/ahn/presentacion.html> · catalogue via [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]]

**The archive of the councils and of the dissolved corporations.** Where Simancas is fiscal and the AGI is colonial, the AHN holds the constitutional paperwork of chartered bodies — which makes it the Spanish counterpart to the series at Kew that the deed-of-settlement coding uses.

## The sections that matter

**`CONSEJOS`** (Consejos Suprimidos) — the Council of Castile and the residue of the Council of the Indies. Charters, privileges and the litigation over them. ⚠️ The Consejo de Indias documentation is **split between the AHN and the AGI**, and the split is not clean; there is a published study of exactly this problem in *Revista de Indias* which should be read before either archive is searched.

**`ESTADO`** — including material on the eighteenth-century privileged companies. The Real Compania Guipuzcoana de Caracas is the obvious case: a chartered monopoly trading company with transferable shares, which is a direct comparandum for the forms already coded in `organizational_forms`. ⚠️ Its records are dispersed across the AHN, the AGI and Basque provincial custody; establish the split before treating any one holding as the company's archive.

**`CLERO SECULAR-REGULAR`** — monastic and ecclesiastical property records, running deep into the middle ages. Relevant to the *censo* and to ecclesiastical pooling forms rather than to the maritime strand.

**`ORDENES MILITARES`** — corporate bodies with perpetual succession and collective property, and therefore a possible parallel case for [[The Roman universitas makes the JSC case parallelism not convergence]]. ⚠️ Speculative; no coding decision rests on this yet.

## Why the privileged companies matter here

The Spanish chartered companies are the missing middle of the comparative set. They are later than the VOC and EIC, explicitly modelled on them, and Spanish — which makes them a test of transmission rather than of independent recurrence, in the terms of [[Transmission versus independent recurrence is the load-bearing test]]. If the project wants a case where the answer is *transmission*, this is it, and the documents are in Madrid.
