# Source editions — maritime law compilations

**The comparative collections in the literal sense**: nineteenth- and twentieth-century editions that put the sea codes of several traditions side by side. They bear on `sea_loan`, `bottomry`, `respondentia`, `general_average` and `particular_average`. **Most are free.** ⚠️ The vault's warning applies to all of them: [[A shared compilation never licenses an inference to shared practice]].

- **Jean-Marie Pardessus, *Collection de lois maritimes antérieures au XVIIIe siècle***, 6 vols (Paris: Imprimerie royale, 1828–45) — original texts with French translation and commentary: Rhodian law, Oléron, Trani, Amalfi, the Consulat de Mar, the Hanseatic and Wisby law, the Guidon de la mer, Italian and Iberian statutes. **All six volumes in full view on HathiTrust**; several on archive.org. **The master comparative corpus.**
- **Sir Travers Twiss (ed.), *Monumenta Juridica: The Black Book of the Admiralty***, 4 vols (London: Longman, 1871–76; Rolls Series 55) — originals facing English translation; vol. 3 is the **Consulate of the Sea in Catalan with English translation**, vol. 4 Amalfi, Trani, Wisby. **Free on archive.org.** The best free English Consulate.
- **Walter Ashburner (ed.), *Nomos Rhodiōn nautikos: The Rhodian Sea-Law*** (Oxford: Clarendon, 1909) — Greek text, translation, commentary; **jettison (the root of general average), sea loans, the *chreokoinonia***. Free on archive.org.
- **Germà Colon & Arcadi Garcia (eds), *Llibre del Consolat de Mar***, 4 vols (Barcelona: Fundació Noguera et al., 1981–87; reissued 2001, Textos i Documents Maior 2, 1,608 pp.) — **the critical text**, from the Real de Mallorca manuscript with all variants.
- **Ernest Moliné i Brasés (ed.), *Les costums marítimes de Barcelona universalment conegudes per Llibre del Consolat de Mar*** (Barcelona, 1914) — with an appendix including **the Barcelona marine-insurance ordinances**; facsimile at the Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes.
- **Stanley S. Jados (tr.), *Consulate of the Sea and Related Documents*** (University of Alabama Press, 1975) — modern English translation.
- **Oléron and the Low German tradition**: Krieger, *Ursprung und Wurzeln der Rôles d'Oléron* (Cologne: Böhlau, 1970); **Jahnke & Graßmann (eds), *Seerecht im Hanseraum des 15. Jahrhunderts: Edition und Kommentar zum Flandrischen Copiar Nr. 9*** (Lübeck: Archiv der Hansestadt, 2003) — an actual edition of the Low German Oléron–Wisby texts. See also [[Visby and the Gotland sea law]].
