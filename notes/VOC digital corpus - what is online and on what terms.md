# VOC digital corpus — what is online and on what terms

The operative fact: the VOC archive is not merely digitised, it is **transcribed, downloadable and free**. That is a different research object from a scanned archive, and the difference decides what can be asked of it.

## Scale and dispersal

Roughly **25 million pages** of VOC record survive, split across The Hague, Jakarta, Colombo, Chennai and Cape Town. ⚠️ The 25-million figure circulates from the TANAP/UNESCO nomination material and should be traced to its source before it is printed. The Netherlands portion is `NL-HaNA 1.04.02` at the Nationaal Archief; the overseas portions sit with ANRI (Jakarta), the Sri Lanka National Archives, the Tamil Nadu Archives and the Western Cape (Cape Town).

**UNESCO Memory of the World.** Inscribed as a joint nomination of the Netherlands, Indonesia, Sri Lanka, India and South Africa. ⚠️ Date discrepancy: Huygens says "since 2003", the ANRI *Sejarah Nusantara* pages say accepted March 2004. Resolve before citing; the register entry is the authority.

## The centre of gravity: GLOBALISE

GLOBALISE (2022–2026; NWO-funded; Huygens Instituut with IISG, Nationaal Archief, KNAW HuC, VU and UvA) targets the **Overgekomen Brieven en Papieren** — the papers sent home to the Heren XVII from the Asian establishments.

- Corpus: OBP inventory numbers **1053–4454 and 7527–11024**, ≈ **5 million scans** supplied by the Nationaal Archief.
- All 5 million scans machine-transcribed with **Loghi** (open-source, KNAW Humanities Cluster). First release **4 October 2023**.
- Transcriptions downloadable from the GLOBALISE **Dataverse**; a prototype Transcriptions Viewer sits at <https://lab.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl/experiments/htr-viewer/>.
- Beyond text: manual entity and event annotation (INCEpTION), reference datasets for polities, persons, places, **weights and measures**, and a project ontology.

⚠️ **Two things to check, both material.** (1) Licence — CC0 is stated in secondary reporting but must be confirmed on the Dataverse deposit itself before the transcriptions are treated as reusable data. (2) The advanced research platform was announced for **autumn 2025** and the project's own timeline promises all tools and data open access by **end 2026**. It is now August 2026: establish what actually shipped rather than citing the roadmap.

The transcription quality is HTR output on early modern Dutch hands, not a critical edition. Any frequency claim drawn from it inherits an unmeasured character error rate. See [[Open-source HTR stack - eScriptorium Kraken Loghi]].

## Structured data, already quantitative

- **Boekhouder-Generaal Batavia** — <https://bgb.huygens.knaw.nl>. The Bookkeeper-General's commodity accounts, eighteenth century; goods moving Republic↔Batavia and between Asian establishments. Built 2008–2013. This is the nearest thing either company has to a machine-queryable ledger series.
- **Dutch-Asiatic Shipping** (Bruijn, Gaastra, Schöffer) — every voyage 1595–1795. ⚠️ Current URL and data format unverified.
- **Generale Missiven** of the Governors-General, RGP series, digital at Huygens. ⚠️ Extent of digital coverage unverified.
- **VOC Opvarenden** (1699–1794) and **VOC testamenten** (1698–1807) at the Nationaal Archief — personnel and wills, indexed.

## The overseas repositories

- **ANRI, Jakarta** — *Sejarah Nusantara* (<https://sejarah-nusantara.anri.go.id>), built with the Corts Foundation from 2011. Daily journals of Batavia Castle; and the **Diplomatic Letters 1625–1812** corpus: 4,300+ letters between the High Government and Southeast Asian rulers, transcribed *and translated*, found buried in the daghregisters by Manse and Niemeijer in 2013–14. Correspondence exchanged principally 1683–1743.
- **TANAP** inventories — <http://databases.tanap.net/ead/>, with a selection re-presented at <https://docs.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl/tanap/>. Finding aids for the Asian and Cape repositories, plus establishment reconstructions. ⚠️ The original TANAP site is partly decayed; treat GLOBALISE's mirror as the stable entry.
- Colombo, Chennai and Cape Town holdings are described but only patchily imaged. ⚠️ Unresearched.

## What this does not include

The corporate-constitutional record is thinner online than the operational one. The chamber archives, resolutions of the Heren XVII, and the Zeeland chamber material at the Zeeuws Archief are not part of the GLOBALISE corpus, which is an *incoming correspondence* series. ⚠️ Verify what of the Heren XVII resolutions is imaged. For the entity-form questions the corpus does not answer, [[Van Dam's Beschryvinge - the VOC described by its own advocaat]] remains the route in.
