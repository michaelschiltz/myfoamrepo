# SilkRoadLanguageWeb — Leiden ERC dating the Indo-European entries into the Tarim Basin

**ERC Consolidator Grant (ERC-2022-COG, GA 101088902) at Universiteit Leiden, 1 Jan 2024 – 31 Dec 2028, €1,999,863: dates the routes and arrival of Khotanese, Tumšuqese and Tocharian in the Tarim Basin from long-term contact evidence. Use it as the current linguistic baseline for who was in the Tarim oases when, and as a pointer to fresh editions of Archaic Tocharian B and Late Khotanese material.**

## Project data

- Acronym: SilkRoadLanguageWeb
- Full title: *The Silk Road Language Web: A linguistic prehistory of the Tarim Basin in Northwest China*
- Grant agreement: 101088902; CORDIS DOI [10.3030/101088902](https://doi.org/10.3030/101088902)
- Programme: HORIZON.1.1 ERC; call ERC-2022-COG; scheme HORIZON-ERC
- Host: Universiteit Leiden, Rapenburg 70, 2311 EZ Leiden (sole beneficiary)
- PI: Michaël Peyrot ⚠️ (not named on the fact sheet; inferred from the author list of project outputs and the objective's reference to the PI's earlier ERC StG and NWO-VIDI)
- Keywords (coordinator's): historical linguistics; linguistic prehistory; language contact; Indo-European; Tarim Basin
- Languages in scope: Khotanese, Tumšuqese, Tocharian; contact layers Middle Indic (Gāndhārī, Niya Prakrit), Bactrian, Sogdian, Middle Iranian
- Stated position: Tocharians arrived later and Iranians earlier and more widely than the "Tarim Mummies" narrative holds

## Period 1 work (2024-01-01 to 2026-06-30)

- Kharoṣṭhī–Brāhmī script interaction
- Bactrian and Sogdian loanwords in Tocharian
- Early Buddhist Chinese transcriptions of Gāndhārī names, for source dialect
- Gāndhārī loanwords in Khotanese
- Editions in preparation: Archaic Tocharian B; Late Khotanese
