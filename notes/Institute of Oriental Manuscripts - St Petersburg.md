# Institute of Oriental Manuscripts — St Petersburg

The IOM of the Russian Academy of Sciences holds **three Inner Asian deposits that matter to this project**, and is the custodian with the most documentary material per item.

- **Dunhuang and Turfan** — from Sergey Oldenburg's expeditions; **20,000 or more fragments** on IDP's summary, the Дх and Ф series. ⚠️ Figure second-hand.
- **Khara-Khoto** — excavated by Pyotr Kozlov, **1907–09**: the first identified Tangut documents, with a substantial Chinese component running from Xixia into the **Yuan**. **Nearly 500 of the Chinese "social documents" — contracts among them — are transcribed in 《俄藏黑水城汉文文献释录》 (2023), four volumes in five books**, edited by Du Jianlu (Ningxia University) with Irina Popova (IOM) over five years.
- **The Mount Mugh archive** — **seventy-four documents in Sogdian, one in Arabic, one in Turkic runic script and several in Chinese**, from the castle of Dēwāštīč of Panjikant, found by a shepherd in **1932** and investigated in 1933; **the documents are in St Petersburg and over 400 excavated objects are in the Hermitage**, while the site is in Tajikistan. See [[Sogdian commercial documents - thin but not empty]].

## What it does to the evidence

**The Khara-Khoto Chinese documents are the survey's nearest thing to an Inner Asian contract archive**, and **they were made usable by a Sino-Russian editorial partnership rather than by the holder alone** — the pattern named at [[The Silk Road layer 3 is a national editorial programme]].

**And the Mugh archive is the survey's clearest case of a site, its objects and its documents in three jurisdictions**: documents in one institution, objects in another, site in a third country whose UNESCO nomination claims them. ⚠️ The UNESCO page could not be fetched; the nomination is reported from search results only.
