# National Library of Wales

<https://www.library.wales/>

⚠️ **A thin note, kept for one reason.**

The Welsh national library holds the Welsh estate, legal and manuscript record, and runs a substantial digitisation programme including **Welsh Newspapers Online**, which is free.

## The one substantive point

**Welsh Newspapers Online is free where the British Newspaper Archive charges.** Within one state, two answers to the same question — see [[The British Newspaper Archive and the commercial full-text layer]].

That is a smaller version of the survey's whole argument and a useful one, because it removes the last confound: **same country, same period, same kind of material, different terms**, and the difference is which institution did the digitising and on whose money.

⚠️ For the census's commercial questions Wales is a secondary region and there is no reason to think otherwise. Recorded for the access point, not for the holdings.
