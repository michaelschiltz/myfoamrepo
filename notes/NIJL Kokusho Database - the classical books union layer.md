# NIJL *Kokusho* Database — the classical books union layer

国書データベース, National Institute of Japanese Literature (国文学研究資料館, Tachikawa). <https://kokusho.nijl.ac.jp/>

**The union catalogue and image base for pre-modern Japanese books**, integrating the older 日本古典籍総合目録データベース and the 新日本古典籍総合データベース built by the historical-texts network project. **Target: original images of 300,000 items public by the end of FY2024**, from the NIJL and many domestic and foreign holders. **IIIF**, Mirador viewer. **NIJL's own images public domain**; others under their holders' terms, expressed with Rights Statements. Some images carry content tags and full transcriptions.

## What it holds for the census

**Books, not documents.** The census's Japanese evidence is overwhelmingly *komonjo* — rulebooks, ledgers, deeds. The *Kokusho* base holds the *printed and manuscript books* around them: commercial manuals, *jikata* handbooks, *sanpō* arithmetic, *ōrai-mono* letter-writing primers with model deeds. **A model *tanomoshi* subscription or pledge form in a letter primer is a drafting convention, not an institution** — the vault already holds the European rule at [[The notarial security clause is boilerplate and cannot carry a typology]]; the same caution applies.

**It is also the source of CODH's open datasets** — see [[CODH datasets - the open machine-learning layer]].

⚠️ Whether the FY2024 target was met, and current image counts, not established.
