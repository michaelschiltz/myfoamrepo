# The three Kelly critiques establish different nulls

Kelly's work on persistence is routinely cited as a single item. It is at least three, they establish different nulls, and WP3 has to beat a specific one.

## The items

**The standard-error critique.** Spatial autocorrelation means most observations add little to the precision of an estimate, so conventional standard errors are badly understated. Published as Conley and Kelly 2025 in the *Journal of International Economics*; the vault has already corrected earlier citations of the 2019 single-authored working paper to this version, per the citation note in [[Deep-roots variables are symplesiomorphies]].

**The robustness-check survey.** *Understanding Persistence* (2020), applying three checks — regional controls, longitude and latitude for directional gradients, and removal of an extreme control region — to twenty-five studies, and ranking them by the fraction of the original coefficient surviving.

**The synthetic-noise simulations.** *Persistence, Randomization, and Spatial Noise* (2021), showing that regressions of one spatial noise series on another appear highly significant.

## The caveat that matters for the Voigtländer–Voth case

In *Understanding Persistence* the paper enters as "Nazi vote on pogroms," with the robustness check consisting of dropping six constituencies in Bavaria. Kelly reports considerable reductions in effect size across all such cases, and the study sits roughly mid-pack among the twenty-five.

**But the specification is a constituency-level Nazi-vote regression, not the city-level main sample or the principal-component specification the paper leads with.** It is therefore not the direct refutation the secondary literature implies, and citing it as one is an error waiting to be caught. [verify] the exact point estimate and specification before any draft claims it. See [[Voigtlander and Voth measured the relaxation time and reported a mixture]].

## What WP3 has to beat

The synthetic-noise result is the operative null. If spatially correlated noise alone reproduces a persistence finding, the ergodicity criterion must deliver something the noise simulation does not.

What it delivers is a statement about the **estimand** rather than the estimator: that the space-for-time substitution was never licensed, because the decomposition is reducible, or the sample is conditioned on non-absorption, or the horizon exceeds the relaxation time. Kelly's conclusion is that the result is indistinguishable from noise; this conclusion is that the quantity was not identified in the first place, and would remain unidentified on a spatially orthogonal grid.

That distinction has to be stated explicitly in the WP3 text. Left implicit, the work package reads as Kelly with a Greek letter — the same failure mode [[Deep-roots variables are symplesiomorphies]] warns against when it declines to restate Galton's problem in cladistic vocabulary.
