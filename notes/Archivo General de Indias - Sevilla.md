# Archivo General de Indias — Sevilla

<https://www.cultura.gob.es/cultura/areas/archivos/mc/archivos/agi/presentacion.html> · catalogue via [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]]

**The single most important Spanish repository for this project**, because it holds the administrative record of the institution the project reads as the pooling-to-corporation hinge: the *averia* and the *asiento de averia*. See [[The asiento de averia is the pooling-to-corporation hinge]] and [[Hierro Anibarro 2005 on the asiento de averia and the privileged company]].

## The sections that matter

**`CONTRATACION`** — the Casa de la Contratacion, **1492–1795, 6,337 legajos, 51 series**. The operational and judicial record of the Carrera de Indias: ship registers, bills of lading, contracts, judicial sentences, *bienes de difuntos*, passenger lists, pilot examinations. This is where the fleet system's paperwork lives.

**`CONSULADOS`** — the Consulado and Universidad de Cargadores a Indias, the merchant corporation that farmed the *averia* and later contracted it as an *asiento*. Libros 1–27 have been digitised and published on PARES. ⚠️ Establish what the remaining libros cover and whether the *averia* accounts proper sit here or in `CONTADURIA`.

**`CONTADURIA`** — the accounting series. ⚠️ The *averia* receipts and disbursements are conventionally cited here; verify against a finding aid before treating any series as a complete run.

**`INDIFERENTE GENERAL`** — general policy and correspondence, including the deliberative record. Olivares's junta, in [[Olivares's junta coded the asiento as a privileged company]], generated exactly this kind of material.

**`ESCRIBANIA DE CAMARA`** and **`JUSTICIA`** — litigation. Where a pooling arrangement broke down, this is where the argument about who bore the loss survives.

## Digitisation

**Partial, and unevenly.** Complete for passenger records, *bienes de difuntos* and *provisiones*; partial for *reales cedulas*, *registros de navios* and the slave-trade series. Layer 2 where imaged, layer 1 elsewhere. The digitised selection is weighted toward genealogical demand — which is a funding-shaped selection of exactly the kind [[The QDL corpus is conditioned on its funder's geography]] warns against, and it does not favour the commercial-institutional series this project needs.

## What to do with it

The stated-rationale rule from [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]] applies with force here. The *averia* documentation records not just what was pooled but the arguments the Consulado and the Crown made about why — and those arguments are the evidence, not the accounting totals.
