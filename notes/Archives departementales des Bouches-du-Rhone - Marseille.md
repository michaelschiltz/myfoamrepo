# Archives départementales des Bouches-du-Rhône — Marseille

**The French Mediterranean archive**, and the one that matters most for the risk-pricing question.

## Why Marseille

Marseille was the principal French marine-insurance market and the hinge between the French and the Italian-Catalan Mediterranean world. The instruments, the disputes and the administrative record of the Levant trade are here and at the chamber of commerce next door — [[Chambre de commerce de Marseille - the 1599 archive]], which is a **separate institution with a separate archive** and must be searched separately.

**Série `B`** holds the *amirauté de Marseille* — see [[The amirautes - a series across the coastal departments]]. **Série `E`** holds the notaries.

⚠️ Reference codes, extents and imaging all unestablished.

## The comparative role

Marseille is the place to ask whether the French Mediterranean practice follows the Italian or the Atlantic pattern. The census's commenda cluster is Genoese, Venetian, Ragusan and Catalan; **Marseille sits geographically and commercially inside that world but under a French crown and, after 1681, under a French ordinance.**

That is a *natural experiment on the relation between legal tradition and commercial practice*: same sea, same counterparties, different sovereign. [[A migrated institution collects local names not local forms]] is the caution; this is the site where the caution can be tested rather than repeated.
