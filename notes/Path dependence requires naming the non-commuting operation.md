# Path dependence requires naming the non-commuting operation

The weak version of path dependence — "history matters" — is nearly vacuous. The strong version is a claim about algebra: **the operations history performs on the system do not commute.** A path-dependence claim that cannot name the non-commuting operation is a description of the past dressed as an explanation of it.

The test has three parts, and a claim must pass all three to be doing work:

1. **The non-commuting operation.** Affine composition (the $b(a-1)$ term in [[Flows make the dynamic affine and affine maps do not commute]]), barrier crossing ([[Terminal wealth is permutation-invariant but survival is not]]), or a reinforcement kernel ([[Reinforcement selects an attractor the ensemble average never occupies]]). If the dynamics are bare multiplicative growth, the claim is already refuted by [[Pure multiplicative dynamics are permutation-invariant]].
2. **The lock-in mechanism.** What makes the increment weight decay fast enough for the system to settle, yet slow enough that it never resets. In the urn this is the pair $\sum 1/n^2 < \infty$ and $\sum 1/n = \infty$; in the barrier case absorption does the work directly.
3. **The counterfactual ordering.** Which permutation would have produced a different attractor, and why that permutation was available. Without availability the counterfactual is idle.

Page (2006) is the formalism that makes this respectable to referees: he separates path dependence proper from *state dependence*, *early-mover advantage*, *phat dependence* — outcomes depending on the *set* of past events rather than their order — and *tipping*. The distinction between path and state dependence is this test in his vocabulary. Phat dependence is the trap: most historical claims labelled path-dependent are phat-dependent at best, and phat dependence is compatible with full permutation invariance.

The argumentative payoff is that this asymmetry is an opening move rather than a caveat. The historical literature routinely asserts sequence-sensitivity about dynamics that are effectively multiplicative and never notices the assertion is empty. Naming the operator converts the same claim into something falsifiable.
