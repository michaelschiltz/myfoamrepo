# Archivio di Stato di Genova

<https://archiviodistatogenova.cultura.gov.it/>

Two things of the first importance are here, and they are quite different objects. The notarial series — treated in this note — and [[Casa di San Giorgio - the Genoese public debt archive]], which has its own.

## Notai Antichi

<https://archiviodistatogenova.cultura.gov.it/patrimonio/archivio-digitale/notai-antichi>

**The earliest surviving notarial registers in Europe**, from the twelfth century, and therefore the earliest documentary witness to the western commenda. The Genoese material is where the *commenda* / *societas maris* distinction is drawn, and it is the anchor for [[The commenda and the qirad do not separate on loss allocation]] and [[A route clause conditions the investor's peril in three traditions]].

Registers are digitised and served through the **Notariorum Itinera** platform, a research project on the Ligurian notariate rather than an archive service. ⚠️ The archive's own page displays some seventy digitised registers as thumbnails without quantifying the whole; establish the extent and the date coverage before planning around it. Layer 2 — images, no transcription.

## Why the earliest evidence carries disproportionate weight

The project's discriminating test is transmission versus independent recurrence — [[Transmission versus independent recurrence is the load-bearing test]] — and that test is decided by the **earliest** instances, not the typical ones. The Genoese registers are, for the western family, as close to the origin as the record goes. What is in them constrains every downstream claim about descent; what is absent from them is evidence about twelfth-century Genoese notarial practice and nothing else, which is [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] applied at the point where it bites hardest.

## Other fondi

⚠️ Unresearched: the `Antico Comune` series, the maritime magistracies, and the insurance material. Genoa was a first-rank insurance centre and the policies should be locatable, but no series has been identified here.
