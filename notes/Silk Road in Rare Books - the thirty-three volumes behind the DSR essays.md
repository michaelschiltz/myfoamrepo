# Silk Road in Rare Books — the thirty-three volumes behind the DSR essays

**The book list for NII's "Silk Road in Rare Books" essays covers 33 digitised volumes from 23 Tōyō Bunko collections.** It is a small subset of the 245 books described in [[Toyo Bunko and the Digital Silk Road - the expedition library not the finds]]. **Most of it is expedition output**: Stein ×3, Le Coq ×4, Grünwedel ×3, Hedin, Bergman, Pelliot, Pumpelly ×2, Kagawa for Ōtani, de Filippi. Two dynastic-history chapters and three pre-1900 works make up the rest.

**What it is good for is locating plates.** Each entry below gives the Tōyō Bunko call number, the DSR volume viewer and the DOI. It then lists the figures DSR took from that volume, each tagged with the essay it illustrates. **DOIs are issued per collection, not per volume.**

Viewer pattern: `https://dsr.nii.ac.jp/toyobunko/<call no.>/<V-n>/page/<nnnn>.html.en`. IIIF manifest: `…/<call no.>/<V-n>/manifest.json`. Collection metadata: `…/toyobunko/<call no.>/`.

## DSR metadata errors

- **DSR's generated citations garble particles**, e.g. "Le, Coq Albert von". Use the forms above.
- **DSR gives *Serindia* the *Ancient Khotan* subtitle.** It is corrected above.
- **DSR's caption writes 張儀潮 for 張議潮.** It is corrected above.
