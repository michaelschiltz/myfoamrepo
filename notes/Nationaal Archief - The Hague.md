# Nationaal Archief — The Hague

<https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/>

The state archive of the Republic and of the Kingdom. For this project it holds the company records and the high-court record, and it is the supplier of the scans behind [[VOC digital corpus - what is online and on what terms]].

## The archives that matter

**`1.04.02` — VOC.** The archive of the United East India Company, and the source of the ≈5 million Overgekomen Brieven en Papieren scans that GLOBALISE transcribed. Layer 3 for the OBP, layer 1–2 for the rest. **The corporate-constitutional record is the part that is not in the transcribed corpus**: the resolutions of the Heren XVII and the chamber archives sit outside an incoming-correspondence series, which is exactly the material the `voc_1602` / `voc_1612` / `voc_1623` rows turn on. See [[GLOBALISE removes the HTR question for the VOC and leaves it standing for the EIC]].

**`1.05` — West-Indische Compagnie.** The WIC, first and second charters. ⚠️ Reference codes and survival need checking; the WIC archive is notoriously incomplete and the gaps are real ones, unlike the Neapolitan case at [[Archivio di Stato di Napoli]].

**`3.01.04.01` — Staten-Generaal** ⚠️ *code to verify*. The charters themselves were granted here. For a project reading the VOC charter as a constitutive document, the granting body's own record is the other half.

**`3.03.01.01` — Hof van Holland, 1428–1811.** Its own note: [[Hof van Holland - the civil litigation record]].

**Admiraliteitscolleges.** ⚠️ Unresearched. Prize, average and maritime jurisdiction; the obvious place for the public-law side of maritime peril.

**`VOC Opvarenden` and `VOC testamenten`** — personnel and wills, indexed. Genealogically driven, but the wills are instruments and may carry more than the index suggests.

## Access

Free, open, CC0 scans, OAI-PMH harvesting. **This is the least constrained major repository in the European set** — see [[Dutch archives online - the access layers]] for the licence position and what it is worth.
