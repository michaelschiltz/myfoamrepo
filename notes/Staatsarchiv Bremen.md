# Staatsarchiv Bremen

<https://www.staatsarchiv.bremen.de/>

The third Hanseatic city archive, with a published *Beständeübersicht* and an online search that the state has been improving.

## What is here

The civic and mercantile record of a port that was Hanseatic, then imperial, then a republic — continuous civic custody throughout, which is rarer than it sounds.

**Haus Seefahrt** is the institution worth naming: a Bremen seafarers' benevolent foundation of the sixteenth century, endowed and self-governing, which still exists. ⚠️ Whether its archive is with the Staatsarchiv or with the foundation is unestablished — **and on the pattern this survey keeps finding, the foundation is the likelier custodian.**

## Why it is not merely a third Hanseatic town

Because **Haus Seefahrt is a pooling form the census does not have**: a maritime mutual-benefit endowment, member-governed, perpetual, and still operating after four centuries. That is the configuration the entity census is emptiest on — it contains no cooperative at all — and it is a *seafaring* one, which ties it to the loss census's maritime rows rather than to the guild-box material.

⚠️ Recorded as a candidate worth a selection brief, not as a coding. Nothing has been read.
