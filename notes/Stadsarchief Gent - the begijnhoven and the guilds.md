# Stadsarchief Gent — the begijnhoven and the guilds

<https://stad.gent/archief> ⚠️ *URL to confirm*

Ghent matters here for one coded form and one uncoded one.

## The beguinages

Ghent had the largest beguinage complex in the Low Countries, and the `begijnhof` row is coded in `organizational_forms`. **Its records are not all in the city archive** — the Groot Begijnhof Gent / Sint-Amandsberg archive is registered separately through Archiefpunt, which is the register for archives in private and institutional custody. See [[Begijnhof archives - where the beguinages records are]] for the general problem.

## The guilds

Ghent's craft guilds are among the best documented in Europe and are the natural comparison set for the guild-box rows already in the loss census. ⚠️ Series unidentified; this is an assertion about a historiography, not about a finding aid.

## Why Ghent is filed after Antwerp and Bruges

Because for the project's spine — maritime peril, company form, transferable claims — it is a secondary site, and saying so is more useful than padding the note. **Its value is to the cooperative and corporate-body questions**, which the entity census is thinnest on: the census contains no cooperative at all, and the Low Countries urban corporations are the obvious European place to look.
