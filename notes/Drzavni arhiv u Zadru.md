# Državni arhiv u Zadru

<https://www.dazd.hr/>

The state archive for northern Dalmatia, holding the record of **Venetian Dalmatia** — and its notarial series run from the thirteenth century.

## Why Zadar rather than only Dubrovnik

**Because Dalmatia was Venetian and Ragusa was not.** Zadar, Split, Trogir and Šibenik were Venetian subject cities; Dubrovnik was an independent republic under nominal Ottoman suzerainty for much of the period.

**So the Adriatic offers two populations of instruments, drawn by notaries in the same sea, in the same Latin, trading with the same partners, under different sovereigns.** That is the configuration [[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]] describes, and the survey has now found it three times — Montpellier, the Basque coast, and here — but here **both sides have deep, surviving notarial series.**

The census's `collegantia` claim is a Venice–Ragusa comparison. **Venetian Dalmatia sits between them**, and if Zadar's instruments follow the Venetian pattern the claim is about law; if they follow the Ragusan, it is about the Adriatic.

⚠️ Series, extents and digitisation unverified. Thirteenth-century Dalmatian notarial records are the subject of an active scholarly literature, which is the cheapest entry.
