# Do not claim history-dependence as the novelty

Economics is not innocent of history-dependence and has not been for forty years. Multiple equilibria and sunspot equilibria; hysteresis in unemployment after Blanchard and Summers; poverty traps; Krugman's economic geography; critical junctures in the Acemoglu–Robinson literature; the entire persistence programme the vault already attacks on other grounds. A manuscript presenting "history matters" as its contribution will be corrected in the first paragraph of the first report, and the correction will be deserved.

**Two claims survive that correction, and only two should be made.**

First, **which average governs** — the hygiene argument. The failure is not that economics uses ensemble averages but that it never developed the discipline of stating the conditions under which time and ensemble averages coincide, and so applies the equivalence unconditionally. See [[Route Kolmogorov to ergodic theory not the theory of means]], and for the specific address at which the assumption is installed in models that do iterate, [[The representative agent is the ergodicity assumption in costume]].

Second, **absorption as a mechanism the path-dependence literature never developed.** That literature runs almost entirely on reinforcement; the eliminative case is formally distinct and unworked, which is the claim of [[Absorption is a path-dependence mechanism distinct from reinforcement]].

Both are narrow. Both are defensible. Neither requires the interlocutor to concede that economics has neglected history, which they will not concede and need not.

## The failure mode this prevents

Overclaiming here is not merely impolite, it is structurally costly. A referee who identifies one overreach reads the rest of the manuscript looking for others, and the project's actually-novel claims — the shielding-as-barrier-relocation argument, the absorption mechanism, the selection reading of institutional survival — are subtler than the overreach and will not survive that reading posture. The general principle is the one the vault already applies to `convergence` versus `parallelism` and to the two senses of lock-in: **claim the narrow thing precisely and the broad thing not at all.**

There is also a specific trap. Because the persistence literature invokes Arthur's lock-in to license its own findings, it is tempting to present the project as more historically-minded than that literature. The opposite framing is stronger and is already recorded: [[Attractor selection bounds what a persistence design can resolve]] shows that the mechanism the literature invokes is the one that costs it the reading. The critique is that they have used the dynamics carelessly, not that they have ignored them.
