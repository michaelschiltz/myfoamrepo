# Parlement de Paris — série X

Série `X` at [[Archives nationales - Paris and Pierrefitte]], with a published *répertoire numérique*.

**The sovereign court of the largest jurisdiction in France**, from the thirteenth century to 1790, and the French member of the litigation family this survey keeps returning to: [[Hof van Holland - the civil litigation record]], [[Real Chancilleria de Valladolid]], `Giudici di Petizion` at [[Archivio di Stato di Venezia]].

## Why the *arrêts* are the evidence

The same reasoning throughout: a contract records what was agreed, a judgment records what the agreement turned out to mean. For the shielding characteristics in particular — whether a partner's creditors reach the firm's assets, in what order, on whose priority — **failure is the only condition under which the answer becomes visible**, and a sovereign court's judgments are where failure was resolved authoritatively.

The Parlement also **registered royal legislation**, which gives série `X` a second use: the *lettres patentes* chartering a company were registered here, often with the court's own remonstrances attached. **A charter plus the objections made to it at registration is a better document than a charter**, and it is precisely the *stated rationale* the coding commons asks for rather than the component presence it usually gets.

⚠️ Extent, the survival of civil as against criminal series, and any imaging are unverified. The series is very large and the finding aids are old.
