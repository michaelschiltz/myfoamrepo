# Archivio di Stato di Milano — the Banco di Sant'Ambrogio

<https://archiviodistatomilano.cultura.gov.it/>

The fourth Italian public-bank case, and the one that completes a set worth having.

## Why Milan

The **Banco di Sant'Ambrogio**, founded **1593**, was a civic bank funded by shares in the city's public debt — a Lombard construction of the same object Genoa built as the *Casa di San Giorgio* and Rome as the *monti camerali*. Four cases now: Genoa, Rome, Naples, Milan. ⚠️ The Sant'Ambrogio records' custody has not been established here — state archive, civic archive, or dispersed — and that is the first thing to settle.

Four independent constructions of transferable claims on a perpetual fiscal body, in four Italian states, within roughly two centuries, is either a family or a repeated arrival. **It is the best-populated comparison the project could run inside a single legal culture**, which is what makes it methodologically awkward as well as valuable: shared ancestral conditions are guaranteed, so the finding can only be parallelism, never convergence in the strict sense. [[Homoplasy is the finding not the noise]] and the `convergence-pair` caution in `tags.md` both apply, and the right claim to make from an Italian-only comparison is about **the conditions under which the form recurs**, not about independence.

## Other fondi

**`Notarile`** — one of the largest notarial archives in Italy. ⚠️ Extent and digitisation unresearched.

**`Fondo di Religione`** — suppressed ecclesiastical institutions, including confraternity and charitable funds. Relevant if the *monte di pietà* or the confraternity fund is ever coded.
