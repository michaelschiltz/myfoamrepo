# Fynn-Paul 2017 on the land commenda of Manresa

Source note for Jeff Fynn-Paul, "The Land Commenda in the Late Medieval Crown of Aragon and the Rise of a 'Democratic' Investment Culture", *TSEG* 14, no. 3 (2017), doi 10.18352/tseg.944 (Zotero W8A2BZYR). The PDF's running pages are 85–107, while its first-page header reads "tseg 14 (3): 5-36"; cite the printed running pages and treat the range as unverified. The DOI does not resolve on CrossRef.

## The corpus

91 commendas tallied from the family notarial books of the Sarta and Talamanca, Arxiu Comarcal del Bages, 1313–1433, set in a database of over 1,200 loans, receipts and part-payments (94). Heyday c. 1280–1348; numbers more than halve after the Black Death and fall to a trickle by the 1380s (102–103, fig. 4).

## Findings relied on

- **The distinction of land and sea was not contemporary.** "Land commenda" is his term; Catalan notaries outside Barcelona drew no such line (91).
- **Brief acts, formulae abridged with "etc."**; duration almost never written, settled orally; one later act lets the capital be returned "quandocunque vobis placuit" but "sine aliqua dilacione … et absque omni dampno" (95–96).
- **Profit share** *ad tertiam partem lucri*: one third to the commendator, two thirds to the tractator (103).
- **Participants**: rural people, tradesmen, knights, a parish priest, Jews; women in about 20 % of acts, three quarters of them with their husbands (99–101). 93 % in cash (96).
- **Decline** read as an effect of falling interest rates and of the spread of *censals* and *violaris* in larger denominations (102–106).

## What his two worked examples actually show

His Example 1 (1318) is drafted "in commanda pura seu deposito … reddere quo velitis" (94 n. 30): a deposit returnable on demand, not a trading comanda. His Example 2 (1323) is a skinner and his wife taking 100 s. "in facto mercaterie ad terciam partem lucre et amissiones" (95 n. 31): **profit and loss** at a third. His translation gives only the profit. The 91 therefore mix at least two of the contracts the word names, and the sample cannot be read as one form without a split; see [[Comanda names several contracts in the Catalan sources]].
