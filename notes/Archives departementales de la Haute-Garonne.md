# Archives départementales de la Haute-Garonne

<https://archives.haute-garonne.fr/> · FranceArchives service record: <https://francearchives.gouv.fr/fr/service/33640>

The departmental archives at Toulouse, and the repository the project has most reason to want and least information about. The custody question is treated separately at [[The Bazacle records - a custody problem]].

## The état des fonds

The archive publishes an *état des fonds* organised on the national frame described at [[The archives departementales system]], with these divisions: *archives anciennes* (before 1790), revolutionary, modern, contemporary, **état civil**, **notaires (14th–20th c.)**, communes, hospitals, public establishments, **archives privées**, iconography, audiovisual and library.

**Two of those are the relevant ones.** *Notaires*, running from the fourteenth century, is where the Toulousain instruments are. *Archives privées* is where a company fonds would sit.

⚠️ The archive states plainly that some fonds have a detailed inventory, some only a summary presentation, **and some are not in the état des fonds at all** — "that does not mean they do not exist", with an invitation to write. For the Bazacle that sentence may be the whole answer.

## Access

⚠️ The online catalogue **refused automated access during this survey**, returning an anti-bot challenge. Digitised material is offered through a *consulter les archives numérisées* section whose coverage is unestablished and, on the national pattern, likely to be weighted to *état civil*.
