# Stora Kopparberg — the 1288 share

Archives described in Riksarkivet's NAD (for example *Gruvrättens arkiv* and *Smedjekontorets arkiv*, `SE/ACD/635`) and at **Arkivcentrum Dalarna** in Falun.

**The rival claimant to the Bazacle, and the census does not code it.**

## The case

The Stora Kopparberg copper mine at Falun was held in **divided shares** from the thirteenth century, and a deed of **1288** recording a transfer of a share in the mine survives. The enterprise operated continuously from then into the modern corporation that became Stora Enso — commonly described as the oldest surviving joint-stock enterprise in the world.

## Why it belongs beside `bazacle_mill` and is not merely a curiosity

The census codes the Bazacle because [[Le Bris Goetzmann Pouget 2023 on convergent evolution toward the joint-stock company]] made it the flagship pre-modern share case. **Stora is the second instance of the same structure in a completely different setting**: not a river mill under a southern French *coutume* but a mine under Swedish law, in a different legal family, with a different king, a different church and a different commercial world.

**Two independent constructions of a transferable share in a perpetual productive asset is a much stronger observation than one** — and it is precisely the configuration [[Divergent arbitrary particulars are the independence diagnostic]] describes. Where the Toulouse *uchaux* and the Falun shares agree in structure and diverge in their incidental drafting, that is evidence about independence rather than descent; where they agree in the incidentals, it is not.

**And the Italian four-case set makes the point sharper.** [[MOC - Italian archives]] records four constructions of transferable claims inside one legal culture, where shared ancestry is guaranteed and the finding can only be parallelism. **Toulouse and Falun are not inside one legal culture**, which is the whole reason the pair is worth more than the quartet.

⚠️ **This is an argument for a selection brief, not a coding.** Nothing has been read; the 1288 deed's terms, the share's transferability, and whether the shares were of the venture or of the physical mine are all unestablished — and that last distinction decides whether the case is what it appears to be.

## Custody

The company's own archives are described through NAD and are partly at Arkivcentrum Dalarna, with a live Swedish discussion of whether the Falun material should be nominated to Memory of the World. ⚠️ How much sits with the corporate successor is unestablished — **and this survey has found that pattern three times already**, at [[Fuggerarchiv - Dillingen]], [[The Bazacle records - a custody problem]] and [[Natie archives - where the Antwerp nations records are]]. Expect it here too.
