# Stadtarchiv Nürnberg

<https://www.nuernberg.de/internet/stadtarchiv/online_recherche.html>

The archive of the other great south German imperial city, with online search over its holdings and a civic historical lexicon alongside it.

## What is here for the project

Nuremberg's merchant capital worked the Italian, Netherlandish and Baltic routes simultaneously, and the city's own institutions are the interest: the **Banco Publico of 1621**, one of the earliest German public banks, and the civic regulation of trade.

**The public-bank thread now runs through four countries.** [[Casa di San Giorgio - the Genoese public debt archive]], [[Archivio di Stato di Milano - the Banco di Sant'Ambrogio]], [[Archivio Storico del Banco di Napoli]], the Amsterdam Wisselbank, and Nuremberg 1621. ⚠️ Whether Nuremberg's belongs with the *transferable-claims* group or with the *deposit and giro* group is a real distinction and is not settled here — **the two are different forms and the literature routinely runs them together.**

That distinction is worth raising before any of these is coded: a bank whose capital is subscribed public debt in transferable shares is a different object from a municipal giro bank with no shareholders, and the census would need separate rows.

⚠️ Everything above is a reason to look. No holding consulted.
