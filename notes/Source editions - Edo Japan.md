# Source editions — Edo Japan

Printed compilations of Edo-period law and commercial records **not already recorded** at [[Historiographical Institute - the Shiryo Hensanjo and the Dai Nihon series]], [[Kyu-bakufu hikitsugisho - the Edo town magistrate records]] and [[Jichitaishi - the municipal histories as Japan's print layer 3]]. **No edited collection reproduces tanomoshi or mujin documents in bulk**; the route to them is the edict collections below, through their indexes, and the municipal histories.

## Law and ordinance

- **高柳眞三・石井良助 (eds), 『御触書集成』** (Tokyo: 岩波書店, 1934–41), 5 vols — the shogunate's own compilations: 寛保 (3,550 edicts, 1615–1743), 宝暦 (2,060, 1744–60), 天明 (3,020, 1761–87), 天保 上・下 (6,607, 1788–1837). **Index: 石井良助・服藤弘司 (eds), 『御触書集成目録』, 3 vols (岩波書店, 2002)** — subject and chronological indexes to c. 15,000 edicts. **The core collection for shogunal rules on mujin, money-changers, interest, nakama and debt suits.** NDL Digital: premises and Japan-resident transmission only.
- **石井良助・服藤弘司 (eds), 『幕末御触書集成』** (岩波書店, 1992–97) — c. 6,000 edicts from 1837, **including the Tenpō dissolution of the nakama (1841) and their restoration (1851)**. ⚠️ Volume count unconfirmed.
- **近世史料研究会 (ed.), 『江戸町触集成』** (Tokyo: 塙書房, 1994–2012), 22 vols (21–22 supplements) — **the Edo magistrates' town ordinances**: mujin, lotteries, nakama, the *machi-kaisho*.
- **『徳川禁令考』** — the Meiji Ministry of Justice compilation (1878–90; later issues), and **石井良助 (ed.), 11 vols (創文社, 1959–61)**. The 後集 carries civil precedent, including debt and mujin cases. **Meiji printings very likely free on NDL** ⚠️ access level to confirm.
- **幸田成友 (comp.), 『大阪市史』** (大阪市参事会, 1911–15) — **vols 3–4 print the Osaka town ordinances (大坂町触)**; index 1970. ⚠️ Free access to confirm volume by volume.

## Osaka commerce and finance

- **大阪市立中央図書館 (ed.), 『大阪編年史』**, 27 vols (1967–79) — year-by-year source extracts to 1867.
- **大阪商工会議所 (ed.), 『大阪商業史資料』**, 35 vols + 別巻 (1963–66) — facsimile of a Meiji manuscript compilation: edicts, prices and **relief (救恤)**, levies, **finance (vols 5–6)**. NDL transmission only.
- **大阪大学近世物価史研究会 (ed.), 『近世大阪の物価と利子』** (創文社, 1963) — **Kōnoike interest-rate series**; and **宮本又次 (ed.), 『大阪の研究』** vols 3–4 (1969–70) with Kōnoike house codes. **No stand-alone edition of the Kōnoike documents exists** — see [[Osaka Museum of History - the Konoike family documents]].

## Merchant houses

- **三井文庫 (ed.), 『三井事業史』 資料篇**, 5 vols (to 2001) — selected Edo and Meiji documents.
- **三井文庫史料叢書 『大坂両替店「聞書」』** (吉川弘文館): vol. 1 1751–1807 (2011), vol. 2 1808–28 (2023), vols 3–7 planned — **the Osaka money-changing branch's intelligence reports**.
- **デジタル版三井文庫史料叢書 — free PDFs (2024–25)**, including an Osaka branch clerk's diary and **the 1833 accounts of the Osaka shogunal treasury**. See [[Mitsui Bunko - the Mitsui house archive]].
- **住友史料館 (ed.), 『住友史料叢書』** (Kyoto: 思文閣出版, 1986– ; vol. 38 in 2025) — house memoranda, the Besshi mine, **and 『札差証文』 (vol. 29, 2014): loan bonds from Sumitomo's Edo *fudasashi* business — direct evidence of credit to shogunal retainers.** See [[Sumitomo Historical Archives - Kyoto]].

## Economic writing and fiscal records

- **滝本誠一 (ed.), 『日本経済叢書』**, 36 vols (1914–17) and **『日本経済大典』**, 54 vols (1928–30; reprints) — Edo treatises on economy and policy; normative writing, not practice. Some 『叢書』 volumes free on NDL.
- **大蔵省 (ed.), 『日本財政経済史料』**, 10 vols + index (1924–25) — bakufu finance, coinage, loans, relief funds.
- **小野武夫 (ed.), 『近世地方経済史料』**, 10 vols (1931–32; repr. 1958) — village handbooks and local economic records: **village kō, relief stores, rural credit**.
- **東京市 / 東京都 (ed.), 『東京市史稿』 市街篇・産業篇** (continuing) — the chronological compilation to read beside 『都史紀要』7 for the *shichibu tsumikin*: [[Tokyo Metropolitan Archives - the Edo town office and the shichibu tsumikin]]. ⚠️ Volume contents not checked.
- **宮本常一 et al. (eds), 『日本庶民生活史料集成』**, 30 vols + 別巻 (三一書房, 1968–84) — vol. 7 on famine and epidemics. Lower priority.
