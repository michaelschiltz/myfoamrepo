# Archivo Historico Provincial de Cadiz — notarial protocols and the Consulado

Catalogue via the Junta de Andalucia's archive system.

**Cadiz is the second half of the Sevillian story, not a separate one.** The Consulado de Cargadores a Indias and the Casa de la Contratacion moved from Sevilla to Cadiz in 1717, and with them the centre of gravity of the Carrera. Any series that looks truncated in Sevilla should be looked for continuing here, and the reverse. Treating either city's holdings as a complete run is the standard error.

## The two fondos that matter

**`Protocolos Notariales`** — the notarial protocols of the province. Eighteenth-century Cadiz was the busiest commercial notariate in the Spanish Atlantic; insurance policies, *companias*, risk loans and freight contracts survive in bulk.

**`Consulado de Cargadores a Indias`** — the corporation's own archive, described in an ISAD record published by the Junta de Andalucia. ⚠️ Reference code, date range and series list need transcribing from that description; the split of Consulado material between the AGI (`CONSULADOS`) and Cadiz has not been established here and is the first thing to settle, because it determines which repository holds the *averia* record after 1717.

## Why it matters for the coding

The eighteenth-century material is where the pooling forms and the corporate forms are visibly in contact — the same merchants appear as parties to a mutual *averia* pool, as shareholders in a privileged company, and as insurers on individual policies. That is the configuration [[The depersonalised share was produced by honour not by pooling logic]] argues about, and Cadiz is where it can be observed in one place.
