# Visby and the Gotland sea law

Riksarkivet's *Landsarkivet i Visby*; Gotland's court records described through NAD.

## Why Visby is in the set

The **Laws of Wisby** — the *Waterrecht* attributed to Gotland — are one of the medieval sea laws that circulated in the Baltic and North Sea alongside the Rôles d'Oléron and the *Consolato del Mare*, and they treat jettison, contribution and the master's authority: **the general-average material, in its northern recension.**

The census codes `general_average` from Mediterranean and Dutch evidence. **The northern sea law is a third recension of the same rules**, and where it differs the difference is the evidence.

⚠️ **The important caution is textual, not archival.** The Laws of Wisby are a compilation whose attribution to Visby is contested and whose manuscript tradition is Low German and Dutch as much as Gotlandic. **The archive at Visby is not where the text is, and there is no reason to think it would be** — this is a question for the manuscript tradition and the printed editions, not for a local *landsarkiv*.

Recorded here so that the distinction is made rather than discovered: **a law named for a place is not evidence about that place.** That is [[A migrated institution collects local names not local forms]] applied to a legal text rather than to an institution.
