# The theory predicts recurrence not independent origins

The ergodicity argument entails that institutions for pooling and mutualising risk should appear wherever multiplicative dynamics and long horizons obtain. It says nothing whatever about whether those appearances are causally independent of one another. **Independent origin is not a prediction of the theory. It is the elimination of a rival explanation.**

Fusing the two is a self-inflicted wound, and it costs twice. It makes the theory answerable for a claim it never made, so that any successful diffusion story looks like a refutation of the mathematics. And it converts an evidentiary task — showing that recurrence is arrival rather than inheritance — into an assumption, which is precisely the move [[Galton's problem]] forbids.

Held apart, there are two claims with two tests and two failure conditions. The **functional** claim is that beneath divergent idioms these institutions discharge the same time-average function; it fails if the traditions' own rationales refuse that reading. The **origination** claim is that some part of the recurrence is arrival rather than inheritance; it is harder, it is not assumed, and it is tested at the one seam that survives scrutiny once contact is admitted.

The separation also reveals how much survives the worst case. Suppose total diffusion — one template, travelling. The question of why the form was *retained* in every environment it reached is untouched, and retention under selection is a time-average question in exactly the sense the framework specifies. A form that spread and was everywhere abandoned would be a different finding from one that spread and everywhere stuck. **Regime-dependent retention is transmission-proof**, because the selection question is separable from the origination question: see [[Time-average optimization is a survivorship property not an intention]] and [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]].

So the framework is not hostage to the independence result at all. It is hostage only to a formulation that promised more than the mathematics delivers. Drop the promise and the theory is more robust, the comparison more honest, and the evidentiary burden lands where it can actually be discharged.
