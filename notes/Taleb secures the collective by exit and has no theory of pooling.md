# Taleb secures the collective by exit and has no theory of pooling

**In ch. 4 of *Antifragile* the collective survives by selection: units fail so that the aggregate improves, provided their failures are independent. Collective survival by pooling, the mechanism that moves the unit's time average together with the aggregate's, is absent from the book. That absence is why his ethics of the collective ends in sacrifice and honour, and why the project's forms fall outside it.**

## The two-level structure

Ch. 4 ("Antifragility by Layers", "Why the Aggregate Hates the Individual") argues from restaurants and start-ups to "local, but not global, overconfidence." In the vault's terms there are two kinds of trajectory:

- the aggregate has one trajectory and must stay off its own barrier;
- the units form an ensemble whose individual absorptions are tolerable, even productive, if they are uncorrelated.

That is the supercritical branching picture of [[Galton-Watson is the extinction branch Peters named and did not develop]]: each lineage has a positive probability of extinction while the whole persists. Taleb's condition — "a system in which nobody's fall can drag others down", airlines against banks — is the independence condition. It is the same ρ that sets [[Pooling's sign is set by the correlation of its baskets]]. Contagion disables selection for the same reason correlation disables pooling.

## What is missing

On Taleb's account the unit's interest and the aggregate's diverge: the aggregate "wants harm to the parts." He says he dislikes the conclusion: "As a humanist, I stand against the antifragility of systems at the expense of individuals." His remedy is compensation in status. The failed entrepreneur is to be honoured as the dead soldier is, on a National Entrepreneur Day. **Nowhere in the book is there a device that moves the unit's own time average and the aggregate's together.**

Peters and Adamou supply exactly that device. Pooling raises each participant's time-average growth rate where the expectation is unchanged; see [[Cooperation is an averaging puzzle and sovereign repayment is a barrier puzzle]]. The two mechanisms are distinct and must not be merged:

- **Exit (selection).** The collective gains from individual absorption. Requires failures to be independent. Leaves survivorship in the record.
- **Pooling (risk-sharing).** The collective gains from shared variance, and no absorption is required. Requires imperfect correlation. Leaves institutions behind: the *qirāḍ*, the commenda, the retail pass-through of [[Syndication and the misma moneda are pooling not prudence]].

The project's forms belong to the second mechanism, and Taleb's ch. 4 is a theory of the first only. Cite it for the independence condition and for the survivorship inversion in "What Does Not Kill Me Kills Others". Do not cite it as an account of why the forms exist.

## Caution on the frame

Ch. 4 runs on organisms and populations, by way of Danchin. Transpose only the dynamics. The project's units are instruments, not populations ([[Never let the units slide from instruments to populations]]), and Taleb's biological framing is precisely what that rule refuses.
