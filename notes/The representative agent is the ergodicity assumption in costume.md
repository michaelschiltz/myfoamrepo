# The representative agent is the ergodicity assumption in costume

The critique must not be aimed at the absence of time. A referee will answer *Ramsey 1928* before finishing the paragraph and will be right: recursive competitive equilibrium, dynamic stochastic general equilibrium and balanced growth paths all iterate. Aiming there loses the exchange in one move.

**Aim at the averaging performed at each step.** Modern macroeconomics iterates and then, at every date, takes an expectation across the ensemble of possible continuations. Rational expectations *is* an ensemble operator. And the representative agent is the device that makes this harmless by construction: a single agent standing for the aggregate has, by definition, the aggregate's trajectory, so the cross-sectional average and the individual path coincide. The equivalence that ought to be a checkable hypothesis is instead a property of the modelling primitive. Nothing is assumed away in the derivation; the assumption sits in the choice of what an agent is.

Two further devices complete the enclosure. Transversality and no-Ponzi conditions exclude the paths that would absorb, and interior-solution assumptions ensure the boundary is never reached — so [[An absorbing barrier breaks ergodicity by construction]], the project's strongest formal card, describes an object the framework has already ruled out of its state space.

Stated this way the objection is narrow, technically correct and hard to deflect, because it does not require the interlocutor to concede anything about time, history or dynamics. It requires only that they say where the ergodic hypothesis is stated and how it is checked. That is the hygiene demand of [[Route Kolmogorov to ergodic theory not the theory of means]] with a specific address, and it should be pressed in the **econometric** sense of the word — the third of the four in [[The word ergodic carries four incompatible senses]] — because that is the sense the tradition already recognises as an assumption capable of failing.

## The stationary-distribution reply, and its answer

The expected counter is that stochastic models with heterogeneous agents do not assume a representative agent and deliver a stationary cross-sectional distribution, which is a dynamic object.

A stationary distribution is an **ensemble object**, and its stability says nothing about any path within it. The distribution of wealth can be constant while every individual trajectory diverges; convergence of the distribution and convergence of trajectories are different statements, and the literature routinely reports the first as though it licensed the second. The empirical form of this reply is already in the library — Berman, Peters and Adamou on the ergodic hypothesis in US wealth data, and Stojkoski et al. on geometric Brownian motion with stochastic resetting, where inequality is regime-dependent and mobility measures behave quite differently from the distribution. Relatedly, [[Stationarity is a precondition of ergodicity not a corollary]]: a stationary law is necessary for the equivalence and nowhere near sufficient.
