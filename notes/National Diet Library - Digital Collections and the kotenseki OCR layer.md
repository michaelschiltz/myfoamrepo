# National Diet Library — Digital Collections and the *kotenseki* OCR layer

国立国会図書館デジタルコレクション, <https://dl.ndl.go.jp/>.

## The layer, as of September 2026

- **About 4.23 million items full-text searchable.**
- **1 September 2026: about 75,000 *kotenseki* added to full-text search**, their text generated by **NDL古典籍OCR ver. 3** from public-domain digitised classical books.
- Additions in batches of tens of thousands at a time through 2025–26.

**That settles one of the "to verify" items in [[Kuzushiji OCR - the NDL and CODH stack]]: ver. 3 is still the production model at NDL as of this month.** And it makes the NDL the first Japanese repository where **pre-modern manuscript and woodblock material is at layer 3 by machine, at scale** — with exactly the failure mode that note warns of: grammatically well-formed errors, highest in numerals and names.

## Three access tiers, and the middle one is a wall

1. **Internet-public** — anyone, anywhere.
2. **Transmission service for individuals** — **registered users resident in Japan only**. See [[NDL transmission service for individuals - the residency wall]].
3. **In-library only.**

## What it holds for the census

- **[[Kyu-bakufu hikitsugisho - the Edo town magistrate records]]** — the *kabu nakama* and money-market record of Edo, digitised except for some items.
- **Printed municipal and prefectural histories** — the source-volumes that are Japan's print layer 3 — largely in tiers 2 and 3. See [[Jichitaishi - the municipal histories as Japan's print layer 3]].
- **NDL is where the book behind a census row is most likely to be**: every Japanese row was coded from a published transcription.
