# Archives départementales du Nord — the Chambre des comptes de Lille

<https://archivesdepartementales.lenord.fr/>

**The Burgundian archive that is in France.** The Chambre des comptes de Lille audited the accounts of the Burgundian and Habsburg Low Countries, and its records are série `B` at Lille — with an *inventaire sommaire* published in multiple volumes from the nineteenth century and **available on Gallica and the Internet Archive**.

## Why it belongs in the French set and the Belgian argument

Because the polity it served is the one [[MOC - Belgian archives]] describes, and its financial record ended up on the French side of a border drawn in 1668. **A researcher working the Low Countries from Brussels and Antwerp will miss it**, which is the same custody trap as [[Rijksarchief te Antwerpen and Beveren - the notarial series]] operating at international scale.

## The finding aid is at layer 3

Bruchet's *inventaire sommaire* and *répertoire numérique* are digitised and full-text on Gallica and archive.org. **That makes a nineteenth-century description of a medieval fiscal archive searchable**, which is the pattern already noted at [[Gallica and RetroNews - the French full-text layer]] and is the cheapest access route in the French set.

⚠️ It is a nineteenth-century summary inventory, with a nineteenth-century selection of what to summarise. Use it to find; never to count.
