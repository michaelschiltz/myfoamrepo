# Kuyûd-ı Kadîme Arşivi — Ankara

The "old records" archive of the **Tapu ve Kadastro Genel Müdürlüğü**, the land registry. It holds the half of the Ottoman *Defterhâne* — the imperial register office — that did not go to Istanbul: **the survey (*tahrir*) registers and the land-registry series are divided between this archive and [[Osmanli Arsivi - Istanbul]]** (*TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi*, "Defterhâne"). Iranica gives 2,322 *tahrir* registers here. ⚠️ Count not confirmed.

## Why it has a note

**Because a *tahrir* is the only Ottoman source in this pass that could be a denominator.** A provincial survey lists the endowments of a district and their revenues at a date; separate *evkaf tahrir* registers do so for waqfs alone. The land registry publishes some in transliteration — the *Defter-i Evkâf-ı Livâ-i Kuds-i Şerîf* for Jerusalem is one.

**That is exactly the kind of series the European survey found once — the Sound Toll registers — and valued above everything else.** A count of how many waqfs a district carried, and of what kind, at two survey dates, would let the census say whether the forms it codes were common or exceptional.

## The honest assessment

**Marginal for coding, possibly important for scope.** A survey records that an endowment existed and what it yielded; it says nothing about how a fund was governed, who bore a loss, or whether contributors were beneficiaries — **nothing any census characteristic asks**. The *tahrir* campaigns also thinned out after the sixteenth century, while the census's Ottoman rows are seventeenth to nineteenth century. ⚠️ Period overlap not checked. **Not a destination until a selection brief asks a frequency question.**
