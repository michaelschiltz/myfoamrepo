# Rigsarkivet Denmark — Daisy and Arkivalieronline

<https://www.rigsarkivet.dk/> · catalogue **Daisy**, <https://daisy.rigsarkivet.dk/> · images **Arkivalieronline**, <https://arkivalieronline.rigsarkivet.dk/>

The Danish national archives, holding the record of a composite monarchy that at various points included Norway, Iceland, Schleswig-Holstein, tropical colonies and the Sound.

## What is digitised and thematically organised

Arkivalieronline presents **themed collections**, and two are directly relevant:

- **`Ostindisk og Asiatisk Kompagni`** — the Danish East India and Asiatic companies
- **Dansk Vestindien** — the Danish West Indies, with its own archival guide, plus Trankebar

**Themed presentation of a company's records is unusual and useful**: it means the digitisation was organised around an institution rather than around a record type, which is the opposite of the genealogically-driven selection this survey has met everywhere else.

## And the Sound

[[The Sound Toll Registers]] are here. That alone would make this a first-rank repository.
