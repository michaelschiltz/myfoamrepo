# Isqa qirad and commenda are a liability-variable triad

The three forms circulate in the same tenth-to-twelfth-century Mediterranean, over shared routes, sometimes among the same merchants. Route, cargo, distance and information environment are approximately held; **agent liability varies**. That is a research design, and it is worth more than the tired commenda-to-corporation diffusion narrative.

Be precise about what the design can and cannot do.

**It is not an independence test.** A shared ancestral substrate across all three is not merely possible but likely ([[The qirad's antecedents are Sasanian not Sogdian]]). Any similarity here is therefore parallelism, never convergence in the strict sense — see [[Homoplasy is the finding not the noise]]. Filing this under recurrence would be a category error; it belongs in the design chapter, not the recurrence chapter.

**What it does do** is isolate the shielding variable while holding the trade environment fixed. It answers *what does the shield do* rather than *did the shield arrive twice*. Those are different questions and the project needs both; the near-identity that disqualifies the triad for the second is exactly what qualifies it for the first.

**It is robust to the origins dispute.** Pryor (1977) put real pressure on Udovitch's Islamic-origin thesis for the commenda, favouring indigenous development from the sea loan. The structural contrast survives either verdict, because it does not depend on who borrowed from whom. This is [[Borrow the diagnostics not the tree]] applied: take the liability diagnostic, leave the genealogy.

## Where this is soft

Terminological drift will eat the comparison if unpoliced. "*ʿIsqa*" in Geniza documents frequently labels an Islamic-form contract in Hebrew or Aramaic dress. The unit has to be the **clause structure of the instrument**, never the name on it — cf. [[Natie is a false cognate]] and [[Never let the units slide from instruments to populations]].
