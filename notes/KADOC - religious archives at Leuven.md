# KADOC — religious archives at Leuven

<https://kadoc.kuleuven.be/>

The KU Leuven documentation and research centre for religion, culture and society, and **the reason a Belgian search that starts at the state archives will miss a great deal**.

## What it is, structurally

A **university** centre holding the archives of religious congregations, orders and Catholic organisations — material that in another country would be in a state or diocesan archive. It also runs a heritage-advice function for religious institutions deciding what to do with their records, which means it is simultaneously a repository and a broker of custody.

For this project that makes it the Belgian counterpart to [[IISG and NEHA - Amsterdam]]: a non-state institution whose collecting policy covers precisely the ground the entity census is thinnest on.

## What it might hold for the census

**Beguinage records**, as one of four possible custodians — see [[Begijnhof archives - where the beguinages records are]].

**Confraternity and charitable-fund records.** The loss census already codes `confraternity_fund_it` and the guild-box rows, and the Low Countries confraternity is an obvious comparison that has not been made. ⚠️ Whether KADOC's holdings reach back before the nineteenth century in quantity is **unestablished and should not be assumed** — a centre founded to document modern Catholic social organisation may hold very little of the medieval and early modern record.

That caveat is the one to test first, because it decides whether this note is about a repository or about a referral service.
