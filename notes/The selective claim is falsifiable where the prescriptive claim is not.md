# The selective claim is falsifiable where the prescriptive claim is not

The attack in [[Toda 2023 on ergodicity economics as pseudoscience]] lands on the *prescriptive* limb of ergodicity economics — whether the framework tells an investor which portfolio to hold, and whether it can solve a consumption–investment problem with labour income and intermediate consumption. **The project does not use that limb**, and the distinction is not a dodge but the vault's oldest discipline.

The claim here is selective, not prescriptive: [[Time-average optimization is a survivorship property not an intention]]. No historical actor computed a growth rate; arrangements whose realised trajectories tracked the time average persisted, and the alternatives crossed the barrier and were removed from the sample. That is a claim about a filter operating on a population of institutional forms over centuries, and its testable content is of a completely different kind from a portfolio prescription.

## What the selective claim forbids

Falsifiability is a matter of what a theory prohibits, so the content has to be stated as prohibitions rather than as a research agenda.

- **Prices.** [[A contingency clause priced at zero is evidence for a time-average criterion]] is the sharpest instance the vault holds: a verifiable contingency written at no premium or a negative one is what a growth-rate criterion predicts and an anomaly for expected utility. That is a number in a notarial archive that one account predicts and the other does not. Find such clauses systematically priced at a positive actuarial premium and the reading fails.
- **Dispersion.** [[Brown tests equality where a risk reading predicts dispersion]] — a risk reading and an equality reading of the same institution predict different second moments, and the data can separate them.
- **Refusals.** [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] and the double-refusal of the sea loan: the theory predicts *which* instruments are declined across independent traditions, and a tradition refusing on grounds unrelated to ruin exposure would count against it.
- **Codings.** The `organizational_forms` and `loss_mitigation_forms` matrices commit to specific values per form in advance of the argument, which is what makes [[Isqa qirad and commenda are a liability-variable triad]] and the *asiento* recoding falsifiable rather than illustrative — the *asiento* coding already falsified an asserted character dependence, which is the pattern working as intended.
- **Enforcement.** [[Institutions substitute state-verification for outcome-inference as ruin exposure rises]] is a comparative prediction with a sign, and the sign can come out wrong.

None of these requires anyone to hold the growth-optimal portfolio, and none is a reformulation of Kelly.

## The move, stated once

**By Toda's own criterion the historical application is better placed than the framework it borrows from.** A programme accused of producing no new testable content over twelve years is answered by producing testable content in a domain it has not entered — which is what an application to the archival record is. The accusation, taken seriously rather than resented, is an instruction to state the prohibitions, and the vault is already organised to do that.

Do not make this argument triumphantly and do not make it as a defence of Peters. It is not a defence of Peters; it is a demarcation between what we use and what we do not, and it obliges us to say plainly that the portfolio-prescription limb is not ours to defend.

## The obligation this creates

The claim above is only as good as the prohibitions, and they must be stated *in advance* of the evidence in every case. Where the vault has not done that, it must say so: [[Bad luck is what the ensemble average calls the modal trajectory]] already refuses the strong Spanish-decline claim on exactly this ground, because naming the multiplicative variable and showing it compounds has not been done. That refusal is the model. A framework that explains any outcome after the fact by locating a multiplicative variable *post hoc* would deserve the pseudoscience charge, and would deserve it more than Peters does — see the mirror-image warning in [[Restate the cost puzzle as a question about a trajectory]] that explaining everything by growth rates is no better than explaining everything by expectations.
