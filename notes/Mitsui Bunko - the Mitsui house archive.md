# Mitsui Bunko — the Mitsui house archive

公益財団法人三井文庫, Kamitakada, Nakano, Tokyo. <https://mitsui-bunko.or.jp/>

**About 100,000 Mitsui documents**, Edo period to the postwar decades: the ***Ōmotokata* account books** of the house's central office, **half-yearly for some 160 years**; the **money-exchange houses' (*ryōgaeten*) ledgers**, some over 40 cm thick; the drapery (*gofukuten*) records.

## The custody chain — pattern 2, with a detour through the state

- **1891** the house begins compiling its history; **1903** a formal history office; **1918** the Mitsui Bunko founded in the Togoshi villa.
- **24 May 1945** the building burns in an air raid, and valuable material is lost.
- **1964** a memorandum with the **Ministry of Education on the return** of the materials; **1965** the Bunko re-founded as a foundation.

**A private house archive passed out of private hands to the state and came back.** ⚠️ The postwar route — presumably to the Ministry's Shiryōkan, the forerunner of the NIJL Archives — and what share of the holdings made the round trip, are not established from what was read. **The European survey's successor-custody instances never did this.**

## Why it is the most important business archive for HistorEE

**Because it is the money market's own record, kept by a participant for 160 years in series.** A house that ran *ryōgae* in Edo, Kyoto and Osaka and served as shogunal financial agent is where clearing and settlement were *done*. **The census's `clearing_records` dataset is synthetic** — see [[The clearing records dataset has no archive yet]] — **and this is the first archive a real one would be drawn from.**

## Access and layer

- **Reading room for researchers.** ⚠️ Eligibility, application procedure, photography and fees not captured.
- Layer 1–2 in print through ***Mitsui jigyōshi*** (1971–2001) and the annual ***Mitsui Bunko ronsō***. **Not online at the document level.**
