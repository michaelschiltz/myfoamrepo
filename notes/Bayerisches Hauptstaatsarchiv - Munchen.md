# Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv — München

<https://www.gda.bayern.de/> — the Bavarian archive administration, which runs the Hauptstaatsarchiv and the regional *Staatsarchive* including [[Stadtarchiv Augsburg and the Staatsarchiv]]

The principal territorial-state archive of the German south, and the one that inherited the records of the imperial cities and ecclesiastical territories mediatised into Bavaria in 1803–06.

## The point about mediatisation, which is general

**The secularisation and mediatisation of 1803–06 moved an enormous quantity of civic and ecclesiastical archives into territorial state custody**, and then the nineteenth-century state reorganisations moved them again.

That is the German equivalent of the dissolution settlement recorded at [[Begijnhof archives - where the beguinages records are]]: **an administrative event that sorted documents by their new owner rather than by their subject.** A free city's commercial record and its religious houses' property record could end up in different buildings under different states, and neither where the city was.

**The practical rule:** for any German institution before 1800, ask what happened to its territory in 1803, not only where it was. ⚠️ This is a statement about a process, not about specific holdings, and each case has to be traced individually.
