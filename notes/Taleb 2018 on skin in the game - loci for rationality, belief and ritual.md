# Taleb 2018 on skin in the game — loci for rationality, belief and ritual

Nassim Nicholas Taleb, *Skin in the Game: Hidden Asymmetries in Daily Life* (New York: Random House, 2018), first edition, ISBN 978-0-425-28462-9. Zotero `TFADV9XP`, citation key `talebSkinGameHidden2018`. **Read this session: Book 3 ch. 2 (opening sections), Book 7 (chs. 15–17), Book 8 (chs. 18–19) and the epilogue.** Loci are given by book, chapter and section heading; the Zotero PDF carries no print pagination [verify page numbers against the hardback before citing].

**Book 8 is where the ethic of *Antifragile* acquires its formal name.** Ch. 18 announces ergodicity ("Ergodicity First"), ch. 19 explains it with the casino thought experiment and names Peters and Gell-Mann, without a dated reference in the passage [verify the notes for the citation]. This resolves the chapter-number flag in [[Taleb 2012 on antifragility - loci for the time-ensemble distinction]]. Earlier chapters, notably ch. 7 on inequality, which ch. 19 cross-refers to, have not been checked for the term.

## Rationality and belief — ch. 18, "How to Be Rational About Rationality"

- **Opening.** Belief is an instrument for action, not its product; the eye is built for useful orientation, not accurate representation.
- **"Ocular Deception."** Entasis and the curved stylobate of the Parthenon, after Vitruvius: distortion in the service of correct perception.
- **"Ergodicity First."** No criterion of rationality exists for actions that bear no cost; Santa Claus is innocent unless it harms. Survival precedes truth, understanding and science; *primum vivere, deinde philosophari*. Ruin defined as the absorbing barrier.
- **Fig. 3.** The bias–variance trade-off: the biased, low-dispersion shooter keeps away from the periphery.
- **"From Simon to Gigerenzer."** Bounded rationality; ecological rationality.
- **"Revelation of Preferences."** Binmore. Three maxims: beliefs are not to be judged scientifically; rationality belongs to action, not belief; action is judged in evolutionary terms. Errors of small cost generate discovery.
- **"What Is Religion About?"** Religion enforces tail-risk management across generations because its rules are binary, unconditional, and so easy to teach and enforce. Diamond's "constructive paranoia" in Papua New Guinea. Fig. 4 caption: science is mainly rigour in the process.
- **"'Tawk' and Cheap 'Tawk'."** Decorative beliefs against beliefs that map to action; belief is measured by what one will risk for it.
- **"What Does Lindy Say?"** *Sōphrosynē* against post-Weberian "rationality"; the rational is what allows survival, at any layer.
- **"The Nondecorative in the Decorative."** Look at the survival of the populations that hold a belief, not at competition between beliefs. Kashrut: some five hundred interdicts, four sets of dishes, two sinks; cohesion ("people who eat together hang together", called a convex heuristic) and trust across remote commerce. Everything that survives survives for a reason. Rationality is risk management.

## Ruin and ergodicity — ch. 19, "The Logic of Risk Taking"

- **Fig. 5 and opening.** One hundred people at the casino against one person a hundred times; ensemble probability against time probability; the uncle point.
- **"Ergodicity."** Non-ergodic where past frequencies do not apply to the future because of an absorbing barrier. Russian roulette's expected return is "not computable".
- **"Repetition of Exposures."** The one-shot experiment against serial exposure; smoking; every risk taken shortens life expectancy; ruin is indivisible and invariant to its source. Risk aversion as "a residual of ergodicity". House-money betting defended against Thaler's "mental accounting".
- **"Who Is 'You'?"** Layers: self, kin, tribe, humanity, ecosystem. The precautionary principle concerns the top layer: Taleb, Read, Douady, Norman and Bar-Yam 2014 (Zotero `Z4UPUWEN`) is the formal referent.
- **"Courage and Precaution Aren't Opposites."** Fig. 6. Courage is sacrifice of one's own layer for the survival of a higher one.
- **"Rationality, Again."** If superstition is what ergodicity needs, obstructing it is irrational. Buffett's filter; refined paranoia need be right only once.
- **"Love *Some* Risks", "Naive Empiricism", "Summary."** Risk-loving yet ruin-averse; Mediocristan against Extremistan; the Chernoff bound. Rationality is avoidance of systemic ruin.

## Religion — Book 7

- **Ch. 15, "They Don't Know What They Are Talking About When They Talk About Religion."** *Dīn* as law in Hebrew and religion in Arabic; law in antiquity as procedurally and mechanically its own thing; the Theodosian Code Christianised only in its preface. **"Belief vs. Belief":** beliefs decorative, functional or literal; Gibbon on modes of worship true, false and useful. **"Libertarianism and Church-Free Religions":** Julian's attempt to give paganism bishops.
- **Ch. 16, "No Worship Without Skin in the Game."** Breaking the Lenten fast; belief cannot be a free option, against Pascal. **"The Gods Do Not Like Cheap Signaling":** the blood drain at Mar Sarkis, Maaloula; *qorban*; Ashura; Hebrews 9:22; the Eucharist as simulacrum of sacrifice; Maimonides on the gradual transfer of sacrifice; Halbertal on post-Isaac reciprocity. **"The Evidence":** a creed's strength rests on the worshippers' skin in the game, not on evidence of its gods' powers.
- **Ch. 17, "Is the Pope Atheist?"** John Paul II taken to the Gemelli clinic, not to a chapel. **"Religious in Words":** atheism defined in deeds, by distance from an atheist's action in the same situation, on matters where life is at stake; most Orthodox and Catholic Christians are atheist in action and religious in words.

## Minority rule — ch. 2, "The Most Intolerant Wins"

- **Opening and fig. 1.** The kosher lemonade at the NECSI barbecue.
- **"Criminals with Peanut Allergies."** The asymmetry: the observant will not eat the non-compliant, the non-observant will eat the compliant. Conditions: even spatial distribution; a small cost differential; and, against halal in Christian countries, a counter-taboo (Al-Akhtal refusing sacrificial meat). Slaughter rules traced to burdensome sacrifice. Organic food and transgenic crops; renormalisation.

## Companion loci in *Antifragile*

The Zotero copy (`XF4VWB3K`) is the Penguin edition of 2016, ISBN 978-0-14-103822-3. The 1 October loci note read the Random House ebook of 2012. The Penguin PDF's footnote anchors carry print-page markers, which may be the route to the Penguin pagination that note lacks [verify].

- **Ch. 17, "Fat Tony Debates Socrates."** *Euthyphro*; knowledge one cannot define; **"Primacy of Definitional Knowledge"**, with Nietzsche on the unintelligible against the unintelligent, and Cato, Burke, Oakeshott, Wittgenstein, Lévi-Strauss and Gray under **"Tradition"**; **"The Sucker-Nonsucker Distinction"** and **"Fragility, Not Probability"**: payoff over True and False.
- **Ch. 21, "Nature's Opaque Logic."** **"Guilty or Innocent":** the burden of proof on whoever departs from nature. **"Plead Ignorance of Biology: Phenomenology":** theories change, the effect stays; keep practice robust to changes in theory. The empiricists and methodists against the rationalists.
- **Ch. 22, "To Live Long, but Not Too Long."** **"Religion and Naive Interventionism"**: religion keeps the marginal patient from the doctor. **"If It's Wednesday, I Must Be Vegan"**: heuristics of religion followed blindly; fasting tames the iatrogenics of abundance; **"Convexity Effects and Random Nutrition"**: Orthodox fasts (fish banned, shellfish allowed, explained by luxury status); **"How to Eat Yourself"**: autophagy, Longo.

Also: Haug and Taleb, "Option Traders Use (Very) Sophisticated Heuristics, Never the Black–Scholes–Merton Formula," *Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization* 77, no. 2 (2011): 97–106, DOI `10.1016/j.jebo.2010.09.013`, Zotero `RNXVCCFB` (abstract read, paper not).
