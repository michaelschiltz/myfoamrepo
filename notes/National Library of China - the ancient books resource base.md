# National Library of China — the ancient books resource base

国家图书馆 中华古籍资源库. <https://www.nlc.cn/pcab/zy/zhgj_zyk/>

**The mainland's free image base for rare books**, fed by the National Library and partner libraries in annual joint releases. On 10 January 2025, eight institutions released **18,705 titles and items**, and the national cumulative total of ancient books and special collections released as images passed **158,000**. Free to the public.

## The honest assessment

**Marginal for the census, and the Japanese lesson applies unchanged**: see [[Books are imaged and documents are not - the Japanese digital split]]. **Books — gazetteers, collected works, commercial manuals — are imaged; the contracts and account books the census needs are not in this programme.** Its use is for the printed context of an institution: a gazetteer's account of a granary, a lineage's printed rules. ⚠️ Whether genealogies are included at scale not checked; see [[Chinese genealogies - Shanghai Library and FamilySearch]].
