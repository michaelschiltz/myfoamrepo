# Chinese contracts — the private document boom and the market that feeds it

**The Chinese commercial and social record is private contracts (契约文书)**, and since the 1980s their discovery "has become a commonplace" (Zelin 2019, 330). Land sales, tenancies, household divisions, loans, **partnership and share agreements**, association account books — kept in the household's contract box. **The census's Chinese institutions live here, not in the state archive.**

## Scale, and where it sits

- **Huizhou (southern Anhui)**: more than 200,000 documents known and probably more than 300,000 — see [[Huizhou documents - the largest private contract corpus]].
- **The Qingshui River (Guizhou)**: about 300,000 in private hands — see [[Qingshui River documents - forest contracts in shares]].
- **Zigong (Sichuan)**: the salt-well share contracts — see [[Zigong salt contracts - shareholding in the wells]].
- **Collectors and university centres**: the **Tian Tao** collection, which Zelin cites; **Cao Shuji's** Shanghai Jiao Tong project, which he likened to "several thousands" of household contract boxes.

## What it does to the evidence

**1. The unit is the household box.** The best collections keep documents together by the household they came from (归户性). **A contract read without its box is an instance without a context** — a partnership agreement whose dissolution, lawsuit or buy-out is in the same box, or nowhere.

**2. The route is the market.** Contracts reached institutions through waves of discovery, sale and purchase — in Huizhou from the 1940s, the 1950s and the 1980s onward. **Pattern 2 inverted, as at the Geniza: acquirer custody.** And the CLIR manual's advice to foreign researchers — collect from online platforms and flea markets — **makes the market an access route as well as a provenance problem.** ⚠️ Forgery in the contract market is a known concern and was not researched here.

**3. Layer 3 is the scholar's compilation.** Contracts are read in printed selections; **the census's own Chinese rows rest on one** — [[Zhang Chuanxi 1995 - the contract compilation behind the census's Chinese rows]].

**4. The script column is not "classical Chinese".** Contracts are in formulaic documentary Chinese in running hands; **account books use commercial numeral systems** — the Suzhou numerals among them — which, like siyakat, few readers and no general OCR handle. ⚠️ From general knowledge; not checked against a source in this pass.
