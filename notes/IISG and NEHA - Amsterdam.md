# IISG and NEHA — Amsterdam

<https://iisg.amsterdam/> · <https://www.neha.nl/>

The International Institute of Social History and the **Nederlandsch Economisch-Historisch Archief**, whose collections and economic-history library the IISG holds. **Not a state archive**, and therefore easy to miss from any search that begins at [[Nationaal Archief - The Hague]] — the Dutch instance of the state/non-state split that [[SIAS SIUSA and SAN - the Italian access layers]] makes structural for Italy.

## What is here that is nowhere else

**Business archives.** Firm-level records — partnerships, shipping houses, trading companies — which the public archives hold only where a firm's papers happened to reach municipal custody.

**Price currents and serial commercial data.** The NEHA collection is the classic source base for Dutch price and wage series, and it is the paper counterpart to what [[Delpher - the full-text layer]] holds in newsprint.

**Mutual and cooperative organisations.** The IISG's core collecting area is labour and social movements, which means **mutual aid societies, cooperatives and friendly societies** — and that is the cooperative pole the entity census is short of. The loss census is full of mutual institutions and the entity census contains no cooperative at all; if that gap is to be closed from Dutch material, the records are here rather than in a state archive.

⚠️ Nothing in the paragraph above has been checked against a finding aid. It is a claim about a collecting policy, not about holdings, and the specific collections need identifying before any of it is relied on.

## Why it also matters as infrastructure

The IISG is a GLOBALISE partner, and its Humanities-Cluster connections are the reason the Dutch HTR stack exists at all — see [[Loghi - the KNAW-Nationaal Archief HTR pipeline]]. An institution that is simultaneously a repository and a builder of transcription pipelines is unusual, and worth approaching as both.
