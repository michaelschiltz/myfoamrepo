# The tontine is pooling in form and dispersal in function

The tontine carries every formal marker of a pooling arrangement — plural contributors, a common fund, a defined membership, a distribution rule — and transforms the dispersion of individual outcomes in the opposite direction. A fixed aggregate flow is concentrated onto surviving trajectories, so the arrangement transfers value from short paths to long ones and widens terminal dispersion relative to the unpooled counterfactual. Harris's `MC1=pooling` is the correct category and it is silent on this. Any identification of pooling that runs on formal markers therefore misclassifies the form at precisely the point where the ergodicity criterion does its work.

This is a *second* way pooling can fail, and it must not be collapsed into the first. [[Pooling's sign is set by the correlation of its baskets]] fixes the sign by ρ: pooling a covariate hazard averages nothing and merely relabels N members as a single unit. The *avería* fails that way. The tontine's hazard is idiosyncratic — mortality across unrelated nominees is about as decorrelated as an early modern hazard gets — so it passes the ρ test and fails anyway. Correlation and redistribution direction are independent failure modes, and a framework that names only the first will certify the tontine as sound pooling.

The discriminating observable is **loss-correspondence**: whether a transfer is conditioned on the recipient's own adverse realisation. In a mutual widows' fund, in indemnity insurance, in general average, value flows toward the party who suffered. In a tontine it flows toward parties who suffered nothing. Reciprocity does not discriminate, because tontine claims are perfectly reciprocal ex ante — every subscriber gains from every other's extinction. The census already holds this distinction in `PY0`, which separates `individual-indemnity` from `individual-draw`; what it does not hold is the locus of the triggering event, and that gap is developed in [[Endogenised absorption has a sign]].

The reframing earns its place by supplying what the criterion otherwise lacks. A comparative grid populated only with cooperative institutions makes the ergodicity claim unfalsifiable by construction, and a referee will say so. The tontine is the negative control — a pooling form predicted to fail — and its subsequent abandonment is the retention datum that makes the selection claim testable rather than asserted. Compare [[The isqa is Lindy because captive not because robust]] on the discipline of distinguishing survival from fitness.

One hazard on the analyst's side. "Pooling in form, dispersal in function" is a satisfying formula and it invites coding by intuition. The classification must be derived from coded contract terms rather than entered as a judgement, or it becomes the theory reading itself back out of the data. See [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]].
