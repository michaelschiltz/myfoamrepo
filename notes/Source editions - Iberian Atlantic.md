# Source editions — Iberian Atlantic

The printed legal and administrative compilations behind `averia_pool`, `asiento_averia` and `nacion_cofradia`. **Most are early-modern printings, free on archive.org.** The archival side is at [[Archivo General de Indias - Sevilla]] and [[Archivo Foral de Bizkaia - the Consulado de Bilbao]].

- ***Recopilación de leyes de los reynos de las Indias*** (Madrid: Julián de Paredes, 1681), 4 vols — **Book IX is the Carrera**: IX.1 Casa de Contratación; IX.6 prior, cónsules and Universidad de Cargadores de Sevilla; **IX.8 Contaduría de Averías; IX.9 contribution, administration and collection of the *avería***; IX.14 estates of the deceased; IX.25 Universidad de Mareantes; IX.48 the Lima and México consulados. Free on archive.org (Getty copy). ⚠️ The insurance *título* (around IX.37–42) not pinned down.
- **Joseph de Veitia Linage, *Norte de la contratación de las Indias Occidentales*** (Seville: Juan Francisco de Blas, 1672) — **a Casa official's systematic account of how the *avería* was assessed and collected**. Free on archive.org (Universidad de Sevilla copy; Larramendi digital edition).
- ***Ordenanzas para el prior y cónsules de la Universidad de los Mercaderes de Sevilla*** (confirmed 1556; printings Madrid 1585, Seville 1739) — the merchant guild behind the Carrera; **known for its insurance chapters**. Free on archive.org. ⚠️ Chapter contents not checked.
- ***Ordenanzas de la Ilustre Universidad y Casa de Contratación de Bilbao*** (confirmed 1737; printings 1775, 1796, 1819, Paris 1844) — **companies, freight, averías, insurance and bankruptcy in one code**; later the *Código de comercio y de navegación*. **The fullest Iberian codification of the loss-sharing and company rules the census codes.** Free on archive.org. ⚠️ First printing not located.
- **Huguette & Pierre Chaunu, *Séville et l'Atlantique (1504–1650)***, 12 vols (Paris: SEVPEN, 1955–60) — voyage and tonnage series reconstructed from the Contratación records, **including *avería* receipts**; the quantitative backbone. ⚠️ Tome structure unverified; no free copy.
- ***As Gavetas da Torre do Tombo***, 12 vols (Lisbon: Centro de Estudos Históricos Ultramarinos, 1960–77) — transcriptions from the Torre do Tombo's *Gavetas*; the only Lisbon collection verified. Free on archive.org.
- **Diego de Encinas, *Cedulario indiano*** (Madrid, 1596), 4 vols — facsimile ed. Alfonso García Gallo (Madrid: Ediciones Cultura Hispánica, 1945–46); reprinted 2018 (BOE). The pre-*Recopilación* compilation of royal orders for the Indies. **Correction**: the *Cedulario* of Vasco de Puga (Mexico, 1563) is a New Spain compilation, not a Casa de Contratación one.
- **Leads, unverified**: *Cedulario americano del siglo XVIII* (Seville, 1956–); Guiard y Larrauri, *Historia del Consulado … de Bilbao* (with printed documents).

## The Casa and the *avería*: ordinances, series, studies (added 2026-09-24)

A second pass, aimed at what the first left open: the Casa's own ordinances and the archival series of the *avería*.

- ***Ordenanzas reales para la Casa de la Contratación de Sevilla y para otras cosas de las Indias y de la navegación y contratación de ellas*** (1552; printing Seville, 1647) — **the Casa's founding code in print.** Free at archive.org (A31319001) and the Biblioteca Digital Hispánica. The manuscript is at AGI Patronato 251, R.1. ⚠️ No modern critical edition found.
- **Veitia Linage, *Norte de la contratación*** — facsimile, ed. Francisco de Solano (Madrid: Instituto de Estudios Fiscales, 1981), with an introductory study; the 1672 printing above remains the free copy.
- **The AGI series, identified from the finding aids and the literature rather than from PARES**:
  - **Contratación, legajos 4475–4531 — the *receptoría de la avería* accounts, 1649–1703**, identified by Díaz Blanco (below). **The nearest thing to the pool's books.**
  - **Consulados (Sección XII)** — the Universidad de Cargadores' own *avería* series, as listed by **Antonia Heredia Herrera, *Inventario de los fondos de Consulados (Sección XII) del Archivo General de Indias*** (Madrid: Ministerio de Cultura, 1979; free PDF from ANABAD): 2.1 *Avería consular*, 1585–1834; 4.11.2 *Avería*, 1572–1710; 4.11.13 *Armamento o avería gruesa*, 1779–1832; 4.11.14 *Avería moderna*, 1797–1837. ⚠️ Legajo numbers not read; PARES digitisation not checked.
- **The studies that read those series**:
  - **Guillermo Céspedes del Castillo, "La avería en el comercio de Indias"**, *Anuario de Estudios Americanos* 2 (1945): 515–698; also as a book (Seville: EEHA, 1945). **Still the institutional history of the *avería*.**
  - **José Manuel Díaz Blanco**, on the *avería* and the Armada de la Guarda, *Gladius* 35 (2015), doi:10.3989/gladius.2015.0007 — the source for the Contratación legajos above. ⚠️ Title not recorded in this pass.
  - **Susana Hierro Anibarro**, the 1998 book on the *avería* and the 2005 *Revista de Historia Económica* article (open access, hdl:10016/3624). ⚠️ Book title not recorded.
  - Castillo Manrubia (1989); Martín Acosta (1990, free PDF); Vas Mingo & Luque Talaván (2004) — ⚠️ cited in the literature, titles not verified here.
- **No printed *asientos de avería*** — the contracts by which the Consulado farmed the *avería* from the crown (1591 onward) — were found. **For `asiento_averia` the layer 3 is the *Recopilación* and the studies; the contracts themselves are archival only.**
