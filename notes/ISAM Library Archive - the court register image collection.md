# İSAM Library Archive — the court register image collection

The archive of the library of **İSAM, the Centre for Islamic Studies** of the Türkiye Diyanet Vakfı, Istanbul. Catalogue: <https://arsiv.isam.org.tr/>. **It owns no Ottoman court register and holds images of nearly all of them.**

## What it holds

**Digital copies of 23,756 court registers, 2,452,437 folios, 860–1368 AH / 1455–1948**, from Istanbul, Anatolia, Thrace, the Balkans and some Middle Eastern cities. The catalogue is arranged by court as fonds and register as item. All are digital; microfilm copies also exist.

**How it was assembled** (Yıldız 2024): microfilms of the Istanbul registers from 1995; about 8,900 microfilms of the registers outside Istanbul obtained from the Milli Kütüphane in **2004–2008**; from the 2010s, digital copies replacing microfilm, from the State Archives and the Müftülük. The *TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi* separately records 1,350 microfilm copies of registers held outside Turkey. ⚠️ How much of the 23,756 is non-Turkish holdings, and from which countries, not established.

## Why this is the most important repository in the pass

**Because it inverts the European aggregator.** In Europe the union layer is **description, online** — Archives Portal Europe has 280 million descriptive units and not one document. **Here the union layer is image, on site.** One reading room in Istanbul reunites, at layer 2, a record the Republic's abolition of the courts and the empire's successor borders dispersed across a dozen custodians. See [[MOC - Ottoman archives]] for that dispersal.

**It also inverts the survival question.** A register imaged here and later lost at its custodian survives as an image; a register transferred between custodians is still findable under its court. For a record that changed hands three times in sixty years — [[The Anatolian court registers - museum to National Library to State Archives]] — **İSAM's court-and-number address is more stable than the owner's shelfmark.**

## Access

- Single pages: no restriction stated.
- **Graduate students: up to five complete registers, with the adviser's written authorisation**; further requests delayed.
- ⚠️ Terms for postdoctoral and senior researchers, fees, remote delivery and permission to publish images were not found. The catalogue describes; it does not display.

## A correction the grant needs

**Decision_log A5 lists "Ottoman court records via ISAM" as an archival *database* under the research-library line.** On what this pass found, İSAM offers a reading room with digital copies under request limits, not a licensable remote database; the remote, free layer is the published edition — [[Istanbul Kadi Sicilleri - the published transliterations]]. **The cost falls under travel and staff time (A7, A6), not under database licences (A5).** ⚠️ To be confirmed with İSAM before the budget is drafted.

## Layer and script

**Layer 2 for the whole corpus; layer 1 at register grain.** Script as for the registers themselves — Arabic and Ottoman Turkish, *ta'lik*, *rik'a*, *dīwānī*, and siyakat in the accounts. **An image of a court register is the weakest "imaged" in this survey**: usable only by a reader of both the language and the hand.
