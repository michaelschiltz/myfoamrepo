# Archives nationales d'outre-mer — Aix-en-Provence

<https://archives-nationales-outre-mer.culture.gouv.fr/> · catalogue: <https://recherche-anom.culture.gouv.fr/archives/egf>

The archives of the French colonial administration, held separately from the Archives nationales and with their own *état des fonds*.

## What is here for this project

**The `Colonies` series** — the administration of the colonial empire, and with it the chartered companies that administered parts of it. The French companies were simultaneously trading bodies and governing bodies, which is the same dual character the project reads in the VOC and the EIC.

⚠️ Which of the Compagnie des Indes series are here and which are at Lorient is **unestablished and is the first thing to settle**; see [[Service historique de la Defense - Lorient and the Compagnie des Indes]]. The two repositories divide a single institution's record and the division is not intuitive.

## The methodological note that belongs on this repository

ANOM holds the archive of a colonial state, and the census's institutions appear in it as **instruments of that state**. The Zeeuws Archief note makes the same point about the MCC; here it applies to a whole repository rather than a single fonds.

The project's interest is in institutional form, and form can be described without endorsing what a form was used for — but the description has to say what it was used for, or the abstraction does the endorsing silently. The data management plan's ethics section is where this is stated; it should not be left to a footnote in either place.
