# Archivo Foral de Bizkaia — the Consulado de Bilbao

<https://apps.bizkaia.eus/ARIT> (the ARIT search application)

**Outside PARES entirely** — Bizkaia runs its own archive and its own catalogue. See [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]] on why that matters.

## Why the Consulado de Bilbao is a first-rank source

The Bilbao *Consulado* produced the **Ordenanzas de Bilbao of 1737**, the most influential commercial code in the Spanish-speaking world before codification — carried wholesale into Spanish American commercial law and still cited into the nineteenth century. It is a codified statement of the law of *companias*, marine insurance, *averia gruesa* and bankruptcy, produced by a merchant corporation for itself.

That combination is unusual and directly useful: **a merchant body writing down its own rules is stated rationale in the sense [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]] demands**, not inferred rationale. The earlier ordinances (1531 and 1560 ⚠️ dates to verify) let the same body's rules be read as a sequence, which is a rare opportunity to observe a form being revised rather than reconstructed from two snapshots.

## The fondo

The `Consulado de Bilbao` fondo is described in an ISAD record published by the Diputacion Foral. ⚠️ Reference code, date range and series list need transcribing from it — the PDF could not be fetched for this note. The archive's notarial section, separately, has been published in an edition covering **1459–1520**, with Consulado material for **1512–1520**.

## Digitisation

The Archivo Historico de Bizkaia has run a substantial programme — over 1.6 million pages from more than 75,000 documents digitised by an external contractor. ⚠️ How much of that touches the Consulado fondo is unverified. Access is through ARIT, which is a bespoke application rather than a standards-based catalogue; assume no IIIF and no bulk download.
