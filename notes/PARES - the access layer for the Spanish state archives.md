# PARES — the access layer for the Spanish state archives

<https://pares.cultura.gob.es/>

**PARES is not a national archive portal. It is the Ministry of Culture's portal for the archives the Ministry runs**, and the distinction decides where half the material this project needs can be found.

## What it covers

The state archives: the Archivo General de Indias, the Archivo General de Simancas, the Archivo Historico Nacional, the Archivo de la Corona de Aragon, the Archivo General de la Administracion, and the network of *Archivos Historicos Provinciales* in so far as the state retains competence over them. Description down to unit level, with images attached where the series has been digitised. Free, no registration for consultation.

## What it does not cover, and this is the trap

Competence over archives in Spain is devolved, and several of the richest holdings for this project sit outside the Ministry's system entirely:

- **Andalusia** runs its provincial archives through the Junta de Andalucia's own catalogue. The Sevilla and Cadiz notarial protocols are described there, not in PARES. See [[Archivo Historico Provincial de Sevilla - notarial protocols]] and [[Archivo Historico Provincial de Cadiz - notarial protocols and the Consulado]].
- **The Basque Country** runs the Archivo Foral de Bizkaia through its own ARIT application. See [[Archivo Foral de Bizkaia - the Consulado de Bilbao]].
- **Catalonia** — the notarial protocols of Barcelona are held by the *Col·legi de Notaris*, a professional corporation, not by any public archive at all. See [[Arxiu Historic de Protocols de Barcelona]].
- **Valencia and the Balearics** run their own *Arxiu del Regne* portals.

A search of PARES that comes back empty is therefore evidence about the Ministry's remit, not about Spain. This is the acquisitions rule from [[Isqa reading list to acquire]] arriving through an administrative rather than a bibliographic door: the query string was wrong because the jurisdiction was wrong.

## Layer

Layer 1 throughout, layer 2 for the digitised series. **Nothing in PARES is at layer 3.** There is no full-text search over transcribed early modern Spanish administrative hands, and no announced project to build one. ⚠️ Confirm whether PARES exposes IIIF or any bulk-download route — this decides whether an HTR pipeline can be pointed at it at all, and it is unresearched.
