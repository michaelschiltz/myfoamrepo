# Begijnhof archives — where the beguinages' records are

⚠️ **An unresolved custody question, recorded as one.** `begijnhof` is a coded row in `organizational_forms`, and the archives hub has carried its placement as a flagged guess since the first pass. This note states the problem properly and names what would settle it.

## Four possible custodians, and the material is split across all of them

- **The Rijksarchief**, through the ecclesiastical and suppressed-institution fonds — [[Rijksarchief in Belgie - the state archives network]].
- **City archives**, where a beguinage's property and jurisdiction touched the town — [[Stadsarchief Gent - the begijnhoven and the guilds]].
- **OCMW / public welfare archives**, successors to the charitable foundations, which in Belgium hold a great deal of pre-modern institutional property record.
- **[[KADOC - religious archives at Leuven]]** and the dioceses, for what stayed in religious custody.

The Groot Begijnhof Gent / Sint-Amandsberg archive is registered through **Archiefpunt**, the Flemish register for archives outside state custody — which is itself the diagnostic: a form whose records are registered in the private-archive index is a form the state catalogues will not find.

## Why the dispersal is not merely inconvenient

The census's `begijnhof` row turns on **property and its holding** — whether the community held goods as an entity, what a beguine could withdraw, what her creditors and heirs could reach. [[Entity shielding and owner shielding protect different trajectories]] is the distinction the cells record.

**Those questions are answered by exactly the records most likely to have been split.** Property deeds follow property into successor custody; internal rules stay with the religious body; litigation goes to the jurisdiction that heard it. A researcher who finds one custodian and stops will code a coherent-looking row from a third of the evidence, and the coherence will be an artefact of the split.

That is a sharper version of the general caution in [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]]: here the filter is not survival but **the dissolution settlement**, and it sorted documents by type rather than at random.

## What would settle it

⚠️ One beguinage, worked through all four custodians, before any general claim about where the material is. Ghent is the obvious candidate on size, and the Archiefpunt registration is the entry point. Until that is done the row's `source_ref` should carry the uncertainty rather than a repository name.
