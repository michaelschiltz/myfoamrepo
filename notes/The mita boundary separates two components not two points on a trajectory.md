# The mita boundary separates two components not two points on a trajectory

The WP3 demonstration case. Unlike the other candidates it returns a *determinate* answer
rather than a caution, and it does so on evidence the author supplies herself.

## Why every obvious attack is already occupied

Dell (2010) is the best-executed design in the genre and must be conceded as such. The
treatment is an explicit coercive rule; the catchment is legally defined; the boundary is
documented rather than inferred; the mechanisms are named and tested rather than gestured
at. Woods (2026), surveying geographic-RD abuse across the missionary literature, holds
this paper up as the genuine article against which the abuses are measured.

Consequently every empirical line of attack is taken, and by people who did it with more
data than WP3 will assemble.

- **The sample and the boundary** belong to Arroyo Abad and Maurer (2025): 500 indigenous
  settlements across all of Peru against Dell's southern band, colonial provincial
  boundaries too unstable to map onto modern districts, and at settlement level a scattered
  mixture of treated and untreated rather than a discontinuity.
- **The standard errors** belong to Kelly. The mita is one of his cases, and the reported
  reversal is severe.
- **The formal identification** belongs to Keele and Titiunik (2015), who name the paper as
  an application that identifies informally, runs a two-dimensional score constant within
  district, and absorbs heterogeneity into boundary-segment fixed effects instead of
  identifying at each boundary point.
- **The functional form** belongs to Karakas, whose double-ML re-estimation keeps the sign
  and loses the magnitude.
- **The mechanism** belongs to Ragas (2011): the hacienda channel is southern-sierra
  specific, and the Mantaro valley is a market-integrated peasant economy without haciendas.

Leading with any of these puts WP3 fifth in a queue and makes the framework decorative —
the failure mode [[The three Kelly critiques establish different nulls]] names when it
warns against reading as Kelly with a Greek letter.

## The framing that survives

Dell's own explanation of the result is a **bifurcation**. Inside the catchment: communal
tenure, no large estates, subsistence agriculture, no roads. Outside: hacienda formation,
wage labour, market access, public goods. Two basins, entered in the seventeenth century
under absorbing selection, never subsequently exchanged.

That is a reducible decomposition, stated by the author as her mechanism. Where the state
space decomposes into components that do not communicate, a cross-sectional contrast
between them is not an estimate of how a shock propagates along a trajectory — it is the
difference between the stationary states of two processes. The quantity is well defined and
precisely estimated. It is simply not the quantity the title reports, and no improvement in
data or inference converts one into the other. See
[[Ergodicity is indecomposability not randomness]] for why the decomposition rather than
the randomness is the load-bearing term.

**The strongest form of the case is that the mechanism section disqualifies the headline
estimand.** The better Dell documents the bifurcation — and she documents it well — the
less the coefficient can mean what it is read to mean. This is the structural counterpart
of the title-text gap in
[[Voigtlander and Voth measured the relaxation time and reported a mixture]]: there the
framing outruns the text, here the mechanism undercuts the framing. Both defects survive
the author's own care because neither lives in the estimate.

## Horizon against relaxation time

The design asserts, without testing, that a 1573 shock abolished in 1812 is still legible in
2001 levels. Only two regimes are clean and the inference needs neither.

- Relaxation time far exceeds the horizon: the components have not mixed, the first argument
  applies, and no treatment effect on a common process is being estimated.
- Relaxation time is short against the horizon: effects dissipate — which is what Arroyo
  Abad and Maurer find, and before independence at that — so a gap observed in 2001 must be
  produced by something contemporaneous.

Dell requires the intermediate case, in which the ratio of observation horizon to relaxation
time is such that the shock is partially present and still readable off a level. Nothing in
the paper establishes that ratio, and the genre has no procedure for establishing it.

## The absorption limb, which is independent

Carpio and Guerrero (2021) run Dell's own design on surname counts and find mita districts
carrying roughly 47 log points fewer surnames, with larger deficits on district- and
area-exclusive names — consistent with the historians' 55 to 80 per cent male depopulation.
They present this as an extension. Read against the design it is a measurement of the
absorbing flow: death in the mines, permanent flight into *forastero* status, absorption
into hacienda dependence beyond the catchment.

The 2001 population inside the boundary is therefore the survivor set of a filter, and the
filter differs across the boundary. This is [[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]]
with a number attached, and the number is unusually large. It should be run separately from
the decomposition argument, for the reason
[[Attractor selection bounds what a persistence design can resolve]] gives for keeping the
selection and resolution objections apart: they attack different halves and their conjunction
is only stronger if each is stated in its own terms.

It also dissolves the apparent conflict between Dell's critics. Severe permanent damage to
trajectories is fully compatible with a null in district ensembles when the damaged
trajectories have left the district. Arroyo Abad and Maurer's dissipation and Carpio and
Guerrero's collapse are the same event described from either end.

## The measure is an ensemble average at one date

Household consumption in 2001, averaged within district, is an ensemble average. Under
multiplicative dynamics it is tail-dominated and diverges from the growth rate of any
trajectory in it — [[Bad luck is what the ensemble average calls the modal trajectory]]. A
25 per cent gap in that statistic is compatible with a wide range of differences in
time-average growth, including none.

The dynamic reading also changes what the treatment was. The mita did not subtract output
from a fixed process; it fell on the pooling arrangements — ayllu tenure, rotational
obligation, reciprocal labour — through which Andean communities converted idiosyncratic
risk into a higher time-average growth rate. Pooling of that kind raises the time average
**without moving the ensemble average**, so its destruction can be severe, compounding and
entirely invisible in the reported statistic; and an ensemble gap can equally be
redistribution with no injury to the dynamic at all. The sign is not free:
[[Pooling's sign is set by the correlation of its baskets]] applies, and a mita levy falling
on a whole catchment at once is closer to a correlated peril than to an idiosyncratic one.

This is the limb that connects the case to the project's own material rather than to the
methodological literature, and it is the one that needs archival work before it can be
asserted.

## Statistical fragility, recorded but not load-bearing

Kelly's spatial treatment moves the mita result from a nominal p of 0.001 to roughly 0.19
under spatial-basis regression with a small-cluster correction, and the synthetic-outcome
test does not reject that the outcome is trend plus spatial noise. Karakas keeps the sign
and returns magnitudes from about −0.13 to −0.91 against Dell's −0.22 to −0.36 while
rejecting the homogeneous-effect assumption outright.

None of this is a WP3 argument. It is inference criticism, it does not need the framework,
and leaning on it would make the work package a literature review with a formalism attached.
Record it, cite it once, and do not build on it.

## What must not be conceded

The paper is careful, the boundary is real, and the mechanism work is genuine empirical
labour rather than decoration. The critique is about what the coefficient *is*, not about
whether it was competently obtained, and any drafting that blurs the two forfeits the
distinction the work package exists to make. The same discipline the vault imposes on the
Voigtländer–Voth case applies here with more force, because Dell's execution is better.

## The symmetric cost

The diagnostic disqualifies the refutations along with the result: Arroyo Abad and Maurer's
null is an ensemble null and does not establish that trajectories were unaffected, and
Kelly's correction leaves the estimand untouched. WP3 cannot cite either as support without
conceding this. Treated at
[[A refutation that shares the estimand inherits the defect]].
