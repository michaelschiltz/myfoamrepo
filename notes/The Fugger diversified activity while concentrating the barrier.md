# The Fugger diversified activity while concentrating the barrier

The Fugger are the cautionary companion to [[Pooling's sign is set by the correlation of its baskets]]: diversification of *activity* is not diversification of the *absorbing barrier*. Read as several baskets — Tyrolean and Hungarian mining, Antwerp exchange, papal transfers, spice ventures — the *konzern* looks decorrelated. Read at the barrier, the baskets were one exposure: claims on the Habsburg dynastic complex. The model was coupled from the origin. The Fugger loan of 1488 advanced capital to Archduke Sigmund of Tyrol against silver output, so the mining basket *was* the collateral of the sovereign-credit basket, not an independent bet beside it; Charles V's imperial election (1519) and the wars that followed only deepened the concentration.

When the shared shock came it entered through the debtor, not the market. The Spanish *decretos* of 1557, 1560, 1575 and 1596 suspended crown payments and struck every basket at once, because each was ultimately a claim on the same payer. Activity ρ was low; counterparty ρ was near one, and it is counterparty ρ that sits at the barrier. The firm's variety of business lines could not avert a ruin that arrived through the single exposure they all shared.

> **Verify.** The precise share of Fugger capital tied to crown credit versus genuinely independent ventures at mid-century should be pinned against the sources. The qualitative concentration is not in doubt; the exact magnitude is.

The claim generalizes the sign rule to the *level* at which correlation is measured. Diversification that lowers variance across *operations* while leaving the *ruin-bearing* exposure concentrated buys the appearance of asynchrony without the substance. The unit of survival is the correlation of exposures at the [[Absorbing barrier|absorbing barrier]], not the count of business lines. The Rothschild five-house structure is the achieved version the Fugger only gesture at: exposures spread across genuinely independent sovereign centres and firewalled as separate houses that could hedge and rescue one another.
