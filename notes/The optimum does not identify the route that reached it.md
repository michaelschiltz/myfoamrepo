# The optimum does not identify the route that reached it

**The hexagonal comb is reached by at least three routes that leave the same structure: selection among swarms on the economy of wax, physical relaxation of a soft film under surface tension, and behavioural shaping by the builders. Soap froth and cooling basalt reach it with no heredity at all. An optimal form therefore says nothing about how it was reached (Räz 2017). For HistorEE the transfer yields a cut the vault has not yet drawn: pooling forms can be reached by selection or by relaxation, both are time-average stories, only one is a survivorship story, and they leave different records.**

## The three routes to the hexagon

- **Selection.** Darwin: the swarms that wasted least honey in secreting wax did best. The form is the residue of differential success.
- **Relaxation.** Bartholin, D'Arcy Thompson, Karihaloo and colleagues: cells are laid as circles and warm wax flows into hexagons at the triple junctions. Each junction moves locally toward lower energy; the global optimum emerges without any agent or lineage having been eliminated.
- **Shaping.** Bauer and Bienefeld, Nazzi: the builders work the walls mechanically, and the hexagon follows from a prior regular arrangement of the cylinders.

Citations at [[Hales 2001 - the honeycomb theorem and the architecture of its proof]]. The dispute between the second and third is unsettled; what matters here is that the structure underdetermines the route.

## The cut for pooling

The vault already separates **transmission** from **arrival** ([[The theory predicts recurrence not independent origins]]). Within arrival it has so far named one mechanism: [[Time-average optimization is a survivorship property not an intention]] — non-pooling trajectories hit the barrier and leave the sample. That is the Darwin route. The honeycomb names a second.

**Relaxation**: parties inside continuing relationships adjust terms locally against outcomes they have experienced as ruinous — a cap on the agent's liability here, a loss-sharing clause there, an extension of the contributors' circle after a bad season — and the form drifts toward the time-average optimum without any population of non-poolers having died out. There is intention at the level of the clause and none at the level of the criterion, which keeps the route inside the selection-not-intention discipline while removing its dependence on extinction.

## Why the distinction pays

The two routes predict different archives.

- **Selection** leaves the dead: failures where failure was administered ([[The record of non-survivors survives where failure was administered]]), refused and abandoned variants ([[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]]), forms appearing whole and changing in population frequency rather than in content.
- **Relaxation** leaves the drafting record: dated formulary series and notarial protocols in which one form changes clause by clause within surviving relationships, approaching the pooled structure gradually and without a cohort of failures.

A form that is complete at first attestation and spreads by displacement is a selection case; a form whose clauses accrete within one documentary series is a relaxation case; most will be mixtures. The diagnostic requires dated clause-level variants within a form, which is a coding requirement, not only an interpretive one [check against the HistorEE_codebooks schema]. It also sharpens the [[Doctrine, deed and conduct are three layers and the time-average test needs all three]] scheme: relaxation is visible in deeds before doctrine names it — Pappus's purity rationale over an operative economy of wax is the type case of a stated reason trailing the mechanism.
