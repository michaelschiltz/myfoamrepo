# Doctrine, deed and conduct are three layers and the time-average test needs all three

The project's sharpest falsifiable claim about loss allocation — that the terms the jurists forbade (a fixed return to either party, a capital guarantee by the agent, distribution before losses are netted) coincide with allocations that push the residual bearer's time-average growth below his outside option — is a claim about **doctrine**. It says what jurists permitted. Two further claims are separate and need separate evidence: that parties contracted within the doctrine (**deed**), and that loss fell where the deed put it when a loss actually arrived (**conduct**). A rule of validity is not a record of practice, and a deed is not a record of what happened.

## The three layers and what each can falsify

- **Doctrine** — *fiqh*, halakha, the canonists. Falsified if the jurists forbade terms that are dynamically harmless, or permitted ones that are not.
- **Deed** — the instrument as drafted: the clauses a court would enforce. If deeds routinely carry the forbidden terms, the doctrine was not the operative rule and its fit to the dynamic explains nothing. Circumvention counts as evidence, not as noise: a device that restores the fixed return shows which exposure the rule was resisting ([[The heter iska restores the fixed return by evidentiary barrier]]).
- **Conduct** — letters and court records. Losses can be reallocated after the event by settlement, arbitration or forbearance, whatever the deed says. Only this layer shows the allocation working on a trajectory.

## Why the Geniza, and why Ackerman-Lieberman

The Geniza is the one corpus in the census that carries **all three layers for one community in one period**: Geonic and Maimonidean doctrine, more than a hundred partnership deeds, and the merchants' letters and court records. Ackerman-Lieberman's work is built on the gap between the layers, which on Schiltz's reading (session of 2026-10-25) is the guiding principle of his books:

- "Contractual Partnerships in the Geniza and the Relationship between Islamic Law and Practice," *JESHO* 54 (2011): 646–676, DOI `10.1163/156852011X614000`, Zotero `ZJ24ZN53`. Deeds read against a consensus built from letters: the Geniza partnership documents allocate both partners a share of profits and losses, with very few indemnifying the active partner (664). Already holding the `isqa_alloc` dispute open as a record-type difference — [[Geniza partnership documents - where the census's isqa and qirad evidence sits]].
- "Commercial Forms and Legal Norms in the Jewish Community of Medieval Egypt," *Law and History Review* 30 (2012): 1007–1052, DOI `10.1017/S0738248012000685`, Zotero `SB7JBG7S`. Reverses the usual question: not how daily life bent the jurists, but whether legal norms shaped daily life.
- "Legal Pluralism among the Court Records of Medieval Egypt" (2015), Zotero `HWEFEB6U` — the enforcement layer.
- *The Business of Identity* (Stanford, 2014), Zotero `TAU5WETW`.

Within the project the three-layer test is his terrain, and his method — deed against doctrine, with the record type stated — is the one to adopt across the census, not only for the Geniza rows.

## Cautions

- **The prohibition is symmetrical.** Shared doctrine never licenses an inference to shared practice, and divergent practice has to be shown in practice too ([[A shared compilation never licenses an inference to shared practice]]).
- **The deposit has a survival rule.** One community, a script-conditioned sample, one dominant phase; a Geniza frequency is never set beside a Genoese notarial one ([[Cairo Geniza - the deposit and its survival rule]]).
- **A letter records what correspondents needed to say, a deed what a court would enforce.** Conduct read from letters is conduct as narrated.
