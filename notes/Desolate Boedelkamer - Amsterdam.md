# Desolate Boedelkamer — Amsterdam

*Archief van de Commissarissen van de Desolate Boedelkamer*, at [[Stadsarchief Amsterdam - the notarial archive]]. ⚠️ Toegang number and date range not yet transcribed.

**The Amsterdam bankruptcy chamber, and therefore the archive where shielding stops being a doctrine and becomes a distribution.**

## Why this is the series the entity census most needs

[[The creditor limb of entity shielding has no witness]] records a specific hole: the census asks whether members' *creditors* can reach a firm's assets, as distinct from whether members can force partition — and no coded source answers the creditor limb. `deed_of_settlement_company AP2` fell from `P` to `.NR` on exactly that reading, because a source rich on the members' limb yielded nothing on the creditors'.

**An insolvency chamber answers the creditor limb directly and cannot avoid it.** Its entire business is deciding, in a concrete estate, who reaches which assets in what order. It does not argue about categories; it ranks claimants, and it does so in writing, repeatedly, over a long run.

⚠️ **This is the most promising hypothesis in the Dutch pass and it is only a hypothesis.** Whether the chamber's records state the *reasoning* or merely the outcome decides everything: an outcome-only series records the ranking but not the ground for it, and [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]] is the rule that would make the difference matter. Establish this before anything is built on it.

## A second reason, distinct from the first

Insolvency is **absorption** — the barrier this project's entire dynamics is organised around. A bankruptcy archive is a register of the firms that hit it, which makes it the natural counterpart to [[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]]: the persistence literature studies survivors, and here is a serial record of non-survivors from the same population. **An archive of failure is the correction to a literature built on the successes**, and there are very few of them.

Whether that can be exploited depends on whether the surviving population of Amsterdam firms is countable at all, which is a question for [[IISG and NEHA - Amsterdam]] and [[Delpher - the full-text layer]] rather than for this archive.
