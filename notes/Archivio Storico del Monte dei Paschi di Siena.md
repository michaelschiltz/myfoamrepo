# Archivio Storico del Monte dei Paschi di Siena

SIUSA: `Monte dei Paschi di Siena` · related state holdings at the Archivio di Stato di Siena.

**The archive of a *monte di pietà* that never stopped operating.** Founded as the *Monte Pio* in **1472**, restructured as the *Monte dei Paschi* in 1624 on the security of the Maremma pasture revenues, and still a bank. The institutional continuity is the asset: this is a form that can be observed across five and a half centuries in one series.

## Why it is a Lindy case, and what that is worth

The vault already has a Lindy argument — [[The naties are a six-century Lindy institution]] — and a caution against reading longevity as robustness, in [[The isqa is Lindy because captive not because robust]]. Siena is the case that tests the caution, because the *Monte* survived through repeated and explicit restructurings rather than by persisting unchanged. **Continuity of name is not continuity of form**, and an archive that runs unbroken across the restructurings is what lets the two be separated.

That question — is this one institution or several under one name — is the same one [[A migrated institution collects local names not local forms]] asks about space, asked about time.

## The charitable-to-financial transition

A *monte di pietà* is a pawn-based charitable lender constituted to undercut usurious lending. That it becomes a commercial bank is a transition the project should be able to describe in its own vocabulary and currently cannot: the components that change are not the ones the coding tracks. Same structure as the Neapolitan case at [[Archivio Storico del Banco di Napoli]], and the two together would make a real comparison.

⚠️ The `monte di pietà` is not yet a coded form. If it becomes one, Siena is the type site.
