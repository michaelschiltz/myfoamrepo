# Perpetual succession redefines the unit of survival from a line to a union of lines

In a Galton–Watson process, $k$ independent lineages are all extinct with probability $q^k$. **Nothing passes between the lines for this to hold.** It is enough that the unit whose survival counts is defined as the union of the lines rather than any one of them. This is the ruin-limb counterpart of pooling, and it is what several continuity-securing institutions do.

- **The lineage trust.** The Song charitable estate (*yizhuang*) and its successors hold property for a whole descent group, so that the estate persists while any branch persists.
- **The *ie* with adoption.** The household survives whoever occupies its name; adoption (*yōshi*) adds a line where the biological line has failed, which lowers $q$ directly rather than through numbers. This is the formal statement the lead at [[Galton-Watson is the extinction branch Peters named and did not develop]] said [[The ie as entity-shielding]] lacked.
- **The family *waqf*.** Beneficiaries defined by descent, with reversion to a charitable purpose when the line ends: the dedication outlives every line it names.
- **Perpetual succession in the corporation.** Members come and go and the person continues; $k$ is unbounded.

This gives Objective 2's claim — each component secures the continuity of a pooled trajectory against death, ruin and partition — a mechanism with a number attached: a trust over five lines that would each die out with probability 0.4 dies out with probability about 0.01.

## The caution that binds most

**Lines within one family are not independent.** They share an estate, a locality, an epidemic, a political fortune. The union-of-lines gain is then far smaller than $q^k$, for the reason set out at [[Extending a pool along kin proximity buys N without buying decorrelation]]. The prediction is therefore not that lineage trusts made descent groups nearly immortal, but that they paid best where branches were dispersed — across places, occupations or, in the *ie*, by adopting from outside the kin group. Adoption from outside is, on this reading, decorrelation as well as replacement.
