# Arxiu del Regne de Valencia

<https://arv.gva.es/>

The third Crown-of-Aragon repository, after [[Archivo de la Corona de Aragon - Barcelona]] and [[Arxiu del Regne de Mallorca]]. Founded 1419; the archive of the kingdom's own institutions.

## The fons that matter

**`Reial Cancelleria`** — privileges and ordinances.

**`Mestre Racional`** — the kingdom's accounting office, parallel to the Catalan one.

**`Batlia General`** and **`Governacio`** — royal patrimony and jurisdiction.

**`Justicia Civil`** — civil litigation, which for commercial partnerships is where the terms of a failed arrangement get stated in the open.

**`Protocols notarials`** — the instruments.

⚠️ All five need reference codes and date ranges transcribed from the archive's own *Contingut i organitzacio dels fons*; that page could not be fetched for this note.

## The Taula de Canvis is somewhere else

Valencia's municipal bank, the *Taula de Canvis* (1408) — the earliest of the Iberian public banks — is a **municipal** institution, and its records are with the Arxiu Historic Municipal de Valencia, not the Arxiu del Regne. ⚠️ Confirm; this is exactly the custodial split that wastes a trip. If the project takes up the public banks as a pooling form, the municipal archives of Valencia and Barcelona are the repositories, not the royal ones.
