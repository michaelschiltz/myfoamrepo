# Staatsarchiv Hamburg

<https://www.hamburg.de/> · catalogue: *ActaPro*, <https://actapro-benutzung.hamburg.de/actaproweb/>

**The German archive this project needs most**, because `bodmerei` is a coded row and Hamburg is where the form was practised, regulated and written about.

## The holdings that matter

**`Admiralitätskollegium`** — the Hamburg admiralty, described in Archivportal-D under repository code `DE-2089`. Maritime jurisdiction: average, bottomry, insurance, convoy. **The German counterpart of the *amirautés*** at [[The amirautes - a series across the coastal departments]] and of the Amsterdam average court at [[Averijgrossen - the general average adjudications]].

⚠️ Extent, date range and imaging unverified. Establish whether the *Admiralitätskollegium* holds adjudications in series, because a serial run is what turns this from a source into a corpus.

**Notarial and civic registers**, the *Assekuranz* material, and the records of the eighteenth-century Hamburg insurance companies ⚠️ *unverified*.

## The printed layer is unusually good here

Hamburg produced a **printed law of insurance and bottomry** — the eighteenth-century systematic treatments of the *Assekuranz- und Bodmereiwesen* drawing on Hamburg's statutes and usages — and this literature is digitised and full-text in legal-history digital libraries.

**That matters for the coding specifically.** The census codes drafted clauses, and where a city codified its own usage the *stated rationale* is printed rather than inferred — which is what [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]] asks for and rarely gets. **Hamburg may be codable from print before anyone enters the archive**, which is an unusual and cheap position.

See [[Bodmerei - where the German bottomry records are]] for the custody question proper.
