# Source editions — New Julfa and the Mongol *ortoq*

The two ends of the grant's "seam" strand. **The headline for both: there is no Yamada-style corpus of contracts.** New Julfa has one printed account book that opens with its founding commenda and a printed law code; the *ortoq* has Yuan legal compilations and Persian chronicles, and **no published private *ortoq* contract**.

## New Julfa

- **The ledger of Hovhannes Ter-Davtian of Julfa (Matenadaran MS 7383), 1682–1693** — a Julfan agent trading in Persia, India, Nepal and Lhasa. **It opens with the 1682 commenda contract between Hovhannes and the khojas Zakaria and Embroom (capital c. 250 tumans) and then records its execution.** **The best single printed New Julfa source for agent–master terms and the settling of accounts.** Three forms:
  - **Armenian text**: L. Khachikian & H. D. Papazian (eds), *Hovhannes Ter-Davt'yan Jughayets'u hashvetumarě* (Yerevan, 1984). ⚠️ Publisher given variously as the Academy of Sciences Press and the Matenadaran.
  - **English**: Levon Khachikian, "The Ledger of the Merchant Hovhannes Joughayetsi", *Journal of the Asiatic Society* (Calcutta), 4th ser., 8/3 (1966), 153–186 — **free on archive.org**.
  - **French**: Khachikian, "Le registre d'un marchand arménien en Perse, en Inde et au Tibet (1682–1693)", *Annales ESC* 22/2 (1967), 231–278, DOI 10.3406/ahess.1967.421520 — **free on Persée**.
- **F. G. Poghosyan (ed.), *Datastanagirk' Astrakhani Hayots'* (the Astrakhan Armenian law code)** (Yerevan: Academy of Sciences, 1967) — the 1760s code whose commercial chapters on the commenda Aslanian cites; normative rules on liability and loss allocation. His preliminary study, *Banber Matenadarani* 6 (1962), 171–195, is free on archive.org. ⚠️ Whether Aslanian uses this edition unconfirmed.
- **The Santa Catarina papers (seized 1748)** — about 2,000 Julfan letters and contracts at The National Archives, Kew, **HCA 42/26 and HCA 30/682**. **Never edited**; Aslanian's monograph is in preparation. ⚠️ Whether the Prize Papers portal has digitised them unconfirmed; see [[The Prize Papers - HCA 32 and the German money]].
- **Aslanian, *From the Indian Ocean to the Mediterranean*** (UC Press, 2011) is reported to carry **translated sample commenda contracts** in an appendix ⚠️ unverified; his JESHO article is 50/2–3 (2007), 124–171.
- **State documents, not contracts**: Parsamian (ed.), *Armiano-russkie otnosheniia v XVII veke*, vol. 1 (Yerevan, 1953); Bournoutian, *Armenians and Russia, 1626–1796: A Documentary Record* (Costa Mesa: Mazda, 2001); Baladouni & Makepeace (eds), *Armenian Merchants of the Seventeenth and Early Eighteenth Centuries: English East India Company Sources* (Transactions of the APS 88/5, 1998) ⚠️ unverified.
- **Unedited archives with Julfan contracts**, per Aslanian: All Saviour's Monastery (New Julfa); Propaganda Fide; Venice (Inquisitori di Stato); Florence (Acquisti e Doni); British Library Lansdowne 1047; Nationaal Archief.

## The Mongol *ortoq*

- **Chen Gaohua, Zhang Fan, Liu Xiao & Dang Baohai (eds), 《元典章》 (*Da Yuan shengzheng guochao dianzhang*)** (Tianjin guji & Zhonghua shuju, 2011), 2,490 pp., 60 *juan*, 2,391 articles — **the critical edition of the Yuan statutes and precedents to 1322**, including rulings on *ortoq* debt, interest ceilings and guarantors' joint liability. ⚠️ 3 or 4 volumes; section-level location of the *ortoq* cases not checked.
- **Academy of Korean Studies (ed.), 《至正條格》** (Seoul: Humanist, 2007), 2 vols (facsimile + annotated text) — the Yuan code of c. 1340s, thought lost until a partial copy surfaced in Korea in 2003.
- **Fang Linggui (ed.), 《通制條格校注》** (Beijing: Zhonghua, 2001) ⚠️ citation from search snippets only.
- **Paul Heng-chao Ch'en, *Chinese Legal Tradition under the Mongols: The Code of 1291 as Reconstructed*** (Princeton, 1979) — English access to Yuan norms.
- **The chronicles behind the census's rows**: Juvaynī, tr. Boyle, *The History of the World-Conqueror*, 2 vols (Manchester, 1958); Rashīd al-Dīn, tr. Thackston, *Jamiʿu't-tawarikh*, 3 parts (Harvard, 1998–99) ⚠️; Waṣṣāf (Hammer-Purgstall vol. 1, 1856; ÖAW edition ⚠️).
- **The two standard articles**: Allsen, "Mongolian Princes and Their Merchant Partners, 1200–1260", *Asia Major* 3rd ser. 2/2 (1989), 83–126; Endicott-West, "Merchant Associations in Yüan China: The Ortoγ", *Asia Major* 3rd ser. 2/2 (1989), 127–154.
- **Where private instruments might be** (none verified): Li Yiyou, 《黑城出土文書（漢文文書卷）》 (Kexue, 1991) — Khara-Khoto loan contracts; Gronke, *Arabische und persische Privaturkunden des 12. und 13. Jahrhunderts aus Ardabil* (Berlin, 1982); Herrmann, *Persische Urkunden der Mongolenzeit* (Wiesbaden, 2004). **With the Uyghur contracts and the Khara-Khoto volume already held, these are the places to look for an *ortoq* instrument.**
