# Landesarchiv Baden-Württemberg

The state archive of the German south-west, running the Hauptstaatsarchiv Stuttgart, the Generallandesarchiv Karlsruhe and several regional archives.

## Why it is in the set

⚠️ **Thinly, and the note says so.** The south-west was a patchwork of small territories, imperial cities and ecclesiastical lordships without a dominant commercial centre of the Augsburg or Nuremberg kind. For this project it is a **secondary region**.

Its interest is the same mediatisation problem set out at [[Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv - Munchen]], in its most extreme form: the south-west had more mediatised territories than anywhere else in Germany, so the custody question is correspondingly tangled.

**The one substantive thread** is the Rhine and Neckar trade and the relation to the Swiss and Alsatian markets, which connects to the Upper Rhine commercial world rather than to the Hanse or the Mediterranean. ⚠️ Whether that yields anything the census could code is unknown and probably should be tested cheaply before more is invested.
