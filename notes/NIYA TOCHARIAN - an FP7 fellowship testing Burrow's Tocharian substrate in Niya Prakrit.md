# NIYA TOCHARIAN — an FP7 fellowship testing Burrow's Tocharian substrate in Niya Prakrit

**Two-year Marie Curie fellowship (FP7-PEOPLE-2013-IEF, GA 626656) at the Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften, 1 Aug 2014 – 31 Jul 2016, €161,968.80: tested whether the Niya Prakrit of the Shanshan (鄯善) chancery carries a Tocharian substrate. Use it as the linguistic orientation for the Niya Kharoṣṭhī documents, the unexamined third-century legal corpus in [[MOC - Silk Road archives]].**

## Corpus and question

- Corpus: Niya Prakrit (Niya Gāndhārī), Middle Indic of the 3rd–4th c. CE, administrative language of Shanshan in the southern Tarim oases; over 750 Kharoṣṭhī documents from the Niya site
- Hypothesis tested: Burrow 1935, a Tocharian substrate in Niya Prakrit ⚠️ CORDIS gives author and year only; presumably T. Burrow, "Tokharian elements in the Kharoṣṭhī documents from Chinese Turkestan", *JRAS* 1935 — verify
- Comparandum: Khotan Prakrit

## Results as reported

- Tocharian elements present, but too few to establish that the Niya chancery's scribes were native speakers of a Tocharian language
- Iranian element larger than Burrow allowed
- Systematic differences from Khotan Prakrit rule out a simple Khotanese-speaker substrate
- No publications listed on CORDIS
