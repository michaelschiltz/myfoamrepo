# The creditor limb of entity shielding has no witness

`AP2` asks two things. Members cannot force partition or withdrawal; and **members' *creditors*
cannot force them either**. The second limb is the characteristic's whole increment over `CI2`,
which already records the members' side — it is the reason the two are not merged.

Re-coding `deed_of_settlement_company` blind against Televantos and Getzler & Macnair, the members'
limb came back richly evidenced and **the creditors' limb was never asked by either source**. The
nearest creditor passage concerns priority in the residue, which is `AP1`'s question, not this one.
`AP2 = .NR`. And the finding generalises past this form: **on that limb no witness in the census has
testified at all.**

## What the cell is a claim about

`AP2 = .NR` here is **not a statement about the deed of settlement company**. It is a statement about
what the literature on English business organisation asks. The historiography interrogates the
members — their power to withdraw, to partition, to bind one another — and does not interrogate the
creditor who wants to reach through a member's share into the fund. A cell that records "not
reached" is therefore recording a property of the discipline, and reading it as a property of the
institution would be exactly the inversion against which
[[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] warns, moved from the
archive to the secondary literature.

**A hazard is discharged by evidence, never by an acquisition**, so this does not resolve when the
next book arrives. It resolves when a source asks the question — and per
[[Independence of apparatus is not independence of question]], the next source has to be chosen for
that question rather than for its frame.

## Why it matters to WP1

The asymmetry is not decorative.
[[Entity shielding and owner shielding protect different trajectories]] rests on entity shielding
being the device that stops **one member's personal ruin
dragging the pool down** — precisely the creditors' limb, not the members'. So the census
is best evidenced on the half of `AP2` that is closest to `CI2` and unevidenced on the half that
carries the ergodicity argument. The evidence base and the theoretical load are pulling in opposite
directions, and that is worth saying out loud before the mechanism is asserted from coded values.

It also sharpens the standing question whether `AP2` and `CI2` should merge. The two limbs are now
known to be **differently evidenced**, which is an argument for keeping them apart independent of
whether they are conceptually distinct: merging them would let the well-attested limb supply a value
for the unattested one.
