# Archivio di Stato di Palermo

<https://saassipa.cultura.gov.it/>

## The holding that matters

**`Archivi notarili`, 14th–20th centuries** — described through the Soprintendenza Archivistica della Sicilia's own research tools. Sicilian notarial protocols run from the fourteenth century and are among the deepest in the Mediterranean.

## Why Sicily is not a footnote to the Italian set

Because it is the place where the **Genoese and Catalan networks meet**, under a crown that was Aragonese and then Spanish. A *commenda* drawn in Palermo may be drafted on a Genoese model, a Catalan one, or a local one, and **which model it follows is evidence about transmission that neither Genoa nor Barcelona can supply from its own side.**

That is the diagnostic use: [[A migrated institution collects local names not local forms]] warns that a shared name proves little, and the corresponding positive test is whether the *clauses* travelled. A jurisdiction where two traditions were simultaneously present is where that test can actually be run, and Sicily is the clearest European case.

⚠️ Entirely unverified as to series, extent and digitisation. This note records why the archive belongs in the register, not what is in it.
