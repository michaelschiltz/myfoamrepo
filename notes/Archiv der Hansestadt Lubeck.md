# Archiv der Hansestadt Lübeck

<https://archiv.luebeck.de/>

**The archive of the Hanse's leading city**, and the anchor of the [[Hanse documents on the Memory of the World register]] inscription of 2023.

## What matters here

**The *Niederstadtbuch*.** Lübeck's civic register of obligations, conveyances and debts — a **serial record of private contracts entered before the council**, running from the fourteenth century. This is the Baltic counterpart of the Antwerp *schepenregisters* and the Leuven registers at [[Itinera Nova - the Leuven aldermen's registers]]: in a city without a developed notariate, the civic book *is* the instrument archive.

⚠️ Extent, date range and the state of digitisation or indexing are unverified and should be established first, because they decide whether this is a corpus or a shelf.

**The Hanseatic correspondence and *Rezesse*** — the records of the Hanseatic diets, which are the constitutional record of a league that was not a state, had no seal of its own for long periods, and no treasury. **That absence is the interesting thing.**

## The Hanse as an uncoded form, and why the census should mind

The census codes bodies with entity shielding, capital lock-in, perpetual succession and transferable claims. **The Hanse has almost none of them** and lasted five hundred years anyway. It is a durable association of *cities* — not of merchants, not of capital — with no shared fund to shield and nothing to transfer.

That is either outside the census's scope or a falsification of something in it, and **the difference matters**. [[The naties are a six-century Lindy institution]] makes a Lindy argument about a merchant body with a monopoly to hold; the Hanse is Lindy with no such asset. ⚠️ Whether it belongs in `organizational_forms` is a selection-brief question and is recorded here, not answered.
