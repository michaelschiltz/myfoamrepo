# Riksarkivet Sweden — the national archives and the AI lab

<https://sok.riksarkivet.se/> · the *Digitala forskarsalen* · NAD, the national archival database

**The only national archive in this survey that is also a publisher of machine-learning models.**

## The AI lab

Riksarkivet maintains a **HuggingFace organisation** releasing open handwritten-text-recognition models and the **HTRflow** pipeline — line-detection models, TrOCR models fine-tuned on historical Swedish hands, and a documented toolchain, all open source.

**This is a different institutional posture from anything else in the survey.** The Dutch built Loghi through a research infrastructure and the KNAW; Transkribus is a cooperative selling a managed service; Itinera Nova is volunteers. **Sweden's national archive is shipping the models themselves.**

Two consequences for the project.

**First, the models may transfer.** The open questions standing at [[Noord-Hollands Archief - Haarlem]] and [[Hof van Holland - the civil litigation record]] are both about whether existing HTR transfers to a new hand. **An openly published, fine-tunable Swedish historical model is a better starting point for a new European hand than a closed one**, whatever the language — the script matters more than the language for line recognition. See [[Open-source HTR stack - eScriptorium Kraken Loghi]].

**Second, it changes what "not digitised" costs.** Where the transcription tooling is free and the images are open, layer 2 to layer 3 is a compute problem rather than a funding one. ⚠️ How far Riksarkivet has applied its own models to its own holdings is unestablished and is the thing to check.

## The holdings

NAD covers the whole Swedish archival landscape, state and local. The company archives of [[Stora Kopparberg - the 1288 share]] and the [[Svenska Ostindiska Companiet - the Gothenburg archive]] are both described here.
