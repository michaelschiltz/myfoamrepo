# Huizhou documents — the largest private contract corpus

徽州文书, from the six counties of Huizhou prefecture, southern Anhui — the home region of the Huizhou merchants.

## Scale and custody

**More than 200,000 documents known, and "probably more than 300,000" — "perhaps a conservative estimate"** (Luan Chengxian). Found in waves: **after 1945; in the 1950s and early 1960s; and from the 1980s onward.** Held across many institutions: the **Institute of History of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences** (more than 10,000 items and volumes, with its own classified catalogue), the **Anhui Provincial Museum**, **Anhui University's Huizhou studies centre**, and others. ⚠️ Holdings at Nanjing University, the National Library and local museums not counted in this pass.

The CASS classification names the types: **land, property, tax and service, commercial, lineage, association (会社), official, and litigation documents**, among others.

## What it holds for the census

**The earliest shareholding examples in the article behind every coded Chinese row are Huizhou contracts** — a 1432 Xiuning mountain partnership in shares, a 1568 Qimen lineage timber agreement for "households with shares and households without", and Xiuning tenancy shares — all read by Zelin from the Zhang Chuanxi compilation. See [[Zhang Chuanxi 1995 - the contract compilation behind the census's Chinese rows]].

**And its "association documents" are the obvious place for two things the census lacks**: the **account books of rotating credit and ritual associations (会簿)**, and the **rules and accounts of lineage trusts**. **Partly confirmed: Tong Xu, 《清代的“会”与乡村秩序》 (Guangxi Normal University Press, 2023), works five kinds of Qing *hui* from the printed Huizhou contract compilations — ancestral (祀会), deity (神会), road-and-bridge (路桥会), literary (文会) and credit (钱会) associations.** The deity association is the census's `shenhui_gu` in another province; the credit association is the Chinese rotating association. ⚠️ Read through a publisher's summary only; whether the documents are rules, account books or both not established. The first would be the Chinese comparandum the vault's `Kye is not a ROSCA and the equation is an artefact of comparison with mujin` implies the census needs; the second is the grant's named "lineage trusts".

## Layer and access

**Layer 3 in print for selections**; originals under the rules of each holder; mainland rules for the mainland institutions. ⚠️ Commercial and institutional databases of Huizhou documents exist and their terms were not checked.
