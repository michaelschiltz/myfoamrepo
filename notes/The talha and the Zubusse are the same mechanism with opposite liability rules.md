# The *talha* and the *Zubusse* are the same mechanism with opposite liability rules

**They look like the same institution and they separate on the one cell that matters. That is why the pair is worth more than either case alone.**

## The mechanism, which really is the same

**Bazacle — the *talha*.** An annual assessment on the *pariers*, voted at the general meeting on the treasurer's estimate of revenue and expenditure, with an **exceptional *talha*** available from an exceptional general meeting when ordinary revenue failed. On non-payment, enforcement ran in two stages: the parier's **wheat dividend was sold** against the debt, and then his **uchau was seized and sold at auction** to bring in outside capital.

**Erzgebirge — the *Zubusse*.** A periodic call on the holders of *Kuxe* when the mine ran at a loss, with the share **forfeited to the other holders** if the call went unpaid.

Periodic call, voted or triggered by need; forfeiture of the claim as the sanction; the forfeited claim recycled to new capital. **Structurally these are one device.**

## The rule, which is opposite

**The Bazacle capped the exposure, and said so in terms.** The Castel statute of **1417** provided that the company could demand no **more from one parier than the value of his share in the mills** ⚠️ — reported in Occitan as *aytant o plus que no val la part que an en los ditz molis*, transcribed here at one remove and to be checked against the statute. Liability was **strictly limited to the value of the share**, and there is no evidence of creditors pursuing personal assets in distress.

**The Kux did not.** Holders were **personally responsible for the enterprise's losses**, not capped at the sum subscribed — with forfeiture operating as an additional enforcement rather than as the boundary of exposure. ⚠️ **This side of the contrast rests on a secondary web survey and is the weaker half.** See [[The Kux carries transferability with unlimited liability]].

## Why the difference is not a detail

**Because forfeiture does double duty, and which duty it is doing is invisible from the mechanism.**

Where liability is capped, forfeiture *is* the whole sanction: losing the share is the worst that can happen, so the call is effectively an option to abandon. **That is limited liability implemented without a liability rule** — and the Bazacle then wrote the rule down anyway.

Where liability is uncapped, forfeiture is merely the first step and the holder remains exposed beyond it. **The same procedure, read off the same documents, means two different things.**

## The coding consequence, which is the point

**Coding these two from mechanism similarity would have produced a false agreement on the census's most theoretically loaded column.** Both rows would show a periodic call and forfeiture; only a reading of the *stated rule* separates them — and the Bazacle's rule is stated, in a statute, with a date.

This is [[The coding commons must record stated rationale not only component presence]] with a worked case attached, and it is the sharpest one the project has: **the component is present in both and the rationale is opposite.**

It also cuts against the standing worry in [[Count degrees of freedom not cells]] from the other direction. Here two characteristics that might have been suspected of redundancy — the call mechanism and the liability rule — are demonstrably independent, because one takes both values while the other is held constant. **That is discriminant validity established by cases rather than argued from the desk**, which `CHARACTER-CODING.md` test 4 says is the only way it can be established.

## What the pair buys, if it survives checking

A **near-minimal pair**: mechanism held constant, liability rule varying, in two mining-and-milling enterprises of roughly the same era in different legal families. The census has few of these and they are what it is for.

⚠️ **And it may not survive.** The secondary account of the *Kux* notes that enforcement of personal liability "proved inconsistent". If in practice the Kux holder's exposure was bounded by forfeiture too, the pair collapses and the two cases agree after all — which would be a different and also useful result. **Graulau is held and unread; this is what to read her for.**
