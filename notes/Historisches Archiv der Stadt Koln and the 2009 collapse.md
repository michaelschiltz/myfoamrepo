# Historisches Archiv der Stadt Köln and the 2009 collapse

<https://www.stadt-koeln.de/>

**The fourth catastrophic loss in the survey and the only one still in progress** — and the one that will teach the project most, because it is a loss whose *repair* is documented in detail.

## What happened, in figures

On **3 March 2009** the archive building collapsed, burying almost the entire holdings of one of the oldest and richest municipal archives in Europe — the record of more than a thousand years of civic, regional and church history.

- Roughly **30 kilometres** of shelving.
- Recovery yielded **45,054 "Cologne metres"** — about 270,000 archival boxes — distributed across **1,255,724 recovery units**.
- Initial recovery and identification was completed at the end of **2021**.
- Restoration is estimated at **6,000 to 6,500 person-years**, requiring more than 200 people in continuous employment, to reach full usability **within thirty years**.
- Of the audiovisual material about 60% and of the image carriers about 55% proved salvageable, much of it severely damaged.

## Why this belongs in the register as a methodological case

Three losses have already been recorded — Naples 1943, Ypres 1914, Paris 1871 — and the rule from each was that **a filter with no shape licenses no inference**. Cologne adds something the others cannot.

**The reconstruction is itself a selection, and unlike Paris's it is documented as it happens.** What gets restored first is decided by condition, by fragility, by conservation cost and by perceived research demand — and none of those correlates with what the material is about. **A researcher working Cologne in 2035 will be working a corpus shaped by a restoration queue**, and unlike the Paris *état civil reconstitué* the queue is knowable.

That is worth something. **Where the selection rule is recorded, the selection can be corrected for**; where it is not, it cannot. [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] usually has to be applied blind. Here it need not be — and saying so is the difference between a caveat and a method.

## What it costs the project directly

Cologne was a Rhineland commercial centre and one of the eleven [[Hanse documents on the Memory of the World register]] archives. Its *Schreinsbücher* — the civic property registers, among the oldest in Europe — are in the affected holdings. ⚠️ Establish what of the medieval commercial material is currently usable before planning anything; the answer changes every year, which is itself unusual.
