# Deutsches Zeitungsportal — the German full-text layer

<https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/newspaper>

The DDB's historical press portal, launched 2021, giving central access to digitised German newspapers **from 1671 to 1950**, full-text searchable and **free**.

## The four-country comparison, which is now complete enough to state

| Corpus | Terms |
|---|---|
| Delpher (NL) | Free; 1618–1879 texts downloadable as open data |
| Deutsches Zeitungsportal (DE) | Free; from 1671 |
| BelgicaPress (BE) | Free |
| RetroNews (FR) | **Subscription** |

**Three of four national libraries give the historical press away and one charges for it.** The variation is in library policy, not in the press, and the operational rule follows unchanged from [[The VOC-EIC digital asymmetry is an access regime not a survival difference]]: **no frequency claim may cross these corpora.**

## What a German press corpus from 1671 is good for

The German commercial press carried *Kursblätter* and price currents, shipping and fair intelligence, and the public notices of failure. **For Hamburg specifically it is the published counterpart of the insurance and bottomry business** whose private side is at [[Staatsarchiv Hamburg]].

⚠️ Coverage before 1750 is thin everywhere in Europe and the German portal is an aggregation of what libraries happened to digitise, so its early decades are a sample of a sample. **Existence claims survive that; frequency claims do not.**

Bavaria separately runs **digiPress**, its own newspaper portal ⚠️ — a reminder that in Germany even the full-text layer is federated.
