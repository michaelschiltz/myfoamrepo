# JTS Library — the Elkan Nathan Adler Geniza collection

The Library of the Jewish Theological Seminary, New York. **About 43,000 Geniza fragments**, mostly from the collection Elkan Nathan Adler bought in Cairo in 1896; shelfmarks `ENA`, `ENA NS`, and `MS`. **The second-largest Geniza collection.**

## Layer — and the images are not in New York

- **Imaged in 2005–2006** by JTS with the Friedberg Genizah Project; shown on the Friedberg site from 2008, moved to Princeton's Geniza Lab in 2017, and now at **Princeton University Library's Digital PUL** — <https://dpul.princeton.edu/cairo_geniza> — **IIIF**. The Digital PUL collection lists 25,453 `ENA` items, 148 `ENA NS` and 1,063 other JTS `MS` items.
- **Rights formula required: "Courtesy of the Jewish Theological Seminary Library"** with the full shelfmark.
- ⚠️ The gap between 43,000 fragments and about 26,700 image items is probably one of counting unit (fragment against folder or item) and was not resolved.

**The collection's owner and its image host are different institutions in different states**, which is the Geniza's condition in miniature: the physical collection is where a dealer sold it, the digital one is where a research group put it.

## What it holds for the census

**`ENA` shelfmarks appear in the partnership literature** — Ackerman-Lieberman cites `ENA NS 17.35` among partnerships contracted for a year or more. See [[Geniza partnership documents - where the census's isqa and qirad evidence sits]].

## The honest assessment

**No trip needed for anything the census asks.** Consult through Digital PUL and Ktiv; request physical examination only for a specific fragment.
