# Kansallisarkisto — Finland

<https://www.kansallisarkisto.fi/>

The National Archives of Finland, with an active digitisation programme. ⚠️ Scale, coverage and reuse terms unverified.

## The honest placement

**Finland was part of Sweden until 1809 and then a Russian grand duchy**, so the state record of Finnish commerce for this project's period is largely in Stockholm — see [[Riksarkivet Sweden - the national archives and the AI lab]]. What is in Helsinki is the local and regional record, and the imperial-period material.

For the census's questions Finland is **a secondary site and this note says so.** Its tar, timber and shipping trades were real but were conducted through Swedish institutions and Baltic intermediaries; the institutional forms are Swedish.

⚠️ One thing worth checking rather than assuming: whether the Finnish coastal towns' *skeppsredare* partnerships — Baltic ship part-ownership — generated a documentary series comparable to the Dutch *partenrederij*. **If they did, this becomes a third leg of a comparison that currently has two**, and the cost of asking is a single enquiry.
