# Polyandry is pooling under an absorbing barrier

**Tarpy 2003 found that colonies of genetically diverse workers had the same mean disease prevalence, mass and final population as genetically uniform ones, lower variance, and fewer failures. Same mean, lower variance, fewer failures is the signature of the barrier route, not of the multiplicative one: pooling under an absorbing barrier lowers the chance of ruin without moving the mean. Patrilines are the colony's separate bottoms.**

## The evidence

† Tarpy 2003, *Proc. R. Soc. B* 270: 99–103 (chalkbrood challenge; 18 and 8 colonies): no difference in means; difference in the variance of disease prevalence, brood viability, hygienic behaviour and final population; polyandry framed as bet-hedging after Gillespie 1976, and monandrous colonies "are more likely to fail". Seeley and Tarpy 2007 found lower mean disease intensity under American foulbrood, so in that system the mean moves too. Soper, Ekroth and Martins 2021: direct evidence only in eusocial Hymenoptera.

## The reading

- **Barrier route.** Colony death is absorbing. Diverse patrilines lower the probability that one pathogen takes the whole colony, which is [[Under absorption pooling raises the probability of ruin to the power of the pool]] in entomological form.
- **Multiplicative route.** Tarpy's own frame, Gillespie's bet-hedging, is a geometric-mean argument, so polyandry runs on both routes at once.
- **The condition.** The gain depends on susceptibilities being uncorrelated across patrilines. A pathogen able to infect every patriline removes it. This is [[Diversification runs across bottoms not across partners]]: ten drones of one susceptibility are one bottom.

## Use

Illustrative, not evidential, for the institutional argument (hive report §§3.4, 7.3). It is the cleanest biological case of the merchant's rule "my ventures are not in one bottom trusted", and it shows the rule with its condition attached, which the proverbs omit ([[The proverbs of diversification state the rule and omit its conditions]]).
