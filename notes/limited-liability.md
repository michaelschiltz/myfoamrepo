# limited-liability

Owner shielding: the cap that insulates a member's personal patrimony from the firm's creditors — the mirror image of [[Entity-shielding|entity shielding]], which insulates firm assets from the members' personal creditors. Together the two partitions convert an individual's multiplicative, time-series exposure into a compartmentalized barrier, so that failure in one cell need not be terminal for the whole. The unlimited-liability partnership forms (fraterna, compagnia) are the unpartitioned baseline against which this cap is the innovation.
