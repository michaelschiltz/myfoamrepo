# Gemeentearchief Zaanstad — the partenrederij country

<https://archief.zaanstad.nl/>

**The Zaan region is where ship part-ownership was the ordinary way to own a ship**, and `partenrederij` is a coded row in `organizational_forms` sourced from a single scholar's reading of Low Countries commercial law. This archive is where the instruments are.

## The holdings

**`OA-0020` Oud Notarieel Archief Zaandam** and **`OA-0170` Nieuw Notarieel archief Zaandam** ⚠️ *date ranges to transcribe*. The notarial protocols are the series; part-ownership contracts, *boekhouder* appointments and the sale of *parten* are notarial acts.

The archive also runs a *beeldbank* and participates in the aggregated search at archieven.nl. ⚠️ Imaging status of the notarial series unverified.

## Why it matters more than its size suggests

The census's `partenrederij` row and both Antwerp rows **share their author**, and the vault has already recorded that they must not be treated as independent witnesses to anything they agree on. A row resting on one scholar's reading is a row whose evidence has a single point of failure.

**Going to the notarial series is how that is repaired** — not by finding a second author who has read the same literature, but by reading the instruments the literature is about. That is the distinction [[Independence of apparatus is not independence of question]] draws, and it is the reason a small municipal archive earns a note here while several larger ones do not.

⚠️ The volume of relevant acts is unknown, and the Zaan notarial series is not in the Amsterdam HTR corpus, so this is manual reading in early modern Dutch hands. Cost it accordingly, and check first whether the Amsterdam models transfer — see [[Stadsarchief Amsterdam - the notarial archive]].
