# An absorbing barrier breaks ergodicity by construction

An absorbing set is invariant by definition — once entered it is never left, so the dynamics cannot carry the trajectory out of it. Two or more absorbing states therefore render the invariant σ-algebra non-trivial *definitionally*, which is ergodicity failure in the strict Birkhoff sense, obtained without analogy and without argument. See [[Ergodicity is indecomposability not randomness]].

This is the strongest formal card the project holds, and it is stronger than the one the ergodicity-economics literature usually plays. The geometric Brownian motion case requires the careful restatement in [[Nothing in the ergodic theorem fails in geometric Brownian motion]], because there the underlying increments are perfectly well behaved and the failure is one of aggregation. The absorbing case requires no such care: the state space is genuinely decomposed, and the referee who wants to object must object to the definition. **Where the historical argument can be run off ruin rather than off variance, it should be.**

The connection to the corporate-form limb is then structural rather than metaphorical. [[Entity-shielding]] does not reduce the barrier; it *relocates* it, removing absorption from the natural person's trajectory and placing it on a separable legal person whose extinction the member survives. In the vocabulary of the previous paragraph, shielding changes which trajectory the invariant set is attached to. That is why the shielded and the unshielded agent face formally different problems rather than the same problem at different scale — see [[The real cut is shielded-and-pooled versus unshielded]] and [[The ensemble average is the local descriptor of a barrier-shielded agent]].

One qualification to carry rather than bury. A process with a single absorbing state has a unique invariant law concentrated on it, so the decomposition claim needs at least two absorbing states, or should be stated over the *killed* process, or over the invariant σ-algebra of the path-space shift. The loose formulation is defensible; the tight one should be in the footnote before a referee supplies it.
