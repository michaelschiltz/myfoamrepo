# A refutation that shares the estimand inherits the defect

The critique of the critics, and the guardrail that keeps the WP3 diagnostic from degrading
into a debunking tool.

## The claim

[[The three Kelly critiques establish different nulls]] establishes that the project's
objection is about the **estimand** and not the estimator: the space-for-time substitution
was never licensed, and the quantity would remain unidentified on a spatially orthogonal
grid with perfect standard errors. That statement has a consequence which is easy to miss
and expensive to have pointed out.

**If a cross-sectional contrast is not a propagation parameter, then a study reporting that
the same contrast is small, unstable or insignificant has not established a null about
propagation either.** Failing to reject on an unlicensed estimand is not evidence that
nothing happened along the trajectories. The defect is a property of the quantity, so every
party who computes that quantity inherits it, whatever sign they report.

The framework therefore cannot recruit the empirical critics as allies. "Even the sceptics
find nothing" concedes the estimand in the act of citing it.

## The mita, where all five positions share it

- **Arroyo Abad and Maurer (2025)** report no significant differences in nineteenth- to
  twenty-first-century literacy, land access, road density or luminosity. Every one of those
  is a district-level ensemble statistic. Their own preferred mechanism for the dissipation
  is out-migration — which is the absorbing flow of
  [[Persistence samples are conditioned on non-absorption]], and which removes the damaged
  trajectories from the cells being averaged. Their evidence is better support for the
  objection than for their conclusion: it tells us the units mixed, not that the units were
  unharmed.
- **Kelly** corrects the standard error. On his own account the finding becomes
  indistinguishable from spatial noise; the quantity it estimates is untouched.
- **Keele and Titiunik (2015)** repair identification by estimating an effect at each
  boundary point rather than pooling into segment fixed effects. That makes the design a
  better-identified comparison *between the two sides*, and no closer to a within-society
  propagation parameter.
- **Karakas** relaxes functional form. More flexible estimation of an unlicensed quantity is
  still an unlicensed quantity, and the widened interval is a fact about the estimator.
- **Bisin and Moro (2021)** ask *whose* effect is identified. A complier-specific level
  contrast is still a level contrast.

Five attacks on four different things, none of them the estimand — catalogued at
[[The mita critiques establish five nulls and none is the estimand]] — and all five
computed on the object the framework says was never licensed.

## Why this is a cost and not a rhetorical win

Stated carelessly the note reads as the framework claiming to beat everybody. It does the
opposite: it forfeits the cheapest available support. Placed in the ordering of
[[Sort the persistence objections by what they cost the referee]], this belongs with the
concessions made early rather than the positive claims, because a referee who notices it
first will use it to argue that the criterion is unfalsifiable — that it is constructed so
that no evidence in the literature can bear on it in either direction.

The answer to that charge is the pre-committed case: a canonical persistence result named in
advance whose relevant sub-process is expected to prove ergodic, so that the substitution is
licensed and the inference stands. That commitment is what makes the present note a
symmetric criterion rather than a machine for producing negatives, and it must be recorded
before the re-analyses are run, not after. Non-ergodicity is model-specific and has to be
established case by case; asserting it everywhere is the mirror of assuming it nowhere, and
is no better — the same discipline
[[Attractor selection bounds what a persistence design can resolve]] imposes on its own
dichotomy.

## What a refutation would have to look like

The evidence that bears on the objection is evidence about trajectories, not about better
cross-sections.

- **An intermediate observation.** Any measurement between treatment and outcome
  discriminates transmission from attractor selection, which a two-date design cannot. For
  the mita this means tribute assessments, community-chest and *cofradía* accounts, tithe
  series — anything giving a middle.
- **Exit measured as a hazard rather than as attrition.** Carpio and Guerrero's surname
  deficit is the raw material; what is missing is the absorption process modelled rather
  than reported, which is what
  [[Endogenised absorption has a sign]] requires before the bias can be signed at all.
- **A mixing test.** Did the two sides exchange population, capital or institutional form
  after 1600? If they did not, the decomposition is reducible and the point is made without
  re-estimating anything.

## The guardrail

**Never cite an ensemble null as evidence for non-ergodicity.** It is the same error as
citing an ensemble mean as evidence about a trajectory, run backwards, and it is more
tempting because the null appears to be on the project's side. The claim licensed by an
ensemble null is narrow: that the cross-sectional contrast has decayed. Whether the
underlying trajectories diverged, converged, or were truncated is exactly what it cannot
say — [[Refusals are observations of the filter not inferences from survivors]] states the
general form of the move for evidence of absence.
