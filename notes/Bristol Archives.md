# Bristol Archives

Bristol was England's second port for much of the early modern period, and its archive holds the Society of Merchant Venturers' world, the Atlantic trade and the slave trade.

## What matters

**The Society of Merchant Venturers** — a chartered merchant corporation with its own archive, governing Bristol's trade from the sixteenth century. **Another member of the merchant-corporation family**, and another instance of a body that keeps its own record. See [[Guildhall Library and the City livery companies]] for the English side of that family and [[Chambre de commerce de Marseille - the 1599 archive]] for the closest continental parallel.

⚠️ Whether the Society's archive is deposited or retained is unestablished, and on the pattern this survey keeps finding, retained is likelier.

**The port books and the merchant papers** for the Atlantic and slaving voyages — the English counterpart of the Nantes *armement* material at [[Archives departementales de la Loire-Atlantique - Nantes]], with the same statement to make about what the structure was used for.

⚠️ Series and extents unverified throughout.
