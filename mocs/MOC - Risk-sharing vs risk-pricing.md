# MOC — Risk-sharing vs risk-pricing

Thematic hub for the instrument axis: risk-*sharing* forms that keep every party bound to the realized trajectory ([[Skin in the game]]) versus risk-*pricing* forms that turn peril into a tradable premium and begin decoupling payoff from participation.
