# MOC — Spanish archives

Country hub under [[MOC - European archives and digital collections]], which carries the three-layer frame, the funding-map argument and the register schema. **Infrastructure layer, not argument layer.**

## The structural fact

**Archival competence in Spain is devolved, so no single catalogue covers the country.** [[PARES - the access layer for the Spanish state archives]] holds the archives the Ministry of Culture runs. Outside it: Andalusia catalogues the Sevilla and Cádiz provincial archives itself, Bizkaia runs its own ARIT application, Valencia and the Balearics run their own portals — and the richest notarial archive of all is held by a **professional corporation** and appears in no public system at all.

**A PARES search that returns nothing is evidence about the Ministry's remit, not about Spain.** That is the `isqa` search-failure rule arriving through an administrative door rather than a bibliographic one.

## The Atlantic complex

- [[Archivo General de Indias - Sevilla]] — `CONTRATACION` (1492–1795, 6,337 legajos, 51 series, partially digitised), `CONSULADOS`, `CONTADURIA`, `INDIFERENTE GENERAL`. The *avería* strand
- [[Archivo Historico Provincial de Sevilla - notarial protocols]] — the instruments behind the institution
- [[Archivo Historico Provincial de Cadiz - notarial protocols and the Consulado]] — the Carrera after the 1717 move
- [[Archivo General de Simancas]] — the fiscal archive; the Philip II *asientos*
- [[Archivo Historico Nacional - Madrid]] — councils, charters, the eighteenth-century privileged companies

## The Crown of Aragon

- [[Archivo de la Corona de Aragon - Barcelona]] — the legal and institutional frame, from the twelfth century
- [[Arxiu Historic de Protocols de Barcelona]] — the *comandes*; corporation-held, outside every state catalogue, images only under a research-and-teaching licence
- [[Arxiu del Regne de Mallorca]] — fifteenth-century marine insurance in volume
- [[Arxiu del Regne de Valencia]] — the third royal archive; note the municipal split over the *Taula de Canvis*

## The consulado towns

- [[Archivo Foral de Bizkaia - the Consulado de Bilbao]] — the Ordenanzas de Bilbao, a merchant body codifying its own rules
- [[Consulado de Burgos - the universidad de mercaderes]] — insurance ordinances of 1538, and an uncoded identity-wrapper at Bruges. Records dispersed; custodial map not yet drawn

## Litigation

- [[Real Chancilleria de Valladolid]] — `Pleitos civiles`. Added on the second pass: the litigation category arrived out of the Dutch and Italian surveys and had no place in the first Spanish scheme

## Open questions

- **Burgos is the weakest placement.** No consolidated archive; the custodial map is a desk task and should precede any trip
- **IIIF and bulk download** unknown for every Spanish portal
- The **Real Compañía Guipuzcoana** records are split across AHN, AGI and Basque custody, and the split is unestablished
- Reproduction rights are unfilled for every row
