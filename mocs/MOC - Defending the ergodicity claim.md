# MOC — Defending the ergodicity claim

Sub-hub of [[MOC - Ergodicity and the time-ensemble distinction]], holding the referee-facing material: what the framework may claim, what it must concede, where the critique lands, and what must not be said. The parent hub holds the apparatus; this one holds the brief.

**Ordering principle, taken from [[Sort the persistence objections by what they cost the referee]].** Arguments are sorted by *what the reader must concede before they work*, not by where they came from or how strong they feel. Framework-dependent arguments go last, because leading with one hands the falsifiability charge a second target and forfeits everything portable. Provenance belongs in a note's `Source`, never in the ordering.

## Tier 0 — costs the reader nothing

Corrections a mathematically literate referee would make anyway. None requires a line of ergodicity economics, and getting them wrong forfeits the exchange before the argument starts.

- [[Ergodicity is indecomposability not randomness]] — time average equals ensemble average is a *consequence* of ergodicity, not its definition; the slogan runs it backwards
- [[Stationarity is a precondition of ergodicity not a corollary]]
- [[The word ergodic carries four incompatible senses]] — press the claim in the econometric sense, the one the tradition already grants can fail
- [[Distinguish strong from weak ergodicity breaking]] — most historical cases are weak, and have been written as though strong

## Tier 1 — concessions to make first

Given away openly, they cost nothing defensible and purchase the credibility the real claim needs. Extracted by a referee instead, they cost the manuscript.

- [[Nothing in the ergodic theorem fails in geometric Brownian motion]] — the mathematics is Itô plus an inequality from 1906, and the growth-rate result predates the programme by decades
- [[Jensen supplies the gap but only the dynamic privileges the logarithm]] — and the theory of means positively licenses the expected-utility patch, so Kolmogorov must be routed elsewhere
- [[Route Kolmogorov to ergodic theory not the theory of means]] — the hygiene framing: physics did not declare ensemble averages bad, it stated its conditions

## Tier 2 — the target, narrowly specified

Not "economics ignores time" — that loses in one move. These locate the disagreement at an address the interlocutor can be asked to defend.

- [[Arrow-Debreu dissolves time rather than neglecting it]] — dated commodities render the intertemporal economy formally static, so the averaging question is never posed
- [[The representative agent is the ergodicity assumption in costume]] — where the equivalence is installed in models that *do* iterate, plus the answer to the stationary-distribution reply
- [[The split is over whether the explanandum is a state or a trajectory]] — the hinge is what is taken to require explanation

## Tier 3 — the positive claims

- [[An absorbing barrier breaks ergodicity by construction]] — the strongest formal card: decomposition obtained by definition, not by analogy. Where the argument can run off ruin rather than off variance, it should
- [[Ergodicity breaking and order-dependence are independent axes]] — the two clusters are orthogonal, with a witness in each direction; absorption is the cell that does both jobs
- [[Restate the cost puzzle as a question about a trajectory]] — the operative move on the "why X despite its cost" class
- [[Cooperation is an averaging puzzle and sovereign repayment is a barrier puzzle]] — the showcase cases are dissolved by different mechanisms and make different evidential demands

## Tier 4 — guardrails

What must not be claimed. Each exists because the overreach it forbids is tempting and fatal.

- [[Do not claim history-dependence as the novelty]] — only two claims survive contact with hysteresis, multiple equilibria and critical junctures
- [[Separate the sociological and the methodological claims about economics]] — welding them asserts a base-superstructure story and calls co-emergence a mechanism
- [[Time-average optimization is a survivorship property not an intention]] — any sentence implying premodern actors computed growth rates will be destroyed

## Known vulnerabilities

Named here so they are answered in text rather than found by a panel.

- **The which-dynamic problem.** Specifying the dynamic is formally as free as specifying the utility function; the apparatus has relocated the arbitrariness, not removed it. The answer — dynamics are observable, preferences are not — is weaker for historical cases, where the dynamic is inferred from the same evidence the argument then explains. Stated at [[Jensen supplies the gap but only the dynamic privileges the logarithm]] and flagged there as live for WP3.
- **Strong/weak slippage.** Using "non-ergodic" unqualified claims the rhetorical force of decomposition on evidence that supports only timescale separation. This is exactly where a referee will locate the slippage; see [[Distinguish strong from weak ergodicity breaking]].
- **Peters's reply to Doctor, Wakker and Wang is unread.** Paywalled beyond its opening line; nothing in the vault should characterise its argument until the text is obtained. See [[Doctor Wakker and Wang 2020 on the ergodicity problem]].
- **Three citation allegations against Peters are unchecked**, one of them serious. Until they are, cite him for the framing and never for the intellectual history — [[Toda's citation allegations must be checked before we cite Peters]].
- **Samuelson (1971) and Latané (1979) are not in the library** and are load-bearing on both sides of the Ford–Kay exchange. Acquire before writing on the finite-horizon question.

## The Ford–Kay exchange

The most serious of the three, and the only one that produced a real argument: a published critique, an eleven-author reply from the ergodicity-economics group, and a rejoinder in which the critics concede an error of their own. Read all three before citing any.

- [[Ford and Kay 2023 on growth-optimal decision making]] — the source note for the full exchange, including the strongest pro-EE argument in the literature: EUT agents maximise *expected* utility while losing actual utility over time
- [[Ford and Kay concede the condition under which their objection lapses]] — the finding. Their central charge is conditional, they state its condition, and the historical case satisfies it because the dynamic is in the archive
- [[EE cannot rank across dynamics and the comparative project must not need it to]] — the one objection that bites on the comparative apparatus, and the three prohibitions it imposes
- [[The three published critiques do not agree with each other]] — Toda and Ford–Kay make incompatible charges, and the incompatibility is the framework's own thesis seen from outside

## The Toda critique

The second published attack, and the more dangerous, because its charge — unfalsifiability — would implicate any application of the framework if it held.

- [[Toda 2023 on ergodicity economics as pseudoscience]] — the source note, including the six "indicators" item by item and which of them to ignore
- [[Toda's own example makes EE more falsifiable than expected utility]] — the decisive reply, taken from a parenthesis in his own §IV: EE issues a point prediction, EUT one up to an unobservable free parameter
- [[The unfalsifiability charge is really a scope-and-productivity charge]] — the title and the argument come apart; the framework he needs is Lakatos, and its obligations go undischarged
- [[The selective claim is falsifiable where the prescriptive claim is not]] — the constructive half, and the one for the WP3 text
- [[Toda's citation allegations must be checked before we cite Peters]] — the part that could damage us directly

## The Doctor–Wakker–Wang exchange

The leading published critique, answered here. The source note carries what they argue and what must be conceded; the four replies take one section each.

- [[Doctor Wakker and Wang 2020 on the ergodicity problem]] — the source note. The substance is in the freely available Supplementary Information, not the one-page article; three of their six sections land and are conceded there
- [[The published critique never engages absorption]] — the structural finding: eleven pages on the variance limb, nothing on ruin, and their own decisive example turns on it
- [[The Wakker counterexample refutes expected log utility too]] — running the arithmetic out shows the counterexample indicting the theory it was written to defend
- [[The growth-rate criterion is asymptotic and the horizon comparison cuts both ways]] — the concession, and why the framework is committed to it
- [[Defending expected utility by axiomatics forfeits its normative claim]] — §§1–2 are correct and self-defeating together
- [[Answering EU's failures with more psychology instantiates the complaint]] — §5 demonstrates the movement it is offered against
