# MOC — Italian archives

Country hub under [[MOC - European archives and digital collections]]. **Infrastructure layer, not argument layer.**

## The structural fact

The mirror of Spain's. **Custody is mostly national; the catalogues are not unified; and the images sit outside all of them.** SIAS describes the state archives, SIUSA the bank, corporate and ecclesiastical ones, SAN aggregates both badly, the *Guida generale* is often the only description a series has — and each archive publishes its own images on its own site.

**A bank archive searched only in SIAS does not appear to exist.** There is no Italian layer 3 outside individual projects.

## The maritime and notarial core

- [[Archivio di Stato di Venezia]] — `Notarile`, `Giudici di Petizion`, `Cinque Savi alla Mercanzia`. The *colleganza* and the *fraterna*
- [[Archivio di Stato di Genova]] — *Notai Antichi*, the earliest notarial registers in Europe
- [[Archivio di Stato di Pisa]] — the *Constitutum Usus* world; Genoa's twelfth-century rival, which makes divergence between them informative
- [[Archivio di Stato di Firenze]] — `Notarile Antecosimiano`, `Mercanzia`, `Arti`, `Monte Comune`, `Monte delle Doti`
- [[Archivio di Stato di Prato - the Datini archive]] — one firm observed as a running system
- [[Archivio di Stato di Lucca]] — a corroborating site, not a primary one
- [[Archivio di Stato di Palermo]] — where the Genoese and Catalan networks meet under an Aragonese crown

## Transferable claims on a perpetual fiscal body — four cases

- [[Casa di San Giorgio - the Genoese public debt archive]] — 1239–1863, c. 40,250 units, `Debito pubblico` from 1346; Felloni's complete inventory online
- [[Archivio di Stato di Roma - the luoghi di monte]] — `Luoghi di monte` 1532–1824, within the `Camerale` sections
- [[Archivio Storico del Banco di Napoli]] — the Fondo Apodissario, UNESCO Memory of the World
- [[Archivio di Stato di Milano - the Banco di Sant'Ambrogio]] — 1593, the Lombard construction

**Four independent constructions inside one legal culture.** Shared ancestral conditions are guaranteed, so the finding can only ever be parallelism; the right claim is about the conditions under which the form recurs, not about independence.

## Doctrine, banks, and the rest

- [[Archivio Apostolico Vaticano]] — the accommodations rather than the refusals. Severe access: no cameras, three units a day
- [[Archivio Storico del Monte dei Paschi di Siena]] — a *monte di pietà* of 1472 that never stopped operating
- [[Archivio Storico Assicurazioni Generali - Trieste]] — the terminal state, named so the earlier forms are not read as approximations to it
- [[Archivio di Stato di Napoli]] — the 1943 destruction, and what it does to arguments from absence
- [[Medici Archive Project - the BIA platform]] — a scholar-annotated selection, not a transcribed series; any count from it is a count of the project's attention

## Open questions

- **Felloni's San Giorgio database URL** was not surfaced and is the most valuable outstanding link in the Italian set
- The `Monte delle Doti` and the Venetian *muda* are parked as candidate forms in the codebooks repo, logbook 2, 2026-09-10
- Palermo, Lucca and Trieste are unverified throughout
