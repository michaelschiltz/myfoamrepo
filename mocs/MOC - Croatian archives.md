# MOC — Croatian archives

Country hub under [[MOC - European archives and digital collections]]. **Infrastructure layer, not argument layer.** The last European pass.

## The structural fact

**One national descriptive system over a network of state archives** — ARHiNET, run from Zagreb — which puts Croatia closer to the French model than the German: a single frame, many custodians. For a small country it works.

**But the structural fact that matters is not administrative.** [[Drzavni arhiv u Dubrovniku]] holds **the whole archive of a medieval commercial republic** — over 490 fonds, 8.5 km, and all 73 Republic-period fonds (1022–1808) on the UNESCO Memory of the World register. Every other repository in this survey holds what survived of an institution. **Dubrovnik holds the institution.**

**And the census already argues from it without having read it.** [[Ragusan collegantia is unilateral and Venetian collegantia is bilateral]] is an argued claim resting on secondary literature about instruments that sit, in series, complete, in one building. **That match between what the census asserts and what one repository holds is exact, and it is not exact anywhere else in Europe.**

## Dubrovnik

- [[Drzavni arhiv u Dubrovniku]]
- [[The Ragusan chancery and notarial series]] — *Diversa Cancellariae*, *Diversa Notariae*, **`Debita Notariae`**, *Testamenta*, *Lamenta*, *Acta Consilii Rogatorum*. **A public registration system, not a notary's private protocol** — which makes maritime obligations findable by series address rather than by research programme
- [[Ragusan maritime insurance]] — statute and instruments in the same building, for the same period ⚠️ unverified
- [[The Dubrovnik quarantine of 1377]] — the paradigm **hazard** institution: compulsory, collective, and with no fund at all
- [[The Ottoman documents at Dubrovnik]] — 15,000 of them, and the hinge to the Ottoman survey

## Dalmatia and the rest

- [[Drzavni arhiv u Zadru]] · [[Drzavni arhiv u Splitu and the Dalmatian notariates]] — **Venetian Dalmatia between Venice and Ragusa.** If Zadar's instruments follow the Venetian pattern the `collegantia` claim is about law; if the Ragusan, it is about the Adriatic
- [[Hrvatski drzavni arhiv - Zagreb]] — a secondary region, and the note says so
- [[Croatian archives online - ARHiNET and the access layer]]
- [[The Adriatic scope edge - Kotor and the modern borders]] — **the unit is the notariate, not the modern state**

## Three things this pass adds to the general argument

**The registration/protocol distinction is now general.** Ragusa is the fifth system in the survey where a public register does a notariate's work — with Lübeck, Leuven, Antwerp, the Swedish *tänkeböcker* and the Scottish registers of deeds. **A difference between two coded rows may be a difference in what the record type records**, and that should now be a standing check rather than a per-case flag.

**A third compulsory collective-good institution with no contributory fund.** With `averia_pool` and the Venetian *muda*, quarantine makes three. **That may be a cell the census cannot occupy**, and whether that is a gap or a correct exclusion is a selection-brief question.

**The border error, caught a second time.** Wallonia was the first instance; the Adriatic is the second and easier one to make. A survey organised by country produces country-shaped findings, and the countries were not there.
